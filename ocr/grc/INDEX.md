# Index of *Δάντου ὁ Ἅδης* (Dante's *Inferno* in Greek Verse)

**Source**: *Dante's Inferno, Translated into Greek Verse by Musurus Pasha, D.C.L.* (*Δάντου ὁ Ἅδης. Μετάφρασις Κωνσταντίνου Μουσούρου*). London: Williams and Norgate, 14 Henrietta Street, Covent Garden; Edinburgh: 20 South Frederick Street, 1882. Printed by Clayton & Co., Temple Printing Works, Bouverie Street, Fleet Street, London.  
**Characteristics**: Classical Katharevousa Greek in accentual twelve-syllable verse (*κεκανονισμένῃ Ἑλληνικῇ, ἀλλ᾽ εὐλήπτῳ*). Contains an extensive scholarly preface, an explicit printed Table of Contents (*Πίναξ Περιεχομένων*), two separate errata lists, the 34 Cantos (*ᾨδαί*), and comprehensive separate endnotes (*Σημειώσεις*).

---

## Overview and Pagination Offsets

Unlike the English Temple Classics edition, Musurus's edition features an **explicit printed Table of Contents** in the front matter ([009.txt](009.txt)–[013.txt](013.txt)), listing all 34 Cantos with their topical arguments and starting printed pages.

The relationship between file numbers (`NNN.txt`) and printed book page numbers is completely uniform throughout the entire volume:

1. **Front Matter ([001.txt](001.txt)–[020.txt](020.txt))**:
   - Title pages, preface (paginated in Greek alphabetic numerals: ε´–ζ´, pp. 5–7), Table of Contents (pp. [9]–13 / [θ´]–ιγ´), and two Corrigenda lists (the second paginated in Arabic numerals 1–4).
2. **Main Translation Text: Cantos I–XXXIV ([021.txt](021.txt)–[285.txt](285.txt))**:
   - Printed pages 1 through 265.
   - **Uniform Offset: +20** (`file_number = printed_page + 20`).
3. **Transition Leaves ([286.txt](286.txt)–[288.txt](288.txt))**:
   - [286.txt](286.txt) is the blank verso of page 265 ([p. 266]).
   - [287.txt](287.txt) is the section half-title for the Notes: *ΣΗΜΕΙΩΣΕΙΣ.* ([p. 267]).
   - [288.txt](288.txt) is the blank verso of the half-title ([p. 268]).
   - Both [286.txt](286.txt) and [288.txt](288.txt) contain no printed text and their transcriptions are intentionally empty.
4. **Explanatory Endnotes: *Σημειώσεις* ([289.txt](289.txt)–[351.txt](351.txt))**:
   - Printed pages 269 through 331.
   - **Uniform Offset: +20** (`file_number = printed_page + 20`).
   - Concludes on page 331 ([351.txt](351.txt)) with *ΤΕΛΟΣ ΤΩΝ ΣΗΜΕΙΩΣΕΩΝ* and the printer's colophon.

No scanned pages are omitted or duplicated; the +20 offset remains perfectly consistent across all 331 numbered pages of the book.

     `file_number = printed_page + 20`
   - This holds from printed page 1 ([021.txt](021.txt)) through the final printed page 331 ([351.txt](351.txt)). No scanned pages are missing or duplicated in this digitisation.
2. **Blank Leaves / Empty Transcriptions**:
   - [286.txt](286.txt) corresponds to unnumbered page [266] (the blank verso following the end of Canto XXXIV).
   - [288.txt](288.txt) corresponds to unnumbered page [268] (the blank verso of the *ΣΗΜΕΙΩΣΕΙΣ* half-title).
