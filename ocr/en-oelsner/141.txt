CANTO XII 129

**The Violent against their neighbours** replied: “Indeed he is alive, and solitary thus
have I to shew him the dark valley; necessity
brings him to it, and not sport.

From singing Alleluiah, came She who gave me
this new office; he is no robber, nor I a
thievish spirit.

But by that virtue through which I move my
steps on such a wild way, give us some one of
thine whom we may follow,

that he may shew us where the ford is, and carry
over him upon his back, for he is not a spirit
to go through the air.”

Chiron bent round on his right breast, and said
to Nessus: “Turn, and guide them then; and
if another troop encounter you, keep it off.”

We moved onwards with our trusty guide, along
the border of the purple boiling, wherein the
boiled were making loud shrieks.

**Tyrants** I saw people down in it even to the eyebrows;
and the great Centaur said: “These are
tyrants who took to blood and plunder.

Here they lament their merciless offences; here
is Alexander; and fierce Dionysius, who
made Sicily have years of woe;

and that brow which has the hair so black is
Azzolino; and that other, who is blonde, is
Obizzo of Este, who in verity
was quenched by his step-son up in the world.”

Then I turned me to the Poet, and he said:
“Let him be chief guide to thee now, and
me second.”

**Murderers** A little farther on, the Centaur paused beside a
people which, as far as the throat, seemed to
issue from that boiling stream.
