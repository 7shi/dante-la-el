INFERNO
FROM the arch of the bridge, to which his Guide
has carried him, Dante now sees the Diviners
Augurs, Sorcerers, &c., coming slowly along the bottom
of the Fourth Chasm. By help of their incantations
and evil agents, they had endeavoured to pry into the
Future which belongs to the Almighty alone, interfer-
ing with His secret decrees; and now their faces are
painfully twisted the contrary way; and, being unable
to look before them, they are forced to walk backwards
(1-30). The first that Virgil names is Amphiaräus;
then Tiresias the Theban prophet, Aruns the Tuscan
