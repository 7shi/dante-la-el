INFERNO

THIS Ninth and Last, or frozen Circle, lowest part
of the Universe, and farthest remote from the
Source of all light and heat, divides itself into four
concentric Rings. The First or outermost is the
Caïna, which has its name from Cain who slew his
brother Abel, and contains the sinners who have done
violence to their own kindred. The Second or Antenora,
so called "from Antenor the Trojan, betrayer of his
country" (Pietro di Dante, &c.), is filled with those
who have been guilty of treachery against their native
land. Dante finds many of his own countrymen,
