**The madness of Athamas and of Hecuba** Athamas grew so insane, that he, seeing his
wife, with two sons, [go] laden on either
hand,

cried: "Spread we the nets, that I may take
the lioness and her young lions at the pass";
and then stretched out his pitiless talons,

grasping the one who had the name Learchus;
and hurled him, and dashed him on a rock;
and she with her other burden drowned herself.

And when Fortune brought low the all-daring
pride of the Trojans, so that the King together
with his kingdom was blotted out;

Hecuba, sad, miserable, and captive, after she
had seen Polyxena slain, and, forlorn, dis-
cerned her Polydorus,

on the sea-strand, she, out of her senses, barked
like a dog: to such a degree had [the]
sorrow wrung her soul.
