**The Simonists** O Simon Magus! O wretched followers of his
and robbers ye, who prostitute the things of God,
that should be wedded unto righteousness,

for gold and silver! now must the trump
sound for you; for ye are in the third
chasm.

Already we had mounted to the following grave,
on that part of the cliff which hangs right
over the middle of the foss.

O Wisdom Supreme, what art thou shewest in
heaven, on earth and in the evil world, and
how justly thy Goodness dispenses!

**Their punishment** I saw the livid stone, on the sides and
bottom, full of holes, all of one breadth; and
each was round.

Not less wide they seemed to me, nor larger,
than those that are in my beauteous San
