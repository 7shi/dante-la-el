**The lustful** While we were thus advancing, one in front of the
other, along the brink, often the good Master
said: “Give heed, let my skill avail thee.”

On my right shoulder the sun was beating, that
already with his rays was changing the whole
face of the west from azure to white;

and with my shadow, ruddier I made the flames
appear, and even at so slight a sign many
shades I saw, as they passed, give heed.

This was the cause which gave them an opening to
speak of me; and one to the other they began
to say: “He doth not seem a shadowy body.”

Then certain of them made towards me, so far as
they could, ever on their guard not to come
forth where they would not be burned.
