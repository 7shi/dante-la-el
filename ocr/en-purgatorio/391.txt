**The Divine Pageant** When the wain of the first heaven which setting
nor rising never knew, nor veil of other mist
than of sin,

and which made there each one aware of his
duty, even as the lower wain guides him who
turns the helm to come into port,

had stopped still, the people of truth, who had
first come between the grifon and it, turned
them to the car as to their peace;

and one of them as if sent from heaven “*Veni*
*sponsa de Libano*” did shout thrice in song,
and all the others after him.
