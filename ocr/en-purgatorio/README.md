# English and Italian text: Purgatorio

Planned, not started. This directory is to contain the page images and the OCR
transcriptions of *The Purgatorio of Dante Alighieri* in The Temple Classics
(London: J. M. Dent & Co.; first edition November 1901, the scan below the
fourth, November 1904), the companion volume of [../en-oelsner/](../en-oelsner/)
(the *Inferno*), in the same layout: the Italian and the English face each
other tercet by tercet.

By the closing note of the series editor (I. G., Israel Gollancz, October 1901):

- the Italian text and the notes are by H. Oelsner;
- the English prose translation is by Thomas Okey (Carlyle translated only the
  *Inferno*);
- the arguments are by Philip H. Wicksteed.
