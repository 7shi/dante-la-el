In order to save time, Cæsar left the siege of Marseilles, on which he had been engaged, in the hands of Brutus, and rushed off to Ilerda (the modern Lerida) in Catalonia, where he defeated Afranius and Petreius, the lieutenants of Pompey (b.c. 49.) Lucan (Phars. i. 151-154), speaks of Cæsar as a thunderbolt.
