As when a ray of light leaps from the water or from the mirror to the opposite direction, ascending at an angle similar

to that at which it descends, and departs as far from the line of the falling stone in an equal space, even as experiment and science shows,

so I seemed to be smitten by reflected light in front of me, wherefore mine eyes were swift to flee.

“What is that, sweet Father, from which I cannot screen my sight so that it may avail me,” said I, “and seems to be moving towards us?”

“Marvel thou not if the heavenly household yet dazes thee,” he answered me, “’tis a messenger that cometh to invite us to ascend.

Soon will it be that to behold these things shall not be grievous to thee, but shall be a joy to thee, as great as nature hath fitted thee to feel.”

When we had reached the blessed angel, with gladsome voice, he said: “Enter here to a stairway far less steep than the others.”

We were mounting, already departed thence, and “Beati misericordes” was sung behind, and “Rejoice thou that overcomest.”

My Master and I, alone we two, were mounting up, and I thought while journeying to gain profit from his words;

and I directed me to him thus asking: “What meant the spirit from Romagna by mentioning ‘exclusion’ and ‘partnership’?”
