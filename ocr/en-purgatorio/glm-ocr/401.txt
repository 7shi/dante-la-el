83, 84. See Ps. xxxi. 1-8: "In thee, O Lord, do I put my trust . . . thou hast set my feet in a large room."

85-90. These lines describe the snow on the ridges of the Apennines, first congealed, when the winds blow from the north; and then dissolved, at the time of the warm and gentle breezes that come from Africa ("where twice a year, at noon, the sun touches the zenith of each point; so that the shadow of an opaque body, in a vertical position, falls at its base and appears nowhere."—Antonelli).
