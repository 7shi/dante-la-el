Willingly he began to say: “So doth your

will I, hide me from you.

I am Arnault that weep and go a-singing; in
thought I see my past madness, and I see
with joy the day which I await before me.

Now I pray you, by that Goodness which guideth
you to the summit of the stairway, be mindful
