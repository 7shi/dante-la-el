*PURGATORIO*

UNWILLING to break short his conference, but
more unwilling yet further to trespass on the
courteous forbearance of his interlocutor, Dante passes
among the weeping souls, through whose eyes that
curse of all the world is distilling itself away! When
will He come who shall chase the wolf of avarice from
earth? (1-15). Dante hears one of the prostrate souls
rehearse examples of generous poverty (16-33), and
learns that he is the ancestor of the royal line of
France, the root of that evil tree that darkens all
the Christian lands with its shadow. Comparatively
