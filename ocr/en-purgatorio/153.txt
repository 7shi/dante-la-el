CANTO XII 141

**The proud** As in order that there be memory of them, the
**Examples of Pride—** tombs on the ground over the buried bear
figured what they were before;

wherefore there, many a time men weep for
them, because of the prick of remembrance
which only to the pitiful gives spur;

so saw I sculptured there, but of better similitude
according to the craftsmanship, all that which
for road projects from the mount.

**Satan** I saw him who was created nobler far than
other creature, on one side descending like
lightning from heaven.

**Briareus** I saw Briareus, transfixed by the celestial bolt,
on the other side, lying on the earth heavy
with the death chill.

**The Giants** I saw Thymbræus, I saw Pallas and Mars,
armed yet, around their father, gazing on the
scattered limbs of the giants.

**Nimrod** I saw Nimrod at the foot of his great labour,
as though bewildered, and looking at the
people who were proud with him in Shinar.

**Niobe** O Niobe, with what sorrowing eyes I saw thee
graven upon the road between seven and
seven thy children slain!

**Saul** O Saul, how upon thine own sword there didst
thou appear dead on Gilboa, which thereafter
felt nor rain nor dew!

**Arachne** O mad Arachne, so saw I thee already half
spider, sad upon the shreds of the work which
to thy hurt was wrought by thee!

**Rehoboam** O Rehoboam, now thine image there seems no
more to threaten; but full of terror a chariot
beareth it away ere chase be given!
