**The Poets go on their way** Although their sudden flight was scattering them
o’er the plain, turned to the mount where
justice probes us,

I drew me close to my faithful comrade; and
how should I have sped without him? who
would have brought me up the mountain?

Gnawed he seemed to me by self-reproach. O
noble conscience and clear, how sharp a sting
is a little fault to thee!

When his feet had lost that haste which mars
the dignity of every act, my mind, that ere-
while was centred within,

widened its scope as in eager search, and I set
my face to the hillside which rises highest
heavenward from the waters.
