79-84. ‘If you delayed repentance till the last
moment, how is it that you are not still in the
Antipurgatorio?’

85-93. In one of the sonnets referred to below
(*note* to *vv.* 115-119) Dante describes Forese’s neglect
of his wife, Nella, but with a coarseness that is well-
nigh incredible. The present passage may have been
intended by the poet to atone in a measure for that
poem, and to offer the widow some consolation by
representing Forese, in his new condition, as one of
the tenderest of husbands.
