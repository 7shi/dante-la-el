7-9.  The  representations  of  the  Mount  of  Purgatory
given  in  the  editions  of  the  Commedia  usually  depict
the  poets  as  having  circled  the  whole  mountain  in  the
course  of  their  journey.  But  this  is  erroneous.  They
circle  only  the  northern  or  sunny  side,  from  east  to
west.  Here,  towards  the  close  of  the  day,  they  are
travelling  almost  due  west,  and  are  almost  at  the
northern  point  of  the  mountain.
