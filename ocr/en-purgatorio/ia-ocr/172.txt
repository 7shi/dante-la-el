22.  The  expression  "so  far  as  here  counts  for  a
mile  "  (that  is  to  say,  "  if  you  think  of  walking  a  mile,
you  will  get  the  right  impression  "),  is  an  indication
which  should  be  carefully  noted,  that  we  must  not
expect  to  be  able  to  arrive  at  any  consistent  representa-
tion by  exact  matter-of-fact  measurements  in  Hell  and
Purgatory.  Dante  was  well  acquainted  with  the
approximate  size  of  the  earth  (Conv.  iii.  5:  100-105,
and  elsewhere),  and  cannot  represent  himself,  for
example,  as  having  literally  climbed  from  the  centre  to
the  circumference  in  something  under  24  hours.
He  is  content  to  avoid  all  glaring  errors  of  principle,
and  to  make  the  several  scenes  realisable  (cf.  Inf.  xxx.
86,  87,  note.  )
