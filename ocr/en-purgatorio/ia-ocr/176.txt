PURGATORIO
AS  Dante  converses  with  Sapia,  revealing  the
wondrous  conditions  of  his  own  pilgrimage
and  the  mysterious  presence  of  his  guide,  he  is  over-
heard by  two  spirits  who  are  leaning  for  support  one
against  another  at  his  right.  Nearest  to  him  is  Guido
del  Duca  of  Bertinoro,  who  is  the  chief  speaker,  the
other  being  Rinieri  da  Calboli  of  Forlì.  They  speak
chiefly  to  each  other,  but  draw  Dante  into  their  con-
versation, questioning  him  as  to  his  origin  ;  and  when
he  indicates  by  a  circumlocution  that  his  birthplace
lies  upon  the  Arno,  Rinieri  asks  Guido  why  Dante
conceals  the  name  under  dark  hints  as  though  it  were
a  shameful  thing  ;  whereon  Guido  approves  of  Dante's
shrinking  from  expressly  naming  this  accursed  ditch
which  rises  in  the  midst  of  brutishness,  and  as  it
