In Purg. xxxii. 143-9, the "Sacred Edifice, transformed" by its profane alliance with temporal things, is described as putting forth seven heads and ten horns; and the Church of Rome under Boniface is spoken of as "a loose harlot" gazing round with wanton eyes.
