1 The Man i' the Moon of Italian children in those old times : here put for the Moon itself. "Round" or full "yesternight;" and consequently exactly opposite to the sun—setting as he rises. Now on the wane, and farther east; so that the time here indicated by the Moon's being on the "confine of both hemispheres," or touching the wave beyond Seville on the western horizon, is about an hour after sunrise on the Saturday morning.
