And as I came amongst them looking, on a yellow purse I saw azure, that had the semblance and gesture of a lion.¹ Then, my look continuing its course, I saw another of them, redder than blood, display a goose more white than butter.² And one who, with a sow azure and pregnant, had his argent sacklet stamped,³ said to me: "What art thou doing in this pit?
