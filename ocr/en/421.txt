And I to him: "Who are the abject two, lying close to thy right confines, and smoking like a hand bathed in winter-time?"

"Here I found them, when I rained into this pinfold," he answered; "and since then they have not given a turn, and may not give, I think, to all eternity. One is the false wife who accused Joseph; the other is false Sinon, the Greek from Troy. Burning fever makes them reek so strongly."
