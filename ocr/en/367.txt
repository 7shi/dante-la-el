“If they within those sparks can speak,” said I, “Master! I pray thee much, and repray that my prayer may equal a thousand, deny me not to wait until the horned flame comes hither. Thou seest how with desire I bend me towards it.”

And he to me: “Thy request is worthy of much praise, and therefore I accept it. But do thou refrain thy tongue.

L’ aguato del caval, che fe’ la porta
Ond’ uscì de’ Romani il gentil seme.
Piangevisi entro l’ arte, per che morta
Deidamia ancor sì duol d’ Achille;
E del Palladio pena vi si porta.
S’ ei posson dentro da quelle faville
Parlar, diss’ io, Maestro, assai ten priego,
E ripriego che il priego vaglia mille,
Che non mi facci dell’ attender niego,
Finchè la fiamma cornuta qua vegna:
Vedi che del disio vêr lei mi piego.
Ed egli a me: La tua preghiera è degna
Di molta lode; ed io però l’ accetto:

1 The wooden horse, by means of which Troy was taken, and Æneas, the founder of Rome, driven forth to Italy.
2 Ulysses induced Achilles to forsake Deidamia; telling him that Troy could not be taken without him, but deceitfully concealing the prediction of his death.
3 Which they carried off.
