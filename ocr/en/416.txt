3 Gianni (Johnny) Schicchi de' Cavalcanti of Florence, a kinsman of Dante's friend Guido, and a soldier. Simon Donati, having privately smothered his sick uncle Buoso Donati, who meant to leave

"his ill-got money for charitable purposes," persuaded this Gianni to put himself in the uncle's bed, assume the voice and features of a dying man, and dictate a will in due form. Gianni made over the whole property to Simon, reserving for himself the uncle's best mare—"Lady, or Queen of the troop"—worth 1,000 gold florens. See Pietro di Dante, Benv. da Imola, and Ottimo. The two last do not mention the murder of Buoso.
