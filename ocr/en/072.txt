1 The Sun. La Bella Stella ch’ il tempo misura (“the beautiful star that measures time”). Canzone xix. 1, page 62 of Fraticelli’s edition, Flor. 1834. “She (Wisdom) is more beautiful than the sun, and above all the order of stars; being compared with light, she is found before it.” Wisdom of Solomon vii. 29.

2 Literally: “Motion.” Tempus est numerus motûs secundum prius et posterius (Aristotle); quoted by Dante (Conv. Tr. iv. c. 2), and translated: “Time, according to Aristotle in the fourth (book) of his Physics, is the number (summation) of motion with respect to first and after.” Many editions read mondo (creation); and perhaps with equally good authority.
