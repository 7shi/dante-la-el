I come where much lamenting strikes me. I came into a place¹ void of all light, which bellows like the sea in tempest, when it is combated by warring² winds. The hellish storm, which never rests, leads the spirits with its sweep; whirling, and smiting it vexes them. When they arrive before the ruin,³ there the shrieks, the moanings, and the lamentation; there they blaspheme the divine power.
