“If he, O wounded spirit!” my Sage replied, “could have believed before, what he has seen only in my verse, he would not have stretched forth his hand against thee; but the incredibility of the thing made me prompt him to do what grieves myself. But tell him who thou wast; so that, to make thee some amends, he may refresh thy fame up in the world, to which he is permitted to return.”
