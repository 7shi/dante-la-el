Thus, between the dry bank and the putrid fen, we compassed a large arc of that loathly slough, with eyes turned towards those that swallow of its filth. We came to the foot of a tower at last.

Così girammo della lorda pozza
Grand' arco, tra la ripa secca e il mezzo,
Con gli occhi volti a chi del fango ingozza.
Venimmo al piè d' una torre al dassezzo.

1 Mezzo (with the e stretta, or close e), a term applied to an apple when it is beginning to rot; and from that transferred to other things in the same state. See Landino, Vellutello, &c.
