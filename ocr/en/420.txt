One is in already, if the mad shadows that are going round speak true. But what avails it me whose limbs are tied? Were I only still so light, that I could move one inch in a hundred years, I had already put myself upon the road, to seek him among this disfigured$^2$ people, though it winds round eleven miles, and is not less than half a mile across. Through them am I in such a crew: they induced me to stamp the florens that had three carats of alloy."

Ch' io potessi in cent' anni andare un' oncia,
Io sarei messo già per lo sentiero,
Cercando lui tra questa gente sconcia, 85
Con tutto ch' ella volge undici miglia,
E men d' un mezzo di traverso non ci ha.
