The kind Master to me directed himself wholly, saying: "Tell them what thou wishest."

And I began, as he desired: "So may your memory not fade away from human minds in the first world, but may it live under many suns: tell me who ye are, and of what people. Let not your ugly and disgusting punishment frighten you from revealing yourselves to me."

Ditemi chi voi siete, e di che genti:
