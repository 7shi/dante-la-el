And they bended their necks; and when they had raised their faces towards me, their eyes, which only inwardly were moist before, gushed at the lids, and the frost bound fast the tears between them, and closed them up again: wood with wood no cramp did ever gird so strongly. Wherefore they, like two he-goats, butted one another; such rage came over them.
