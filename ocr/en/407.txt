"O thou!" began my Guide to one of them, "who with thy fingers dismailest thyself, and sometimes makest pincers of them; tell me if there be any Latian among these who are here within: so may thy nails eternally suffice thee for that work."

"Latians are we, whom thou seest so disfigured here, both of us," replied the one weeping; "but who art thou that hast inquired of us?"
