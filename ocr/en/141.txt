But in my ears a wailing smote me, whereat I bent my eyes intently forwards. And the kind Master said: “Now, Son, the city that is named of Dis draws nigh, with its grave citizens, with its great company.”

And I: “Master, already I discern its mosques, distinctly there within the valley, red as if they had come out of fire.”

And to me he said: “The eternal fire, which causes them to glow within, shows them red, as thou seest, in this low Hell.”
