# Index of *Dante's Divine Comedy: The Inferno* (Carlyle's Translation)

**Source**: *Dante's Divine Comedy: The Inferno. A Literal Prose Translation, with the Text of the Original Collated from the Best Editions, and Explanatory Notes*, by John A. Carlyle, M.D. Fifth Edition. London: George Bell & Sons, York Street, Covent Garden, 1889 (Chiswick Press: C. Whittingham and Co., Tooks Court, Chancery Lane).  
**Digitization**: McMaster University Library (MacSphere: [hdl:11375/14640](http://hdl.handle.net/11375/14640), DOI: [10.71548/2741](https://doi.org/10.71548/2741)). Copy with bookplate of Bertrand and Alys Russell ([`002.txt`](002.txt)).  
**Layout**: Each page of the main text presents Carlyle's English prose translation in the upper section, the Italian verse text in the lower section, and Carlyle's explanatory footnotes at the bottom. Each Canto begins with an Argument (summary) printed on an unpaginated recto page preceding the text, and the main text consistently opens on the following page.

## Overview and Pagination Offsets

The volume contains no printed Table of Contents for the individual Cantos. The only index in the book is the **Index of Proper Names** at the end of the volume on printed pages 425–432 ([`479.txt`](479.txt)–[`486.txt`](486.txt)).

The relationship between scan file numbers (`NNN.txt`) and printed book page numbers follows three distinct pagination systems across the volume:

1. **Unpaginated Preliminaries ([`001.txt`](001.txt)–[`010.txt`](010.txt))**:
   - Dark cloth publisher's binding (`[Cover]`, [`001.txt`](001.txt)), front pastedown with Bertrand & Alys Russell bookplate ([`002.txt`](002.txt)), blank flyleaves (`[Blank page]`, [`003.txt`](003.txt)–[`004.txt`](004.txt)), half-title ([`005.txt`](005.txt)), frontispiece portrait plate of Dante Alighieri engraved by R. Young ([`006.txt`](006.txt)), protective tissue guard leaves (`[Blank page]`, [`007.txt`](007.txt)–[`008.txt`](008.txt)), main title page ([`009.txt`](009.txt)), and Chiswick Press imprint ([`010.txt`](010.txt)).
2. **Roman-Paginated Front Matter ([`011.txt`](011.txt)–[`054.txt`](054.txt))**:
   - Numbered with lower-case Roman numerals continuously from page [v] through page xlviii.
   - **Uniform Offset: +6** (`file_number = roman_numeral + 6`).
   - Comprises Carlyle's Preface to the First Edition (1848, pp. [v]–xii), Preface to the Second (1867) and Third Editions (p. [xiii]), blank leaf (p. [xiv], [`020.txt`](020.txt)), prefatory essay *Manuscripts and Editions* (pp. [xv]–xxv), *Comments and Translations* (pp. [xxvi]–xl), and *The Inferno of Dante* introduction (pp. [xli]–xlviii).
3. **Arabic-Paginated Main Body and Index ([`055.txt`](055.txt)–[`486.txt`](486.txt))**:
   - Numbered continuously with Arabic numerals from page [1] through page 432.
   - **Uniform Offset: +54** (`file_number = printed_page_number + 54`).
   - Every single printed page from 1 through 432 is present in unbroken sequence: there are zero omitted, duplicated, or misordered leaves.
   - Page [1] ([`055.txt`](055.txt)) is an unpaginated recto bearing signature mark `B` at the bottom, containing the Argument to Canto I. The main text of Canto I begins on the following page 2 ([`056.txt`](056.txt)).
   - Cantos I through XXXIV span pages [1]–422 ([`055.txt`](055.txt)–[`476.txt`](476.txt)).
   - The *Index of Proper Names* spans pages [423]–432 ([`477.txt`](477.txt)–[`486.txt`](486.txt)), beginning with an unpaginated half-title leaf (recto p. [423], verso p. [424] `[Blank page]`), followed by index pages [425]–432 ([`479.txt`](479.txt)–[`486.txt`](486.txt)), concluding with the Chiswick Press colophon.
4. **Blank Versos and Canto Transitions**:
   - In accordance with the volume's book design, every Canto's Argument is printed on an odd-numbered recto page, and its verse and prose text opens on the following page.
   - When a Canto's text ends on an even-numbered page (verso), the next Canto's Argument naturally falls on the succeeding odd-numbered page (recto) without interruption.
   - When a Canto's text ends on an odd-numbered page (recto) — as occurs in Cantos VIII, XI, XIII, XIX, XX, XXI, XXVIII, XXIX, and XXX — the following even-numbered verso was left blank by the printer ([`146.txt`](146.txt), [`184.txt`](184.txt), [`210.txt`](210.txt), [`284.txt`](284.txt), [`298.txt`](298.txt), [`312.txt`](312.txt), [`398.txt`](398.txt), [`412.txt`](412.txt), [`426.txt`](426.txt)), so that the next Canto's Argument always occupies a recto.
   - All 9 blank versos participate regularly in the book's pagination (+54 offset).
5. **Publisher's Catalogue / Advertisements ([`487.txt`](487.txt)–[`510.txt`](510.txt))**:
   - An independent 24-page publisher's advertisement insert: *Catalogue of Bohn's Libraries* (dated November 1888; printed by Strangeways & Sons).
   - Separately paginated as pages [1]–[3], 4–23, [24].
   - **Uniform Offset: +486** (`file_number = catalogue_page + 486`).
6. **Back Matter ([`511.txt`](511.txt)–[`514.txt`](514.txt))**:
   - Three blank flyleaves (`[Blank page]`, [`511.txt`](511.txt)–[`513.txt`](513.txt)) and the publisher's dark cloth back cover (`[Cover]`, [`514.txt`](514.txt)).

## Verification Notes and Observations

1. **Strict Uniformity of Pagination**:
   - Every file from [`001.txt`](001.txt) through [`514.txt`](514.txt) has been verified against page images and transcriptions. There are no omitted pages, no duplicated scans, and no misordered leaves in this copy.
   - Front matter Roman pagination ([`011.txt`](011.txt)–[`054.txt`](054.txt)) has a completely constant offset of **+6** (`file = roman_page + 6`).
   - Main text and Index Arabic pagination ([`055.txt`](055.txt)–[`486.txt`](486.txt)) has a completely constant offset of **+54** (`file = printed_page + 54`).
   - The publisher's catalogue insert ([`487.txt`](487.txt)–[`510.txt`](510.txt)) has an independent 24-page Arabic pagination with a constant offset of **+486** (`file = catalogue_page + 486`).
2. **Page Architecture and Blank Versos**:
   - The typography and layout follow a strict structural pattern: each Canto is introduced by an unnumbered Argument on an odd-numbered recto page, and the Canto's verse and prose start on the following page.
   - In 9 instances (Cantos VIII, XI, XIII, XIX, XX, XXI, XXVIII, XXIX, and XXX), the Canto's text concluded on an odd-numbered recto. The printer inserted a blank verso page ([`146.txt`](146.txt), [`184.txt`](184.txt), [`210.txt`](210.txt), [`284.txt`](284.txt), [`298.txt`](298.txt), [`312.txt`](312.txt), [`398.txt`](398.txt), [`412.txt`](412.txt), [`426.txt`](426.txt)) to ensure that the next Canto's Argument opened on a recto.
   - All 9 blank pages participate normally in the running Arabic page count (e.g. p. [92], p. [130], etc.).
3. **Plates and Illustrations**:
   - The sole illustrative plate in the volume is the engraved portrait frontispiece of Dante Alighieri facing the title page ([`006.txt`](006.txt), engraved by R. Young). Unlike the later Temple Classics edition (which added 10 maps, plates, and genealogical tables), Carlyle's fifth edition (1889) contains no embedded woodcuts, maps, or charts in the text or notes.
4. **Minor Typographical Observations**:
   - The Argument heading for Canto XXXIV ([`465.txt`](465.txt)) is printed as `ARGUMENT` without a trailing period, unlike Cantos I–XXXIII which are styled `ARGUMENT.`. The transcription faithfully preserves this typographical detail.
