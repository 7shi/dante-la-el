And he: “If I were of leaded glass, I should
not draw thy outward image more quickly to
me, than I impress that from within.
Even now thy thoughts [were] enter[ing] among
mine, with similar act and similar face; so that
of both I have made one resolve.
In case the right coast so slopes, that we may
descend into the other chasm, we shall escape
the imagined chase!”
He had not ended giving this resolve, when I
saw them come with wings extended, not far
off, in will to seize us.
My Guide suddenly took me, as a mother—that Virgil
is awakened by the noise, and near her sees rescues
the kindled flames—
