And I: “Master, thy words are to me so certain, and so take hold of my belief, that all others would be to me extinguished coals.$^1$ But tell me of the people that are passing,$^2$ if thou seest any of them worthy of note; for to that alone my mind recurs.”$^3$

Then he said to me: “That one, who from the cheek stretches forth his beard upon his dusky shoulders, was an augur, when Greece was so empty of males, that hardly they remained even in the cradles;$^4$ and in Aulis he, with Calchas, gave the time for cutting the first cable.
