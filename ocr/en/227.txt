I said: “With all my power I do beseech it of you. And if you wish me to sit down with you, I will do so, if it pleases him there, for I go with him.”

“O my son,” he said, “whoever of this flock stops one instant, lies a hundred years thereafter, without fanning himself² when the fire strikes him. Therefore go on: I will follow at thy skirts;³ and then will I rejoin my band, that go lamenting their eternal losses.”

Io dissi lui: Quanto posso ven preco;
