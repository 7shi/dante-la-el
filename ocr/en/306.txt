"Dost thou expect, Malacoda," said my Master, "to find I have come here, secure already against all your weapons, without will Divine and fate propitious? Let me pass on; for it is willed in Heaven that I shew another this savage way."²

Then was his pride so fallen, that he let the hook drop at his feet, and said to the others: "Now strike him not!" And my Guide to me: "O thou that sittest cowering, cowering amongst the great splinters of the bridge, securely now return to me!" Sicuramente omai a me ti riedi.
