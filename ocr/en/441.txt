When we were down in the dark pit, under the Giant's feet, much lower, and I still was gazing at the high wall, I heard a voice say to me: "Look how thou passest: take care that with thy soles thou tread not on the heads of the weary wretched brothers." Whereat I turned myself, and saw before me and beneath my feet a lake, which through frost had the semblance of glass and not of water.
