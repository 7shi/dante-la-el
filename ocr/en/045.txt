Charon the demon, with eyes of glowing coal,
beckoning them, collects them all; smites with
his oar whoever lingers.
As the leaves of autumn fall off one after the
other, till the branch sees all its spoils upon the
ground:
so one by one the evil seed of Adam cast them-
selves from that shore at signals, as the bird at
its call.
Thus they depart on the brown water; and ere
they have landed on the other shore, again a
fresh crowd collects on this.
