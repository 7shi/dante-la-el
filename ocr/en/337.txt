Thou wast then so totally entangled upon him who once held Altaforte, that thou didst not look that way; so he departed."
"O my Guide! his violent death, which is not yet avenged for him," said I, "by any that is a partner of his shame, made him indignant: therefore, as I suppose, he went away without speaking to me; and in that has made me pity him the more."

Thus we spake, up to the first place of the cliff, which shows the other valley, if more light were there, quite to the bottom.
The Falsifiers

When we were above the last cloister of Malebolge, so that its lay-brethren could appear to our view, lamentations pierced me, manifold, which had their arrows barbed with pity: whereat I covered my ears with my hands.
