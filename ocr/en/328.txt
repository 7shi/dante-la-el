He had not ended giving this resolve, when I saw them come with wings extended, not far off, in will to seize us. My Guide suddenly took me; as a mother—that is awakened by the noise, and near her sees the kindled flames—who takes her child and flies, and caring more for him than for herself, pauses not so long as even to cast a shift about her.
