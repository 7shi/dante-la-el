And he to me: "If thou wilt have me carry thee down there, by that lower bank, thou shalt learn from him about himself and about his wrongs."

And I: "Whatever pleases thee, to me is grateful. Thou art my Lord; and knowest that I depart not from thy will: also thou knowest what is not spoken."

Then we came upon the fourth bulwark. We turned and descended, on the left hand, down there into the perforated and narrow bottom. Ed io: Tanto m' è bel, quanto a te piace:
Tu se' Signore, e sai ch' io non mi parto

Allor venimmo in su l' argine quarto;
Volgemmo, e discendemmo a mano stanca
Laggiù nel fondo foracchiato ed arto.
E il buon Maestro ancor dalla sua anca
Non mi dipose, sin mi giunse al rotto
