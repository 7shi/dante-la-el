There is a place in Hell called Malebolge, all
of stone, and of an iron colour, like the barrier
which winds round it.
Right in the middle of the malignant field
yawns a well exceeding wide and deep, whose
structure [I] shall tell [in] its own place.
The border therefore that remains, between the well
and the foot of the high rocky bank, is round;
and it has its bottom divided into ten valleys.
