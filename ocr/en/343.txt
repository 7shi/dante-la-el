I: “Master, see thou get to the other boundary,¹ and let us dismount the wall; for as I hear from hence and do not understand, so I see down and distinguish nothing.”

“Other answer I give thee not,” he said “than the deed: for a fit request should be followed with the work² in silence.”

We went down the bridge, at the head where it joins with the eighth bank; and then the chasm was manifest to me. And I saw within it a fearful throng of serpents, and of so strange a look, that even now the recollection scares³ my blood.
