They “came to bloodshed” on the evening of May-day, 1300, at “a grand ladies’ dance” on the Piazza di Santa Trinità; which was to conclude the festivities of the day, and had attracted all the most distinguished of the young men. In 1301 the Whites expelled the Blacks; and were in their turn expelled, by help of Charles de Valois, in the year following, i.e. within less than “three suns” (solar years) of the time at which Ciacco speaks.
