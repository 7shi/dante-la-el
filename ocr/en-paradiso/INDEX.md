# Index of *The Paradiso of Dante Alighieri* (Temple Classics, reprint of November 1910)

**Source**: *The Paradiso of Dante Alighieri*, The Temple Classics (London: J. M. Dent &
Co., Aldine House, MCMX; first issued December 1899, the reprint of November 1910): the
Italian text edited by H. Oelsner, the English prose translation and the arguments by
Philip H. Wicksteed, the notes by both.
**Digitization**: Internet Archive (`paradisoofdante00dant`), PIMS, University of Toronto.
**Layout**: see README.md, "Layout". The printed page = the leaf − 10 (`NNN.txt`, NNN the
leaf), from printed page 2 (`012`) to 420 (`430`).

Built from Internet Archive's OCR (`iascan.py heads`, `ia-ocr/`) and the printed Index to
Maps, Diagrams and Tables (p. 419, `429`); the figures, the front and end matter, and the
leaves where the notes begin read in the images. The book has no table of contents; the
first lines of the cantos are from the converted text (misprints corrected).

## The cantos

As in the *Purgatorio* (../en-purgatorio/INDEX.md): each canto begins with its argument
across the first spread (`*PARADISO*` and `*CANTO N*` at the head, no page numbers there);
the Italian on the even pages, the English on the odd; the notes begin at the foot of a
spread (the leaf under "Notes from") and run on over the following pages. Maps, diagrams
and tables belong to the canto in whose pages they are printed, which is not always the
canto they illustrate (the caption names it: *To Canto VIII*).
