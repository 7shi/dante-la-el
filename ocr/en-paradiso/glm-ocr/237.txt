O soldiery of heaven, whom I look upon, pray for them who have all gone astray on earth, following the ill example.

Erst’twas the wont to make war with swords; now it is made by witholding, now here, now there, the bread the tender father bars from none;
but thou, who but to cancel, dost record, reflect that Peter and Paul who died for the vineyard thou layest waste, are living yet.

Though thou indeed mayst urge: “I have so fixed my longing on him who lived a solitary, and by tripping steps was drawn to martyrdom, that I know not the fisherman nor Paul.”

109-111. Dante is describing the work of God, whom no one can instruct (Isaiah xl. 13, 14: Job xxxviii. 4 sqq.), and from whom all knowledge comes into every mind. But why nests? Are the nests the heavens, nestling one within another? Or is the instinct of birds selected as the symbol of all intelligence save the divine?
