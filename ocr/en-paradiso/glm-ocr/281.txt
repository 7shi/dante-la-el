I stood as one repressing in himself the prick of his desire, who doth not frame to ask, so feareth he to exceed.

That mount, upon whose slope Casino lieth, was erst thronged on its summit by the folk deceived and ill-disposed.

And I am he who first bore up there his name, who brought to earth that truth which doth lift us so high;
