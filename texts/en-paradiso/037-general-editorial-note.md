*The present edition of the* “Paradiso” *has been specially prepared for* “The Temple Classics,” *by the* Rev. Philip H. Wicksteed, M.A. (*who is responsible for the English version and for the Arguments*), *and* Mr H. Oelsner, M.A., Ph.D. (*who is responsible for the Italian text, based on the editions of* Witte, Moore *and* Casini). Mr Wicksteed *and* Mr Oelsner *are jointly responsible for the notes at the end of each Canto.*

Maps and Charts have been inserted, and no pains have been spared to provide the text, translation, and annotation in one small volume. I. G. *October* 13, 1899.
