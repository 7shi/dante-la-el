# Serravalle's Translation and Commentary (1891)

*Fratris Iohannis de Serravalle Ordinis Minorum Episcopi et Principis Firmani Translatio et Comentum Totius Libri Dantis Aldigherii cum Textu Italico Fratris Bartholomaei a Colle*, edited by Fr. Marcellino da Civezza and Fr. Teofilo Domenichelli (Prato: Marcello Vestri, 1891). Each canto gives Serravalle's Latin summary (*Summarium*), the Italian text with his line-by-line Latin translation, his Latin commentary (*Comentum*), and an Italian essay by the editors.

These files are generated from the page transcriptions in [`ocr/la/`](../../ocr/la/) by [`convert_la.py`](../convert_la.py); see [`ocr/la/INDEX.md`](../../ocr/la/INDEX.md) for the volume and [`../PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md) for the full conventions. Do not edit them by hand: correct `ocr/la/` and run `uv run texts/convert_la.py`.

**Only the Inferno and Purgatorio I–XI are converted so far** (scans `0079`–`0608`). Their transcription has been proofread against the print: the verse completed from the images, every disagreement with an independent OCR decided in the image, the line breaks and blank lines checked; text that both OCRs misread alike may remain ([`ocr/la/PLAN.md`](../../ocr/la/PLAN.md) gives the method and its limits); the rest of `ocr/la/` is not yet completed, and the front matter, the rest of the Purgatorio, the Paradiso and the appendix will follow.

## Reading the files

- **Pages**: every printed page starts with a comment such as `<!-- p. 26 (0080) -->`: the printed page number (`[25]` if not printed) and the scan, `ocr/la/0080.png`.
- **Headings**: as printed: `# Capituli Primi Summarium`, `# Capitulum Primum`, and the essay's title (`# La divina commedia`).
- **Verse**: the Italian and the Latin columns interleaved per tercet, each starting with its number (`¹`), the second and third lines indented. The numbers are counted, not copied: the print omits two on p. 45 (Italian tercets 32 and 33 of canto II) and misnumbers one on p. [296] (Italian tercet 2 of canto XXIV printed as 3); the tercet that the editors added in brackets (Purgatorio VI 19–21, p. 491) keeps its brackets. A line too long for its column, printed at the right of the next line (`[togle.`), is joined to its line; the gloss line under the verse (`(¹) costui. — (²) sarà.`) follows the verse of its page. The Italian text is this edition's, which often differs from modern editions.
- **Commentary**: the left column, then the right one, one printed paragraph per line. Paragraph ends are not marked in the transcription; a line that ends a sentence well short of the column width is taken as one, so a paragraph whose last line happens to be full runs on into the next. The short rules between the glosses are not reproduced. A paragraph that runs over a page is left split at the page marker; a word hyphenated across the page break is joined on the earlier page.
- **Essays**: one paragraph per line, verse quotations line by line.
- **Not transcribed**: italics (the commentary's lemmas are printed in italics), the ornaments, and the physical copy's blank pages.
