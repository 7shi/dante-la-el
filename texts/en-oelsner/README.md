# The Temple Classics Inferno (1903)

*The Inferno of Dante Alighieri*, The Temple Classics (London: J. M. Dent & Co., 1903), with the Italian text edited by H. Oelsner and the English prose translation of John Aitken Carlyle, revised by Oelsner. The Italian and the English face each other tercet by tercet; each canto has an argument, the editor's marginal labels, and notes, and some cantos are followed by maps, plates, or genealogical tables.

## Reading the files

- **Pages**: every printed page starts with a comment such as `<!-- p. 3 (015) -->`: the printed page number and the scan, `ocr/en-oelsner/015.png`.
- **Spreads**: the Italian is on the left-hand page and the English on the right-hand page. A spread is read band by band: what is printed across the top of both pages (the argument), then the Italian page, then the English page, then what is printed across the bottom of both pages (the notes). A page that appears again in a later band gets its marker again with the band named, e.g. `<!-- p. 2 (014), text -->`, `<!-- p. 10 (022), notes -->`.
- **Headings**: `## Argument` and `## Notes` are added; the book prints no heading for them.
- **Verse**: tercets with the second and third lines indented; the first line of each tercet ends with its number, as printed (`… fianco 4`). The canto's last line, printed alone, is translated in the last English paragraph.
- **English**: one paragraph per tercet, as printed. Words in square brackets (`I [came to] myself`) are Oelsner's revisions of Carlyle's text.
- **Marginal labels**: the editor's sidenotes (`Proemio`, `Cerchio VIII. Bolgia 3` beside the Italian; `Dante`, `The Leopard` beside the English) are comments at the end of the line or paragraph they stand beside: `<!-- label: Il Colle -->`.
- **Notes**: keyed to verse numbers (`73-75. …`), as plain paragraphs. A note continued on the next page is joined to its start.
- **Maps, plates, tables**: kept where they are printed, with their text line by line.
- **Missing leaf**: pp. 385–386 (Canto XXXIV, the English of vv. 19–51 and the Italian of vv. 52–84) are missing from the scan; the place is marked `<!-- pp. 385–386 missing from the scan -->`.
- **Not transcribed**: the physical copy's covers, bookplate, blank pages, library stamps and loan slips, and pencil notes.
