# Musurus's Greek Inferno (1882)

*Δάντου ὁ ᾍδης. Μετάφρασις Κωνσταντίνου Μουσούρου* (*Dante's Inferno, Translated into Greek Verse by Musurus Pasha*; London: Williams and Norgate, 1882): a line-for-line translation in twelve-syllable verse, with a prologue, a table of contents that repeats the argument of every canto, two lists of corrigenda, and a section of notes at the end of the book.

## Reading the files

- **Pages**: every printed page starts with a comment such as `<!-- p. 2 (022) -->`: the printed page number and the scan, `ocr/grc/022.png`. Unnumbered pages have the number in brackets (`[1]`); the front matter is numbered in Greek numerals (`ε΄`); the title pages have only the scan number.
- **Cantos**: the printed heading (`ΩΔΗ Α΄.` → `# ᾨδὴ Α΄`), the argument as a plain paragraph, then the verse.
- **Verse**: twelve-syllable lines, one per line, not in tercets. The translation is line for line, so Greek line *n* renders Italian verse *n* and every canto has the verse count of the Italian. Every fifth line ends with its number (`… στυγήρης, 5`), counted by the script; the book prints the numbers at the line start. A long line whose end the book sets on the next line is joined.
- **Notes**: one file, as printed, under `# Σημειώσεις`; the notes to each canto start with `## Εἰς ᾨδὴν Α΄`, often in the middle of a page. Each note is a paragraph keyed to a line number (`105\. …`); a note continued on the next page is joined to its start. Quotations in verse keep their printed lines. The two asterisk footnotes are `[^269-1]` and `[^323-1]`, after the printed page.
- **Front matter**: the title pages and the place and date of the prologue line by line; the table of contents one entry per paragraph, ending with the page (`. . . 1`); the corrigenda as tables.
- **Not transcribed**: blank pages and the library stamps.
