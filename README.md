# dante-la-el

Textualization of the translations of Dante's Divine Comedy in Latin, Greek, and other languages.

Beside the transcriptions, the repository holds new translations of these books ([translations/](translations/)) and commentaries generated by an LLM ([commentary/](commentary/), [grammar/](grammar/)); see the sections below.

English (The Temple Classics: the three volumes, with the Italian text edited by H. Oelsner; London: J. M. Dent & Co.)

Carlyle's prose translation was valued for its fidelity to the Italian, but he translated
only the *Inferno*. The Temple Classics (1899–1901) completed it: Carlyle's *Inferno*
revised by Oelsner, with the *Purgatorio* by Thomas Okey and the *Paradiso* by Philip H.
Wicksteed, on one Italian text edited by Oelsner, in one layout (the Italian and the
English facing tercet by tercet, an argument and notes to each canto). The set was
reprinted by Dent into the 1960s, but no later edition or usable digital text of it has
been found. Here the three volumes are transcribed, checked against the page images and
converted, so that the whole *Comedy* in this translation can be read and used again.

## OCR

[ocr/](ocr/) contains the scanned page images of the Latin (`la/`), Ancient Greek (`grc/`), and English/Italian (`en/` for Carlyle's own edition; `en-oelsner/`, `en-purgatorio/` and `en-paradiso/` for the *Inferno*, the *Purgatorio* and the *Paradiso* of the Temple Classics) sources, their OCR transcriptions (`.txt`), and the scripts used to produce them. See [ocr/README.md](ocr/README.md) for details.

## Texts

[texts/](texts/) holds the transcriptions of `ocr/` converted into one Markdown file per section of each book, following the printed book and marking its page boundaries. The files are generated and not edited by hand; corrections go into `ocr/`. See [texts/README.md](texts/README.md) for the books and the conventions.

## Translations

[translations/](translations/) holds new translations of the books of `texts/`, made file by file in their layout: English and Japanese of Musurus's Greek *Inferno* (`en/grc/`, `ja/grc/`), and Japanese of the four English volumes (`ja/en/`, `ja/en-oelsner/`, `ja/en-purgatorio/`, `ja/en-paradiso/`). The translation of Serravalle's Latin edition (`en/la/`, `ja/la/`) is in progress. The plan is in [translations/PLAN.md](translations/PLAN.md), and what is done in [translations/DONE.md](translations/DONE.md).

## Commentary

[commentary/](commentary/) holds a commentary on each canto, generated by an LLM from the Italian of dante-corpus, with a line-by-line translation of the whole canto in Modern Greek, Esperanto, and English; and line-by-line translations of printed translations: Musurus's Greek *Inferno* into English (`en-grc/`) and Modern Greek (`el-grc/`), and Serravalle's Latin into English (`en-la-la/`). See [commentary/README.md](commentary/README.md).

> [!NOTE]
> The commentaries and translations are machine-generated and have not been reviewed by translators or scholars.

## Grammar

[grammar/](grammar/) holds grammar commentaries in English on every line, generated by an LLM: the Italian of Carlyle's *Inferno* and of the Temple Classics explained against their English (`en/`, `en-oelsner/`, `en-purgatorio/`, `en-paradiso/`), Musurus's Greek against the Italian (`grc/`) and against Modern Greek (`el-grc/`). Serravalle's Latin explained against the Italian of the same edition (`la/`) is to follow. See [grammar/README.md](grammar/README.md).

> [!NOTE]
> The commentaries are machine-generated and have not been reviewed by translators or scholars.

## Editions

[editions/](editions/) holds reports comparing the Italian and the English of Carlyle's edition, dante-corpus, and Oelsner's edition, on which the commentaries of `grammar/en/` and `grammar/en-oelsner/` rest. See [editions/README.md](editions/README.md).

## Earlier work

[Inferno/](Inferno/), [summaries/](summaries/), and [scripts/](scripts/) are from earlier experiments: the opening cantos of the *Inferno* in Latin, Greek, and English, compared with translations by AI and machine translation; summaries of Longfellow's translation by Claude; and the scripts that processed their logs.

## Dependency

Some of the checks read the Italian text of [dante-corpus](https://github.com/7shi/dante-corpus) through its API, as an editable path dependency (`../dante-corpus`), so both repositories must share one parent directory:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/7shi/dante-corpus.git
git clone https://github.com/7shi/dante-la-el.git
make -C dante-corpus
cd dante-la-el
uv sync
```
