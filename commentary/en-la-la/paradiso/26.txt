While I was doubting because my sight was extinguished,
From the radiant flame that had put it out,
A breath came forth and made me attentive,
Saying: While you recover
From the sight you have consumed in me,
It is well for you to make up for it by reasoning.
Begin, then, and say where it is fixed,
Your soul, and consider that it is,
Your sight, strayed from you but not dead;
For the lady who through this divine
Region guides you has in her gaze
The power that Ananias's hand once had.
I said: At her pleasure, soon or late,
May healing come to the eyes that were the gates
When she entered with the fire I always burn with.
The Good that satisfies this court,
Is Alpha and Omega of every Scripture that
Reads love to me, lightly or strongly.
That same voice, which the fear
Had taken from me of the sudden dazzle,
Once again set me the care of reasoning;
And it said: Certainly, with a finer sieve
You must clarify; you must say
Who aimed your bow at such a target.
And I: Through philosophical arguments
And through authority that descends from here,
Such love must be impressed upon me;
For good, insofar as it is good, when understood,
Kindles love in the same measure, and all the more,
The more goodness it contains in itself.
Therefore toward that essence, so superior
That every good found outside it
Is only a light from its ray,
More than toward any other must move
In love the mind of whoever perceives
The truth on which this proof is founded.
This truth is laid before my intellect
By the one who shows me the first love
Of all eternal substances.
The voice of the truthful author shows it,
Who, speaking of himself, told Moses:
I will make you see every good.
You show it to me too, beginning
The lofty proclamation, which cries out the mystery
From here below, above every other message.
And I heard: Through human understanding,
And through authorities in agreement with it,
You look to God as the highest of your loves.
But say of love whether you feel other cords
Drawing you toward Him, so that you may sound
With how many teeth this love bites you.
Not hidden was the holy intention
Of Christ's eagle; rather, I understood
Where he meant to lead my profession.
So I began again: All those bites
That can turn the heart toward God
Have combined to form my charity;
For the world's existence, and my own,
The death He suffered so that I might live,
And what every faithful person hopes for, as I do,
Together with the living knowledge already named,
Have drawn me from the sea of crooked love
And set me on the shore of right love.
The leaves that green the whole garden anew
Of the eternal Gardener I love as much
As goodness from Him has been given to them.
As soon as I was silent, a sweetest song
Resounded through the heavens; and my lady,
With the others, said: Holy, Holy, Holy.
And as one wakes at a piercing light,
When the visual spirit races back
Toward the brightness passing from layer to layer,
And the awakened person recoils from what is seen,
So ignorant from sudden waking,
Until judgment comes to help him;
So from my eyes every speck
Beatrice drove with one of her rays,
Which shone for more than a thousand miles;
From then on I now saw better than before,
And almost stunned, I asked
About a fourth light that I saw with us.
And my lady: Within those rays
The first soul woos its Maker,
Which the First Power ever created.
Like a leafy branch that bends its tip
When the wind passes, and then rises again
By its own power that lifts it,
So I did for just as long as she spoke,
In wonder; then what made me confident again
Was a desire to speak, for which I burned;
And I began: O fruit, which ripe
Alone was brought forth, O ancient father,
To whom every woman is daughter and daughter-in-law;
I beg you, as devoutly as I can,
To speak to me: you see my wish;
And to hear you soon, I will not voice it.
Sometimes a covered animal shakes itself,
So that its feelings must show,
Because of what follows its desire;
And in the same way the first soul
Made visible to me through its covering
How gladly it came to please me.
Then it spoke: Without its having been told me
By you, I discern your wish better
Than you do anything of which you are most certain,
For I see it in the truthful mirror,
Which makes other things present to itself,
While no thing makes itself present to that mirror.
You wish to know how long ago God placed me
In the lofty garden, whence this lady
Prepared you for so high a stairway;
And how great a delight it was to my eyes,
And the actual cause of the great wrath,
And the language I used and created.
O my son, not the tasting of the tree
Was in itself the cause of so great an exile,
But only the crossing of the boundary.
There, whence your lady moved Virgil,
For four thousand three hundred and two revolutions
Of the sun I longed for this assembly;
And I saw it return to all the lights
Of its path nine hundred and thirty
Times while I was on earth.
The language I spoke was entirely extinct
Before to the unfinishable work
The people of Nimrod turned their attention;
For no rational product ever,
Because of human pleasure, which changes
By following the heavens, has remained forever.
It is a natural act for a human being to speak;
But whether this way or that, nature leaves
Thereafter to you to do, according to your liking.
Before I descended to infernal anguish,
On earth the highest Good was called I,
From whom comes the joy that enfolds me;
Later it was called EL, and rightly so,
For mortal usage is like a leaf
On a branch, for one falls and another comes.
On the mountain that rises highest above the waves,
I was, with a pure life and a sinful one,
From the first hour to the one that follows,
When the sun changes its quarter at the sixth hour.
