If I set you aflame with love's heat
beyond the measure seen on earth,
so that I overcome your strength,
Do not marvel, for this proceeds
from perfect vision, which, as it apprehends,
so it moves its foot toward the good.
I see well how there already shines
in your intellect the eternal light,
which, once seen, kindles love alone, always;
And if some other thing entices your love,
it is only some trace of that,
poorly known, shining through there.
You wish to know whether, by another service,
for a vow left unfulfilled, so much can be rendered
that the soul is safe from dispute.
Thus Beatrice began this canto;
and, like one who does not break off his speech,
she thus continued the holy discourse:
The greatest gift that God, in His bounty,
gave in creation, and to His goodness
most conformed, and most highly prized by Him,
was freedom of the will,
with which intelligent creatures,
all of them and they alone, were and are endowed.
Now, if you reason from this, you will see
the great worth of a vow to be such,
that God consents when you consent;
for, in sealing the pact between God and man,
a victim is made of this treasure,
the kind I describe, and it is made by its own act.
So what can be given in return?
If you think you can use well what you offered,
you would make good work from what was wrongly taken.
By now you are certain of the chief point;
but since Holy Church grants dispensation here,
which seems contrary to the truth I have shown you,
you must sit a little longer at the table,
for the hard food you have taken
needs help with your digestion.
Open your mind to that truth which I reveal to you,
and fix it within you; for it does not make knowledge
to have understood without retaining.
Two things belong to the essence
of this sacrifice: one is that
of which it is made, the other is the agreement.
This latter can never be cancelled,
unless it is kept in that regard,
if its worth does not speak from above:
therefore it was necessary for the Hebrews
only to make the offering, although this offering
were exchanged, as you should know.
The other part, which I have explained to you as matter,
can indeed be such that it is not violated
if it is exchanged for other matter.
But let the burden not be shifted from one's shoulder
by anyone at his own discretion, without the turning
of both the white key and the yellow;
and deem every exchange foolish
if the thing relinquished, in the thing received,
as four in six, is not contained.
Therefore, whatever thing weighs so much
by its value that it tips every scale
cannot be satisfied by another payment.
Therefore let mortals not take vows as a joke:
be faithful, and not crooked in doing so,
as Jephthah was with his first victory;
He should rather have said, I did wrong,
than, by keeping it, do worse; and thus foolish
you find the great leader of the Greeks,
because Iphigenia wept for her fair face,
and made foolish and wise alike weep over it
when they heard tell of such beauty.
Christians, be more deliberate in your actions;
do not be like a feather tossed by every wind,
and do not think every water washes you clean.
You have the New and the Old Testament,
and the Church's shepherd to guide you;
let this suffice for your salvation.
If wicked desire draws you elsewhere,
be men, not foolish sheep,
so that the Jew among you does not laugh at you.
Do not be like the lamb that leaves the milk
of its mother, simple and playful,
and fights itself for its own pleasure.
So Beatrice spoke to me, as I write;
then, wholly eager, she turned
to the region where the world is most alive.
Her silence and changed appearance
silenced my eager mind,
which already had another question before it.
And as an arrow that, at its mark,
strikes before the bowstring comes to rest,
so we sped into the second realm.
There I saw my lady so joyful
as soon as she entered the light of that heaven,
that the planet grew brighter from her.
And if star changed and smiled at star,
what was I like, who by my nature alone
can be changed in every way!
As in a pond, calm and clear,
fish dart toward whatever they see outside,
thinking it is their food;
so I saw well over a thousand lights
draw toward us, and in each was heard:
There is one who will increase our loves.
And as soon as each one came toward us,
the shade was seen full of joy
in the clear radiance streaming from it.
Reader, consider: if what begins here
did not continue, how you would feel
an anxious hunger to know more;
and you will see for yourself how from these spirits
I longed to hear of their state,
as soon as they were made plain to my eyes.
O blessed one, to whom to see the thrones
of eternal triumph is granted by grace
before the army's service is over,
by the light that spreads through all heaven
we are kindled; so, if you wish
to learn about us, satisfy your desire.
So by one of those blessed spirits
I was told; and Beatrice said, Speak, speak
freely, and trust them as you would gods.
I see well how you nest
in your own light, which you draw from your eyes,
for it sparkles as soon as you smile;
But I do not know who you are, or why you hold,
worthy soul, the rank of a sphere
that is veiled from mortals by another's rays.
I said this to the light
that had first spoken; then it made itself
far more luminous than it had been.
As the sun hides itself
through too much brightness, when heat has consumed
the moderating power of dense vapors,
so, for greater joy, there hid itself
within its own ray the holy figure;
and thus enclosed, enclosed, it answered me
in the way the next canto will tell.
