As soon as the last word
the blessed flame took up to speak,
the holy millstone began to turn;
and in its circle it had not turned all the way round
before another enclosed it in a circle,
and gathered motion from motion, and song from song;
a song that so far surpasses our Muses,
our Sirens, on those sweet trumpets,
as the first splendor surpasses that which shone back.
As there curve through a tender cloud
two parallel bows with their colors,
when Juno commands her handmaid,
the one that is outside springing from the one that is within,
after the likeness of the speech of that wandering one
whom love consumed as the sun consumes vapors;
and they make the people here foreknow,
because of the covenant that God made with Noah,
that the world will nevermore be flooded;
so those everlasting roses
revolved about us in two garlands,
and so the outermost answered the innermost.
After the dance and the other great rejoicing,
both the singing and the flaming
of light with light, joyous and gentle,
rested together in one instant and with one will,
and just like eyes which, at the pleasure that moves them,
must close and lift themselves together,
From the heart of one of those new lights
a voice moved, which a needle to the star
made me seem, turning me toward its place;
and it began: The love that makes me beautiful
draws me to speak of the other leader,
because of whom my own is so well praised here.
It is fitting that where one shines, the other should shine,
and as they fought together,
so may their glory shine together.
Christ's army, which so dearly
cost to reclaim, behind its banner
moved slowly, wary and in sparse ranks;
when the Emperor who reigns forever
provided for the army in uncertainty,
by grace alone, not because it deserved it.
And, as I have said, he aided his bride
with two champions; by their deeds and words
the straying people found their way again.
In that region where there rises, to open
the new leaves, gentle Zephyr,
with which Europe is seen to clothe herself,
not very far from the crashing of the waves,
beyond which, in their long sweep,
the sun sometimes hides from everyone,
lies fortunate Calahorra,
under the protection of the great shield
where the lion is subject and rules.
Within it was born the ardent lover
of the Christian faith, the great athlete,
kind to his own, harsh to his foes;
and as soon as it was created, it was filled,
his soul, so richly with divine power,
that while still in his mother it made her a prophet.
After the betrothal was sealed
at the holy font between him and the faith,
where they endowed each other with salvation,
The woman who answered for him
saw in a dream the wondrous fruit
that would come from him and his heirs;
and, so that his name might fit the nature he possessed,
thence a spirit moved to name him
by the possessive of what he wholly was.
He was called Dominic; and I speak of him
as of the farmer whom Christ
chose for his garden to help him.
He truly seemed Christ's messenger and friend,
for his first love, made clear in him,
was for the first counsel that Christ gave.
Often he was, silent and awake,
found on the ground by his nurse,
as if to say: I have come for this.
O his father, truly Felix!
O his mother, truly Giovanna,
if her name means what it is said to mean!
Not for the world, which people now pursue
by following the Bishop of Ostia and Thaddeus,
but for love of the true manna,
in a short time he became a great teacher,
so that he set out to circle the vineyard,
which soon grows pale if its vinedresser is wicked.
And of the See, once kind
in greater measure to the poor and just, not because of the See,
but because of the one who sits there and strays,
not to exchange two or three for six,
not the fortune of the first vacancy,
not the tithes belonging to God's poor
did he ask for, but, against the wandering world,
leave to fight for the seed
that clothes twenty-four plants.
Then, with learning and, together with it, the will
of the apostolic office, he set out,
like a torrent forced down by a spring on high;
and against the heretics' brambles struck
his force most vigorously there
where resistance was strongest.
Many different streams flowed from him,
watering the Catholic garden,
so its young trees grew more vigorous.
If one wheel of the chariot was such,
on which the holy Church defended herself
and won her civil war in the field,
You should know clearly enough
the excellence of the other wheel, which Thomas
praised so graciously before I came.
But the track made by the outer part
of its rim has been abandoned,
so that there is mold where once there were lees.
His family, which set out straight
with their feet following his footsteps, has turned so far
that the one ahead casts at the one behind;
Soon will be seen the harvest
of bad cultivation, when the tares
complain that their barn has been taken from them.
I say truly that if one searched, leaf by leaf,
our book, one would still find a page
where one would read: I am what I used to be.
But it will not come from Casale or Acquasparta,
from which such men come to Scripture,
that one flees it and the other constrains it.
I am the soul of Bonaventure
of Bagnoregio, who in high offices
always put lesser concerns last.
Illuminato and Augustine are here,
who were among the first barefoot poor ones,
who made themselves God's friends with a cord.
Hugh of St Victor is here with them,
and Pietro Mangiadore and Pietro Ispano,
who now shines in twelve little books;
the prophet Nathan, and the metropolitan
Chrysostom, Anselm, and that Donatus
who deigned to lay a hand on the first art;
Rabanus is here, and beside me shines
the Calabrian Abbot Joachim,
endowed with prophetic spirit.
To praise so great a champion
what moved me was the burning courtesy
of Saint Thomas and his discerning Latin,
and it moved this company with me.
