Once that contemplative spirit was attuned to his delight,
He freely took up the office of teacher,
And began these sacred words:
The wound that Mary closed and anointed,
She who is so beautiful at her feet,
Is the one who opened and pierced it.
In the order formed by the third seats,
Rachel sits below her,
With Beatrice, as you see.
Sarah, Rebecca, Judith, and the one
Who was the great-grandmother of the singer who, in grief
For his sin, said: Have mercy on me,
You can see them thus, from tier to tier,
Descending, as I, who name each one,
Go down through the rose, from leaf to leaf.
From the seventh tier downward, as far
As it extends, the Hebrew women follow,
Dividing all the flower's heads;
For, according to the faith's view
Of Christ, this is the wall
That divides the sacred stairways.
On this side, where the flower is ripe
With all its leaves, sit
Those who believed in Christ to come.
On the other side, where the cut-away
Semicircles are, stand
Those who looked toward Christ after he came.
And just as on this side the glorious seat
Of the Lady of Heaven and the other seats
Beneath her form such a division,
So on the opposite side, facing that of great John,
Who was sanctified from the desert and endured martyrdom,
And then spent two years in Hell;
Beneath him, in such order, were allotted places
To Francis, Benedict, and Augustine,
And the others down to here, circle by circle.
Now behold the high divine providence,
Which will fill this garden equally
With either aspect of the faith.
And know that from the lower tier, which reaches
The midpoint of the two divisions,
They sit through no merit of their own,
But through another's, under certain conditions;
For all these spirits were released
Before they had true choices.
You can perceive this well from their faces,
And also from their childish voices,
If you look at them carefully or listen.
Now you doubt, and in doubt you are silent;
But I will loosen the strong knot
In which subtle thoughts have bound you.
Within the breadth of this kingdom,
Chance has no place,
Any more than sadness, thirst, or hunger;
For by eternal law all you see
Is established, so that justly
Here ring and finger fit one another.
And so this people, hastening
To true life, are not here without cause;
Here one is more and another less excellent.
The King, through whom this realm rests
In such love and such delight
That no will dares desire more,
In his joyful presence, as he creates,
Endows all minds with grace as he pleases,
Differently; and here let the effect suffice.
This is made plain and clear to you
In Holy Scripture, in those twins
Who were stirred to anger in their mother's womb.
Therefore, according to the color of their hair,
The highest light is rightly crowned
With such grace.
Thus, without reward for their conduct,
They are placed on different tiers,
Differing only in the first sharpness of sight.
In the earliest ages, for them
Innocence and only their parents' faith
Were enough to gain salvation.
When the first ages were complete,
Innocent males had to gain virtue
Through circumcision.
But when the age of grace arrived,
Without the perfect baptism of Christ,
Such innocence could no longer remain below.
Look now into the face of Christ
That most resembles him, for its brightness
Alone can prepare you to see Christ.
I saw such joy rain down upon her,
Borne by the holy minds
Created to fly across that height,
That nothing I had seen before
Had so filled me with wonder,
Or shown me so much of God's likeness.
And that Love who first descended there,
Singing, Hail Mary, full of grace,
Spread his wings before her.
The blessed court from every side
Answered the divine song,
And every face grew brighter.
O holy Father, who for my sake endure
Being down here, leaving the sweet place
Where you sit by eternal lot,
Who is that angel, with such delight
Gazing into the eyes of our Queen,
So enamored that he seems made of fire?
So I returned again to the teaching
Of him who was made beautiful by Mary,
As a morning star is made beautiful by the sun.
And he said to me: All the joy and beauty
That can exist in angel or in soul
Is in him, and we will that it be so;
For he is the one who carried the palm
Down to Mary, when the Son of God
Chose to take on our human burden.
But come now with your eyes, as I
Go on speaking, and observe the great patricians
Of this most just and pious empire.
Those two who sit highest and are most blessed,
Because they are closest to the Empress,
Are almost two roots of this rose.
The one who stands beside her on the left
Is the father whose daring taste
Makes the human race taste such bitterness.
On the right you see that ancient Father
Of Holy Church, to whom Christ entrusted the keys
To this beautiful flower.
And beside him sits the one who saw all
The grievous times of the fair Bride
Before he died, she won with spear and keys;
And beside the other rests the leader
Under whom the ungrateful, fickle, stubborn people
Lived on manna.
Across from Peter you see Anne seated,
So content to gaze at her daughter
That she does not move her eyes to sing Hosanna.
Across from the head of the family
Sits Lucy, who moved your lady
When you bowed your eyes toward ruin.
But since time flies, and makes you drowsy,
Here we will make a point, like a good tailor
Who makes a gown according to his cloth;
And we will turn our eyes toward the First Love,
So that, looking toward him, you may penetrate,
As far as possible, through his radiance.
Indeed, lest you draw back,
Moving your wings and thinking to advance,
Grace must be obtained by prayer,
Grace from her who can truly help you;
And follow me with your love,
So your heart does not stray from my words.
And he began this holy prayer.
