He was already rejoicing in his own word
That blessed mirror, while I savored
Mine, mingling the sweet with the bitter;
And that lady who led me to God
Said: Change your thoughts; remember that I am
Near Him who lifts every wrong.
I turned toward the loving sound
Of my comfort; and what I then saw
In her holy eyes, I leave undescribed;
Not because I distrust my speech,
But because the mind cannot return
So far above itself unless another guide it.
I can report this much of that moment:
As I gazed at her, my desire
Was freed from every other longing,
Until the eternal pleasure, which directly
Shone into Beatrice, from her lovely face
Satisfied me in its reflected form.
Overcoming me with the light of a smile,
She said: Turn and listen,
For Paradise is not only in my eyes.
As sometimes one can see
Feeling in a face, if it is so strong
That it takes the whole soul from it,
So, in the flame of that holy radiance
Toward which I turned, I knew its wish
To speak with me a little longer.
It began: On this fifth threshold
Of the tree that lives from its crown,
Ever bears fruit and never loses leaves,
Are blessed spirits who, below, before
They came here to Heaven, were famed so greatly
That every Muse would find rich matter in them.
So look at the arms of the Cross:
The one I name will there flash
As quick fire flashes in a cloud.
Across the Cross I saw a light drawn
At the name Joshua; and its flash
Was no sooner seen than the name was spoken.
At the name of the great Maccabee
I saw another wheel and turn;
Joy was the lash that spun its top.
For Charlemagne and Orlando,
Two lights followed my intent gaze,
As an eye follows its falcon in flight.
Then William and Rinoardo drew
My sight along that Cross, and Duke Godfrey,
And Robert Guiscard.
Then, moving and mingling with the other lights,
He showed me the soul that had spoken,
How skilled it was among Heaven's singers.
I turned toward my right
To learn from Beatrice what I should do,
By word or by a sign,
I saw her eyes so clear,
So joyful, that her appearance
Surpassed all others and her former beauty.
As a person, working well,
Feels greater delight and day by day
Sees his own virtue grow,
So I perceived my circling
Had widened with the heavens,
Seeing that more splendid marvel.
As, in a brief passage of time,
A woman turns pale when her face
Loses the burden of her shame,
So it was in my eyes when I was turned,
By the brightness of the temperate sixth star
That had gathered me within itself.
In that Jovial flame
I saw the flashing of the love there,
Writing our words before my eyes.
As birds rising from a riverbank,
As if rejoicing together in their feeding,
Form now a round, now another flock,
So within the lights the holy creatures
Sang as they flew, and shaped themselves
Now into D, now I, now L.
First, singing their tune, they moved;
Then, becoming one of these signs,
They paused a little and fell silent.
O divine Pegasean Muse, who makes
Minds glorious and grants them long life,
And with them grants life to cities and kingdoms,
Illuminate me with your power, so I may show
Their shapes as I conceived them;
Let your power appear in these brief verses.
They appeared in five times seven,
Vowels and consonants; I noted
The phrases as they seemed to be spoken.
Love justice were the first
Words and verb of the whole design;
You who judge the earth were the last.
Then in the M of the fifth word
They remained arranged, so Jupiter
Seemed silver, traced there with gold.
And I saw the other lights descend
To where the M reached its peak and rest,
Singing, I think, the Good that draws them to itself.
Then, as when burning logs are struck,
Countless sparks leap up,
From which fools are wont to make predictions,
More than a thousand lights seemed to rise
From there, climbing some high, some low,
As the Sun that kindles them assigns them;
And as each settled in its place,
I saw an eagle's head and neck
Take shape against that distinct flame.
The One who paints there has no guide,
But guides Himself; from Him is recalled
That power which gives form to the nests of heaven.
The other blessed souls, who first seemed
Content to flower into the M,
Followed the imprint with little movement.
O sweet star, what precious gems,
And how many, showed me that our justice
Is the effect of the heaven you adorn!
Wherefore I pray the Mind, in which begin
Your motion and your power, to look
From where comes the smoke that clouds your ray,
So that once more it may grow angry
At buying and selling inside the Temple,
Built up with blood and martyrdom.
O host of Heaven, whom I behold,
Pray for those on earth, all astray
Following the bad example.
Once war was made with swords;
Now it is waged by taking, here and there,
The bread the loving Father denies to none.
But you who write only to cancel,
Remember Peter and Paul, who died
For the vineyard you ruin, and still live.
You may well say: I have fixed my desire
So firmly on him who chose to live alone,
And who was led to martyrdom through dances,
That I know neither the Fisherman nor Paul.
