O little nobility of our blood,
if you make people boast of you
down here, where our affections grow faint,
it will never seem a marvel to me;
for there, where desire is not distorted,
I mean in Heaven, I too boasted of it.
You are a little mantle that soon shrinks,
so that unless it is lengthened day by day,
Time goes round it with shears.
My words began again with You,
the form first offered at Rome,
in which its people persevere least.
And Beatrice, who had been a little apart,
smiling, seemed like the one who coughed
at the first fault written of Guinevere.
I began: You are my father;
you give me all boldness to speak;
you lift me so that I am more than myself.
Through so many streams my mind
is filled with joy and rejoices in itself,
because it can bear it without breaking.
Tell me, then, my dear first beginning,
who were your ancestors, and what years
were marked in your childhood.
Tell me of Saint John's fold,
how large it was then, and which people
within it deserved the highest seats.
As a coal is rekindled by the breath of winds,
so I saw that light grow bright
at my gracious words;
and as it grew more beautiful to my eyes,
so it spoke with a sweeter, gentler voice,
though not in our modern speech.
It said: From the day when Hail was spoken
to the birth when my mother, now a saint,
was relieved of the burden she carried,
this fire came beneath its Lion
five hundred and fifty, and thirty times,
to rekindle itself beneath his foot.
My ancestors and I were born where
the last sixth district is first found,
by the runner in your yearly contest.
Let it suffice to hear this of my elders;
who they were and whence they came here,
it is more honorable to leave unsaid.
All those who then were there,
able to bear arms between Mars and the Baptist,
were a fifth of those alive today.
But the citizen body, now mixed
with folk from Campi, Certaldo, and Figline,
was pure down to the humblest artisan.
How much better it would be for those people
I speak of, and Galluzzo and Trespiano,
to have your borders as neighbors,
than to have them within, enduring the stench
of the peasant from Aguglione and the man from Signa,
whose eye is already sharp for fraud!
If the people who most degenerate in the world
had not been a stepmother to the Caesars,
but kind as a mother to her child,
such a Florentine would trade and barter
only in Semifonte, where my grandfather
used to go on his rounds.
Montemurlo would still belong to the counts;
the Cerchi would be in the parish of Acone,
and perhaps the Buondelmonti in Valdigreve.
The mixing of peoples has always
been the beginning of a city's troubles,
like food piled on top of what is already eaten.
A blind bull falls sooner
than a blind lamb; and often one sword
cuts more and better than five.
If you look at Luni and Urbisaglia,
how they have passed away, and how Chiusi
and Sinigaglia are passing after them,
hearing how families are destroyed
will seem neither strange nor hard,
since cities too have their end.
All your things have death,
as you do; but it is hidden in some,
which last a long time, while lives are short.
As the turning of the heavens and the Moon
covers and uncovers shores without rest,
so Fortune acts with Florence:
therefore what I tell of other Florentines,
whose fame has faded with time,
should seem no marvel.
I saw the Ughi, Catellini,
Filippi, Greci, Ormanni, and Alberichi,
already declining, though illustrious citizens;
and I saw those as great as they were ancient,
with the Sannella, the Arca,
Soldanieri, Ardinghi, and Bostichi.
Above the gate, now burdened
with such weight of new wickedness
that the ship will soon founder,
were the Ravignani, from whom descended
Count Guido and all who later took
the name of the noble Bellincione.
The della Pressa already knew how
to govern; Galigaio had in his house
a gilded sword hilt and pommel.
Great was the column of the Vajo,
the Sacchetti, Giuochi, Fifanti, Barucci,
the Galli, and those who blush for the bushel.
The stock from which the Calfucci sprang
was already great; the Sizi and Arrigucci
had already been raised to office.
Oh, how great I saw those now ruined
by their pride! Their golden balls
made Florence flourish in all their great works.
So did the fathers of those
who grow fat whenever your Church
falls vacant, sitting in consistory.
The arrogant brood, dragonlike
after those who flee, but tame as a lamb
to whoever shows a tooth or purse,
was already rising, though of humble stock,
so that Ubertin Donato disliked it,
though later his father-in-law made him kin.
Caponsacchi had settled in the market,
having come down from Fiesole; already
Guido and Infangato were good citizens.
I shall tell you something incredible and true: entering
the little circle, one passed through a gate
named after the people of the Pear.
Whoever bears the fair device
of the great Baron, whose name and worth
the feast of Thomas renews,
received knighthood and privilege from him;
though now he joins the common people,
the one who rims it with a border.
The Gualterotti and Importuni were there;
the Borgo would still be more peaceful
if it lacked its new neighbors.
The house that caused your weeping,
through the just anger that brought you death
and ended your joyful life,
It was honored, and so were its allies.
O Buondelmonte, how badly you fled
your promised marriage, at another's urging!
Many who are sad would be happy
if God had drowned you in the Ema
the first time you came to the city.
Yet it was fitting for that broken stone
that guards the bridge to split Florence,
a victim of its final peace.
With these people and others among them,
I saw Florence in such peace
that it had no cause to weep.
With these people I saw her glorious,
and her people so just that the lily
was never set crosswise on its staff,
nor turned red through faction.
