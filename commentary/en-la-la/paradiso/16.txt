O little nobility of our blood,
if you make people boast of you
down here, where our affections grow faint,
it will never seem a marvel to me;
for where desire is not distorted,
I mean in Heaven, I boasted of it.
You are indeed a little mantle that soon shrinks,
so that unless it is lengthened day by day,
Time goes round it with shears.
With You, the form first offered at Rome,
in which its people persevere least,
my words began again.
And Beatrice, who had been a little apart,
smiling, seemed like the one who coughed
at the first fault written of Guinevere.
I began: You are my father;
you give me all boldness to speak;
you lift me so that I am more than myself.
Through so many streams is filled with joy
my mind, that it rejoices in itself,
because it can bear it without breaking.
Tell me, then, my dear first beginning,
who were your ancestors, and what were the years
that were marked in your childhood.
Tell me of Saint John's fold,
how large it was then, and which people were
within it worthy of the highest seats.
As there is rekindled by the breath of winds
a coal into flame, so I saw that
light grow bright at my gracious words;
and as it grew more beautiful to my eyes,
so with a sweeter, gentler voice,
though not in our modern speech,
it said to me: From the day when Hail was spoken
to the birth when my mother, now a saint,
was relieved of me, the burden she carried,
to its Lion five hundred and fifty
and thirty times came this fire,
to rekindle itself beneath his foot.
My ancestors and I were born in that place
where the last sixth district is first found,
by the runner in your yearly contest.
Let it suffice to hear this of my elders;
who they were and whence they came here,
it is more honorable to leave unsaid than to discuss.
All those who then were there,
able to bear arms between Mars and the Baptist,
were a fifth of those there today.
But the citizen body, now mixed
with folk from Campi, Certaldo, and Figline,
seemed pure down to the humblest artisan.
Oh, how much better it would be to have as neighbors
those people I speak of, and at Galluzzo
and Trespiano to have your borders,
than to have them within, enduring the stench
of the peasant from Aguglione and the man from Signa,
whose eye is already sharp for fraud!
If the people who most degenerate in the world
had not been a stepmother to the Caesars,
but kind as a mother to her child,
one has become a Florentine, and trades and barters,
who would have turned back to Semifonte,
where my grandfather used to go on his rounds.
Montemurlo would still belong to the counts;
the Cerchi would be in the parish of Acone,
and perhaps the Buondelmonti in Valdigreve.
Always the mixing of peoples
has been the beginning of a city's troubles,
as of yours the food piled on top.
And a blind bull falls sooner
than a blind lamb; and often there cuts
more and better one sword than five.
If you look at Luni and Urbisaglia,
how they have passed away, and how there pass
after them Chiusi and Sinigaglia,
hearing how families are destroyed
will seem to you neither strange nor hard,
since cities have their end.
All your things have their own death,
as you do; but it is hidden in some,
which last a long time, while lives are short.
And as the turning of the heavens and the Moon
covers and uncovers shores without rest,
so Fortune acts with Florence:
therefore it should seem no marvel,
whatever I tell of other Florentines,
whose fame has faded with time.
I saw the Ughi and saw the Catellini,
Filippi, Greci, Ormanni, and Alberichi,
already declining, illustrious citizens;
and I saw those as great as they were ancient,
with the Sannella, the Arca,
and the Soldanieri, and the Ardinghi, and the Bostichi.
Above the gate, now burdened
with such weight of new wickedness
that the ship will soon founder,
were the Ravignani, from whom descended
Count Guido and all who the name
of the noble Bellincione later took.
The della Pressa already knew how
one should govern; and Galigaio had
already in his house his gilded sword hilt and pommel.
Already great was the column of the Vajo,
the Sacchetti, Giuochi, Fifanti, and Barucci,
and the Galli, and those who blush for the bushel.
The stock from which the Calfucci sprang
was already great, and already there had been raised
to office the Sizi and Arrigucci.
Oh, how great I saw those now ruined
by their pride! And the golden balls
made Florence flourish in all their great works.
So did the fathers of those
who, whenever your Church falls vacant,
grow fat sitting in consistory.
The arrogant brood, dragonlike
after those who flee, but to whoever shows a tooth
or purse, tame as a lamb,
was already rising, though of humble stock,
so that Ubertin Donato disliked it,
whom his father-in-law later made kin to himself.
Caponsacchi had already settled in the market,
having come down from Fiesole; and already there were
as good citizens Guido and Infangato.
I shall tell you something incredible and true:
entering the little circle, one passed through a gate
named after the people of the Pear.
Whoever bears the fair device
of the great Baron, whose name and whose worth
the feast of Saint Thomas renews,
received knighthood and privilege from him;
though with the common people joins
now the one who rims it with a border.
The Gualterotti and Importuni were already there;
and the Borgo would still be more peaceful
if it lacked its new neighbors.
The house that caused your weeping,
through the just anger that brought you death
and ended your joyful life,
It was honored, and so were its allies.
O Buondelmonte, how badly you fled
your marriage, at another's evil urging!
Many who are now sad would be happy
if God had given you over to the Ema
the first time you came to the city.
Yet it was indeed fitting for that broken stone
that guards the bridge to split Florence,
a victim of its final peace.
And with these people, with others and with them themselves,
I saw Florence in such peace
that it had no cause to weep.
With these people I saw glorious
and just folk of hers, so much so that the lily
was never set crosswise on its staff,
nor turned red through faction.
