After your Charles, beautiful Clemence,
had clarified me, he told me of the deceptions
your descendants were destined to suffer;
but he said: Be silent, and let the years pass.
So, since I can say only that grief
that is just will come after your losses.
And already the sight of that holy light
had turned toward the good that fills it,
as toward the good that is so much to all things;
Ah, souls deceived, and impious creatures,
who turn your hearts away from such a good,
raising your heads toward vanities!
And then another of those splendors
came toward me, and its wish to please me
was shown in its outward brightness.
Beatrice's eyes, fixed
upon me, as before, with loving assent,
reassured me as to my desire.
Ah, quickly satisfy my wish,
blessed spirit, I said, and give me proof
that what I think may be reflected in you.
Then the light, still new to me,
from its depths, where it had sung before,
continued, as one does who loves to do good:
In that part of the wicked land
of Italy which lies between Rialto
and the sources of the Brenta and Piave,
a hill rises, and it does not rise very high,
from which a torch once descended
and struck the region with a great assault.
From one root, she and I were born.
I was called Cunizza, and here I shine
because the light of this star overcame me.
But I gladly forgive myself
for the cause of my fate, and it does not grieve me,
though it may seem harsh to your people.
Of this bright and dear joy
of our Heaven, who is nearest to me;
great fame remains, and before she dies,
this hundredth year will yet be multiplied fivefold.
See whether a person should make himself excellent,
so that the first life leaves behind another!
And the people living now do not consider this,
whom the Tagliamento and Adige enclose,
nor, though they have been punished, do they yet repent.
But soon it will be that Padua into a marsh
will change the water that bathes Vicenza,
because its people are cruel in their duty.
And where the Sile and Cagnano meet,
such a lord rules and walks with head held high
that a net is already being woven to catch him.
Feltre will still lament the betrayal
of its wicked shepherd, who will be vile
to such a degree that no one ever entered Malta for a like offense.
Too large would be the vat
that would receive the blood of Ferrara,
and the one weighing it ounce by ounce would grow tired,
which this courteous priest will give
to show his partisanship; and such gifts
will suit the way of life in that region.
Above us are mirrors you call Thrones,
from which God shines upon us as judge,
so that these words seem good to us.
Here she fell silent, and showed me
that she had turned to something else, by way of the wheel
in which she placed herself as she was before.
The other joy, already known to me,
as a precious thing, showed itself before me,
like a perfect ruby struck by the sun.
Joy makes its radiance grow there,
as laughter does here; but below there darkens
the outward shadow, as when the mind is sad.
God sees everything, and your sight is made one with Him,
I said, blessed spirit, so that nothing
of His will can be hidden from you.
Then your voice, which delights Heaven
always with the song of those holy flames
that made a hood of six wings,
why does it not satisfy my desires?
I would not now wait for you to ask,
if I were within you, as you are within me.
The greatest valley into which water spreads,
thus did his words then begin,
outside that sea which girdles the earth,
between discordant shores, toward the sun
travels so far that it makes noon
where it formerly used to make the horizon.
I was a dweller on the shore of that valley,
between the Ebro and the Macra, which in a short stretch
separates Genoese territory from Tuscany.
At nearly the same sunset and sunrise
sit Bougiah and the land where I was born,
which once made its harbor hot with its own blood.
That people called me Folco, to whom
my name was known; and this Heaven
bears my imprint, as I bore its imprint.
For no more did the daughter of Belus burn,
wronging both Sichæus and Creüsa,
than I, for as long as youth suited me;
nor did that Rhodopean woman, deceived as she
was by Demophoön, nor Alcides,
when he had enclosed Iole within his heart.
No one here repents of this, but here there is laughter,
not for the fault, which no longer returns to mind,
but for the power that ordered and provided.
Here one looks upon the art that is adorned
with so great an effect, and sees the good,
because the lower world follows the world above.
But so that all your wishes, fulfilled,
you may carry back with you, which were born in this sphere,
I must proceed further still.
You wish to know who is in this light,
which sparkles thus here beside me
like a sunbeam in clear water.
Know now that within it rests
Rahab, and so joined is she to our order,
she is sealed in its highest rank.
From this Heaven, where the shadow comes to a point
which your world casts, before any other soul
she was taken into Christ's triumph.
It was most fitting for Him to leave her as the palm
in a Heaven, of the high victory
which He gained with both His palms,
because she aided the first triumph
of Joshua in the Holy Land,
the memory of which scarcely touches the Pope.
Your city, which is the plant of him
who first turned his back on his Maker,
and whose whole growth is envy,
produces and spreads the accursed flower
that has led sheep and lambs astray,
because it has made the shepherd a wolf.
Because of it the Gospel and the great Doctors
are abandoned, and only the Decretals
are studied to such a degree that their hems make it plain.
The Pope and cardinals devote themselves to this;
their thoughts do not go to Nazareth,
where Gabriel opened his wings.
But the Vatican and the other sacred parts
of Rome, which were the cemetery
of the army that followed Peter,
will soon be free of adultery.
