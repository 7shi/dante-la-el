When both the sons of Latona,
Covered by Aries and by Libra,
Together make a belt of the horizon,
For as long as it takes from the point that is the Zenith in Libra,
Until each one from that circuit,
Changing hemisphere, frees itself;
For just so long, with a face painted with a smile,
Beatrice was silent, gazing
Fixedly at the point that had overcome me.
Then she began: I speak; I do not ask,
What you wish to hear, because I have seen it
Where every where and every when converge.
Not to acquire goodness for himself,
Which cannot be, but so that his splendor
Might, by shining, say, I subsist,
In his eternity, outside time,
Beyond every other measure, as it pleased him,
Eternal Love opened itself in nine loves.
Nor did he lie before, as though sluggish;
For neither before nor afterward preceded
God's movement over these waters.
Form and matter, joined and pure,
Came into being without defect,
Like three arrows from a three-stringed bow;
And as in glass, or in amber, or in crystal
A ray descends so that from its arrival
To its whole being there is no interval,
So the threefold effect of its Lord
Wills to be in his ray all at once and whole,
Without unfolding at its beginning.
Order was created and constituted together
With substances, and these were the summit
Of the world, in which pure act was produced.
Pure potentiality held the lowest part;
In the middle, potentiality to act was bound
By such a bond that it can never be undone.
Jerome wrote for you a long account
Of ages, of angels created
Before the other world was made;
But this truth is written in many places
By the writers of the Holy Spirit;
And you will see it if you look carefully;
And reason also sees it to some extent,
For it would not allow the movers
To remain so long without their perfection.
Now you know when and where these loves
Were created, and how; so that extinguished
In your desire now are three fires.
You would not reach twenty by counting
As quickly as part of the angels
Disturbed the foundation of your elements.
The other part remained and began this art,
Which you perceive with such delight,
That it never ceases to circle.
The beginning of the fall was the accursed
Pride of him whom you saw
Bound by all the weights of the world.
Those whom you see here were humble
In acknowledging that they came from the goodness
That made them ready for such understanding;
And so their vision was exalted
By illuminating grace and by their merit,
So that their will is firm and complete.
And I do not want you to doubt, but to be certain
That receiving grace is meritorious,
According as desire opens to it.
From now on, about this assembly
You can contemplate much, if words
Of mine have been gathered up, without further help.
But because on earth, in your schools,
It is read that angelic nature
Is such that it understands, remembers, and wills,
I will speak again, so that you may see clearly
The truth confused down there
By equivocation in such a reading.
These substances, once they rejoiced
In God's face, did not turn their gaze
Away from it, from which nothing is hidden;
Therefore their seeing is not interrupted
By a new object, and therefore they need not
Remember by means of separate concepts.
Thus, down there, people dream without sleeping,
Believing and not believing they speak the truth;
But one is more blameworthy and more ashamed.
Down there you do not all go down one path
In philosophizing; so far are you carried by
Love of appearance and its thought,
And even this is tolerated here
With less indignation than when there is put second
Divine Scripture, and when it is twisted.
There they do not consider how much blood it cost
To sow it in the world, and how pleasing are
Those who approach it humbly.
To be seen, each one exercises his wit and devises
His own inventions; and these are repeated
By preachers, and the Gospel falls silent.
One says that the Moon turned back
At Christ's Passion and interposed itself,
So that the Sun's light did not reach us;
And meanwhile, that the light hid itself
Of its own accord; and so to Spaniards and Indians,
As to Jews, the eclipse appeared.
Florence does not have as many Lapos and Bindos
As fables of this kind every year
Are shouted from pulpits here and there;
So the little sheep, who do not know,
Return from pasture fed on wind,
And ignorance does not excuse their loss.
Christ did not say to his first company:
Go and preach nonsense to the world;
Rather, he gave them the true foundation.
And that teaching sounded so in their mouths
So that, in fighting to kindle faith,
They made shields and spears of the Gospel.
Now people go with jokes and with obscenities
To preach; and if only they laugh,
The hood swells, and no more is asked.
But such a bird nests beneath the cowl
That, if the crowd saw it, it would see
The indulgence in which it trusts;
And so such folly grew on earth
So much and to such an extent that, without proof from any witness,
People would run after every promise.
Saint Anthony's pig fattens on this,
And many others, worse than pigs,
Paying with money that has no stamp.
But since we have strayed so far, turn
Your eyes now toward the straight road,
So that you may shorten the journey with time.
This nature rises so far beyond
In number that never was there speech,
Or mortal thought, that could reach so far.
And if you look at what is revealed
Through Daniel, you will see that beyond the thousands
A definite number is concealed.
The first light, which illumines all of them,
Is received in it in as many different ways
As there are splendors that appear here.
Thus, as the act of understanding
Is followed by desire and the sweetness of loving,
In each one it burns or cools differently.
See now the height and the abundance
Of eternal Power, since so many
Mirrors it made for itself, in which it is reflected,
Remaining one in itself, as it was before.
