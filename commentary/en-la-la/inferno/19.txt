O Simon Magus, O wretched followers,
because the things of God, which to goodness
ought to be brides, and you, rapacious ones,
for gold and silver you adulterate;
now I must sound the trumpet for you,
for you are in the third bolgia.
We were already at the next tomb,
climbing the rock, at the place
where it plunges straight above the middle of the ditch.
O highest Wisdom, how great is the art
you show in heaven, on earth, and in the evil world,
and how much justice your power brings!
I saw, along the sides and bottom,
the livid stone full of holes,
all of one width, and each one round.
They seemed to me neither smaller nor larger
than those in my fair San Giovanni,
made for the place of the baptisteries;
One of them, not many years ago,
I broke because someone inside was drowning;
and let this be a seal to disabuse everyone.
Outside each one's mouth protruded
the feet and legs of a sinner,
up to the thigh, while all the rest was inside.
The soles of both feet were burning for them all;
which is why they twitched so violently
that they would have snapped cords and bindings.
As the flame of things smeared with oil is wont
to move over the outer skin,
so it was there from heel to toe.
Who is that, master, who is distressed or angry,
twitching more than the others among his companions,
I said, and whom a redder flame consumes?
And he answered me: If you want me to carry you
down there, along the bank that slopes more steeply,
you will learn from him about himself and his wrongs.
And I: It pleases me as much as it pleases you;
you are lord, and you know that I do not depart
from your will, and you know, too, what remains unspoken.
Then we came to the fourth bank,
turning, and descended to the left,
down into the perforated, narrow bottom.
And the good master still, from his hip,
did not set me down until he had brought me to the hole
of him who lamented so with his leg.
O whoever you are, with your upper part held below,
sad soul, planted like a stake,
I began to say, if you can, speak.
I stood like the friar who hears the confession
of a treacherous assassin who, once thrust into the ground,
calls him back so that his death may be delayed.
And he cried out: Are you already here,
are you already here, Boniface?
The writing deceived me by several years.
Are you so quickly sated with that wealth
for which you did not fear to take by deceit
the lovely lady, and then abuse her?
I became like those who stand,
because they do not understand what has been said to them,
as if mocked, and unable to answer.
Then Virgil said: Tell him quickly,
I am not he, I am not he whom you think;
and I replied as I had been instructed.
At this the spirit wholly twisted his feet;
then sighing, and with a voice of lament,
he said to me: So, what do you ask of me?
If it matters so much to you to know who I am
that you have crossed the bank for this,
know that I wore the great mantle.
And truly I was a son of the Bear,
so greedy to enrich the little bears
that above I put wealth in my purse, and here myself.
Beneath my head are the others drawn down,
who before me practiced simony,
through the fissures of the rock of the abyss.
I too shall fall down there when
the one I thought you were arrives,
at that time when I made that sudden inquiry.
But I have already spent more time with my feet roasting,
since I came to stand upside down like this,
than he will stand planted with red feet;
for after him will come, of fouler deeds,
from the west a shepherd without law,
to such a degree that he must cover both him and me.
He will be a new Jason, of whom we read
in the book of Maccabees; and as to him was pliant
his king, so will the man who rules France be to him.
I do not know if I was too rash here,
for I answered him only in this verse:
Pray tell me now how much treasure was wanted
by Our Lord first from Saint Peter,
before he put the keys in Peter's keeping?
Surely he said only, Follow me.
Neither Peter nor the others asked of Matthias
gold or silver, when he was chosen by lot
to the place lost by the wicked soul.
So stay there, for you are justly punished;
and guard well the ill-gotten money
that made you bold against Charles.
And were I not still forbidden by
reverence for the supreme keys
that you held in your joyful life,
I would use much harsher words;
for your greed saddens the world,
trampling the good and exalting the wicked.
Of you shepherds the Evangelist had foreknowledge,
when she who sits upon the waters
was seen by him whoring with kings:
she who was born with seven heads
took her power from ten horns,
so long as virtue pleased her husband.
You have made yourselves a god of gold and silver.
What else separates you from idolaters,
except that they worship one, and you a hundred?
Ah, Constantine, how much evil was mothered
not by your conversion, but by that dowry
the first rich father received from you!
And while I sang these notes,
whether anger or conscience gnawed at him,
he kicked fiercely with both soles.
I truly believe my guide was pleased,
for with lips so content he listened all the while
to the sound of those true and forceful words.
Therefore he took me in both his arms,
and once he had held all of me against his chest,
he climbed back up the way he had come down.
Nor did he tire of holding me close,
until he carried me over the crest of the arch
that spans from the fourth bank to the fifth.
There he gently set down his burden,
welcome, given the rough and narrow rock,
which would be a hard passage for goats;
from there another valley came into my view.
