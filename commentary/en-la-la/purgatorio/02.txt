Now had the sun reached the horizon,
whose meridian circle overshadows
Jerusalem with its highest point:
And night, which circles opposite it,
was coming out of the Ganges with its scales,
which fall from its hands when overcome;
so that the white and rosy cheeks,
where I was, of lovely Dawn,
had grown tawny with age.
We still walked along the sea,
like people thinking of their journey,
who go with their hearts while their bodies stay;
and suddenly, as near morning
Mars reddens through dense vapors
low in the west above the sea's surface,
so there appeared to me—may I see it again—
a light coming over the sea so swiftly
that no flight could match its motion;
when I had briefly withdrawn my eye
to ask my guide about it,
I saw it again, brighter and larger.
Then at each side of it there appeared
something white that I could not identify; below,
little by little another emerged from it.
My master still said nothing
until the first white shapes opened their wings;
then he recognized the boatman,
and cried: Kneel, kneel!
Behold God's angel; fold your hands.
From now on you will see such ministers.
See how he scorns human devices,
wanting neither oar nor any other sail
but his wings, even across such distant shores.
See how he holds them upright toward heaven,
beating the air with eternal feathers
that do not change like mortal plumage.
As the divine bird came nearer and nearer,
it appeared brighter,
so that my eye could not endure it close by;
but I lowered my gaze, and it came to shore
with a little, swift, light boat,
so light that the water swallowed none of it.
At the stern stood the heavenly pilot,
so blessed he seemed by his very appearance;
and more than a hundred spirits sat inside.
When Israel went out of Egypt
they all sang together with one voice,
with all that is written afterward in that psalm.
Then he made the sign of the holy cross over them;
at that they all leapt onto the shore,
and he went away swiftly, as he had come.
The crowd that remained there seemed strange,
gazing all around at that place,
like someone tasting new things.
The sun shot daylight from every side,
and with its keen arrows had driven Capricorn
from the middle of the sky,
when the new arrivals raised their heads
toward us, saying: If you know the way,
show us how to go up the mountain.
Virgil answered: Perhaps you think
we know this place well;
but we are pilgrims, as you are.
We came here a little before you,
by another way, so harsh and hard
that climbing now will seem like play.
The souls who noticed me,
because I was still breathing and alive,
turned pale with wonder;
and just as people press toward a messenger
who brings an olive branch, to hear the news,
with no one seeming to mind being jostled,
so all those fortunate souls
fixed their gaze upon my face,
almost forgetting to go and make themselves fair.
I saw one of them come forward
to embrace me with such affection
that she moved me to do the same.
O empty shades, except in appearance!
Three times I clasped my hands behind her,
and each time brought them back to my chest empty.
I think I showed my astonishment,
for the shade smiled and drew back,
while I, following her, pressed forward.
She gently told me to stop.
Then I recognized who she was and begged her
to pause a little and speak with me.
She answered: As I loved you
in my mortal body, so I love you, freed from it;
so I will stop. But why are you traveling?
My Casella, to return again
to where I am, I am making this journey;
but why was so much time taken from you?
And he to me: No one has wronged me,
if the one who takes whom and when he pleases
has many times denied me this passage;
for his will is made of what is just.
Indeed, for three months he has welcomed
whoever wished to enter in peace.
Therefore I, who had turned toward the shore
where the waters of the Tiber grow salty,
was kindly received by him
at that mouth where he now directs his wing;
for there he always gathers
whoever does not descend toward Acheron.
And I: If a new law does not take away
your memory or skill in the song of love,
which used to quiet all my desires,
may it please you to comfort a little
my soul, which, with my body,
has grown so weary on the way here.
Love, that speaks within my mind,
he began so sweetly then
that its sweetness still sounds within me.
My master, I, and all those people
who were with him seemed so content,
as if nothing else touched anyone's mind.
We all stood fixed and attentive
to his notes; and then the revered old man,
shouting: What is this, sluggish spirits?
What negligence, what lingering is this?
Run to the mountain, to strip off the husk
that keeps God manifest from you.
And as doves gathering grain or tares,
assembled at their feeding place,
sit quietly, showing no customary boldness,
if something appears to frighten them,
suddenly they leave their food untouched,
for a greater concern has seized them;
so I saw that fresh band leave off its song
and head toward the slope,
like someone who goes without knowing where;
and we left no less swiftly.
