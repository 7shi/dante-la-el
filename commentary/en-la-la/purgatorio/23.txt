While my eyes through the green leaves
I was fixing, just as one is wont to do
who loses his life following little birds;
my more-than-father said to me: My son,
come now, for the time allotted to us
should be spent to better purpose.
I turned my face and steps no less swiftly
toward the sages who spoke in such a way
that they made the journey cost me nothing.
And then a sound of weeping and singing was heard:
My lips, O Lord, in a manner
such as gave birth to both delight and grief.
O sweet Father, what is this that I hear?
I began; and he replied: Spirits who go along,
perhaps loosening the knot of their debt.
As thoughtful pilgrims do
when on the road they meet people they do not know,
turning toward them but not stopping,
so behind us, moving more quickly,
coming and passing, looking at us was
a silent, devout crowd of souls.
Each had dark, hollow eyes,
a pale face, and was so wasted
that the skin took the shape of the bones.
I do not believe that, so far as the barest skin,
Erysichthon grew dry
from starvation, when he feared it most.
I said to myself, thinking: Behold
the people who lost Jerusalem,
when Mary bit into her son.
Their eye sockets looked like rings without gems.
Anyone who reads man in a human face
would surely have recognized the M there.
Who would believe that the scent of an apple,
working in this way, could create desire,
or the scent of water, without our knowing how?
I was already wondering what made them so hungry,
because of the still unknown cause
of their wasting and their sorrowful scales;
when, behold, from deep within its head
a shade turned its eyes toward me and stared fixedly at me;
then it cried aloud: What favor is this?
I would never have recognized him by his face,
but his voice made clear to me
what his appearance had hidden within itself.
That spark, his speech, rekindled for me all
my recognition, and his changed features,
and I recognized Forese's face.
Ah, do not look at the dried scab,
which drains the color from my skin, he begged,
or at the lack of flesh on my body;
but tell me the truth about yourself, and who are those
two souls accompanying you there?
Do not leave without speaking.
Your face, which I once wept over as dead,
now gives me no less grief to weep over,
I answered him, seeing it so distorted.
Then tell me, for God's sake, what strips you so bare.
Do not make me speak while I am wondering,
for one filled with another desire cannot speak well.
And he said to me: By eternal counsel
a power descends into the water and the tree
left behind, and so I grow this thin.
All these people, weeping as they sing,
for following gluttony beyond measure,
are made holy here through hunger and thirst.
What kindles the desire to drink and eat is
the scent rising from the apple, and from the spray
that spreads upward through its foliage.
And not just once, in this place,
as we circle, our punishment is renewed;
I call it punishment, but should call it comfort,
for that desire leads us to the tree
that led Christ, joyful, to cry out, Eli,
when by his blood he freed us.
And I said to him: Forese, from the day
you changed the world for a better life,
five years have not yet passed.
If earlier there had ended in you the power
to sin more, than there came the hour
of good sorrow that remarries us to God,
how have you come up here so soon?
I thought I would find you lower down,
where time is repaid with time.
At that he answered me: So quickly she brought me
to drink the sweet wormwood of martyrdom:
my Nella, with her broken-hearted weeping.
With her devout prayers and her sighs
she drew me from the slope where one waits,
and freed me from the other circles.
So much dearer and more beloved to God is
my little widow, whom I loved so much,
the more solitary she is in doing good;
for Sardinia's Barbagia is far
more modest in its women
than the Barbagia where I left her.
O sweet brother, what would you have me say?
The future is already before my eyes,
to which this hour will not be very old,
when from the pulpit it will be forbidden
for shameless Florentine women
to go about showing their chest and breasts.
What barbarian women were there ever, what Saracen women,
for whom there were needed, in order to induce them
to go about, spiritual or other laws?
But if these shameless women truly knew
what swift heaven is preparing for them,
their mouths would already be open to howl.
For, if my foresight here does not deceive me,
they will be sorrowful before the cheeks grow bearded
on the child now soothed with a lullaby.
Ah, brother, now do not hide yourself from me any longer;
you see that not I alone, but these people,
all gaze where you block the sun.
At that I replied to him: If you bring to mind
what you were with me and what I was with you,
the present memory will still be painful.
From that life I was turned by this man
who walks ahead of me, the other day, when round
his sister showed herself to you.
And I pointed to the sun. This man through deep
night led me toward the dead,
with this real flesh that follows him.
Then his encouragement drew me out,
as we climbed and circled the mountain
that straightens you whom the world made crooked.
He promises so much to make me his companion
that I shall be where Beatrice is;
there I must be left without him.
This is Virgil, who speaks to me this way,
and I pointed him out with my finger; the other is that shade
for whom just now every slope shook
in your realm, which sends him forth from itself.
