It was already the hour that turns longing
for sailors and softens the heart,
on the day they said to dear friends: Farewell to God;
and that the new pilgrim of love
stirs himself, if he hears a bell from far away
that seems to mourn the dying day;
when I began to render vain
my hearing, and to admire one of the souls,
risen, who asked with her hand for silence.
She joined her hands and raised both palms,
fixing her eyes toward the east,
as if saying to God: I desire nothing else.
Te lucis ante so devoutly
came from her mouth, and in such sweet notes,
that it made me forget myself.
And the others then, sweetly and devoutly,
followed her through the whole hymn from start to finish,
their eyes fixed on the heavenly spheres.
Reader, sharpen your eyes well here toward the truth,
for the veil is now so very thin
that it is easy, indeed, to pass through.
I saw that noble company
afterward silently gazing upward,
as if waiting, pale and humble;
and I saw come from above and descend below
two angels with two flaming swords,
blunted and stripped of their points.
Green as little leaves just newly born,
were they in appearance, which, by green wings
beaten and fanned, they trailed behind them.
One came to stand a little above us,
and the other descended to the opposite bank,
so the people were enclosed between them.
I could clearly make out their fair hair,
but my eyes strayed from their faces,
like a faculty overwhelmed by excess.
Both come from Mary's bosom,
said Sordello, to guard the valley,
because of the serpent that will soon come.
So I, not knowing by which path,
turned around and pressed close,
all frozen, to the trusted shoulders.
And Sordello said: Let us now descend to the valley
among the great shades, and speak with them;
they will be very glad to see you.
I think I went down only three steps,
and I was below, where I saw one gazing
as if he wished to know me.
It was already the time when the air was darkening,
but not so much that between his eyes and mine
what had been hidden could not be seen.
He came toward me, and I came toward him:
Noble Judge Nino, how glad I was
to see you were not among the lost!
No gracious greeting between us was left unsaid.
Then he asked: How long ago did you come
to the mountain's foot across distant waters?
Oh, I said to him, through the sorrowful places
I came this morning, and I am in the first life,
though by going thus I gain the other.
And when my answer was heard,
Sordello and he drew back,
like people suddenly bewildered.
One turned to Virgil, the other to me,
who was sitting there, calling: Rise, Conrad,
come see what God has graciously willed.
Then, turning to me: By that singular love
you owe to Him who so conceals
His origin, for it is not pleasing to Him,
when you are beyond the broad waters,
tell Giovanna to call for me
where the innocent are answered.
I do not think her mother loves me more,
since she has exchanged her white veils,
which, poor woman, she must still long for.
Through her one can quite easily understand
how long love's fire lasts in a woman,
if sight or touch does not often kindle it.
Will not make her so fine a tomb
the viper the Milanese take to war,
as the rooster of Gallura would have done.
So he spoke, marked by the imprint,
in his face, of that righteous zeal
that sets hearts aflame beyond all measure.
My eager eyes went toward heaven,
only where the stars move more slowly,
like a wheel nearer its axle.
And my guide: Son, what do you gaze at above?
And I to him: At those three little flames
that set all this pole ablaze.
And he to me: The four bright stars
you saw this morning are low on the other side,
and these have risen where they were.
While I was speaking, and Sordello drew him to himself,
saying: Look, there is our adversary!
And he pointed so that I would look there.
On that side where no shelter has
the little valley, stood a serpent,
perhaps like the one that gave Eve bitter food.
Through grass and flowers came the evil serpent,
turning its head now and again toward its back,
licking itself like a beast grooming itself.
I did not see it, so I cannot say
how the heavenly hawks took flight,
but I clearly saw both of them move.
Feeling the air split by their green wings,
the serpent fled; the angels wheeled
upward to their posts, flying back as one.
The shade who had drawn close to the Judge,
when he called out, throughout that whole encounter
was not once drawn away from me in speech.
If the lamp that leads you upward
finds in your will as much wax
as is needed all the way to the green summit,
she began, if true news
from Valdimacra or its nearby region,
you know, tell it to me, who was once great there.
I was called Conrad Malaspina:
I am not the elder, but descend from him;
I bore love for my kin, which is purified here.
Oh, I said to him, in your lands
I have never been; but where does anyone dwell
in all Europe that you are not known?
The fame that honors your house
is voiced by lords, is voiced by the countryside,
so those who have never been there know you.
And I swear to you, if I rise above,
your honored family does not lose
its worth in generosity and the sword.
Custom and nature favor it,
for, though the guilty head twists the world,
your house alone walks straight and scorns the bad road.
And he: Now go, for the sun will not recline
seven times in the bed the Ram
covers and straddles with all four feet,
before this gracious opinion
is nailed into the middle of your head
with stronger nails than another's report;
if judgment's course is not halted.
