“Ah! alas, Satan, alas, Satan, Aleppe!”
Plutus began to cry out with a hoarse voice.
And the gracious sage, who knows all things,
Encouraging me, said: “Let not your fear harm you;
„For whatever power he may possess,
„He shall not hinder you from descending the crag.”
Then turning toward those swollen lips,
He said to him: “Silence, accursed wolf;
„Swallow and digest your own fury.
„Not without reason is the descent hither.
„They will it on high, where Michael vengeance
„Took once for the arrogant violence.”
As sails puffed out by the wind
Collapse together when the masts are broken,
So fell the pitiless beast.
Into the fourth depth we thus descended,
Advancing onward toward the bank of the abyss
That swallows down all the evils of the Universe.
O Divine Justice! Who could heap together
So many terrors as I saw there?
Why do our offences thus tear us apart?
As above Charybdis the rushing wave
Against another wave is shattered by it,
So here it befalls them to clash together.
More souls than elsewhere I saw here,
On this side and on that with loud howlings
Rolling weights with their breasts.
They dashed against one another; and then each
Turned round and retreated backward,
Crying out: “Why do you keep?” and “Why do you cast away?”
Thus they returned along the circle
From either side toward the opposite part,
Chanting again this shameful song of theirs.
Then each turned back, having run through
Her half-circle, toward another clash.
And I, deeply moved in my heart,
Said: “Master, now teach me who
„These indeed are, and whether all were priests
„Whom I see tonsured on the left.“
And he said: “All of them were squinting
„In mind so much during their former life,
„That they kept no measure in their spending.
„This their barking clearly shows,
„When they come to the two parts of the circle,
„Where opposite faults divide them.
„And these, whose heads no hair covers,
„Were priests, Popes, and Cardinals,
„Whose avarice was beyond measure.“
And I: “Master, among these it would be fitting
„For me also to recognize some, who with such
„Vices were defiled.“
And he to me: “You nourish a vain hope;
„The obscure life that sullied them
„ Now renders them obscure and unrecognizable here.
„ Forever shall they come to the two clashes:
„ These shall rise from the tomb with fist
„ Held closed, and those with shaven hair.
„ By ill giving and ill keeping, of the world
„ They are deprived—the fair one—and now in strife
„ They are, which I cannot depict in words.
„ Now you see, my son, how much it is of brief
„ Duration, all the goods of Fortune,
„ For which the human race is thrown into turmoil.
„ For all that now exists beneath the moon
„ Of gold, and that existed before, to the weary
„ Souls, these here, could not give even a slight respite.”
And I: “Master, now tell me this also:
„ Who then is Fortune, of whom you make mention?
„ And how holds she in her hands the goods of the world?”
And he replied: “O senseless beings!
„ O the ignorance that blinds you!
„ Listen now to what I wish to tell you.
„ He who in wisdom surpasses all,
„Who created the heavens and directs them
„So that every part shines toward every other part,
„Distributing the light equally to all,
„Likewise ordained for earthly
„Dignities ministers and guides,
„So that she might in time transfer the fleeting goods
„From people to people, from one race to another,
„Whatever foresight mortals may exercise.
„For of nations, one rules and another bows down,
„According to the decree alone of Fortune,
„Who is hidden, like a serpent beneath the grass.
„Your understanding has no power against her:
„She foresees, judges, and administers
„Her own dominion, like every other deity.
„Of her changes there is no cessation;
„Necessity makes her to be swift,
„As others in turn continually claim their share.
„She it is who is hung upon a cross
„By those who ought to give her praise,
„And instead speak evil of her unjustly;
„But she is blessed and hears not their reproaches.
„Joyful among the other primal creatures,
„She turns her sphere, and being blessed rejoices.
„Let us now descend into greater agonies;
„Already every star sinks that was rising
„When I set out; and to linger longer is forbidden.”
We crossed over to the other bank of the circle
Above a boiling spring, flowing down
Into a trench hollowed out by its streams;
And the water was turbid, or rather black.
And we, amidst the miry waves,
Came down by another way.
A marsh called Styx is formed by
This dismal stream, when it descends
To the foot of the malignant and dark banks.
And I, gazing intently there, saw
In that mire mud-covered shades,
All naked and savage of aspect.
These struck one another not only with their hands,
But with head and with feet and with breast,
And with their teeth tore off their flesh piecemeal.
And the Guide said to me: “Now, my son, you see
„ The souls of those mastered by wrath.
„ And this too I would have you know for certain,
„ That a multitude here sighs beneath the water,
„ Making the bubbles on the surface,
„ As wherever you turn you now behold.
„ Fixed in the mud they cry: ‘“We were
‚ „ Wretches in the air that rejoices in the sun,
‚ „ Bearing within us the smoke of sullenness;
‚ „ But now we suffer in the black mire.”’
„ This chant they gurgle in their throats,
„ Unable to utter whole words.”
Thus then we traversed of the foul marsh
The arc between it and the bank,
Gazing intently at those who swallow the mire;
And at length we came to the foot of a tower.
