Upon the edge of a high bank, composed
Of great broken rocks in a circle,
We arrived at a more grievous throng.
And there, on account of the horrible stench,
Ascending from the deep abyss,
Behind the lid of a great tomb
We stood; and upon it I read an inscription
Saying: “Pope Anastasius I guard,
„Whom Photinus drew out of the straight way.”
—“It is fitting that we descend slowly,
„So that we may first become accustomed to the foul
„Exhalation; for thereafter there will be no harm.”
Thus spoke the Protector; and I in turn:
“In compensation, find how may not pass
„Time in vain.” And he resumes:
“Behold what I am thinking, child: within these
„Rocks there are three lesser circles of different
„Grades each, similar to those you have passed through,
„All of them full of accursed spirits.
„But so that hereafter the mere sight may suffice you,
„Know why and how they are prisoners.
„The end of every malice hated by God
„Is injustice, and every such end
„Harms others either by violence or by fraud.
„And fraud, being by nature a human evil,
„Is more hateful to God, and further
„Below the fraudulent lie and are punished.
„The first lesser circle is that of the violent.
„But since violence is committed against persons
„Three, it is divided into three precincts:
„Against God, against oneself, against one's neighbour
„And what pertains to these, one employs violence,
„ As through clear explanations you shall learn.
„ Violent murder and painful wounds
„ Harm one's neighbours; and their substance,
„ Arsons, destructions, plunderings.
„ Wherefore murderers and all who do evil,
„ Destroyers and robbers, are punished
„ In troops within the first precinct.
„ Himself with violence and his own substance to harm
„ Is in a man's power; therefore in the second
„ Precinct must repent in vain
„ He who deprives himself of the world of the living,
„ Or who wastes his own property,
„ And weep instead of being joyful;
„ One can use violence against the Deity
„ Blaspheming and denying God in private,
„ Or despising nature and her bounty.
„ Wherefore the third precinct seals
„ With the same mark Sodom and Cahors
„ And those who despise God in soul and words.
„ Fraud, the sting of every conscience,
„ Is committed either to the harm of those who gave trust,
„ Or of those to whom no trust is owed.
„ The second kind of fraud breaks
„ Only the bond of natural love;
„ So that the second lesser circle holds within it
„ Hypocrisy, flattery, jugglery,
„ Simony, counterfeiters, thieves, swindlers,
„ Pandars, and similar filth.
„ And the other fraud forgets the love
„ Which nature inspires, and that also which is bound close
„ By the bond of personal trust;
„ Wherefore into the lowest lesser circle, the centre
„ Of the Universe, where Dis holds his seat,
„ Everyone who betrays shall be destroyed eternally.”
And I: “Protector, clearly your words
„ Explain distinctly that
„ Pit and the people dwelling therein.
„ But tell me now: those of the muddy marsh,
„ And those driven by the wind, and struck by the rain,
„ And those clashing together with harsh words,
„ Why are they not punished in the fiery city,
„ If indeed God holds them in wrath?
„ And if not, why do they suffer in another manner?”
And he said to me: “Why do your thoughts
„ Wander so far beyond what you usually seem?
„ Or is your mind looking elsewhere?
„ Do you not remember the words, by which in accordance
„ With the rules of your Ethics three dispositions
„ Are opposed to the divine Will,
„ Incontinence, malice, and bestiality?
„ And how against God less offends
„ Incontinence, being less worthy of blame?
„ If you reflect carefully upon this maxim,
„ And hold in memory who those are,
„ Who not here, but above are punished,
„ You will see then how they are separated
„ From these guilty ones here, and how they are hammered
„ Less by Divine Justice.”—
—“O luminary, who heal every dimmed
„ Sight, you gladden me by enlightening me, so that
„ No less dear is it to me to know than to doubt.
„ Return a little now,” I said, “backward,
„ Where you say usury is hateful
„ In the sight of God, and resolve my perplexity.”
And he: “Philosophy, to those who study
„ Her, in many chapters teaches,
„ How nature draws her own course
„ From the divine mind and from divine art.
„ And if you read through your Physics carefully,
„ You will find after not many pages
„ That your own art, as far as it can, nature
„ Follows, as a pupil follows his teacher;
„ So that your art is as it were a grandchild of God.
„ From these two sources, if you call to mind
„ Genesis at its beginning, mankind must
„ Draw forth both livelihood and substance.
„ And since the usurer lives otherwise,
„ Nature herself and her follower
„ He insults; for in another he places his hope.—
„ But follow me now; for it is time to go forward:
„And the Fishes already appear upon the horizon;
„And the Wain lies wholly over Caurus;
„And the descent down the cliff is yonder.”
