Here are the gates of the city of lamentations;
Here are the gates of eternal sorrow;
Here is the region of the accursed race.
The purpose of my Creator was Justice.
Divine Power fashioned me,
The supreme Wisdom, and primal love.
Never was there anywhere any creature before me,
Save eternal things, of which I too am one;
Abandon, every one entering here, all hope.
These words in dark colour
Written upon a gateway, when I read them,
I said: “Master, harsh seems of these words
„The meaning.“ And he, as one prudent, spoke:
“All suspicion here it behoves to leave behind;
„Every feeling of cowardice here let die.
„To the place we have come, where I said you
„Would see sorrowful and wretched souls,
„Who have lost the intellectual gift.“
And then, placing his hand in my hand,
With a cheerful countenance that made me take courage,
Into the secret sights he led me.
There sighs, weepings, countless lamentations
Resounded through the starless air,
At which I at first began to weep.
Diverse tongues, dreadful speeches,
Shrieks of grief, accents of wrath,
Shrill hoarse cries, and beating of hands
Made up a tumult whirling round
Always about that colourless air,
Like sand whirled together by a tempest.
And I, wholly dizzy in mind,
Said: “Master, what is this that I hear?
„ And who are they that suffer under distress?”
And he to me: “This piteous lot
„ Have received the sorrowful souls of all who
„ Lived without blame and without praise.
„ Mingled with this wicked band are
„ Angels who were neither rebellious
„ Nor faithful, but friends to themselves alone.
„ And they were thrust out of the heavens, which they dimmed,
„ And unreceived in deep Hell they remain,
„ Lest by them the wicked be glorified.”
And I: “Master, what is the suffering weighing
„ Upon them that makes them lament so bitterly?”
And he: “In brief,” he said, “I will tell you:
„ These have not the hope of dying;
„ And their life is so lowly
„ That they covet every other lot.
„ No fame of them has remained in the world;
„ They are not worthy of justice and of pity.
„ Take no heed of them; look and pass on.”
Then, gazing intently, I saw a banner
Whirling and running so swiftly,
That it seemed to disdain all pause.
And behind it so long a train
Of people followed, that I had never thought
Death had destroyed so many.
And when I had recognized some of them, then gazing intently
I saw the shade of a familiar man, who
Cowardly made the great refusal.
Straightway I understood, and was well assured,
That this was the sect of wretched men,
Hateful to God and to God's enemies.
These wretches, who had never been alive,
Were stark naked and wounded by stings
Of wasps and gadflies swarming there.
And from their cheeks flowed blood,
Which, mingled with tears, falling to their feet,
Was sucked up by loathsome worms.
And then, turning my eyes farther onward,
I saw upon the banks of a river a multitude;
And I said: “Master, now grant me
„To know who these are, and what necessity
„Urges them swiftly to cross over,
„As through the dim light I discern.“
And he to me: “All these things you shall know,
„When once we stay our steps
„Upon the mournful river Acheron.”
Then, casting my eyes downward in shame,
And fearing lest I become troublesome,
As far as the river I refrained from speaking.
But lo, toward us comes in a skiff
A white-haired old man, ancient in hoary locks,
Crying aloud: “Woe to you, accursed souls!
„No hope is yours to see the light of Heaven.
„I come to ferry you to the farther shore,
„Into eternal darkness, into fire and frost.
„And you, what seek you here, living soul?
„Separate yourself now from these dead.”
But when he saw me delaying to depart,
He said: “By another way, by another passage,
„Not from here, you shall find a means of exit.
„ Yet a lighter skiff you need for this.”
And my Master to him: “Charon, be not wroth;
„ This is the will on high of those who have power to do
„ What they will; inquire no further.”
So then unmoved his shaggy jaws
Kept the ferryman of the livid marsh,
Around whose eyes circled flames.
And these souls, naked and weary,
Gnashing their teeth, changed colour,
When they heard the harsh words of the ferryman;
They blasphemed God and their parents,
The race of men, the place, the time, and the seed
Of their own lineage and of their birth.
Then they all moved forward together in a throng,
Weeping bitterly, toward the accursed shore,
Where every one shall come who fears not God.
The demon Charon, with fiery eyes
Beckoning, herded them all together,
Smiting with his oar those slow to hasten.
Just as in autumn the leaves fall
One after another, until the bough at last
Gives back to the earth all its adornment,
So the evil-natured seed of Adam
Darts toward that shore one after another
At his signs, like a bird at the call;
Thus toward the dark wave they go,
And before they have all been ferried across to the other side,
A new throng is gathered there.
“My son,” says my gentle Master to me,
“All who have died in the wrath of God
„From every land assemble here,
„And hasten to cross the river;
„For divine Justice spurs them on,
„Turning their fear into desire.
„This way never passed a righteous soul;
„So if Charon now complains against you,
„You can understand what this signifies.”
And when he had said these things, the deep-gloom region
Shook so violently that even now from terror
The memory bathes me all in sweat.
A wind, blowing forth from that tear-bearing
Land, flashed forth a crimson light,
Overpowering every sense within me;
And I fell like a man overcome by sleep.
