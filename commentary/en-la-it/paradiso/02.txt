O you who are in a little boat,
desirous to hear, having followed
behind my ship that singing crosses over,
turn back to see your shores again;
do not put out upon the open sea; for perhaps,
losing me, you would be left bewildered.
The water I take was never yet traversed;
Minerva blows, and Apollo guides me,
and new Muses show me the Bears.
You other few, who stretched your necks
in good time toward the bread of angels, on which
one lives here, but never grows sated with it,
may well put out upon the deep salt sea
your vessel, keeping to my furrow
ahead of the water that grows level again.
Those glorious ones who crossed to Colchis
did not marvel as you will
when they saw Jason made a plowman.
The inborn and perpetual thirst
for the godlike kingdom was bearing us on,
swift almost as you see heaven.
Beatrice was looking upward, and I at her;
and perhaps in as much time as a bolt comes to rest,
and flies, and is loosed from the nut,
I saw myself arrived where a wondrous thing
turned my gaze toward itself; and therefore she,
from whom my action could not be hidden,
turned toward me, as glad as she was beautiful:
Direct your mind in gratitude to God, she said to me,
who has joined us to the first star.
It seemed to me that a cloud covered us,
luminous and thick, solid and polished,
like a diamond upon which the sun struck.
Within itself the eternal pearl
received us, as water receives
a ray of light, remaining undivided.
If I was body, and here one cannot conceive
how one dimension admitted another,
as must be if body penetrates body,
all the more should it kindle in us the desire
to see that essence in which is seen
how our nature was united to God.
There shall be seen what we hold by faith,
not demonstrated; but it will be known of itself,
in the manner of the first truth that man sees.
I answered then: My lady, as devout
as ever I can be, I thank Him
who has removed me from the mortal world.
But tell me, what are the dark marks
of this body, which down there on earth
make people tell fables of Cain?
She smiled a little, and then: If it errs,
the opinion, she said to me, of mortals,
where the key of sense does not unlock,
surely they ought not to prick you, the arrows
of wonder, any longer; following the senses
you see that reason has short wings.
But tell me what you, Dante, think of it.
And I: What appears diverse to us up here,
I believe that rare and dense bodies make it so.
And she: Surely, you will see deeply submerged
in falsehood your belief, if you listen well to
the argument that I shall make against it.
The eighth sphere shows you many
lights, which in quality and in quantity
may be marked as different in aspect.
If rare and dense alone made this so,
a single power would be in them all,
distributed in greater and lesser and equal measure.
Diverse powers must be fruits
of formal principles, and these, save one,
would, by your reasoning, stand destroyed.
Again, if rarity were of that darkness
the cause that you ask about, then either all the way through in places
would be thus bare of its matter
this planet; or, just as shares out
fat and lean a body, so this one
in its volume would change leaves.
If the first were so, it would be manifest
in the eclipse of the sun, by the shining through
of the light, as when admitted into any other rare substance.
This is not so; therefore we must examine
the other case: and if it happens that I set the other aside,
falsified will be your opinion.
If it is the case that this rare substance does not pass through,
there must be a limit beyond which
its contrary does not let it pass farther;
and from there the other's ray is poured back
just as color returns through glass
which hides lead behind itself.
Now you will say that it shows itself dark
there, the ray, more than in other parts,
through being there reflected farther back.
From this objection you can be freed by
experiment, if ever you try it,
which is wont to be the source of the streams of your arts.
Three mirrors you shall take, and set two apart
from you at the same distance, and the other, farther off,
may meet your eyes between the first two.
Turned toward it, see that behind the back
there stands for you a light that lights up the three mirrors
and returns to you reflected from them all.
Although in size it does not extend so far,
the more distant image, you will see
how it must shine equally bright.
Now as beneath the blows of the warm rays
the substance of the snow is left bare
both of its color and of its former cold;
thus left you in the intellect,
I wish to inform with light so living
that it will shimmer to you at the sight of it.
Within the heaven of divine peace
revolves a body, in whose power
the being of all that it contains lies.
The next heaven, which has so many eyes,
divides that being among diverse essences
distinct from it and contained by it.
The other circles, through various distinctions,
the dispositions that they have within themselves
direct to their ends and to their seeds.
These organs of the world proceed thus,
as you now see, from degree to degree,
that from above they take, and below they act.
Look well now at how I go
through this place to the truth that you desire,
so that afterward you may know how to keep to the ford alone.
The motion and the power of the holy circles,
as from the smith the art of the hammer,
must breathe forth from the blessed movers.
The heaven that so many lights make beautiful,
from the deep mind that turns it
takes the image and makes itself its seal.
And as the soul within your dust
through members different and fitted
for diverse powers unfolds itself,
so the intelligence its goodness,
multiplied through the stars, unfolds,
revolving itself upon its own unity.
A different power makes a different alloy
with the precious body that it quickens,
in which, like life, in it it binds itself.
Through the glad nature from which it derives,
the mingled power shines through the body,
as gladness through a living pupil.
From it comes what from light to light
appears different, not from dense and rare;
it is the formal principle that produces,
in keeping with its goodness, the murky and the clear.
