As soon as the last word
the blessed flame took up to speak,
the holy millstone began to turn;
and in its circuit it had not turned all the way
before another enclosed it in a circle,
and matched motion to motion, and song to song;
song that so surpasses our Muses,
our sirens, in those sweet trumpets,
as the first splendor surpasses that which it reflected.
As through a tender cloud there curve
two bows, parallel and of like color,
when Juno commands her handmaid,
the outer born of the inner,
after the manner of the speech of that wandering maid
whom love consumed as the sun consumes vapors;
and they make people here foreknow,
by the covenant that God made with Noah,
that the world will never again be flooded;
so, of those everlasting roses,
the two garlands turned about us,
and so the outer answered the inner.
After the dance and the other great rejoicing,
both of the singing and of the flashing
of light with light, joyous and gentle,
together at one instant and with one will grew still,
just as the eyes, at the pleasure that moves them,
must close and lift together,
from the heart of one of the new lights
came a voice, which the needle to the star
made me resemble, and made me turn to where it was;
and it began: The love that makes me beautiful
draws me to speak of the other leader,
on whose account mine is so well spoken of here.
It is fitting that where one is, the other be brought in,
so that, as they fought together,
so their glory may shine together.
The army of Christ, which so dear
it cost to arm anew, behind the standard
was moving slowly, mistrustful and thin of rank;
when the emperor who reigns forever
provided for the host that was in jeopardy,
through grace alone, and not because it was worthy;
and, as has been said, came to his bride's aid
with two champions, at whose deeds, at whose words,
the people who had gone astray rallied.
In that region where there rises to open
sweet Zephyr the new leaves
in which one sees Europe clothe herself anew,
not very far from the beating of the waves
behind which, through its long career,
the sun at times hides itself from every man,
sits fortunate Caleruega,
under the protection of the great shield
in which the lion lies beneath and holds sway above.
Within it was born the loving suitor
of the Christian faith, the great athlete,
kindly to his own and harsh to his enemies;
and as soon as it was created, filled was
his soul with divine power
that in his mother it made her a prophet.
After the nuptials were accomplished
at the sacred font between him and the faith,
where they endowed each other with mutual salvation,
the woman who gave assent for him
saw in sleep the wondrous fruit
that was to come from him and from his heirs:
and that he might be construed as what he was,
a spirit went forth from here to name him
with the possessive of him whose he wholly was.
Dominic he was called; and I speak of him
as of the husbandman whom Christ
chose for his garden to help him.
Well did he seem a messenger and familiar of Christ;
for the first love that was manifest in him
was for the first counsel that Christ gave.
Many times was he, silent and awake,
found on the ground by his nurse,
as if he were saying: I have come for this.
O father of his, truly Felix!
O mother of his, truly Joanna,
if, interpreted, it means what they say!
Not for the world, for which men now toil
in the footsteps of Hostiensis and Taddeo,
but for love of the true manna,
in little time he became a great doctor,
such that he set about going round the vineyard
that soon turns white if the vine-dresser is bad.
And of the see that once was kind
more to the righteous poor, not through itself,
but through him who sits there and falls away,
not to dispense either two or three for six,
not the fortune of the first vacant benefice,
not the tithes that belong to God's poor,
gathering; but against the erring world
leave to fight for the seed
from which twenty-four plants gird themselves round.
Then with learning and with will together,
with the apostolic office he set forth
like a torrent driven by a lofty spring;
and against the heretical thickets struck
his onrush most fiercely there
where the resistance was greatest.
From him there flowed then diverse streams
whereby the Catholic garden is watered,
so that its saplings stand more vigorous.
If such was one wheel of the chariot
in which Holy Church defended herself
and won in the field her civil strife,
well should it be plain enough to you,
the excellence of the other, of which Thomas
before my coming spoke so courteously.
But the track that made the highest part
of its circumference is forsaken,
so that there is mold where the wine-crust was.
His family, which went straight
with its feet in his footprints, has turned so far
that it casts the foot in front toward the one behind;
but soon it will know by the harvest
of the bad tillage, when the darnel
will lament that the bin is denied it.
I do say, whoever should search leaf by leaf
our volume will still find a page
where he would read: I am what I used to be.
But it will not be from Casale or from Acquasparta,
whence such men come to the written rule
that one shuns it and the other constricts it.
I was the life of Bonaventure
of Bagnoregio, who in high offices
always put the left-hand care last.
Illuminato and Augustine are here,
who were among the first barefoot poor
who in the cord made themselves friends of God.
Hugh of Saint Victor is here with them,
and Peter Comestor, and Peter of Spain,
who shines below in twelve little books;
Nathan the prophet and the metropolitan
Chrysostom, and Anselm, and that Donatus
who deigned to set his hand to the first art;
Rabanus is here, and at my side shines
the Calabrian abbot Joachim,
endowed with prophetic spirit.
To extol so great a paladin
I was moved by the ardent courtesy
of Friar Thomas, and his discerning speech;
and it moved this company with me.
