O scant nobility of our blood,
if you make people glory in you
down here, where our affection languishes,
it will never be a wonder to me;
for there, where desire does not turn awry,
I mean in heaven, I gloried in it.
Truly you are a mantle that soon grows short,
so that, unless more is added day by day,
time goes round about it with the shears.
With the Voi, which first was offered in Rome,
in which her people least persist,
my words began again.
And Beatrice, who was a little apart,
laughing, seemed like her who coughed
at the first fault written of Guinevere.
I began: You are my father;
you give me all boldness to speak,
you lift me so that I am more than I am.
Through so many streams is ever full of gladness
my mind, that it rejoices in itself
because it can bear it without breaking.
Tell me then, dear first fruit of mine,
who were your forebears, and what were the years
that were marked in your childhood.
Tell me of the sheepfold of Saint John,
how large it was then, and who were the families
within it worthy of the highest seats.
As there is quickened by the blowing of winds
a coal into flame, so I saw that
light shine bright at my endearments:
and as to my eyes it grew more beautiful,
so with a voice sweeter and gentler,
but not with this worldly speech,
it said to me: From that day when Ave was said
to the birth in which my mother, who is now holy,
was lightened of me, with whom she was heavy,
to the solar Lion five hundred and fifty
and thirty times came this fire
to rekindle itself beneath his paw.
My forebears and I were born in the place
where the last sixth is first encountered
by him who runs in your annual games.
Let it suffice to hear this of my ancestors;
who they were, and whence they came there,
it is more honorable to be silent than to speak.
All those who in those days were there
fit to bear arms, between Mars and the Baptist,
were a fifth of those who are alive.
But the citizenry, which is now mixed
with those of Campi, of Certaldo and of Feghine,
was seen to be pure down to the lowliest craftsman.
O how much better it would be to have as neighbors
those people of whom I speak, and at Galluzzo
and at Trespiano to have your boundaries,
than to have them within, to endure the stench
of the peasant of Aguglon, of him from Segna,
who already has a sharp eye for graft!
If the people that degenerates most in the world
had not been a stepmother to the Caesars,
but, like a mother to her son, kindly,
many a man has become a Florentine, and changes money and trades,
who would have turned back to Semifonti,
there where his grandfather went begging.
Montemurlo would still belong to the counts;
the Cerchi would be of the parish of Acrone,
and perhaps in Valdigrieve the Buondalmonti.
Always the mingling of peoples
was the beginning of the sickness of cities,
as of yours is the food that is added.
And a blind bull falls sooner
than a blind lamb, and many times there cuts
more and better one sword than the five.
If you look at Luni and Orbisagla,
how they have gone, and how there go
after them Chiusi and Sinigagla,
to hear how lineages die out
will not seem to you a thing strange or hard,
since cities have an end.
All your things have their death,
just as you do; but it lies hidden in a thing
that lasts long, and lives are short.
And as in the turning of the heavens the moon
covers and uncovers the shores without rest,
so does fortune with Florence:
wherefore it ought not to seem a wonder,
what I shall say of the other Florentines,
whose fame is hidden in time.
I saw the Ughi, and I saw the Catellini,
Filippi, Greci, Ormanni and Alberichi,
already in decline, illustrious citizens;
and I saw, as great as they were ancient,
with those of the Sannella, those of the Arca,
and the Soldanieri, Ardinghi and Bustichi.
Above the gate that at present is laden
with new treachery of such weight
that soon it will be the loss of the boat,
were the Ravignani, from whom is descended
Count Guido, and whoever of the name
of the lofty Bellincione has since partaken.
Those of the Pressa already knew how
one ought to govern, and Galigaio had
already gilded in his house the hilt and the pommel.
Great already was the column of vair,
the Sacchetti, Giuochi, Sifanti and Barucci,
the Galli, and those who blush for the bushel.
The stock from which sprang the Calfucci
was already great, and already had been drawn
to the round dances the Sitii and Arrigucci.
O in what state I saw those who are undone
by their pride! And the balls of gold
adorned Florence with flowers in all their great deeds.
So did the fathers of those
who, whenever your church is vacant,
grow fat sitting in consistory.
The overweening race that turns dragon
after him who flees, and before him who shows his teeth
or else his purse, grows mild as a lamb,
was already rising, but from humble folk,
so that Donato did not please the Uberti,
that afterward his father-in-law made him their kinsman.
Already Caponsacco had into the marketplace
come down from Fiesole, and already was
a good citizen, Guida and Infangato.
I shall say a thing incredible and true;
into the little circuit one entered through a gate
that was named after those of the Pera.
Each one who bears aught of the fair arms
of the great Baron, whose name and whose worth
the feast of Thomas renews,
from him received knighthood and privilege;
although with the people sides
today he who encircles those arms with the border.
Already there were Gualterotti and Importuni,
and the Borgo would still be quieter
if they had been without new neighbors.
The house from which sprang your weeping,
through the just indignation that has slain you
and has put an end to your glad life,
was honored, it and its kinsmen.
O Buondalmonte, how ill you fled
marriage with that house at others' urging!
Many would be glad who are sad,
if God had yielded you to the Ema
the first time you came to the city.
But it was fitting to that maimed stone
which guards the bridge that Florence should make
a sacrifice at the close of her peace.
With these families, and with others alongside them,
I saw Florence in such repose
that she had no cause to weep.
With these families I saw glorious
and so just her people, that the lily
was never set reversed upon the lance,
nor by division made vermilion.
