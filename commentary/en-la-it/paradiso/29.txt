When both the children of Latona,
covered by the Ram and by the Scales,
make of the horizon together a girdle,
as long as from the point that holds the zenith in balance
until the one and the other from that belt,
changing hemispheres, free themselves,
so long, with her face painted with a smile,
Beatrice kept silent, gazing
fixedly at the point that had vanquished me.
Then she began: I tell, I do not ask,
what you wish to hear, because I have seen it
where every where and every when comes to a point;
not to have within himself an increase of good,
which cannot be, but that his splendor
might, in shining forth, say I subsist,
in his eternity outside of time,
beyond all other comprehension, as it pleased him,
eternal Love opened into new loves.
Nor before did he lie as though torpid;
for neither before nor after preceded
the moving of God over these waters.
Form and matter, conjoined and pure,
came forth into being that had no flaw,
like three arrows from a three-stringed bow;
and as into glass, into amber or into crystal
a ray descends so that from its coming
to its being wholly there is no interval,
so the threefold effect from its Lord
shone forth into its being all at once,
without distinction in the beginning.
Together with them was order created and built
into the substances, and those were the summit
of the world in which pure act was produced.
Pure potentiality held the lowest part;
in the middle, potentiality and act were bound by
such a bond as is never unbound.
Jerome wrote for you of a long stretch
of centuries, of the angels created
before the rest of the world was made:
but this truth is written in many places
by the writers of the Holy Spirit;
and you will perceive it if you look closely;
and reason too sees it to some extent,
for it would not grant that the movers
should be without their perfection for so long.
Now you know when and where these loves
were created, and how; so that quenched
in your desire are three ardors already.
Nor would you reach, in counting, twenty
so soon as a part of the angels
disturbed that which underlies your elements.
The other part remained, and began this art,
which you discern, with such delight
that it never departs from circling.
The beginning of the fall was the accursed
pride of him whom you saw
constrained by all the weights of the world.
Those whom you see here were humble
in acknowledging themselves to be of the goodness
that had made them ready for such great understanding;
wherefore their powers of sight were exalted
with illuminating grace, and with their merit,
so that they have a steadfast and full will.
And I do not wish you to doubt, but to be certain,
that to receive grace is meritorious
according as the affections are open to it.
By now, concerning this consistory
you can contemplate much, if the words
of mine are gathered up, without further aid.
But because on earth throughout your schools
it is taught that the angelic nature
is such that it understands, remembers, and wills,
I shall speak further, that you may see pure
the truth that down there is confounded
by equivocation in such teaching.
These substances, since they took joy
in the face of God, have not turned their gaze
from it, from which nothing is hidden:
therefore their sight is not interrupted
by a new object, and therefore there is no need
to recollect by a separate concept.
Thus down there men dream without sleeping,
believing and not believing they speak the truth;
but in the one case there is more guilt and more shame.
You do not go down there by one path
in philosophizing; so far are you carried away by
the love of show and the thought of it,
and even this up here is borne
with less indignation than when set aside
is divine Scripture, or when it is twisted.
No thought is given there to how much blood it costs
to sow it in the world, and how pleasing is
he who humbly draws close to it.
To make a show each one strives, and makes
his own inventions; and those are run through
by the preachers, and the Gospel is passed over in silence.
One says that the moon turned back
at the Passion of Christ, and interposed itself,
so that the light of the sun reached out no more:
and others, that the light hid itself
of its own accord; therefore to the Spaniards and to the Indians,
as to the Jews, such an eclipse appeared.
Florence has not so many Lapos and Bindos
as there are such fables each year
cried from the pulpit here and there;
so that the little sheep, who do not know,
return from pasture fed on wind,
and not seeing their harm does not excuse them.
Christ did not say to his first assembly:
Go, and preach idle talk to the world;
but he gave them a true foundation:
and that alone resounded in their cheeks,
so that to fight, to kindle the faith,
of the Gospel they made shield and lances.
Now men go with jests and buffoonery
to preach; and provided there is a good laugh,
the hood swells, and nothing more is asked.
But such a bird nests in the tip of the hood
that if the common people saw it, they would see
the pardon in which they trust;
through which such folly has grown on earth
that, without proof from any witness,
people would run to every promise.
On this Saint Anthony fattens his pig,
and many others who are worse than pigs,
paying with money without a stamp.
But because we have digressed far, turn
your eyes now toward the straight road,
so that the way may be shortened with the time.
This nature mounts so far
in number that never was there speech,
nor mortal conception, that goes so far.
And if you look at what is revealed
through Daniel, you will see that in his thousands
a definite number is concealed.
The primal light, which irradiates it all,
in as many ways is received in it
as there are splendors to which it joins itself.
Hence, because upon the act that conceives
follows the impulse to love the sweetness,
diversely in it that love burns and is warm.
See now the height and the breadth
of the eternal power, since so many
mirrors it has made for itself, in which it is broken,
remaining one, just as before.
