Looking upon his Son with the Love
that both the one and the other eternally breathe forth,
the primal and ineffable Power
made all that turns through mind and eye
with such order that none can be
without a taste of him who gazes on this.
Raise then, reader, to the lofty wheels
with me your sight, straight toward that part
where the one motion and the other strike each other;
and there begin to delight in the art
of that master who within himself loves it
so much that his eye never leaves it.
See how from there branches off
the oblique circle that bears the planets,
to satisfy the world that calls for them;
and if their path were not bent,
much of their power would be in vain,
and almost every potency down here dead.
And if farther from the straight or less far
were the departure, much would be lacking
both above and below in the order of the world.
Now stay, reader, upon your bench,
thinking back on what is tasted beforehand,
if you wish to be glad well before you are tired.
I have set it before you; now feed yourself:
for to itself turns all my care
that matter of which I have been made scribe.
The greatest minister of nature,
who with the power of heaven imprints the world,
and with his light measures time for us,
with that part which is recalled above
conjoined, was turning through the spirals
in which he presents himself earlier each time:
and I was with him; but of the ascent
I was not aware, save as one is aware,
before the first thought, of its coming.
O Beatrice, she who passes
from good to better so suddenly
that her act does not extend through time,
how radiant she must have been in herself!
For what was within the Sun, where I entered,
apparent not through color but through light,
though I call upon talent and art and practice,
I could not tell it so that it might ever be imagined;
but it can be believed, and let one long to see it.
And if our imaginations are too low
for such a height, it is no wonder,
for there was never an eye that went beyond the sun.
Such was there the fourth family
of the high Father, who always satisfies it,
showing how he breathes forth and how he begets.
And Beatrice began: Give thanks,
give thanks to the sun of the angels, who to this
perceptible sun has raised you by his grace.
No mortal heart was ever so disposed
to devotion, and to surrender itself to God
with all its pleasure so ready,
as at those words I became;
and so wholly did my love set itself on him
that Beatrice was eclipsed in oblivion.
It did not displease her; but she so smiled at it
that the splendor of her smiling eyes
divided my undivided mind among many things.
I saw many lights, living and surpassing,
make of us a center and of themselves a crown,
sweeter in voice than bright to the sight.
Thus do we see the daughter of Latona encircled
at times, when the air is laden
so that it holds the thread that makes the girdle.
In the court of heaven, whence I return,
are found many jewels so precious and beautiful
that they cannot be taken out of the kingdom;
and the song of those lights was one of them:
whoever does not grow wings so as to fly up there,
let him expect tidings thence from the mute.
Then, when, thus singing, those burning suns
had wheeled around us three times,
like stars close to the fixed poles,
they seemed to me ladies not released from the dance,
but stopping silent, listening
until they have caught the new notes;
and within one I heard a voice begin: Since
the ray of grace, by which is kindled
true love, and which then grows through loving,
multiplied in you, shines so brightly
that it leads you up that stair
where none descends without ascending again,
whoever should deny you the wine of his flask
for your thirst would not be at liberty,
just as water is not that does not run down to the sea.
You wish to know with what plants comes into flower
this garland that gazes lovingly round about upon
the beautiful lady who gives you strength for heaven.
I was one of the lambs of the holy flock
that Dominic leads along a path
where one fattens well if one does not stray.
This one, who is nearest me on the right,
was brother and master to me, and he was Albert
of Cologne, and I Thomas Aquinas.
If of all the others you wish to be certain,
follow behind my speech with your gaze
turning along the blessed wreath.
That other flaming light issues from the smile
of Gratian, who both the one court and the other
so aided that it pleased heaven.
The other who next adorns our choir
was that Peter who, with the poor woman,
offered his treasure to holy Church.
The fifth light, which among us is most beautiful,
breathes forth such love that all the world
down there hungers to know news of it.
Into its lofty mind so profound
a wisdom was put that, if the truth is true,
no second arose to see so much.
Next you see the light of that candle
which, below in the flesh, saw more deeply into
the angelic nature and ministry.
Within the other little light smiles
that advocate of Christian times
whose Latin Augustine drew upon.
Now if you draw the eye of your mind
from light to light after my praises,
already you remain athirst for the eighth.
In seeing every good, therein rejoices
the holy soul that makes the deceitful world
manifest to whoever listens well to it.
And the body from which it was driven lies
below in Cieldauro, and it from martyrdom
and from exile came to this peace.
See farther on the burning breath flame
of Isidore, of Bede and of Richard,
who in contemplation was more than man.
This one from whom your gaze returns to me
is the light of a spirit to whom, in thoughts
full of gravity, death seemed slow in coming.
It is the eternal light of Siger,
who, lecturing in the Street of Straw,
reasoned out truths that stirred envy.
Then like a clock that calls us
at the hour when the bride of God rises
to sing her bridegroom a morning song that he may love her,
of which one part pulls the other and urges it on,
sounding ting ting with so sweet a note
that the well-disposed spirit swells with love,
so I saw the glorious wheel
move and answer voices with voices in harmony
and in a sweetness that cannot be known
save there where joy becomes forever.
