Already was rejoicing alone in his word
that blessed mirror, and I was tasting
my own, tempering with the sweet the bitter;
and that lady who was leading me to God
said: Change your thought; think that I am
close to Him who lightens every wrong.
I turned to the loving sound
of my comfort; and what I then saw
of love in the holy eyes, here I leave untold;
not because I merely mistrust my speech,
but because of the mind, which cannot return
so far above itself unless another guides it.
This much I can recount of that moment,
that, gazing at her, my affection
was free of every other desire,
so long as the eternal beauty, which directly
shone into Beatrice, from her fair face
contented me with its reflected aspect.
Overcoming me with the light of a smile,
she said to me: Turn and listen,
for not in my eyes alone is paradise.
As one sees here at times
affection in the look, if it is so great
that by it the soul is wholly taken up,
so in the flaming of the holy radiance
to which I turned, I recognized the desire
in him to speak to me a little more.
He began: On this fifth tier
of the tree that lives from its crown,
and bears fruit always, and never loses a leaf,
are blessed spirits who down below, before
that they came to heaven, were of great renown,
so that every Muse would be rich in them.
Therefore look at the arms of the cross:
the one I shall name will there make the movement
that its swift fire makes in a cloud.
I saw across the cross a light drawn
by the naming of Joshua, as soon as it was done;
the word was not known to me before the act.
And at the name of the lofty Maccabee
I saw another move, spinning,
and gladness was the whip of the top.
So at Charlemagne and at Roland
two of them my intent gaze followed,
as an eye follows its falcon in flight.
Then William drew, and Rainouard,
and Duke Godfrey, my gaze
across that cross, and Robert Guiscard.
Then, moving and mingling among the other lights,
the soul that had spoken to me showed me
what an artist he was among the singers of heaven.
I turned to my right side
to see in Beatrice what I was to do,
either by words or by gesture indicated,
and I saw her eyes so pure,
so joyous, that her appearance
surpassed the others and the latest I had known.
And as, through feeling greater delight,
a man, doing good, from day to day
perceives that his virtue increases,
so I perceived that my circling
together with the heaven had increased its arc,
seeing that miracle so adorned.
As is the change in a brief span
of time in a fair-skinned lady, when the face
of hers sheds its burden of shame,
such it was in my eyes, when I had turned,
through the whiteness of the temperate star,
the sixth, which had received me within itself.
I saw in that little torch of Jove
the sparkling of the love that was there
trace our speech before my eyes.
And as birds risen from a riverbank,
as if rejoicing together over their feeding grounds,
form of themselves now a round flock, now another shape,
so within the lights holy creatures,
flying about, sang and formed themselves
now into D, now I, now L, in their shapes.
First, singing, they moved to their own tune;
then, becoming one of these signs,
they stopped a little and fell silent.
O goddess of Pegasus, who minds
make glorious, and make them long-lived,
and they with you the cities and the kingdoms,
illumine me with yourself, so that I may bring out
their shapes as I conceived them;
let your power appear in these brief verses.
They showed themselves, then, in five times seven
vowels and consonants; whence I noted
the parts just as they seemed to me to be spoken.
Love justice, the first
were verb and noun of all that was depicted;
you who judge the earth, were the last.
Then in the M of the fifth word
they remained arrayed, so that Jove
seemed of silver there picked out in gold.
And I saw the other lights descend to where
the summit of the M was, and there come to rest,
singing, I believe, the good that moves them toward itself.
Then, as at the striking of burning logs
innumerable sparks rise,
from which fools are wont to draw omens,
there seemed to rise again from there more than a thousand
lights, and to climb, some much and some little,
just as the Sun that kindles them allotted them:
and, each having come to rest in its place,
the head and the neck of an eagle I saw
depicted by that patterned fire.
He who paints there has none to guide him,
but himself guides, and from him is recalled
that power which gives form throughout the nests.
The other blessed throng, which content
seemed at first to form a lily on the M,
with little movement followed the imprint.
O sweet star, what gems and how many
showed me that our justice
is an effect of the heaven that you adorn as a gem!
Wherefore I pray the Mind in which begins
your motion and your power, that it may look
whence comes the smoke that taints your ray;
so that once again it may now be angered
at the buying and the selling within the temple
that was walled with blood and with martyrs.
O host of heaven, whom I contemplate,
pray for those who are on earth
all led astray after the bad example.
Once men used to make war with swords;
but now it is made by taking away, now here, now there,
the bread that the loving Father withholds from no one.
But you who write only to erase,
remember that Peter and Paul, who died
for the vineyard you lay waste, are still alive.
Well may you say: I have fixed my desire
so on him who willed to live alone,
and who for dancing was led to martyrdom,
that I know neither the Fisherman nor Paul.
