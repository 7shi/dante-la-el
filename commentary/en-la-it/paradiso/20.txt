When he who illumines all the world
descends from our hemisphere,
so that the day on every side is spent,
the sky, which first is kindled by him alone,
suddenly becomes visible again
through many lights, in which one shines.
And this act of the sky came to my mind,
when the ensign of the world and of its leaders
in its blessed beak fell silent;
for all those living lights,
far more luminous, began songs
that slip and fall from my memory.
O sweet love, who cloak yourself in smiles,
how ardent you appeared in those sparks,
which had breath only of holy thoughts!
After the clear and lustrous gems,
with which I saw the sixth light bejeweled,
put their angelic peals to silence,
I seemed to hear the murmuring of a river
that descends clear down from stone to stone,
showing the abundance of its mountain height.
And as sound at the neck of the cithara
takes its form, and as at the opening
of the pipe the wind that enters does,
so, delay of waiting removed,
that murmuring of the Eagle rose
up through its neck, as if it were hollow.
It became a voice there, and thence issued
through its beak in the form of words
such as the heart in which I wrote them awaited.
The part in me that sees and endures the sun
in mortal eagles, it began to tell me,
must now be looked at steadily;
because of the fires from which I form my figure,
those from which the eye in my head sparkles
they are the highest of all their ranks.
He who shines in the middle as the pupil
was the singer of the Holy Spirit,
who moved the ark from town to town:
now he knows the merit of his song,
insofar as it was the effect of his own counsel,
by the recompense, which is as great.
Of the five who make a circle along the brow,
he who comes nearest my beak
consoled the poor woman for her son:
now he knows how dearly it costs
not to follow Christ, by the experience
of this sweet life and of its opposite.
And he who follows on the circumference
of which I speak, along the upper arc,
delayed death by true repentance:
now he knows that the eternal judgment
does not change when a worthy prayer
makes a tomorrow down there of today.
The next who follows, with the laws, with me,
with a good intention that bore bad fruit,
to yield to the Shepherd made himself Greek:
now he knows how the evil derived
from his good doing does not harm him,
although the world is thereby destroyed.
And he whom you see on the descending arc
was William, whom that land mourns
which bewails Charles and the living Frederick:
now he knows how enamored is
heaven of the just king, and by the appearance
of his splendor he makes it seen as well.
Who would believe down in the erring world
that Ripheus the Trojan in this circle
was the fifth of the holy lights?
Now he knows much of what the world
cannot see of divine grace,
though his sight does not discern the bottom.
Like a little lark that ranges in the air,
first singing, and then falls silent, content
with the utmost gladness that sates it;
such seemed to me the image of the imprint
of the eternal pleasure, at whose desire
each thing becomes what it is.
And although I was to my doubt
there as glass to the color that clothes it,
it did not suffer me to wait in silence for a time;
but from the mouth, What things are these?
it thrust out of me with the force of its weight;
whereupon I saw great rejoicing in flashes of light.
Then afterward, with its eye more brightly kindled,
the blessed ensign answered me,
so as not to keep me suspended in wonder:
I see that you believe these things
because I say them, but you do not see how;
so that, if not believed, they are hidden.
You do as one who a thing by name
apprehends well; but its quiddity
he cannot see unless another discloses it.
The kingdom of heaven suffers violence
from ardent love and from living hope,
which conquers the divine will;
not as one man overcomes another,
but conquers it because it wills to be conquered,
and conquered, it conquers with its benevolence.
The first life of the brow and the fifth
make you marvel, because with them you see
the region of the angels painted.
From their bodies they did not depart, as you believe,
as pagans, but as Christians, in firm faith,
one in the feet that were to suffer, one in the feet that had suffered.
For one from Hell, where one does not return
ever to willing good, returned to its bones:
and this was the reward of living hope;
of living hope, which put its power
into the prayers made to God to raise it up,
so that its will might be moved.
The glorious soul of which we speak,
having returned to the flesh, in which it was but briefly,
believed in him who could help it;
and believing, it kindled into so great a fire
of true love that at its second death
it was worthy to come to this place.
The other, through grace that from so deep
a fountain trickles that never did a creature
press its eye as far as the first wave,
set all his love down there on righteousness;
wherefore from grace to grace God opened his
eye to our redemption yet to come:
whereupon he believed in it, and did not endure
from then on the stench of paganism any longer,
and for it he rebuked the perverse peoples.
Those three ladies served him for baptism
whom you saw at the right wheel,
before baptism by more than a thousand years.
O predestination, how remote
is your root from those gazes
that do not see the First Cause entire!
And you, O mortals, hold yourselves back
from judging; for we, who see God,
do not yet know all the elect;
and sweet to us is such a lack,
because our good in this good is perfected,
that what God wills we too will.
Thus by that divine image,
to make my short sight clear,
sweet medicine was given me.
And as for a good singer a good cithara player
makes the quiver of the string follow,
whereby the song gains more delight;
so, while it spoke, I do remember
that I saw the two blessed lights,
just as the blinking of eyes is in accord,
move their little flames with the words.
