After your Charles, fair Clemence,
had enlightened me, he told me of the deceits
that his seed was to suffer;
but he said: Be silent, and let the years move on.
So that I can say nothing except that weeping
justly shall follow upon your wrongs.
And already the gaze of that holy light
had turned back to the good that fills it,
as to that good which suffices for all things.
Alas, deceived souls and impious creatures,
who from so great a good turn your hearts,
directing your temples toward vanity!
And behold, another of those splendors
drew toward me, and its wish to please me
it signified by growing brighter outwardly.
The eyes of Beatrice, which were fixed
upon me as before, of her dear assent
to my desire made me certain.
Ah, grant satisfaction to my wish at once,
blessed spirit, I said, and give me proof
that what I think can be reflected in you.
Then the light that was still new to me,
from its depths, whence it sang before,
continued, as one who takes delight in doing good:
In that part of the wicked land
of Italy that lies between Rialto
and the sources of the Brenta and the Piave,
rises a hill, and it does not rise very high,
whence once descended a little torch
that made a great assault upon the region.
From one root were born both I and it;
Cunizza I was called, and here I shine,
because the light of the star overcame me.
But gladly I forgive myself
the cause of my lot, and it does not grieve me,
which would perhaps seem hard to your common folk.
Of this bright and precious jewel
of our heaven, which is nearest me,
great fame has remained, and, before it dies,
this hundredth year shall yet grow fivefold.
See whether a man ought to make himself excellent,
so that his first life may leave another life behind!
And of this the present throng takes no thought,
which the Tagliamento and the Adige enclose,
nor, though beaten, does it yet repent.
But soon it shall come to pass that Padua at the marsh
shall change the water that bathes Vicenza,
because its people are stubborn toward duty.
And there where Sile and Cagnan join,
one lords it and goes with head held high,
though already the net is being made to catch him.
Feltre too shall weep for the treachery
of its impious shepherd, which shall be foul,
so that for the like no one ever entered Malta.
Very broad would be the vat
that could hold the Ferrarese blood,
and weary whoever weighed it ounce by ounce,
which this courteous priest shall give away,
to show himself a partisan; and such gifts
shall accord with the ways of the land.
Above are mirrors, which you call Thrones,
from which God in judgment shines upon us,
so that these words seem good to us.
Here she fell silent, and gave me the impression
that she had turned to something else, by the round
into which she entered as she had before.
The other joy, already known to me,
became a resplendent thing in my sight,
like a fine balas ruby on which the sun strikes.
Through joy radiance is gained up there,
as a smile is here; but down below there darkens
the shade without, as the mind is sad.
God sees all, and your sight enters into him,
I said, blessed spirit, so that no
desire can steal itself away from you.
Then your voice, which delights heaven
always with the song of those devout fires
that made of six wings their cowl,
why does it not satisfy my desires?
Surely I would not await your question,
if I entered into you as you enter into me.
The greatest valley in which water spreads,
his words then began,
apart from that sea which encircles the earth,
between discordant shores, against the sun's course,
extends so far that it makes a meridian
where at first it is wont to make a horizon.
On the shore of that valley I dwelt,
between the Ebro and the Macra, which in its short course
divides the Genoese from the Tuscan.
At almost one sunset and one sunrise
lie Bougie and the city whence I came,
which once made its harbor warm with its blood.
Folco those people called me to whom
my name was known; and this heaven
takes my imprint, as I took its;
for the daughter of Belus burned no more,
wronging both Sychaeus and Creusa,
than I, so long as it befitted the color of my hair;
nor did that Rhodopean woman who deceived
by Demophoon was, nor yet Alcides
when he held Iole enclosed in his heart.
Yet here one does not repent, but smiles,
not at the fault, which does not return to mind,
but at the power that ordained and provided.
Here one gazes upon the art that adorns
so great an effect, and one discerns the good
through which to the world above the one below turns.
But so that all your desires fulfilled
you may bear away, which were born in this sphere,
I must proceed still further.
You wish to know who is in this light,
which here beside me so sparkles
like a ray of sunlight in pure water.
Now know that there within rests peacefully
Rahab, and to our order she is joined,
and with her its highest rank is sealed.
By this heaven, in which the shadow comes to a point
that your world casts, before any other soul
of Christ's triumph she was received.
Fitting indeed it was to leave her as a palm
in some heaven, of the high victory
that was won with the one and the other palm;
for she furthered the first glory
of Joshua in the Holy Land,
which little stirs the pope's remembrance.
Your city, which is the plant of him
who first turned his back upon his Maker,
and whose is envy in its entirety,
produces and scatters the accursed flower
that has led astray the sheep and the lambs,
because it has made a wolf of the shepherd.
For this the Gospel and the great Doctors
are forsaken, and only to the Decretals
is such study given that it shows at their edges.
To this the pope and cardinals attend:
their thoughts do not go to Nazareth,
there where Gabriel spread his wings.
But the Vatican and the other chosen parts
of Rome, which have been a burial ground
for the soldiery that followed Peter,
shall soon be free of the adultery.
