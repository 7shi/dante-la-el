In the form, then, of a white rose
the holy host showed itself to me,
which Christ in his blood made his bride.
But the other, which in flight sees and sings
the grace of him who makes it love him,
and the goodness that makes it so great,
just as a swarm of bees that enters the flowers
at one time, and at another returns
there where its labor takes on savor,
descended into the great flower that adorns itself
with so many petals, and thence rose again
there where its love always dwells.
Their faces they all had of living flame,
and their wings of gold, and the rest all white,
so that no snow attains such a degree.
When they descended into the flower, from white to white
they imparted of the peace and of the ardor
that they acquire there, fanning their flanks.
Nor did the interposing, between the height above and the flower,
of so great a flying multitude
impede the sight or the splendor;
for the divine light is penetrating
throughout the universe, according as it is worthy,
so that nothing can stand in its way.
This secure and joyous kingdom,
thronged with people ancient and new,
had sight and love all on one mark.
O threefold light, that in a single star
sparkling in their sight so satisfies them,
look down here upon our tempest.
If the barbarians, coming from such a region
as each day is covered by Helice,
revolving with her son, of whom she is fond,
seeing Rome and her lofty works,
were stupefied, when the Lateran
rose above all mortal things;
I, Dante, who to the divine from the human,
to eternity from time had come,
and from Florence among a people just and sound,
with what amazement must I have been filled!
Indeed, between it and the joy, it was
my pleasure not to hear and to remain mute.
And like a pilgrim who takes delight
in the temple of his vow, looking about,
and hopes already to tell again how it stands;
so, through the living light ranging,
I led my eyes along the tiers,
now up, now down, and now circling.
I saw there faces persuasive of charity,
adorned with another's light and with their own smile,
and gestures graced with every dignity.
The general form of Paradise
my gaze had by now taken in entire,
not yet resting fixed on any part;
and I turned with desire rekindled
to ask my lady about things
concerning which my mind was in suspense.
One I had in mind, and another answered me:
I thought to see Beatrice, and I saw an elder
clothed like the people in glory.
His eyes and his cheeks were suffused
with kindly gladness, his bearing tender,
such as befits a loving father.
And, Where is she? I said at once.
Whereupon he: To bring your desire to fulfillment
Beatrice moved me from my place;
and if you look up to the third ring
from the highest tier, you will see her again
on the throne that her merits allotted her.
Without answering I raised my eyes,
and saw her, who made herself a crown,
reflecting from herself the eternal rays.
From that region where it thunders highest
no mortal eye is so far distant,
even one that lets itself sink deepest in the sea,
as my sight was from Beatrice;
but it mattered nothing to me, for her image
did not descend to me mingled with any medium.
O lady, in whom my hope lives strong,
and who endured for my salvation
to leave in Hell your footprints;
for all the many things that I have seen,
as coming from your power and from your goodness
I acknowledge the grace and the strength.
You have drawn me from bondage to freedom,
by all those ways, by all the means
that you had in your power to do this.
Preserve in me your magnificence
so that my soul, which you have made whole,
you may unbind from the body pleasing to you.
Thus I prayed; and she, as far away
as she seemed, smiled and looked at me;
then she turned back to the eternal fountain.
And the holy elder: That you may complete
perfectly, he said, your journey,
to which prayer and holy love have sent me,
fly with your eyes through this garden;
for seeing it will fit your gaze
the better to mount along the divine ray.
The Queen of Heaven, for whom I burn
all with love, will grant us every grace,
because I am her faithful Bernard.
As is one who perhaps from Croatia
comes to see our Veronica,
who for its ancient fame cannot have his fill of it,
but says in thought, while it is shown:
My Lord Jesus Christ, true God,
was your countenance then fashioned so?
Such was I, gazing on the living
charity of him who in this world,
in contemplation, tasted of that peace.
Son of grace, this joyous state,
he began, will not be known to you
keeping your eyes only down here at the bottom;
but look upon the circles as far as the most remote,
until you see seated the Queen
to whom this kingdom is subject and devoted.
I raised my eyes; and as in the morning
the eastern part of the horizon
surpasses that where the sun declines,
so, as if going from valley to mountain
with my eyes, I saw a part at the farthest edge
surpass in light all the rest of the expanse.
And as there, where the chariot pole is awaited
that Phaethon guided ill, the sky flames up more,
and on this side and that the light grows less;
so that peaceful oriflamme
grew brighter in the middle, and on every side
in equal measure slackened its flame.
And in that middle, with feathers spread,
I saw more than a thousand angels rejoicing,
each distinct both in splendor and in art.
I saw, at their sports there and at their songs,
a beauty smile, that was gladness
in the eyes of all the other saints.
And if in speech I had as much wealth
as in imagining, I would not dare
attempt the least part of her gladness.
Bernard, when he saw my eyes
on the warmth of his ardor fixed and intent,
turned his own with such affection to her
that mine grew more intent on gazing.
