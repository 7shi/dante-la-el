As he came to Clymene, to make sure
of what he had heard against himself,
he who still makes fathers sparing toward their sons;
such was I, and such was I perceived to be
by Beatrice and by the holy lamp
that earlier for my sake had changed its place.
Wherefore my Lady: Send forth the flame
of your desire, she said to me, so that it may come forth
clearly marked with the eternal stamp;
not that our knowledge may grow
through your speaking, but that you may accustom yourself
to tell your thirst, so that one may pour for you.
O dear stone of mine, who rise so high,
that, just as earthly minds see
that two obtuse angles cannot fit in a triangle,
so you see contingent things,
before they exist in themselves, gazing upon the point
to which all times are present,
while I was joined to Virgil
up along the mountain that heals souls,
and descending into the world of the dead,
there were spoken to me of my future life
grave words; although I feel myself
quite four-square against the blows of fortune.
Wherefore my desire would be content
to learn what fortune draws near to me;
for an arrow foreseen comes more slowly.
Thus I spoke to that very light
that earlier had spoken to me, and as was the will
of Beatrice, my desire was confessed.
Not in riddles, in which the foolish people
once ensnared themselves before there was slain
the Lamb of God who takes away sins,
but with clear words and with precise
speech, that fatherly love replied,
enclosed and yet apparent in its bright smile:
Contingency, which beyond the volume
of your material world does not extend,
is all depicted in the eternal sight.
Yet it takes no necessity from this,
any more than from the gaze in which it is mirrored does
a ship that descends a torrent.
From there, just as there comes to the ear
sweet harmony from an organ, there comes to me
into view the time that is being prepared for you.
As Hippolytus departed from Athens
because of his pitiless and treacherous stepmother,
so from Florence you must depart.
This is willed, and this is already being sought,
and soon it will be accomplished for him who plans it,
there where Christ is traded in everything.
The blame will follow the injured party
in public report, as it usually does; but vengeance
will bear witness to the truth that dispenses it.
You will leave everything loved
most dearly; and this is that shaft
which the bow of exile shoots first.
You will learn how salt it tastes,
the bread of others, and what a hard path it is
to ascend and climb the stairs of others.
And what will most weigh down your shoulders
will be the wicked and foolish company
with which you will fall into this valley;
for, all ungrateful, all mad and impious,
it will turn against you; but shortly afterward
it, not you, will have its temple red from it.
Of its brutishness its own conduct
will furnish proof, so that it will be to your credit
to have made a party of yourself alone.
Your first refuge and your first lodging
will be the courtesy of the great Lombard,
who upon the ladder bears the holy bird;
who will have such kindly regard for you
that of giving and asking, between you two,
that will come first which among others comes later.
With him you will see him who was stamped,
at birth, so strongly by this star
that notable will be his deeds.
People have not yet become aware of it,
because of his tender age; for only nine years
have these stars wheeled around him.
But before the Gascon deceives lofty Henry,
sparks of his virtue will appear
in his caring neither for silver nor for hardships.
Known his acts of magnificence
will yet be so, that his enemies
will not be able to keep their tongues silent about them.
Look to him and to his benefits;
through him many people will be transformed,
rich and beggars changing condition;
and you will carry it written in your mind
concerning him, and will not tell it. And he said things
incredible to those who will be present.
Then he added: Son, these are the glosses
on what was said to you; behold the snares
that within a few revolutions lie hidden.
Yet I do not wish you to envy your neighbors,
since your life reaches into the future
far beyond the punishment of their treacheries.
After, by falling silent, it showed itself to have finished,
the holy soul, laying the weft
in that web whose warp I had set before it,
I began, like one who longs,
being in doubt, to have counsel from a person
who sees and wills rightly, and loves:
Well do I see, my father, how it spurs
toward me, time, to deal me a blow
such as falls heavier on him who gives way more.
Therefore it is well that I arm myself with foresight,
so that, if the place most dear is taken from me,
I may not lose the others through my verses.
Down through the world bitter without end,
and over the mountain from whose fair summit
the eyes of my lady raised me,
and then through heaven, from light to light,
I have learned that which, if I tell it again,
to many will be a taste of piercing sharpness;
and if to the truth I am an intimate friend,
I fear to lose life among those
who will call this time ancient.
The light in which my treasure smiled,
whom I found there, first grew radiant
like a mirror of gold in a ray of sun;
then he replied: A conscience darkened
either by its own or by another's shame
will indeed find your word harsh.
But nonetheless, all falsehood set aside,
make all your vision manifest,
and just let them scratch where the scab is:
for if your voice should be troublesome
at the first taste, vital nourishment
it will afterward leave, when it has been digested.
This cry of yours will be like the wind
that strikes the highest towers hardest;
and that will be no small proof of honor.
Therefore there are shown to you in these spheres,
upon the mountain and in the dolorous valley,
only the souls that are known to fame;
for the mind of him who hears neither rests
nor fixes its faith on an example that has
its root unknown and hidden,
nor on any other argument that is not apparent.
