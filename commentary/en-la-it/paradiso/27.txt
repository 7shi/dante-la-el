To the Father, to the Son, to the Holy Spirit
all Paradise began Glory,
so that the sweet song inebriated me.
What I heard seemed to me a smile
of the universe; wherefore my drunkenness
entered me through hearing and through sight.
O joy! O ineffable gladness!
O life entire of love and peace!
O wealth secure without desire!
Before my eyes the four torches
stood kindled, and the one that came first
began to grow more vivid;
and such in its appearance it became
as Jupiter would become, if he and Mars
were birds and exchanged feathers.
Providence, which here apportions us
turn and office, in the blessed choir
had imposed silence on every side,
when I heard: If I change color,
do not marvel; for, as I speak,
you will see all these change color.
He who usurps on earth my place,
my place, my place, which is vacant
in the presence of the Son of God,
has made of my burial ground a sewer
of blood and stench, in which the perverse one,
who fell from up here, takes his ease down there.
With that color with which the sun opposite
paints a cloud at evening and at morning,
I then saw the whole heaven sprinkled;
and as an honorable woman, who remains
sure of herself, yet at another's fault,
merely hearing of it, grows timid,
so Beatrice changed her appearance;
and such an eclipse I believe there was in heaven
when the supreme Power suffered below.
Then his words proceeded
in a voice so changed from itself
that his appearance changed no more than that;
the bride of Christ was not reared
on my blood, on that of Linus and of Cletus,
to be used for the gaining of gold;
but for the gaining of this glad life
Pius, with Sixtus, Callistus and Urban,
shed their blood after much weeping.
It was not our intention that on the right hand
of our successors a part should sit,
a part on the other, of the Christian people;
nor that the keys that were granted me
should become an emblem on a banner
that fought against the baptized;
nor that I should be the image on a seal
for privileges sold and lying,
at which I often redden and flash sparks.
In shepherds' garb ravenous wolves
are seen from up here throughout the pastures:
O vengeance of God, why do you still lie!
Of our blood men of Cahors and Gascons
are preparing to drink: O good beginning,
to what vile end must you fall!
High Providence, which with Scipio
defended for Rome the glory of the world,
will soon bring aid, as I conceive.
And you, son, who by reason of your mortal weight
will return below again, open your mouth,
and do not hide what I do not hide.
Just as with frozen vapors it snows
downward in our air, when the horn
of the Goat of heaven touches the Sun;
upward I thus saw the ether adorned
become, and snow with triumphant vapors
that had made their stay there with us.
My sight followed their forms,
and followed until the intervening space, by its vastness,
deprived it of the power to reach farther.
Whereupon the lady, who saw me freed
from attending upward, said to me: Lower
your gaze, and see how far you have turned.
From the hour when I had looked before,
I saw myself moved through the whole arc
which the first clime makes from its middle to its end;
so that I saw beyond Gades the passage,
mad, of Ulysses, and on this side nearly to the shore
on which Europa became a sweet burden.
And more would have been revealed to me of the site
of this little plot; but the sun went ahead,
beneath my feet, a sign and more apart.
My enamored mind, which courts
my lady always, to turn back
to her my eyes burned more than ever:
and if nature or art made pastures
to catch the eyes so as to possess the mind,
in human flesh or in its pictures,
all gathered together would seem nothing
beside the divine beauty that shone on me
when I turned to her smiling face.
And the power that her gaze bestowed on me
tore me from the fair nest of Leda,
and into the swiftest heaven impelled me.
Its most living and exalted parts
are so uniform that I cannot say
which Beatrice chose for me as my place.
But she, who saw my desire,
began, smiling, so glad
that God seemed to rejoice in her face:
The nature of motion, which keeps still
the center and moves all else around it,
begins from here as from its boundary.
And this heaven has no other where
than the divine mind, in which is kindled
the love that turns it and the power it rains down.
Light and love enclose it in a circle,
as this one does the others; and that encircling
he alone who girds it understands.
Its motion is not measured by another;
but the others are measured by this one,
as ten by its half and its fifth.
And how time holds in such a pot
its roots, and in the others its leaves,
can now be manifest to you.
O cupidity, which sinks mortals
so far beneath you that none has the power
to draw his eyes back out of your waves!
Well does the will blossom in men;
but the continual rain turns
into blighted fruit the true plums.
Faith and innocence are found
only in little children; and then each
flees before their cheeks are covered.
One, still babbling, fasts,
who later devours, with tongue loosed,
any food whatever in any moon whatever;
and one, babbling, loves and listens to
his mother, who, with speech complete,
desires thereafter to see her buried.
Thus does white skin become black
at the first sight of the fair daughter
of him who brings morning and leaves evening.
You, that this may not make you marvel,
think that on earth there is no one who governs;
whence the human family goes astray.
But before January passes wholly out of winter,
through the hundredth part that is neglected down there,
these spheres on high will roar so
that the storm so long awaited
will turn the sterns to where the prows are,
so that the fleet will run straight;
or true fruit will come after the flower.
