Who could ever, even with words unfettered,
tell of the blood and of the wounds in full
that I now saw, though he told it many times?
Every tongue would surely fall short
by reason of our speech and of the mind,
which have too little room to comprehend so much.
If there were gathered again all the people
who lie upon the fateful land
of Apulia and suffered the loss of their blood
through the Trojans, or through the long war
that made so high a pile of rings as spoils,
as Livy writes, who does not err;
with those who felt the pain of blows
for resisting Robert Guiscard;
and those others whose bones are still gathered
at Ceprano, there where false was
every Apulian, and there by Tagliacozzo
where without arms the old Alardo won;
and one a pierced limb of his, another a severed one
were to show, it would be nothing to match
the foul manner of the ninth ditch.
Never does a cask, through losing a middle or side piece of its head,
gape so wide as one I saw,
split from the chin down to where one farts:
between his legs hung his entrails;
his pluck was visible, and the wretched sack
that makes excrement of what one gulps down.
While I fix myself wholly on looking at him,
he looked at me, and with his hands opened his breast,
saying: Now see how I tear myself apart:
see how Muhammad is split open.
Ahead of me goes weeping Ali,
cleft in the face from chin to forelock:
and all the others whom you see here
sowers of scandal and of schism
were in life; therefore they are cleft thus.
A devil is here behind who cleaves us
so cruelly, to the edge of the sword
putting again each one of this crew,
when we have gone round the sorrowful road;
because the wounds have closed again
before one goes before him again.
But who are you who muse upon the crag,
perhaps to delay going to the punishment
that is adjudged upon your own accusations?
Neither has death ever overtaken him, nor does guilt lead him,
replied my master, to torment him;
but to give him full experience,
I, who am dead, must lead him
through Hell down here from circle to circle:
and this is as true as that I speak to you.
More than a hundred there were who, when they heard him,
stopped in the ditch to look at me,
in wonder forgetting their torment.
Now tell Fra Dolcino, then, to arm himself,
you who perhaps will see the sun shortly,
if he does not wish so soon to follow me,
so well with provisions that a siege of snow
may not bring victory to the men of Novara,
which otherwise would not be easy to win.
After lifting one foot to go his way,
Muhammad spoke this word to me,
then to depart he set it down on the ground.
Another, who had his throat pierced
and his nose cut off right up to his brows,
and had but a single ear,
having stopped to look in wonder
with the others, before the others opened his windpipe,
which on the outside was vermilion all around;
and said: You whom guilt does not condemn,
and whom I saw up in the Latin land,
if too great a likeness does not deceive me,
remember Pier da Medicina,
if ever you return to see the sweet plain
that slopes from Vercelli to Marcabò.
And make it known to the two best men of Fano,
to Messer Guido and also to Angelello,
that, if foresight here is not vain,
they will be cast out of their vessel
and drowned with weights near Cattolica,
through the treachery of a wicked tyrant.
Between the island of Cyprus and Majorca
Neptune never saw so great a crime,
not by pirates, not by the people of Argos.
That traitor who sees with only one eye
and holds the city that one who is here with me
would wish never to have seen,
will have them come to confer with him;
then he will see to it that against the wind of Focara
they will have no need of vow or prayer.
And I to him: Show me and make clear,
if you wish me to carry news of you up above,
who is the one to whom the sight was bitter.
Then he placed his hand on the jaw
of a companion of his, and opened his mouth,
crying: This is he, and he does not speak:
this man, banished, drowned the doubt
in Caesar, affirming that a man prepared
has always suffered delay to his hurt.
Oh how dismayed he seemed to me,
with his tongue cut out at the throat,
Curio, who was so bold in speech!
And one who had both the one hand and the other cut off,
raising his stumps up through the murky air,
so that the blood befouled his face,
cried: You will remember Mosca too,
who said, alas! A thing done has an end,
which was an evil seed for the Tuscan people.
And I added: And the death of your line.
Whereupon he, heaping grief upon grief,
went his way like one sorrowful and mad.
But I remained looking at the throng,
and saw a thing that I would be afraid,
without further proof, even to recount,
were it not that conscience reassures me,
the good companion that emboldens a man
under the hauberk of feeling itself pure.
I saw for certain, and still I seem to see it,
a trunk without a head walking, just as
the others of the sorry flock walked.
And the severed head it held by the hair,
dangling from its hand like a lantern,
and that head looked at us and said: Woe is me!
Of himself he made a lamp for himself,
and they were two in one, and one in two;
how this can be, he knows who governs above.
When he was right at the foot of the bridge,
he raised his arm with the head and all
to bring his words nearer to us,
which were: Now see the grievous punishment,
you who, breathing, go about seeing the dead:
see if any is as great as this;
and so that you may carry news of me,
know that I am Bertran de Born, the one
who gave King John evil counsels.
I made the father and the son rebels against each other:
Ahithophel did no more with Absalom
and with David by his wicked promptings.
Because I parted persons so joined,
parted I carry my brain, alas!
from its source, which is in this trunk.
Thus is retribution in kind observed in me.
