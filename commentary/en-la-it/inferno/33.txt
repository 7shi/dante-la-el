His mouth he lifted from the savage meal,
that sinner, wiping it on the hair
of the head that he had laid waste from behind.
Then he began: You wish me to renew
desperate grief that presses on my heart
already at the mere thought, before I speak.
But if my words are to be seed
that bears infamy as fruit for the traitor I gnaw,
speaking and weeping you shall hear together.
I do not know who you are, nor in what way
you have come down here; but a Florentine
you truly seem to me, when I hear you.
You must know that I was Count Ugolino,
and this is Archbishop Ruggieri:
now I shall tell you why I am such a neighbor.
That through the working of his evil designs,
trusting in him, I was taken
and afterward put to death, there is no need to tell.
But what you cannot have heard,
that is, how cruel my death was,
you shall hear, and you shall know whether he has wronged me.
A narrow opening within the mew
which on my account has the title of Hunger,
and which must yet shut others in,
had shown me through its hole
more light already, when I dreamed the evil dream
that tore for me the veil of the future.
This one seemed to me master and lord,
hunting the wolf and the wolf cubs on the mountain
because of which the Pisans cannot see Lucca.
With lean hounds, eager and trained,
the Gualandi with the Sismondi and the Lanfranchi
he had set out in front of him.
After a short run they seemed to me weary,
the father and the sons, and with sharp fangs
I seemed to see their flanks torn open.
When I awoke before the morning,
I heard my sons weeping in their sleep,
who were with me, and asking for bread.
Cruel indeed are you, if you do not grieve already,
thinking what my heart foretold to itself:
and if you do not weep, at what do you usually weep?
Already they were awake, and the hour was drawing near
when food used to be brought to us,
and because of his dream each was afraid:
and I heard the door below being nailed shut
in the horrible tower; whereupon I looked
into my sons' faces without a word.
I did not weep; so turned to stone was I within:
they wept; and my little Anselmo
said: You look so, father: what is it?
For all that I shed no tears, nor did I answer
all that day, nor the night after,
until the next sun came forth upon the world.
As soon as a little ray had made its way
into the sorrowful prison, I discerned
in four faces my own very look;
both my hands I bit for grief.
And they, believing that I did it from a desire
to eat, suddenly rose up,
and said: Father, far less will be our pain
if you eat of us: you clothed us in
this wretched flesh, and you strip it off.
I quieted myself then so as not to make them sadder:
that day and the next we remained as if mute:
ah, hard earth, why did you not open?
After we had come to the fourth day,
Gaddo threw himself full length at my feet,
and said: Father, why do you not help me?
Thus he died: and as you see me,
I saw the three fall one by one
between the fifth and the sixth day: whereupon I began,
already blind, to grope over each one,
and for two days I called them after they were dead:
then hunger proved mightier than grief.
When he had said this, with eyes askew
he seized the wretched skull again with his teeth,
which were strong upon the bone, like a dog's.
Ah, Pisa, disgrace of the peoples
of the fair land there where sì sounds;
since your neighbors are slow to punish you,
let Capraia and Gorgona move
and make a barrier for the Arno at its mouth,
so that it may drown every person in you:
for if Count Ugolino had such a name
for having betrayed you in the matter of the castles,
you should not have put his sons to such a cross.
Their tender age made them innocent,
you new Thebes, Uguccione and Brigata,
and the other two whom the canto names above.
We passed on, where the ice
roughly swathes another people,
not turned downward, but all turned on their backs.
Weeping itself there lets them not weep;
and the grief that meets an obstacle at the eyes
turns inward to make the anguish grow:
for the first tears form a knot
and, like visors of crystal,
fill the whole socket beneath the brow.
And although, as from a callus,
because of the cold all feeling
had ceased to dwell in my face,
already I seemed to feel some wind.
Whereupon I: My master, who moves this?
Is not every vapor quenched down here?
Whereupon he to me: Soon you will be where
your eye will give you the answer to this,
seeing the cause that showers down the blast.
And one of the wretches of the frozen crust
cried to us: O souls cruel
so much that the last station has been given you,
lift from my face the hard veils,
so that I may vent the grief that fills my heart
a little, before the tears freeze again.
Whereupon I to him: If you wish me to help you,
tell me who you are; and if I do not free you,
may I have to go to the bottom of the ice.
He answered then: I am Friar Alberigo;
I am he of the fruit of the evil garden,
who here get back a date for a fig.
Oh, I said to him, are you dead already, then?
And he to me: How my body fares
in the world above, I have no knowledge.
Such a privilege has this Ptolomea,
that often the soul falls here
before Atropos sets it in motion.
And that you may the more willingly scrape for me
the glassy tears from the face,
know that as soon as the soul betrays,
as I did, its body is taken from it
by a demon, who thereafter governs it
until its time has wholly run its course.
It plunges into such a cistern,
and perhaps there still appears above the body
of the shade that winters here behind me.
You must know it, if you have only now come down:
he is Ser Branca d'Oria, and several years have
since passed since he was shut in here.
I believe, I said to him, that you deceive me;
for Branca d'Oria has not yet died,
and eats and drinks and sleeps and wears clothes.
In the ditch above, he said, of the Malebranche,
there where the sticky pitch boils,
Michel Zanche had not yet arrived
when that man left a devil in his stead
in his body, as did a kinsman of his
who committed the betrayal together with him.
But stretch your hand this way now,
open my eyes: and I did not open them for him,
and courtesy made me be rude.
Ah, Genoese, men estranged
from every custom, and full of every vice,
why are you not scattered from the world?
For with the worst spirit of Romagna
I found such a one of you that, for his deed,
in soul he already bathes in Cocytus,
and in body still appears alive above.
