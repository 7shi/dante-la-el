I have seen horsemen before break camp,
And begin battle, and make their muster,
And sometimes depart for their safety;
Scouts I have seen through your land,
O Aretines, and I have seen raiding parties go forth,
Blows struck in tournaments, and jousts run,
Now with trumpets, and now with bells,
With drums and with signals from castles,
And with things of our own and with foreign ones;
Yet never to so strange a shawm
Did I see horsemen move, or footmen,
Or a ship at a sign from land or star.
We were going with the ten demons:
Ah, fierce company! but in church
With the saints, and in the tavern with the gluttons.
Still on the pitch was my attention fixed,
To see every feature of the ditch,
And of the people who burned within it.
As dolphins, when they make a sign
To sailors with the arch of their backs,
That they should contrive to save their ship;
So sometimes, to ease the pain,
One of the sinners would show his back,
And hide it in less time than lightning flashes.
As at the water's edge in a ditch
The frogs stay with just their snouts out,
So that they hide their feet and the rest of their bulk;
So on every side the sinners stayed:
But as Barbariccia drew near,
So they drew back beneath the seething.
I saw, and still my heart shudders at it,
One wait thus, as it happens
That one frog stays, and another leaps away.
And Graffiacan, who was most directly opposite him,
Hooked his pitch-smeared locks,
And drew him up, so that he seemed to me an otter.
I already knew the name of every one of them,
So well did I mark them when they were chosen,
And afterward, when they called each other, I heeded how.
O Rubicante, see that you set
Your great claws on him so that you flay him,
Cried the accursed ones all together.
And I: My master, see, if you can,
That you learn who the wretch is
Who has come into the hands of his adversaries.
My guide drew close to his side,
Asked him whence he was, and he answered:
I was born in the kingdom of Navarre.
My mother placed me as servant to a lord,
She who had borne me by a knave,
Destroyer of himself and of his possessions.
Then I was a household servant of good King Theobald;
There I set myself to practicing barratry,
For which I give account in this heat.
And Ciriatto, out of whose mouth came
On either side a tusk as on a pig,
Made him feel how one of them rips.
Among wicked cats had the mouse come;
But Barbariccia enclosed him in his arms,
And said: Stay there, while I hold him fast.
And toward my master he turned his face:
Ask, he said, again, if you desire more
To know from him, before another tears him apart.
The guide: Now then, tell of the other guilty ones:
Do you know anyone who is Italian
Beneath the pitch? And he: I parted
A short while ago from one who was from near there;
Would that I were still covered with him,
For I should fear neither claw nor hook.
And Libicocco: Too much have we borne,
He said; and seized his arm with the hook,
So that, pulling, he carried off the muscle.
Draghignazzo too wanted to seize him
Down by the legs; whereupon their decurion
Turned all about with an angry look.
When they were a little pacified,
Of him who still gazed at his wound
My guide inquired without delay:
Who was he from whom so ill a parting
You say you made in coming to the bank?
And he answered: It was Friar Gomita,
He of Gallura, vessel of every fraud,
Who had his lord's enemies in hand,
And dealt with them so that each praises him for it:
Money he took from them, and let them go summarily,
As the saying goes: and in his other offices too
A barrator was he, not a petty one but supreme.
Lord Michel Zanche keeps company with him,
He of Logodoro: and in speaking of Sardinia
Their tongues do not feel weary.
Alas! see the other one gnashing his teeth:
I would say more; but I fear he
Is preparing to scratch my scabby head.
And the great chief, turning to Farfarello,
Who was rolling his eyes to strike,
Said: Get over there, wicked bird.
If you wish to see or hear,
The frightened one then began again,
Tuscans or Lombards, I shall make some come.
But let the Evil Claws stand a little aside,
So that I need not fear their vengeance;
And I, sitting in this very place,
For the one that I am, shall make seven come,
When I whistle, as it is our custom
To do when one of us comes out.
Cagnazzo at these words raised his muzzle,
Shaking his head, and said: Hear the trick
That he has thought up, to throw himself down.
And he who had snares in great abundance
Answered: Malicious am I all too much,
When I contrive greater sorrow for my own.
Alichino did not hold back, and in opposition
To the others said to him: If you go down,
I shall not come after you at a gallop,
But shall beat my wings above the pitch:
Leave him the ridge, and let the bank be a shield,
To see whether you alone are a match for more than us.
O you who read, you shall hear of a strange sport.
Each toward the other slope turned his eyes;
He first who had been most loath to do so.
The Navarrese well chose his moment,
Planted the soles of his feet on the ground, and in an instant
Leaped, and freed himself from their chief.
At which each was pricked with guilt,
But most of all he who was the cause of the failure;
Therefore he set off and cried: You are caught.
But little did it avail; for the wings could not
Outstrip fear: the one went under,
And the other, flying, turned his breast upward:
Not otherwise does the duck, all at once,
When the falcon draws near, dive down,
And he goes back up angry and spent.
Calcabrina, angered by the trick,
Followed him flying, eager
That the fellow should escape, so as to have the fight.
And as soon as the barrator had disappeared,
He turned his talons on his companion,
And was locked with him above the ditch.
But the other too was a fierce sparrowhawk
In clawing him well, and both
Fell into the middle of the boiling pool.
The heat at once parted the fighters:
But for all that there was no rising,
So limed were their wings.
Barbariccia, distressed with the rest of his band,
Made four of them fly to the other slope
With all their grappling hooks, very swiftly.
On this side, on that, they descended to their posts:
They held out their hooks toward the limed pair,
Who were already cooked beneath the crust:
And we left them thus entangled.
