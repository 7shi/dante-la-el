In the time when Juno was enraged
on account of Semele against the Theban blood,
as she showed time and again,
Athamas became so mad
that, seeing his wife with two sons
go laden on either hand,
he cried: Let us spread the nets, so that I may catch
the lioness and the lion cubs at the pass.
And then he stretched out his pitiless claws,
taking the one who had the name Learchus,
and whirled him round, and dashed him against a rock;
and she drowned herself with the other burden.
And when fortune turned downward
the eminence of the Trojans, which dared all,
so that together with the kingdom the king was undone,
Hecuba, sad, wretched and captive,
after she saw Polyxena dead,
and of her fair Polydorus on the shore
of the sea she, sorrowing, had caught sight,
out of her mind barked like a dog;
so awry did grief make her mind.
But neither Theban nor Trojan furies
were ever seen in anyone so cruel,
in goading beasts, let alone human limbs,
as I saw in two pale and naked shades,
who ran biting in that manner
of a hog when it breaks out of the sty.
One overtook Capocchio, and in the nape
of the neck sank its fangs into him so that, dragging him,
it made him scrape against the solid bottom itself.
And the Aretine, who remained trembling,
said to me: That goblin is Gianni Schicchi,
who goes about rabid, treating others so.
Oh, I said to him, as you hope the other will not pierce you
with its great claws upon your back, let it be no trouble to you
to say who it is, before it darts away from here.
And he to me: That is the ancient soul
of wicked Myrrha, who became
her father's lover, outside rightful love.
She contrived to sin with him,
counterfeiting herself in another's form,
as the other, who goes off yonder, undertook,
to gain the lady of the herd,
to counterfeit in himself Buoso Donati,
making a will and giving the testament due form.
And after the two rabid ones had passed,
on whom I had kept my eye,
I turned to look at the other ill-born.
I saw one shaped in the likeness of a lute,
if only he had had his groin
cut off at the part where man is forked.
The grievous dropsy, which so mismatches
the limbs with the humour it turns to ill
that the face does not correspond to the belly,
made him hold his lips open,
as the consumptive does, who for thirst
turns one toward the chin and the other upward.
O you, who without any pain are
(and indeed I know not why) in the woeful world,
he said to us, look and attend
to the misery of Master Adam:
I had, when alive, enough of what I wanted,
and now, alas, a drop of water I crave.
The little streams that from the green hills
descend down from the Casentino into the Arno,
making their channels cold and moist,
stand ever before me, and not in vain;
for their image dries me up far more
than the ill that strips the flesh from my face.
The stern justice that goads me
draws occasion from the place where I sinned
to set my sighs the more in flight.
There is Romena, there where I falsified
the alloy stamped with the Baptist,
for which I left my body burnt up above.
But if I saw here the sorrowful soul
of Guido, and of Alessandro, and of their brother,
for Fonte Branda I would not give up the sight.
One is already in here, if the rabid
shades that go about speak true:
but what good is it to me, whose limbs are bound?
If I were still even so far light of limb
that I could in a hundred years go an inch,
I would already have set out along the path,
seeking him among these disfigured people,
for all that it measures eleven miles around,
and no less than half a mile is it across.
It is on their account I am among such a family:
for they induced me to strike the florins
that had three carats of dross.
And I to him: Who are the two wretches
who steam like wet hands in winter,
lying close together at your right side?
Here I found them, and since then they have not turned,
he answered, when I rained down into this gully,
and I do not believe they will turn in all eternity.
One is the false woman who accused Joseph;
the other is the false Sinon, the Greek from Troy:
from acute fever they give off such a stench.
And one of them, who took offence
perhaps at being named so darkly,
with his fist struck him on the leathery belly:
it sounded as if it were a drum:
and Master Adam struck him in the face
with his arm, which seemed no less hard,
saying to him: Although I am deprived of
the movement of my limbs that are heavy,
I have my arm free for such a task.
To which he answered: When you were going
to the fire, you did not have it so ready;
but you had it as ready and more so when you were coining.
And the dropsical one: You speak quite truly of this;
but you were not so true a witness
there where at Troy you were asked for the truth.
If I spoke falsely, you falsified the coinage,
said Sinon, and I am here for one fault,
and you for more than any other demon.
Remember the perjury about the horse,
answered the one who had the swollen belly;
and may it gall you that all the world knows it.
To you be evil the thirst whereby cracks
your tongue, said the Greek, and the foul water
that hedges your belly up before your eyes.
Then the coiner: Thus does it gape wide,
that mouth of yours, to speak ill as it is wont;
for if I have thirst, the humour stuffs me full:
you have the burning thirst and the head that aches;
and to lick the mirror of Narcissus
you would not need many words of invitation.
On listening to them I was wholly intent,
when the master said to me: Just keep looking now,
for I am very near to quarrelling with you.
When I heard him speak to me in anger,
I turned toward him with such shame
that it still turns about in my memory.
And like one who dreams of his own hurt,
who, dreaming, desires to be dreaming,
so that he longs for what is as though it were not,
so I became, unable to speak,
for I desired to excuse myself, and was excusing
myself all the while, and did not believe I was doing so.
A greater fault is washed away by less shame,
said the master, than yours has been;
therefore unburden yourself of all sadness:
and reckon that I am always at your side,
if ever again it happens that fortune catches you
where there are people in a like dispute;
for to wish to hear that is a base desire.
