Already I was in a place where one heard the rumble
of the water that was falling into the next circle,
like the drone the beehives make;
when three shades together broke away,
running, from a throng that was passing
beneath the rain of bitter torment.
They were coming toward us; and each cried:
Stop, you who by your dress seem to us
to be someone from our wicked land.
Alas, what wounds I saw upon their limbs,
fresh and old, burnt in by the flames!
So that it pains me still, if I but remember it.
To their cries my teacher gave heed,
turned his face toward me, and said: Wait,
for to these one must be courteous:
and were it not for the fire that rains down,
by the nature of the place, I would say
that haste better became you than them.
They began again, when we stopped,
their old refrain; and when they had reached us,
they made a wheel of themselves, all three.
As champions are wont to do, naked and anointed,
eyeing their hold and their advantage,
before they are struck and pricked by one another;
and just so, wheeling, each his face
turned toward me, so that between them and the neck
made a continual journey on foot.
And though the misery of this loose-soiled place
brings us and our prayers into contempt,
one began, and our sad and bare appearance too;
let our fame incline your spirit
to tell us who you are, who living feet
so securely through Hell do trail.
This man, in whose footprint you see me tread,
although he goes naked and stripped of hair,
was of higher rank than you believe.
Grandson he was of the good Gualdrada:
Guido Guerra was his name, and in his life
he did much with his counsel and with his sword.
The other who beside me grinds the sand
is Teghiaio Aldobrandi, whose voice
in the world above ought to be prized.
And I, who am set with them upon the cross,
was Iacopo Rusticucci; and surely
my fierce wife harms me more than anything else.
If I had been sheltered from the fire,
I would have thrown myself down among them,
and I believe my teacher would have allowed it.
But because I would have been burnt and cooked,
fear overcame my good will,
which made me eager to embrace them.
Then I began: Not contempt, but grief
your condition fixed within me
so deeply that only late will it wholly leave me,
as soon as this my lord spoke to me
words from which I thought
that such people as you are were coming.
I am of your land; and always
your deeds and your honored names
with affection I recounted and heard.
I leave the gall, and go for the sweet fruits
promised to me by my honored guide;
but first to the very center I must plunge.
So may your soul long guide
your limbs, that one then replied,
and so may your fame shine after you,
do courtesy and valor, he said, dwell
in our city as they used to,
or have they gone out of it altogether?
For Guglielmo Borsier, who has suffered
with us but a little while, and goes there with our companions,
troubles us greatly with his words.
The new people and sudden gains
have bred pride and excess
in you, Florence, so that already you weep for it.
Thus I cried with my face raised:
and the three, who took this for an answer,
gazed at one another as one gazes at the truth.
If at other times it costs you so little,
they all replied, to satisfy others,
happy you, who speak so at your pleasure.
Therefore if you escape these dark places,
and return to see the lovely stars,
when it will please you to say: I was,
see that you speak of us to the people.
Then they broke the wheel, and as they fled
their nimble legs seemed wings.
An Amen could not have been said
so quickly as they had vanished:
wherefore the master thought it time to leave.
I followed him, and we had gone but a little way
when the sound of the water was so near us
that had we spoken we would scarcely have been heard.
As that river which has its own course,
the first from Mount Veso toward the east
on the left slope of the Apennine,
which is called Acquaqueta above, before
it descends into the low bed,
which at Forlì is without that name,
resounds there above San Benedetto
dell'Alpe, from falling at a single drop
that ought to be a receptacle for a thousand springs;
so, from a sheer bank,
we found that stained water resounding,
so that in a short while it would have hurt our ears.
I had a cord girded round me,
and with it I once thought
to take the leopard with the painted hide.
After I had wholly loosed it from me,
just as my guide had commanded me,
I handed it to him knotted and coiled.
Whereupon he turned toward the right side,
and somewhat clear of the edge
he threw it down into that deep chasm.
And surely some new thing must answer,
I said within myself, the novel signal
that the master so follows with his eye.
Ah! how cautious men must be
near those who see not only the deed,
but with discernment look into the thoughts within!
He said to me: Soon there will come up
what I await and what your thought dreams of;
soon must it reveal itself to your sight.
Always to that truth which has the face of a lie
a man must close his lips as long as he can,
because through no fault it brings shame;
but here I cannot keep silent; by the notes
of this comedy, reader, I swear to you,
so may they not be void of lasting favor,
that I saw through that thick and dark air
a figure come swimming upward,
wondrous to every steadfast heart,
just as he returns who goes down
at times to loose the anchor that catches
on a rock or something else hidden in the sea,
who stretches upward and draws in his feet.
