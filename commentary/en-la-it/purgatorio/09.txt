The concubine of ancient Tithonus
was already whitening at the eastern height,
out of the arms of her sweet friend:
with gems her brow was shining,
set in the figure of the cold animal
that with its tail strikes people:
and the night, of the steps with which it climbs,
had taken two in the place where we were,
and the third was already bending its wings downward:
when I, who had with me something of Adam's,
overcome by sleep, on the grass sank down
there where all five of us were already sitting.
In the hour when she begins her sad laments,
the little swallow, close to morning,
perhaps in memory of her first woes;
and when our mind, wandering
farther from the flesh and less held by thought,
in its visions is almost divine;
in a dream I seemed to see suspended
an eagle in the sky with feathers of gold,
with wings spread, and intent on descending.
And I seemed to be where they were
abandoned by Ganymede, his own people,
when he was snatched up to the highest council.
Within myself I thought: Perhaps this one strikes
only here by custom, and perhaps from another place
disdains to carry anything up in its claws.
Then it seemed to me that, having circled a little more,
terrible as lightning it descended,
and snatched me up as far as the fire.
There it seemed that it and I were burning,
and so the imagined blaze scorched me
that my sleep had to break.
No otherwise did Achilles start awake,
turning his awakened eyes about,
and not knowing where he was,
when his mother from Chiron to Scyros
stole him away, sleeping in her arms,
from where the Greeks afterwards departed;
than I started, as from my face
sleep fled me, and I turned pale,
as does a man who is frightened on a hunt.
Beside me was my Comfort alone,
and the sun had already been up more than two hours,
and my face was turned toward the sea.
Have no fear, said my lord:
take heart, for we are well placed:
do not constrain, but give full scope to, all your strength.
You have by now arrived at Purgatory:
see there the cliff that shuts it in all around;
see the entrance where it seems divided.
A little while ago, in the dawn that precedes the day,
when your soul was sleeping within
upon the flowers with which the place below is adorned,
a lady came, and said: I am Lucia:
let me take this man who sleeps,
so shall I ease him on his way.
Sordello remained, and the others except me:
she took you up; and when the day was bright,
she came on up, and I in her footprints.
Here she set you down: and then showed me,
her beautiful eyes, that open entrance:
then she and sleep together went away.
Like a man who in doubt grows certain again,
and into comfort changes his fear,
after the truth is disclosed to him,
I changed in turn: and when, free from care,
my guide saw me, up along the rock
he moved, and I behind him toward the height.
Reader, you see well how I raise
my subject, and therefore, with greater art,
do not marvel if I reinforce it.
We drew near, and were at a point,
there where it had seemed to me broken before,
just like a cleft that divides a wall,
I saw a gate, and three steps beneath,
for going to it, of different colors,
and a gatekeeper who had not yet said a word.
And as I opened my eyes wider and wider there,
I saw him sitting above the highest step,
such in the face that I could not bear the sight:
and a naked sword he had in hand
that so reflected the rays toward us
that I often directed my gaze in vain.
Say it from where you are: what do you want?
he began to say: where is your escort?
Take care that coming up does not harm you.
A lady of heaven, versed in these things,
my master answered him, just a little while ago
told us: Go there, here is the gate.
And may she speed your steps well,
the courteous gatekeeper began again:
come forward then to our steps.
There we came; and the first step
was white marble so polished and clear
that I saw myself mirrored in it just as I appear.
The second, darker in tint than deepest purple, was
of a rough and scorched stone,
cracked lengthwise and crosswise.
The third, which lies massive on top,
seemed to me porphyry as flaming
as blood that spurts out of a vein.
Upon this rested both the soles of his feet,
the angel of God, sitting on the threshold,
which seemed to me a stone of adamant.
Up the three steps, with me quite willing,
my guide drew me, saying: Ask
humbly that he undo the lock.
Devoutly I threw myself at the holy feet:
I asked for mercy and that he open to me;
but first three times I struck my breast.
Seven P's on my forehead he inscribed
with the point of the sword, and: See that you wash,
when you are within, these wounds, he said.
Ash or earth dug up dry
would be of one color with his garment,
and from beneath it he drew two keys.
The one was of gold and the other was of silver:
first with the white, and then with the yellow,
he did so at the gate that I was content.
Whenever one of these keys fails
to turn aright in the lock,
he said to us, this passage does not open.
More precious is the one; but the other requires much
art and ingenuity before it unlocks,
because it is the one that undoes the knot.
From Peter I hold them; and he told me to err
rather in opening than in keeping it locked,
provided people cast themselves down at my feet.
Then he pushed the door of the sacred gate,
saying: Enter; but I make you aware
that he goes back outside who looks behind him.
And when there were turned in their hinges
the pivots of that sacred portal,
which are of metal, resonant and strong,
not so did it roar, nor show itself so harsh,
the Tarpeian rock, when from it was taken the good
Metellus, wherefore it afterwards was left lean.
I turned attentive at the first thunder,
and, You, God, we praise, I seemed
to hear in a voice mingled with the sweet sound.
Just such an impression did it give me,
what I heard, as one is wont to receive
when singing is done in order:
for now one understands the words, now not.
