So fixed and intent were my eyes
on slaking their ten-year thirst,
that my other senses were all extinguished:
and they on this side and on that had a wall
of indifference, so the holy smile
drew them to itself with the ancient net;
when by force my face was turned
toward my left by those goddesses,
for I heard from them a: Too fixedly.
The condition that, as to sight, is
in eyes just now struck by the sun
made me for a while be without sight;
but after my sight recovered a little,
I say a little in relation to the great
object of sense from which by force I turned,
I saw that to the right arm had turned
the glorious host, and was returning,
with the sun and the seven flames in its face.
As beneath their shields, to save themselves,
a troop turns, and wheels with its standard,
before it can turn about in its entirety;
that soldiery of the heavenly kingdom
which went before had all passed us
before the chariot bent its foremost timber.
Then to the wheels the women returned,
and the griffin moved the blessed load,
so that not a feather stirred for that.
The fair lady who drew me to the crossing,
and Statius and I, were following the wheel
that made its track with the smaller arc.
Thus walking through the lofty forest, empty
through the fault of her who trusted the present,
an angelic strain measured our steps.
Perhaps in three flights as much space was covered
by an unbridled arrow as we had
advanced at the time when Beatrice descended.
I heard them all murmur: Adam!
Then they encircled a tree stripped
of leaves and of every bough with its branch.
Its crown, which spreads so much
wider the higher it goes, would be by the Indians
in their woods marvelled at for height.
Blessed are you, griffin, who do not tear
with your beak at this wood sweet to the taste,
since the belly is painfully twisted by it.
Thus all around the sturdy tree
the others cried to the creature of two natures;
thus is preserved the seed of all that is just.
And turning to the pole that he had drawn,
he drew it to the foot of the widowed bough;
and that which was of her he left bound to her.
As our trees, when falls
down the great light mingled with that
which shines behind the heavenly fish,
grow swollen, and then is renewed
in its own color each one, before the sun
yokes his coursers beneath another star;
less than of roses and more than of violets
a color the tree takes on, so new,
that before had its branches so bare.
I did not understand it, nor is it sung here yet,
the hymn that those people then sang,
nor could I bear the strain in its entirety.
If I could portray how there fell asleep
the pitiless eyes, hearing of Syrinx,
the eyes that not keeping watch cost so dear;
like a painter who paints from a model,
I would sketch how I fell asleep;
but let whoever will depict falling asleep well.
Therefore I pass on to when I awoke,
and say that a splendor tore for me the veil
of sleep, and a call: Rise, what are you doing?
As, to see some of the little blossoms of the apple tree
that makes the angels eager for its fruit
and makes a perpetual wedding feast in heaven,
Peter, John and James, led
and overcome, came back to themselves at the word
by which deeper sleeps were broken,
and saw their company diminished
by Moses as well as by Elijah,
and their master's robe changed;
so I came back to myself, and saw that devout lady
standing over me, who the guide
of my steps along the river had been before;
and all in doubt I said: Where is Beatrice?
To which she: See her beneath the foliage
newly sprung, sitting upon its root.
See the company that surrounds her;
the others after the griffin are going up,
with sweeter song and more profound.
And whether her speech was more extended
I do not know, for already in my eyes was
she who had closed me to all other thought.
Alone she sat upon the very earth,
like a guard left there over the chariot
that I saw the two-formed beast bind.
In a circle they made an enclosure of themselves about her,
the seven nymphs, with those lights in hand
that are safe from north wind and south.
Here you will be a short time a forest dweller,
and you will be with me, without end, a citizen
of that Rome of which Christ is a Roman;
therefore, for the good of the world that lives ill,
on the chariot keep now your eyes; and what you see,
once returned yonder, see that you write.
Thus Beatrice; and I, who wholly at the feet
of her commands was devoted,
gave my mind and my eye where she willed.
Never did there descend with so swift a motion
fire from thick cloud, when it rains down
from that boundary which lies most remote,
as I saw descend the bird of Jove
down through the tree, breaking off some of the bark,
not to speak of the flowers and the new leaves;
and it struck the chariot with all its force,
so that it heeled over like a ship in a storm,
overcome by waves, now to leeward, now to windward.
Then I saw hurl itself into the cradle
of the triumphal vehicle a fox
that seemed to have fasted from all good food.
But, rebuking it for foul offenses,
my lady put it to as swift a flight
as its bones without flesh allowed.
Then, by the way it had first come,
the eagle I saw descend down into the body
of the chariot, and leave it feathered with its own plumage.
And as comes from a heart that grieves,
such a voice came from heaven, and thus it said:
O little ship of mine, how ill you are laden.
It seemed to me then that the earth opened
between the two wheels, and I saw come out of it a dragon
that thrust its tail up through the chariot:
and like a wasp that draws back its sting,
drawing to itself its malicious tail,
it pulled away part of the floor, and went idly on its way.
What remained, as with couch grass
fertile earth is, with the plumage, offered
perhaps with sound and kindly intent,
covered itself, and with it were covered
both the one wheel and the other and the pole in so short a time
that a sigh holds the mouth open longer.
Thus transformed, the holy structure
put forth heads on its several parts,
three above the pole, and one at each corner.
The first were horned like oxen,
but the four had a single horn to each forehead:
such a monster has not yet been seen.
Secure as a fortress on a high mountain,
sitting upon it a wanton whore
appeared to me, with eyes alert on every side.
And, as if lest she be taken from him,
I saw standing beside her a giant,
and they kissed each other from time to time:
but because her greedy and wandering eye
she turned to me, that fierce lover
scourged her from her head to the soles of her feet.
Then, full of spite and cruel in his wrath,
he unbound the monster and dragged it through the forest
so far that its ground made me a screen
against the whore and the strange beast.
