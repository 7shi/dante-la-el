Already had the sun to the horizon come,
whose meridian circle covers
Jerusalem with its highest point:
and the night, which circles opposite to him,
was coming forth from Ganges with the Scales,
which fall from her hand when she outweighs him;
so that the white and the vermilion cheeks,
there where I was, of the fair dawn,
through too great age were turning orange.
We were going along the very sea still,
like people who think of their journey,
who go in heart, and in body stay:
and behold, just as is its wont, near morning
through the thick vapors Mars glows red
down in the west above the ocean floor,
just so there appeared to me, so may I see it again,
a light across the sea coming so swiftly
that its motion no flight can match;
from which, when I had a little withdrawn
my eye to question my guide,
I saw it again, grown brighter and larger.
Then on each side of it there appeared to me
a white I knew not what, and beneath,
little by little, another came forth from it.
My master still spoke not a word,
while the first white shapes opened their wings:
then, when he clearly recognized the boatman,
he cried: See, see that you lower your knees;
behold the angel of God; fold your hands:
henceforth you will see such ministers.
See how they disdain human contrivances,
so that he wants neither oar nor other sail
than his wings between shores so far apart.
See how he is pointed toward heaven,
plying his wings with their eternal feathers,
which do not change like mortal hair.
Then as more and more toward us came
the divine bird, brighter he appeared;
so that my eye at close range could not bear him:
but I bent it down; and he came to shore,
with a little vessel slender and light,
so much so that the water swallowed none of it.
At the stern stood the celestial helmsman,
such that he seemed blessed by written decree;
and more than a hundred spirits sat within.
At the going forth of Israel from Egypt
they sang all together with one voice,
with all that of that psalm is written thereafter.
Then he made over them the sign of the holy cross;
whereupon they all cast themselves upon the beach,
and he went off, as he had come, swiftly.
The throng that remained there, strange
it seemed, marveling at that place all around,
like one who tastes new things.
On every side was shooting forth the day
the sun, who had with his sure arrows
from the middle of the sky driven Capricorn,
when the new people raised their brows
toward us, saying to us: If you know,
show us the way to go to the mountain.
And Virgil answered: You believe
perhaps that we are versed in this place;
but we are pilgrims, as you are.
We came here a little before you,
by another way that was so rough and hard
that climbing now will seem to us play.
The souls who had perceived of me,
by my breathing, that I was still alive,
marveling, turned pale;
and as to a messenger who bears an olive branch
the people flock to hear news,
and of crowding no one shows himself shy;
so upon my face were fastened those
fortunate souls, every one,
almost forgetting to go make themselves fair.
I saw one of them draw forward
to embrace me with so great affection
that it moved me to do the like.
Shades they were, empty, for in appearance
three times behind it I clasped my hands,
and as often I came back with them to my breast.
With wonder, I believe, I was painted;
for the shade smiled and drew back,
and I, following it, pressed farther on.
Gently it told me to rest:
then I knew who it was, and I begged him
that to speak to me a little he would stay.
He answered me: Just as I loved you
in the mortal body, so I love you freed from it;
therefore I stay: but why do you go?
My Casella, to return another time
there where I am, I make this journey;
but from you how has so much ground been taken?
And he to me: No wrong has been done me,
if he who takes up when and whom he pleases
several times has denied me this passage;
for of a just will his own is made.
In truth for three months he has taken
whoever has wished to enter, with all peace.
Whence I, who had now turned to the seashore
where the water of Tiber grows salt,
was kindly taken up by him
at that river mouth, whither he has turned his wing:
because there always gathers
whoever does not descend toward Acheron.
And I: If a new law does not take from you
the memory or practice of the song of love
that used to quiet all my desires,
with it may it please you to console me a little
my soul, which, with my body
coming here, is so wearied.
Love, who within my mind speaks to me,
he began then so sweetly
that the sweetness still sounds within me.
My master, and I, and those people
who were with him, seemed so content
as if nothing else touched anyone's mind.
We went on, all intent and attentive
to his notes; and behold the venerable old man,
crying: What is this, slow spirits?
What negligence, what standing still is this?
Run to the mountain to strip off the slough
that does not let God be manifest to you.
And as, gathering grain or darnel,
the doves assembled at their feeding,
these, without showing their accustomed pride,
if something appears of which they are afraid,
suddenly leave the food alone,
because they are assailed by a greater care;
so I saw that newly arrived company
leave the song, and go toward the slope,
like one who goes and knows not where he is heading:
nor was our departure less swift.
