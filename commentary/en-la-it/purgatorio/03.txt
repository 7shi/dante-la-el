Although the sudden flight
scattered those souls across the plain,
turned toward the mountain, where reason spurs us on,
I drew close to my faithful companion:
and how could I have run without him?
Who would have drawn me up the mountain?
He seemed to me reproached by his own self:
O dignified conscience and pure,
how bitter a bite to you is a small fault!
When his feet left off their haste,
which mars the dignity of every act,
my mind, which at first had been confined,
broadened its attention again, eager as it was,
and I turned my face toward the hill
that toward the sky rises highest out of the waters.
The sun, which behind me flamed red,
was broken before me in the shape I made,
because in me its rays found their support.
I turned to the side in fear
of being abandoned, when I saw
only in front of me the ground darkened.
And my comfort: Why do you still doubt,
he began to say to me, turned wholly toward me;
do you not believe I am with you and that I guide you?
It is already evening there, where lies buried
the body within which I cast a shadow:
Naples has it, and from Brindisi it was taken.
Now, if before me nothing is shadowed,
do not marvel any more than at the heavens,
of which one does not obstruct another's ray.
To suffer torments, heats and frosts,
things like bodies are disposed by the power
that does not will that how this is should be revealed to us.
Mad is he who hopes that our reason
can traverse the infinite way
that one substance in three persons takes.
Be content, human race, with the fact that it is so;
for if it were possible to see everything,
there would have been no need for Mary to give birth;
and you saw desiring without fruit
such men as would have had their desire stilled,
which is given them eternally for grief.
I speak of Aristotle, of Plato,
and of many others. And here he bowed his head;
and he said no more, and seemed troubled to me.
Meanwhile we came to the foot of the mountain:
there we found the rock so steep
that nimble legs would have been of no avail there.
Between Lerici and La Turbie the most deserted,
the most remote path is a stairway,
compared with that one, easy and open.
Now who knows on which side the slope descends,
said my master, halting his step,
so that one who goes without wings can climb?
And while he kept his face lowered,
questioning his mind about the way,
and I looked up around the rock,
on the left there appeared to me a company
of souls, who moved their feet toward us,
and did not seem to: so slowly did they come.
Raise, I said, master, your eyes:
behold on this side those who will give us counsel,
if you cannot have it from yourself.
He looked then, and with untroubled brow
replied: Let us go over there, for they come slowly;
and you, hold your hope firm, O sweet son.
That people were still as far away,
I mean, after a thousand steps of ours,
as a good thrower could throw with his hand,
when they all pressed close to the hard rocks
of the high bank, and stood still and close together,
as one who goes in doubt stands still to look.
O you whose end was good, and spirits already chosen,
Virgil began, by that peace
which I believe is awaited by you all,
tell us where the mountain slopes
so that it may be possible to go up;
for to lose time displeases most him who knows most.
As the little sheep come out of the fold
by ones, by twos, by threes, and others stand
timidly lowering eye and muzzle to the ground;
and what the first does, the others too do,
crowding against her if she stops,
simple and quiet, and they do not know why;
so I saw the foremost start to come
of that fortunate flock then,
modest in countenance and dignified in gait.
As soon as those in front saw broken
the light on the ground at my right side,
so that the shadow stretched from me to the cliff,
they stopped and drew back a little;
and all the others who came after,
not knowing why, did likewise.
Without your asking I confess to you
that this is a human body that you see,
which is why the sunlight on the ground is cleft.
Do not marvel; but believe
that not without power that comes from heaven
does he seek to surmount this wall.
Thus the master. And that worthy company:
Turn back, they said, go on ahead then,
with the backs of their hands making signs.
And one of them began: Whoever
you are, as you go thus, turn your face,
consider whether you ever saw me on the other side.
I turned toward him and looked at him steadily:
fair-haired he was and handsome, and of noble aspect;
but one of his brows a blow had split.
When I had humbly denied
ever having seen him, he said: Now look.
And he showed me a wound at the top of his chest.
Then smiling he said: I am Manfred,
grandson of Constance the empress:
wherefore I pray you, when you return,
go to my fair daughter, mother
of the honor of Sicily and of Aragon,
and tell her the truth, if otherwise is said.
After I had my body pierced
by two mortal thrusts, I surrendered myself
weeping to him who willingly forgives.
Horrible were my sins;
but infinite goodness has arms so great
that it takes in whatever turns to it.
If the shepherd of Cosenza, who to the hunt
for me was set by Clement then,
had rightly read this page in God,
the bones of my body would still be
at the head of the bridge, near Benevento,
under the guard of the heavy cairn.
Now the rain wets them and the wind moves them
outside the kingdom, almost along the Verde,
where he transferred them with lights extinguished.
Through their curse one is not so lost
that eternal love cannot return,
so long as hope has any trace of green.
True it is that whoever dies in defiance
of Holy Church, though he repent at the end,
must stay outside this bank
for thirty times as long as he remained
in his presumption, unless such a decree
becomes shorter through good prayers.
See now whether you can make me glad,
by revealing to my good Constance
how you have seen me, and also this prohibition;
for here, through those on the other side, one advances much.
