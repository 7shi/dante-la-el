Singing like a woman in love,
she continued, upon the end of her words:
Blessed are those whose sins are covered.
And like nymphs who used to go alone
through the woodland shades, desiring,
one to see, another to flee the sun,
then she moved against the stream, going
up along the bank, and I abreast of her,
little step with little step following.
There were not a hundred between her steps and mine,
when the banks alike made a turn,
so that I turned to the east.
Nor yet had our way thus been long,
when the woman turned wholly toward me,
saying: My brother, look and listen.
And behold, a sudden brightness swept
from every side through the great forest,
such that it made me wonder if it was lightning.
But because lightning, as it comes, ceases,
and that, enduring, shone more and more,
in my thought I said: What thing is this?
And a melody sweet was running
through the luminous air; whence good zeal
made me reprove the boldness of Eve,
who, there where earth and heaven obeyed,
a woman only just, just formed,
could not bear to stay beneath any veil;
beneath which, had she been devout,
I would those ineffable delights
have tasted sooner, and for a longer time.
While I went on, amid so many first fruits
of eternal pleasure, all in suspense,
and desirous still of greater joys,
before us, just like a kindled fire,
so the air became, beneath the green branches,
and the sweet sound was by now discerned as songs.
O most holy virgins, if hunger,
cold, or vigils I ever suffered for you,
cause spurs me to call on you for grace.
Now must Helicon pour forth for me,
and Urania aid me with her choir,
things hard to conceive to put in verse.
A little farther on, seven trees of gold
were counterfeited to the eye by a long stretch
of the intervening air, the ground still between us and them:
and when I had drawn so near to them
that the common object, which deceives the sense,
lost through distance nothing of its effect,
the faculty that furnishes reason with matter for thought
apprehended them as what they were, candlesticks,
and in the voices of the singing, Hosanna.
Above, the fair array flamed
far brighter than the moon in the clear sky
at midnight in the middle of its month.
I turned, full of wonder,
to good Virgil, and he answered me
with a look laden with no less astonishment.
Then I turned my gaze back to the other things,
which were moving toward us so slowly
that they would have been outstripped by new brides.
The woman chided me: Why do you burn only
so at the sight of the living lights,
and what comes behind them do you not watch?
People I saw then, as after their leaders,
coming behind, clothed in white;
and such whiteness was never here on this side.
The water gleamed on my left flank,
and gave back to me my left side,
if I looked into it, like a mirror too.
When on my bank I had such a station
that only the river kept me apart,
to see better, I brought my steps to a halt:
and I saw the little flames go on ahead,
leaving behind them the air painted,
and they had the semblance of brushes drawn along;
so that overhead it remained marked out
in seven bands, all in those colors
with which the sun makes its bow, and Delia her girdle.
These banners extended farther back
than my sight; and, to my judgment,
ten paces apart were the outer ones.
Beneath so fair a sky as I describe,
twenty-four lords, two by two,
came crowned with lilies.
All sang: Blessed are you
among the daughters of Adam, and blessed
forever be your beauties.
After the flowers and the other fresh little herbs,
opposite me on the other bank,
were free of those chosen people,
just as light follows light in heaven,
there came after them then four living creatures,
crowned each with green foliage.
Each was feathered with six wings,
the feathers full of eyes; and the eyes of Argus,
if they were alive, would be such.
To describe their form I spend no more
words, reader; for other expense presses me
so much that on this I cannot be lavish.
But read Ezekiel, who depicts them
as he saw them from the cold quarter
coming to him with wind and with fire;
and such as you will find them in his pages,
such were they here, except that as to the wings
John is with me, and from him departs.
The space within the four of them contained
a chariot, on two wheels, triumphal,
which came drawn at a griffin's neck.
It stretched upward the one and the other wing
between the middle band and the three and three,
so that in cleaving the air it did harm to none.
So high did they rise that they were not seen;
its limbs were of gold insofar as it was bird,
and the others white mingled with vermilion.
Not only did Rome with no chariot so fair
gladden Africanus or Augustus,
but that of the sun would be poor beside it;
that of the sun which, straying, was consumed by fire,
at the prayer of the devout Earth,
when Jove was in secret just.
Three women in a circle about the right wheel
came dancing: the one so red
that scarcely would she have been discerned within the fire;
the other was as if her flesh and bones
had been made of emerald;
the third seemed snow just stirred.
And now they seemed led by the white one,
now by the red, and from the song of this one
the others took their pace, slow and swift.
By the left wheel four made merry,
clothed in purple, following the measure
of one of them who had three eyes in her head.
After all the group portrayed,
I saw two old men in unlike dress,
but alike in their bearing, dignified and steady.
The one showed himself one of the household
of that supreme Hippocrates whom nature
made for the creatures she holds most dear.
The other showed the contrary care
with a sword bright and sharp,
such that on this side of the stream it frightened me.
Then I saw four of humble appearance,
and behind all the others an old man alone
coming, sleeping, with a keen face.
And these seven, like the first company,
were clothed; but of lilies
around their heads they made no garden,
but rather of roses and other vermilion flowers:
a gaze from a little way off would have sworn
that they all burned above their brows:
and when the chariot was opposite me,
a thunderclap was heard; and those worthy people
seemed to have further advance forbidden them,
halting there with the foremost standards.
