Neither speaking walking, nor walking it, any slower
made; but talking we went swiftly,
as a ship driven by a good wind.
And the shades, who seemed so twice dead,
through the hollows of their eyes wonder
drew from me, aware that I was alive.
And I, continuing my discourse,
said: She goes up perhaps more slowly
than she would, on another's account.
But tell me, if you know, where Piccarda is;
tell me if I see anyone worth noting
among these people who so look at me.
My sister, who between beautiful and good
I know not which she was the more, triumphs joyful
on high Olympus already in her crown.
So he said first, and then: Here it is not forbidden
to name each one, since so drained is
our likeness away through the diet.
This one, and he pointed with his finger, is Buonagiunta,
Buonagiunta of Lucca; and that face
beyond him, more than the others pricked all over,
held Holy Church in its arms;
from Tours he was, and purges by fasting
the eels of Bolsena and the Vernaccia wine.
Many others he named to me one by one;
and at the naming they all seemed content,
so that I saw not one dark look on that account.
I saw for hunger ply their teeth on nothing
Ubaldin della Pila, and Boniface
who pastured many people with his crook.
I saw Messer Marchese, who had occasion
once to drink at Forlì with less thirst,
and yet was such that he did not feel sated.
But, as one does who looks, and then makes
more of one than of another, so did I with him of Lucca,
who seemed to have more knowledge of me.
He was murmuring, and I know not what about Gentucca
I heard where he felt the wound
of the justice that so plucks them bare.
O soul, said I, who seem so eager
to speak with me, make it so that I understand you,
and both yourself and me content with your speech.
A woman is born, and does not yet wear the veil,
he began, who will make you like
my city, however men reproach it.
You will go away with this foreknowledge;
if in my murmuring you took something amiss,
the true events will yet make it clear to you.
But say if I see here him who forth
brought the new rhymes, beginning:
Ladies who have understanding of love.
And I to him: I am one who, when
Love breathes into me, write down, and in the manner
that he dictates within, go on expressing it.
O brother, now, said he, I see the knot
that held the Notary, and Guittone, and me
on this side of the sweet style and the new nail.
I see well how your pens
follow close behind the one who dictates,
which with ours certainly did not happen.
And whoever sets himself to look further
sees no more between the one style and the other.
And as if contented he fell silent.
As the birds that winter toward the Nile
sometimes form a flock in the air,
then fly more swiftly and go in file;
so all the people who were there,
turning their faces, quickened their pace,
light both from leanness and from willingness.
And as the man who is weary of trotting
lets his companions go, and walks
until the panting of his chest subsides;
so let the holy flock pass by
Forese, and behind them he came along with me,
saying: When will it be that I see you again?
I do not know, I answered him, how long I shall live;
but my return will surely not be so soon
that I shall not in desire be at the shore before it.
For the place where I was set to live
from day to day is stripped barer of good,
and seems disposed to grievous ruin.
Now go, said he, for the one most to blame for it
I see him dragged at the tail of a beast
toward the valley where guilt is never purged.
The beast at every step goes more swiftly,
ever gaining speed, until it strikes him
and leaves the body vilely mangled.
These wheels have not far to turn,
and he raised his eyes to heaven, before it will be clear to you
what my speech cannot make clearer.
Now stay behind, for time is precious
in this kingdom to such a degree that I lose too much
going along with you thus side by side.
As sometimes at a gallop there comes
a knight out of a troop riding,
and goes to win himself honor in the first encounter;
so he left us with longer strides;
and I remained on the way with those two
who were such great marshals of the world.
And when he had gone ahead of us,
so that my eyes followed him,
as my mind did his words,
there appeared to me the laden and lively branches
of another fruit tree, not very far away,
since only then had I turned that way.
I saw people beneath it raise their hands,
and cry I know not what toward the leaves,
like little children eager and vain,
who beg, and the one besought does not respond;
but to make their longing truly keen,
holds high what they desire, and does not hide it.
Then the people departed as if disabused;
and we came to the great tree, to the very one
that refuses so many prayers and tears.
Pass on beyond without drawing near there;
higher up is a tree that was bitten by Eve,
and this plant sprang from it.
Thus among the branches someone, I know not who, was saying;
wherefore Virgil and Statius and I, drawn close together,
went onward on the side that rises.
Remember, it said, the accursed ones
formed in the clouds, who, glutted,
made Theseus fight them with their double breasts;
and the Hebrews who showed themselves weak at drinking,
wherefore Gideon did not have them as companions
when toward Midian he descended the hills.
Thus, keeping close to one of the two edges,
we passed, hearing of sins of the throat
followed once by wretched gains.
Then, spreading out again along the lonely road,
a good thousand steps and more carried us onward,
each contemplating without a word.
What are you three alone so deep in thought about as you go?
a sudden voice said; at which I started,
as beasts do when frightened and skittish.
I raised my head to see who it was,
and never were there seen in a furnace
glass or metals so bright and red
as one I saw who said: If it pleases you
to climb up, here you must turn;
this way goes whoever wishes to go toward peace.
His appearance had taken my sight away:
wherefore I turned to follow my teachers,
like a man who goes by what he hears.
And as, herald of the dawn,
the May breeze stirs and breathes fragrance,
all steeped in herbs and flowers;
so I felt a wind strike the middle
of my forehead, and distinctly felt the feather move
that made the breeze smell of ambrosia.
And I heard it said: Blessed are those whom illumines
so much grace that love of taste
sends up no smoke of excess desire within their breasts,
hungering always for as much as is just.
