It was already the hour that turns the longing
of seafarers and softens the heart,
the day they have said to their dear friends: Farewell;
and that the new pilgrim with love
pricks, if he hears a bell from afar
that seems to mourn the day that dies;
when I began to let fall idle
my hearing, and to gaze at one of the souls,
risen, who asked with her hand to be heard.
She joined and raised both palms,
fixing her eyes toward the east,
as if saying to God: For nothing else do I care.
Before the close of light, to Thee, so devoutly
came from her mouth, with such sweet notes,
that it made me forget my very self.
And the others then, sweetly and devoutly,
followed her through the whole hymn,
having their eyes upon the spheres on high.
Sharpen here, reader, your eyes well upon the truth,
for the veil is now indeed so thin,
surely, that to pass within is easy.
I saw that noble host
then silently look upward,
as if waiting, pale and humble:
and I saw issue from on high, and descend,
two angels with two flaming swords,
truncated and deprived of their points.
Green, like little leaves just now born,
were their robes, which, by green feathers
beaten and fanned, they trailed behind them.
The one came to stand a little above us,
and the other descended on the opposite bank,
so that the people were held between them.
Well could I discern their fair heads;
but in their faces the eye lost its way,
like a faculty confounded by excess.
Both come from the bosom of Mary,
said Sordello, to guard the valley,
against the serpent that will come presently.
Whereupon I, who did not know by what path,
turned about, and pressed close,
all frozen, to the trusted shoulders.
And Sordello said: Let us now go down into the valley
among the great shades, and we shall speak to them:
most pleasing to them will it be to see you.
Only three steps, I think, did I descend,
and I was below, and saw one who gazed
at me alone, as if he wished to know me.
It was already the hour when the air grew dark,
but not so dark that between his eyes and mine
they did not make clear what it had hidden before.
Toward me he came, and I toward him came:
noble Judge Nino, how it pleased me,
when I saw you were not among the guilty!
No fair greeting between us was left unsaid;
then he asked: How long is it since you came
to the mountain's foot across the distant waters?
Oh, said I to him, through the places of sorrow
I came this morning, and I am in the first life,
though the other I gain by going thus.
And as soon as my answer was heard,
Sordello and he drew back,
like people suddenly bewildered.
The one to Virgil, and the other turned to one
who sat there, crying: Up, Corrado,
come see what God in His grace has willed.
Then, turned to me: By that singular gratitude
that you owe to Him who so conceals
His first why that there is no ford to it,
when you are beyond the wide waters,
tell my Giovanna to cry out for me
there where the innocent are answered.
I do not believe her mother loves me any longer,
since she exchanged the white veils,
for which, poor woman, she must yet long.
From her one may very easily understand
how long in woman the fire of love endures,
if eye or touch does not often kindle it.
Not so fair a tomb will it make her,
the viper that leads the Milanese into the field,
as the cock of Gallura would have made.
Thus he spoke, marked with the impress
in his countenance of that righteous zeal
that sets the heart aflame in due measure.
My eyes were eager to go ever heavenward,
just there where the stars are slowest,
as is a wheel nearer its axle.
And my guide: Son, what do you gaze at up there?
And I to him: At those three little torches,
with which the pole on this side is all ablaze.
Whereupon he to me: The four bright stars
that you saw this morning are low on the other side,
and these have risen where those were.
As he spoke, Sordello drew him to himself,
saying: See there our adversary;
and he pointed his finger so that he would look that way.
On that side where there is no defense
for the little valley, there was a snake,
perhaps such as gave Eve the bitter food.
Through the grass and the flowers came the evil streak,
turning from time to time its head toward its back,
licking it like a beast that smooths itself.
I did not see, and therefore cannot say,
how the celestial goshawks set out,
but I saw well that both were in motion.
Hearing the green wings cleave the air,
the serpent fled; the angels wheeled
back up to their posts, flying back abreast.
The shade that had drawn near the Judge
when he called, throughout that assault
did not for a moment cease to gaze at me.
May the lamp that leads you on high
find in your will as much wax
as is needed to reach the highest enamel,
it began, if any true news
of Valdimagra, or of parts nearby,
you know, tell it to me, who once was great there.
I was called Currado Malaspina:
I am not the elder, but from him I descended;
for my own I bore the love that here is refined.
Oh, said I to him, in your lands
I have never been; but where does one dwell
in all Europe that they are not known?
The fame that honors your house
proclaims the lords and proclaims the land,
so that even one who has not yet been there knows of them.
And I swear to you, so may I go above,
that your honored people do not strip themselves
of the merit of goodness and of the purse.
Custom and nature so favor your house
that, though the guilty head twists the world awry,
it alone goes straight, and scorns the evil path.
And he: Now go, for the sun shall not lie down again
seven times in the bed of the Ram,
which with all four feet it covers and bestrides,
before that courteous opinion
shall be nailed into the middle of your head
with greater nails than others' speech,
if the course of judgment is not stayed.
