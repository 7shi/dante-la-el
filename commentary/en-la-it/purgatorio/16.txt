Darkness of hell, and of night deprived
of every planet under a meager sky,
as darkened by cloud as it can be,
did not make so thick a veil for my sight
as that smoke which there covered us,
nor one of such rough hair to the touch;
for the eye could not bear to stay open:
wherefore my wise and faithful escort
drew close to me and offered me his shoulder.
Just as a blind man goes behind his guide,
so as not to lose his way or strike
against something that might hurt him or kill him,
so I went through the bitter and foul air,
listening to my leader, who kept saying
only: Take care not to be cut off from me.
I heard voices, and each seemed
to be praying, for peace and for mercy,
to the Lamb of God who takes away sins.
Only Agnus Dei were their opening words:
one utterance was in them all, and in one manner,
so that among them there seemed to be complete concord.
Are those spirits, master, that I hear?
said I. And he to me: You apprehend the truth,
and of wrath they go untying the knot.
O who are you, who cleave our smoke,
and speak of us just as if you
still divided time by calends?
Thus by one voice it was said.
Wherefore my master said: Answer,
and ask him whether from here one goes up.
And I: O creature, who cleanse yourself
to return beautiful to him who made me,
a marvel you will hear if you follow me.
I shall follow you as far as I am allowed,
he answered; and if smoke does not let us see,
hearing will keep us joined instead.
Then I began: With that wrapping
which death undoes I go upward,
and I came here through the anguish of hell.
And if God has enfolded me in his grace
so far that he wills that I see his court
in a manner wholly outside present custom,
do not hide from me who you were before death,
but tell me, and tell me if I go aright toward the pass;
and your words will be our guides.
A Lombard I was, and I was called Marco:
of the world I knew, and I loved that worth
at which everyone now has drawn his bow:
to mount upward you go the right way.
Thus he answered; and added: I pray you
to pray for me when you are above.
And I to him: By my faith I bind myself to you
to do what you ask of me; but I shall burst
within a doubt unless I disentangle myself from it.
Before it was single, and now it is made double
by your judgment, which makes certain to me
here and elsewhere that with which I couple it.
The world is indeed as wholly bereft
of every virtue as you tell me,
and pregnant with malice and covered with it:
but I pray you tell me the cause,
so that I may see it and show it to others;
for one places it in heaven, and another down here.
A deep sigh, which grief drove into an alas,
he first sent forth, and then began: Brother,
the world is blind, and you do indeed come from it.
You who live trace every cause
only up to heaven, just as if everything
it moved along with itself of necessity.
If this were so, in you would be destroyed
free will, and it would not be justice
for good to have joy, and for evil to have grief.
Heaven initiates your movements:
I do not say all; but suppose I say it,
light is given you to discern good and evil,
and free will, which, though it struggles,
endures in its first battles with heaven,
then conquers all, if it is well nourished.
To a greater force and to a better nature
you are freely subject, and that nature creates
the mind in you, which heaven has not in its keeping.
Therefore, if the present world goes astray,
in you is the cause; in you let it be sought,
and I shall now be your true informant about it.
There issues from the hand of him who gazes fondly on it
before it exists, like a little girl
who, weeping and laughing, plays the child,
the simple little soul, which knows nothing
save that, moved by a joyful Maker,
it willingly turns back to what delights it.
Of a small good it first tastes the savor;
there it is deceived, and after it runs,
if guide or rein does not turn its love aside.
Hence it was needful to set law as a rein;
it was needful to have a king who could discern
of the true city at least the tower.
The laws exist, but who lays a hand to them?
No one; for the shepherd who goes before
can chew the cud, but has not cloven hooves.
Therefore the people, seeing their guide
aim only at that good for which they are greedy,
feed on that and ask for nothing beyond.
Well can you see that bad guidance
is the cause that the world has grown wicked,
and not nature that is corrupt in you.
Rome, which made the world good, used
to have two suns, which the one road and the other
made see, both that of the world and that of God.
The one has quenched the other; and the sword is joined
with the pastoral staff; and the one together with the other
must of sheer necessity go ill;
for, joined, the one does not fear the other.
If you do not believe me, heed the ear of grain,
for every plant is known by its seed.
In the land which the Adige and the Po water,
worth and courtesy used to be found
before Frederick had his strife:
now safely may passage be made through there
by anyone who would refrain, for shame,
from speaking with the good or drawing near them.
Yet there are three old men there still, in whom rebukes
the ancient age the new, and it seems long to them
before God restores them to a better life:
Currado da Palazzo, and the good Gherardo,
and Guido da Castel, who is better named,
in the French manner, the simple Lombard.
Say henceforth that the Church of Rome,
by confounding in herself two governments,
falls in the mire, and fouls herself and her burden.
My Marco, I said, you reason well;
and now I discern why from the inheritance
the sons of Levi were exempted.
But which Gherardo is the one you, as a sample,
say remains of the people now extinct,
as a reproach to this savage age?
Either your speech deceives me, or it tests me,
he answered me; for, speaking Tuscan to me,
you seem to have heard nothing of the good Gherardo.
By no other surname do I know him,
unless I were to take it from his daughter Gaia.
God be with you, for I come no farther with you.
See the dawn that through the stream sends its rays
already whitening; and I must depart,
the angel is there, before I appear there.
Thus he turned back, and would hear me no more.
