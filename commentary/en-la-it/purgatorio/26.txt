While thus along the edge, one before the other,
we went, and often the good master
saying: Take heed; let it profit you that I make you wary;
the sun struck me on the right shoulder,
for already, beaming, all the west
it was changing from azure to a white aspect:
and with my shadow I made more intense
seem the flame; and to so great a sign alone
I saw many shades, as they went, give heed.
This was the cause that gave a beginning
to their speaking of me; and they began
to say: That one is no semblance of a body.
Then toward me, as near as they could come,
some came, always taking care
not to come out where they would not be burned.
O you who go, not for being slower,
but perhaps out of reverence, after the others,
answer me, who burn in thirst and in fire.
Not to me alone is your answer needful;
for all these have greater thirst for it
than for cold water does an Indian or an Ethiopian.
Tell us, how do you make of yourself a wall
against the sun, as if you had not yet
entered into the nets of death?
Thus spoke one of them to me, and I would have made myself
already known, had I not been attentive
to another new thing that appeared then;
for through the middle of the burning path
came people with their faces toward these,
who held me in suspense, gazing.
There I see on either side make haste
each shade, and kiss one another, one with one,
without stopping, content with a brief greeting.
Thus within their dark file
one ant touches muzzles with another,
perhaps to inquire of their way and their fortune.
As soon as they break off the friendly greeting,
before the first step there is past,
each strives to outshout the other;
the new people: Sodom and Gomorrah;
and the others: Into the cow went Pasiphaë,
that the young bull might run to her lust.
Then as cranes that to the Riphaean mountains
might fly in part, and in part toward the sands,
these shunning the frost, those the sun;
the one people goes away, the other comes,
and they return weeping to their first songs,
and to the cry that best befits them:
and they drew near me again, as before,
those very ones who had entreated me,
intent on listening, to judge by their looks.
I, who twice had seen their wish,
began: O souls assured
of having some day a state of peace,
there are not left behind, either unripe or ripe,
my limbs back there, but they are here with me
with their blood and with their joints.
This way upward I go that I may be blind no more:
a lady is above who wins us grace,
whereby I bear what is mortal through your world.
But so may your greatest desire, satisfied
soon become, so that the heaven may house you
that is full of love and spreads widest,
tell me, that I may yet cover pages with it,
who are you, and who is that throng
that goes away behind your backs?
No differently, dumbfounded, is he bewildered,
the mountaineer, and gazing falls mute,
when, in himself rough and wild, he enters the city,
than was each shade in its appearance;
but when they were rid of their amazement,
which in lofty hearts soon changes,
Blessed are you, who from our marches,
began again the one who first questioned me,
to die better, take experience aboard!
The people who do not come with us offended
in that for which once Caesar, in triumph,
heard himself called Queen, in derision;
therefore they depart crying Sodom,
reproaching themselves, as you have heard,
and aid the burning by feeling shame.
Our sin was hermaphrodite;
but because we did not keep human law,
following like beasts our appetite,
in reproach of ourselves, by us is recited,
when we part, the name of her
who made herself a beast in the beast-shaped timbers.
Now you know our acts, and of what we were guilty;
if perhaps by name you wish to know who we are,
there is no time to tell, nor would I know how.
I will indeed lessen your desire to know of me:
I am Guido Guinizelli; and already I purge myself,
for having truly repented before the end.
As in the grief of Lycurgus
two sons became on seeing their mother again,
so did I become, but I do not rise to such lengths,
when I hear him name himself, the father
of me and of those others, my betters, who ever
used rhymes of love sweet and graceful:
and without hearing or speaking, pensive I went
a long while gazing at him,
nor, because of the fire, did I draw any nearer in that direction.
When I had had my fill of gazing,
I offered myself wholly, ready for his service,
with the affirmation that makes another believe.
And he to me: You leave such a trace,
by what I hear, in me, and one so clear,
that Lethe cannot take it away nor make it dim.
But if your words now swore truly,
tell me, what is the cause why you show
in speech and in look that you hold me dear?
And I to him: Those sweet sayings of yours,
which, as long as modern usage lasts,
will make dear even their inks.
O brother, he said, this one whom I single out for you
with my finger, and he pointed to a spirit ahead,
was a better craftsman of the mother tongue.
In verses of love and prose romances
he surpassed all; let the fools talk
who believe that the man of Limousin excels him.
To report rather than to truth they turn their faces,
and thus they fix their opinion
before art or reason gets a hearing from them.
So did many of old with Guittone,
from cry to cry giving him alone the praise,
until truth prevailed over it with more people.
Now, if you have so ample a privilege
that you are permitted to go to the cloister
in which Christ is abbot of the brotherhood,
say to him for me an Our Father,
as much as is needful to us of this world,
where the power to sin is no longer ours.
Then, perhaps to give place to another after him,
whom he had near, he vanished through the fire,
as through water a fish going to the bottom.
I moved a little forward toward the one pointed out,
and said that for his name my desire
was preparing a place of welcome.
He began freely to say:
So pleasing to me is your courteous request
that I neither can nor wish to hide myself from you.
I am Arnaut, who weep and go singing;
contrite I see the folly that is past,
and go rejoicing in the joy I hope for ahead.
Now I beg you by that power
which guides you to the summit of the stair,
remember in due time my sorrow.
Then he hid himself in the fire that refines them.
