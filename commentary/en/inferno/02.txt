The day was departing, and the darkening air
was releasing the living creatures that are on earth
from their labors; and I alone
was preparing myself to sustain the warfare
both of the journey and of pity,
which memory that does not err will recount.
O Muses, O high genius, now help me;
O memory that wrote down what I saw,
here your nobility will be revealed.
I began: “Poet who guide me,
look whether my strength is sufficient,
before you entrust me to the arduous passage.
You say that the father of Silvius,
still corruptible, to the immortal
world went, and did so in the flesh.
Therefore, if the adversary of every evil
was gracious to him, considering the lofty outcome
that was to issue from him, and who and what he was,
it does not seem unfitting to a person of understanding;
for he was, of nurturing Rome and of its empire,
in the Empyrean heaven chosen as father:
which city and which empire, to speak the truth,
were established for the holy place
where sits the successor of the great Peter.
Through this journey for which you give him praise,
he learned things that were the cause
of his victory and of the papal mantle.
Then the Vessel of Election went there,
to bring back from there support for that faith
which is the beginning of the way of salvation.
But I, why come there? Or who permits it?
I am not Aeneas, I am not Paul;
neither I nor anyone else believes me worthy of this.
Therefore, if I give myself up to coming,
I fear that the journey may be folly.
You are wise; you understand better than I can say”.
And like one who no longer wills what he once willed
and through new thoughts changes his purpose,
so that he withdraws entirely from his undertaking,
so I became on that dark slope,
because, thinking, I wore away the resolve
that at the outset had been so eager.
“If I have rightly understood your words”,
replied the shade of that magnanimous one,
“your soul is afflicted by cowardice;
which many times obstructs a person
so that it turns him away from an honorable undertaking,
as mistaken seeing turns a beast when it shies.
So that you may free yourself from this fear,
I will tell you why I came and what I heard
at the first moment that I felt pity for you.
I was among those who are suspended,
and a lady called me, blessed and beautiful,
such that I asked her to command me.
Her eyes shone more than the star;
and she began to speak to me, gentle and calm,
with an angelic voice, in her own words:
‘O gracious Mantuan soul,
whose fame still endures in the world,
and will endure as long as the world lasts,
my friend, and not fortune's,
on the deserted slope is hindered
so in his journey that he has turned back for fear;
and I fear that he is already so lost
that I have risen too late to his aid,
from what I have heard of him in heaven.
Now go, and with your eloquent words
and with whatever is needed for his deliverance,
help him so that I may be comforted by it.
I am Beatrice, who make you go;
I come from the place where I desire to return;
Love moved me, which makes me speak.
When I am before my Lord,
I will often praise you to him’.
Then she fell silent, and afterward I began:
‘O lady of virtue, through whom alone
the human species surpasses everything contained
within that heaven whose circles are smallest,
so pleasing to me is your command,
that obedience, were it already accomplished, seems late to me;
you need only reveal your wish to me.
But tell me the reason you do not refrain
from descending here below into this center
from the spacious place where you burn to return’.
‘Since you wish to know so deeply,
I will tell you briefly’, she answered me,
‘why I do not fear to come in here.
One should fear only those things
that have the power to do one harm;
not the others, for they are not fearsome.
I am made by God, through his grace, such,
that your misery does not touch me,
nor does a flame of this burning assail me.
There is a gracious lady in heaven who grieves
over this impediment to which I send you,
so that she breaks stern judgment above.
She summoned Lucia with her request
and said:—Now your faithful one has need
of you, and I commend him to you—.
Lucia, enemy of every cruel person,
set forth and came to the place where I was,
where I sat with ancient Rachel.
She said: Beatrice, true praise of God,
why do you not help the one who loved you so much,
who for you came forth from the common crowd?
Do you not hear the anguish of his weeping,
do you not see the death that assails him
on the torrent over which the sea has no advantage?—.
Never in the world were people so swift
to seek their good or to flee their harm,
as I, after such words were spoken,
came down here from my blessed seat,
trusting in your honorable speech,
which honors you and those who have heard it’.
After she had spoken these things to me,
she turned her shining eyes, weeping,
by which she made me come all the more swiftly.
And I came to you as she wished:
I drew you away from before that beast
that denied you the short way up the lovely mountain.
What is it, then? Why, why do you linger,
why do you harbor so much cowardice in your heart,
why do you lack courage and confidence,
since three such blessed ladies
care for you in the court of heaven,
and my words promise you so much good?”.
As little flowers, by the night's frost
bent and closed, after the sun whitens them,
straighten themselves, all open, upon their stems,
so I became in my exhausted strength,
and so much good courage rushed into my heart,
that I began like a person made bold:
“Oh, how compassionate she who came to my aid!
and how gracious you who promptly obeyed
the true words that she addressed to you!
You have disposed my heart with desire
so toward coming, through your words,
that I have returned to my first resolve.
Now go, for one single will belongs to us both:
you my guide, you my lord and you my master”.
So I said to him; and after he had set forth,
I entered upon the arduous and wooded way.
