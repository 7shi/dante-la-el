There is a place in Hell called Malebolge,
entirely of stone of an iron color,
like the encircling wall that surrounds it.
In the very middle of the evil field
gapes a well, very wide and deep,
whose structure I shall describe in its proper place.
That belt which therefore remains is circular
between the well and the foot of the high, hard cliff,
and has its floor divided into ten valleys.
As, where for the defense of the walls
many moats encircle castles,
the ground in which they lie presents a pattern,
such an image did those here present;
and as at such fortresses from their thresholds
there are little bridges to the outer bank,
so from the foot of the cliff rocky ridges
ran out, cutting across the banks and ditches
as far as the well that cuts them off and gathers them.
In this place, shaken off the back
of Geryon, we found ourselves; and the poet
kept to the left, and I moved on behind him.
On the right hand I saw new misery,
new torment and new scourgers,
with which the first bolgia was filled.
At the bottom the sinners were naked;
on this side of the middle they came toward our faces,
on the other, along with us, but with longer strides,
as the Romans, because of the great multitude,
in the year of the Jubilee, upon the bridge,
have adopted a way of getting the people across,
so that on one side all have their faces
toward the castle and go to Saint Peter's,
on the other side they go toward the hill.
On this side, on that side, upon the dark rock
I saw horned demons with great whips,
who beat them cruelly from behind.
Ah, how they made them lift their heels
at the first blows! Indeed, no one
waited for the second or the third.
While I was walking, my eyes upon one
fell; and I immediately said:
“I am not unfamiliar with the sight of this man”.
So I planted my feet to make him out;
and the gentle guide stopped with me,
and agreed that I might go back a little.
And that scourged one thought to hide himself
by lowering his face; but it did him little good,
for I said: “O you who cast your eyes upon the ground,
if the features you bear are not misleading,
you are Venedico Caccianemico.
But what brings you to such stinging sauces?”.
And he to me: “Unwillingly I tell it;
but your clear speech compels me,
making me remember the former world.
I was the one who Ghisolabella
led to fulfill the marquis's desire,
however the disgraceful story may be told.
And I am not the only Bolognese who weeps here;
indeed, this place is so full of them,
that so many tongues are not now trained
to say ‘sipa’ between the Savena and the Reno;
and if you want assurance or testimony of this,
call to mind our avaricious hearts”.
As he spoke thus, a demon struck him
with his lash, and said: “Away,
pimp! Here there are no women to coin money from”.
I rejoined my escort;
then with a few steps we came
to where a rocky ridge jutted from the bank.
We climbed it quite easily;
and turning right along its jagged rock,
we left those eternal circles.
When we were where it is hollow
beneath to let the scourged ones pass,
the guide said: “Stand still, and let strike
upon you the faces of these other ill-born souls,
whose faces you have not yet seen
because they have been walking along with us”.
From the ancient bridge we watched the troop
that came toward us from the other side,
and that the whip likewise drives onward.
And the good master, without my asking,
he said to me: “Look at that great one who comes,
and despite his pain does not seem to shed tears:
how much royal bearing he still retains!
That is Jason, who through courage and intelligence
deprived the Colchians of the ram.
He passed through the island of Lemnos
after the bold, merciless women
had put all their males to death.
There, with gestures and polished words
he deceived Hypsipyle, the young woman
who earlier had deceived all the others.
He left her there, pregnant, all alone;
such guilt condemns him to such torment;
and vengeance is also exacted for Medea.
With him goes whoever deceives in this way;
and let this suffice about the first valley
to know, and about those it grips in its jaws”.
Already we were where the narrow path
crosses the second embankment,
and makes of it a support for another arch.
From there we heard people moaning
in the next bolgia and snorting with their muzzles,
and striking themselves with their palms.
The banks were crusted with a mold,
from the breath below that cakes upon them,
which fought with the eyes and with the nose.
The bottom is so deep that there is no suitable
place to see it without climbing to the crest
of the arch, where the rocky ridge rises highest.
We came there; and from there, down in the ditch,
I saw people plunged into excrement
that seemed to have come from human privies.
And while I search down there with my eyes,
I saw one with his head so soiled with shit,
that you could not tell if he was layman or cleric.
He shouted at me: “Why are you so eager
to look at me more than at the other filthy ones?”.
And I to him: “Because, if I remember rightly,
I have seen you before with your hair dry,
and you are Alessio Interminei of Lucca:
therefore I eye you more than all the others”.
And he then, beating his pate:
“Down here the flatteries have submerged me
with which I never had my tongue sated”.
After this the guide, “See that you push”,
he said to me, “your gaze a little farther forward,
so that with your eyes you clearly reach the face
of that filthy, disheveled wench
who scratches herself there with shitty nails,
and now squats and now stands upright.
She is Thais, the prostitute who replied
to her lover when he said “Have I thanks
great with you?”: “Rather, marvelous ones!”.
And with this let our eyes be satisfied”.
