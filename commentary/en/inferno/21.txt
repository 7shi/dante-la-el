Thus from bridge to bridge, speaking of other things
that my comedy does not care to sing,
we came; and we were on the crest, when
we stopped to see the other cleft
of Malebolge and the other vain laments;
and I saw it was marvellously dark.
As in the arsenal of the Venetians
the clinging pitch boils in winter
to coat anew their unsound ships,
for they cannot sail—instead,
one builds his ship anew and another caulks
the ribs of one that has made more voyages;
one hammers at the bow and another at the stern;
others make oars and others twist ropes;
one patches the topsail and the mizzen—:
so, not through fire but through divine art,
a thick pitch boiled down there,
which coated the bank with stickiness on every side.
I could see it, but I could see in it
nothing except the bubbles that the boiling raised,
and all of it swell, and settle back compressed.
While I was gazing intently down there,
my leader, saying “Look, look!”,
drew me to him from the place where I stood.
Then I turned like a man who is eager
to see what he must flee
and whose strength sudden fear takes away,
who, though looking, does not delay his departure:
and I saw behind us a black devil
coming at a run up the rocky ridge.
Ah, how fierce he was in appearance!
and how savage he seemed to me in his bearing,
with wings spread and light upon his feet!
His shoulder, which was sharp and high,
a sinner burdened with both his haunches,
and he held the sinews of the feet in his grip.
From our bridge he said: “O Malebranche,
here is one of the elders of Saint Zita!
Put him under, for I am going back for more
to that city, which is well supplied with them:
everyone there is a barrator, except Bonturo;
out of no, for money, there they make yes”.
He flung him down there, and along the hard ridge
turned back; and never was a mastiff unleashed
with such haste to pursue a thief.
That one plunged in, and came back up coated;
but the demons who had the bridge for cover,
they shouted: “Here the Holy Face has no place!
here one swims differently than in the Serchio!
So, if you do not want any of our scratches,
do not jut out above the pitch”.
Then they bit into him with more than a hundred hooks,
they said: “Covered you must dance here,
so that, if you can, you may grab secretly”.
No differently do cooks make their scullions
plunge into the middle of the cauldron
the meat with hooks, so that it does not float.
The good master, “So that it may not be seen
that you are here”, said to me, “crouch down
behind a crag, so that you may have some shelter;
and whatever harm may be done to me,
do not be afraid, for I know how things stand,
since once before I was in such a fray”.
Then he passed beyond the end of the bridge;
and when he reached the sixth bank,
he had need of a fearless face.
With that fury and that uproar
with which dogs rush at a poor man
who suddenly begs where he stops,
they came out from beneath the little bridge,
and turned all their hooks against him;
but he cried: “Let none of you be vicious!
Before your hook takes hold of me,
let one of you come forward to hear me,
and then let him consider whether to hook me”.
They all cried: “Let Malacoda go!”;
so one moved—and the others stood still—
and came to him, saying: “What good does it do him?”.
“Do you think, Malacoda, that you see me here
having come”, said my master,
“safe already from all your hindrances,
without divine will and favourable destiny?
Let us go, for in heaven it is willed
that I show another this wild path”.
Then his pride fell so low,
that he let his hook drop at his feet,
and said to the others: “Now let him not be struck”.
And my leader to me: “O you who sit
among the crags of the bridge, crouching low,
now return to me in safety”.
So I moved and came to him quickly;
and the devils all came forward,
so that I feared they would not keep their pact;
thus I once saw the foot soldiers fear
who were leaving Caprona under agreed terms,
seeing themselves among so many enemies.
I pressed close with my whole body
beside my leader, and did not turn my eyes
from their appearance, which was not good.
They lowered their hooks, and “Do you want me to touch him”,
one said to another, “on the rump?”.
And they answered: “Yes, see that you give it to him”.
But that demon who was holding converse
with my leader turned round at once
and said: “Steady, steady, Scarmiglione!”.
Then he said to us: “Further along this
rocky ridge you cannot go, because there lies
all shattered at the bottom the sixth arch.
And if going onward still pleases you,
make your way along this rocky bank;
nearby is another ridge that provides a way.
Yesterday, five hours later than this hour,
one thousand two hundred and sixty-six
years were completed since the road here was broken.
I am sending some of my fellows that way
to see whether anyone comes out to air himself;
go with them, for they will not be wicked”.
“Step forward, Alichino, and Calcabrina”,
he began to say, “and you, Cagnazzo;
and let Barbariccia lead the ten.
Let Libicocco come forward, and Draghignazzo,
tusked Ciriatto and Graffiacane
and Farfarello and mad Rubicante.
Search around the boiling birdlime;
let these two be safe as far as the other ridge
that passes unbroken over the pits”.
“Alas, master, what is this I see?”,
I said, “oh, let us go alone without an escort,
if you know the way; for myself, I do not ask for one.
If you are as watchful as you usually are,
do you not see that they grind their teeth
and with their brows threaten us with pain?”.
And he to me: “I do not want you to be afraid;
just let them grind their teeth as they please,
for they do that for the suffering boiled ones”.
They turned along the left bank;
but first each had held his tongue
between his teeth, toward their leader, as a signal;
and he had made a trumpet of his arse.
