Already the flame was upright and still
from speaking no more, and already was leaving us
with the leave of the gentle poet,
when another, which came behind it,
made us turn our eyes to its tip
through a confused sound that issued from it.
Like the Sicilian bull that first bellowed
with the lament of that man, and this was just,
who had fashioned it with his file,
bellowed with the voice of the tormented man,
so that, although it was of bronze,
it seemed itself pierced by pain;
so, through having neither passage nor opening
at first within the fire, into its language
the sorrowful words were transformed.
But after they had found their way
up through the tip, giving it that flicker
which the tongue had given in their passage,
we heard it say: “O you to whom I direct
my voice, and who just now spoke Lombard,
saying ‘Now go your way, I urge you no further’,
though I may have arrived somewhat late,
let it not trouble you to stay and speak with me;
see how it does not trouble me, though I burn!
If only just now into this blind world
you have fallen from that sweet
Latin land whence I bring all my guilt,
tell me whether the Romagnols have peace or war;
for I was of the mountains there between Urbino
and the ridge from which the Tiber springs”.
I was still intent and bending downward,
when my leader nudged me in the side,
saying: “You speak; this one is Italian”.
And I, who already had my answer ready,
without delay began to speak:
“O soul who are hidden down there,
Your Romagna is not, and never was,
without war in the hearts of its tyrants;
but openly I left none there just now.
Ravenna stands as it has stood for many years:
the eagle of Polenta broods over it,
so that it covers Cervia with its wings.
The city that once endured the long ordeal
and made a bloody heap of Frenchmen,
finds itself beneath the green claws.
And the old mastiff and the new one from Verrucchio,
who dealt cruelly with Montagna,
where they are accustomed, make augers of their teeth.
The cities of the Lamone and the Santerno
the young lion of the white lair rules,
who changes sides from summer to winter.
And that city whose flank the Savio bathes,
just as it lies between the plain and the mountain,
lives between tyranny and a free state.
Now who you are, I pray you tell us;
do not be harder than another has been,
so may your name hold its own in the world”.
After the fire had roared awhile
in its own fashion, it moved its sharp tip
this way and that, and then gave forth this breath:
“If I believed that my answer were
to one who would ever return to the world,
this flame would remain without further stirrings;
but because never from this depth
has anyone returned alive, if what I hear is true,
without fear of infamy I answer you.
I was a man of arms, and then a wearer of the cord,
believing, thus girded, to make amends;
and surely my belief would have been fulfilled,
had it not been for the great priest, whom evil take!,
who put me back into my former sins;
and how and why, I want you to understand me.
While I was a form of bones and flesh
that my mother gave me, my deeds
were not those of a lion, but of a fox.
The cunning tricks and the hidden ways
I knew them all, and so practiced their art
that the sound went forth to the ends of the earth.
When I saw myself come to that part
of my life when everyone should
lower the sails and gather in the ropes,
what formerly pleased me then displeased me,
and repentant and confessed, I entered religious life;
ah, wretched and weary me! and it would have availed.
The prince of the new Pharisees,
being at war near the Lateran,
and not with Saracens or with Jews,
for every enemy of his was Christian,
and none had been at the conquest of Acre
nor a merchant in the Sultan's land,
neither his supreme office nor holy orders
did he respect in himself, nor in me that cord
which used to make those girded with it leaner.
But as Constantine sought Sylvester
from within Soracte to heal him of leprosy,
so this man sought me as a master
to heal him of his proud fever;
he asked me for counsel, and I kept silent
because his words seemed drunken.
Then he spoke again: ‘Let your heart not fear;
I absolve you even now, and you teach me to act
so that I may raze Palestrina to the ground.
Heaven I can lock and unlock,
as you know; for that purpose there are the two keys
which my predecessor did not hold dear”.
Then the weighty arguments drove me
to where keeping silent seemed to me the worse,
and I said: ‘Father, since you wash me clean
of that sin into which I must now fall,
a long promise with a short fulfillment
will make you triumph upon the lofty throne”.
Francis came afterward, when I was dead,
for me; but one of the black cherubim
said to him: ‘Do not take him: do not wrong me.
He must come down among my servants
because he gave the fraudulent counsel,
since which I have been at his heels;
for one cannot absolve someone who does not repent,
nor can one repent and will at the same time,
because of the contradiction that does not permit it”.
Oh, wretched me! how I started
when he seized me, saying: ‘Perhaps
you did not think that I was a logician!’.
He carried me to Minos; and Minos wound
his tail eight times around his hard back;
and after he bit it in great rage,
he said: “This is one of the guilty of the thieving fire”;
and so I am lost there where you see,
and, thus clothed, I go my way lamenting”.
When he had thus finished speaking,
the flame departed in sorrow,
twisting and tossing its sharp horn.
We passed onward, both I and my leader,
up along the rocky ridge as far as the next arch
that spans the ditch where the penalty is paid
by those who incur guilt through sowing division.
