‘Through me one goes into the sorrowful city,
through me one goes into eternal pain,
through me one goes among the lost people.
Justice moved my exalted maker;
divine power made me,
highest wisdom and primal love.
Before me no things were created
except eternal ones, and I endure eternally.
Abandon every hope, you who enter’.
These words of dark color
I saw written at the top of a gate;
at which I said: “Master, their meaning is hard for me”.
And he to me, like one who understands:
“Here one must leave behind every doubt;
every cowardice must here be dead.
We have come to the place where I told you
that you would see the sorrowful people
who have lost the good of the intellect”.
And after he had placed his hand on mine
with a cheerful face, from which I took comfort,
he led me within, among the hidden things.
There sighs, weeping, and loud cries of anguish
resounded through the starless air,
so that at first I wept at them.
Different tongues, horrible speech,
words of pain, accents of anger,
voices loud and faint, and the sound of hands with them
made a tumult, which whirls
forever in that air darkened beyond time,
like sand when a whirlwind blows.
And I, whose head was bound about with confusion,
said: “Master, what is this that I hear?
and what people are these who seem so overcome by pain?”.
And he to me: “This miserable manner
is kept by the wretched souls of those
who lived without infamy and without praise.
They are mingled with that cowardly company
of the angels who were not rebellious
nor were faithful to God, but were for themselves.
The heavens drive them out so as not to be less beautiful,
nor does the depth of hell receive them,
for the wicked would gain some glory from them”.
And I: “Master, what is so grievous
to them that it makes them lament so loudly?”.
He answered: “I will tell you very briefly.
These have no hope of death,
and their blind life is so low
that they are envious of every other lot.
The world allows no fame of them to remain;
mercy and justice disdain them:
let us not speak of them, but look and pass on”.
And I, looking again, saw a banner
that ran circling so swiftly,
that it seemed to me unworthy of any rest;
and behind it came so long a train
of people, that I would not have believed
that death had undone so many.
After I had recognized some among them,
I saw and knew the shade of him
who through cowardice made the great refusal.
At once I understood and was certain
that this was the company of the cowardly,
displeasing to God and to his enemies.
These wretches, who never were alive,
were naked and fiercely goaded
by horseflies and wasps that were there.
They streaked their faces with blood,
which, mingled with tears, at their feet
was gathered by loathsome worms.
And when I turned to look farther,
I saw people on the bank of a great river;
at which I said: “Master, now grant me
that I may know who they are, and what custom
makes them seem so ready to cross,
as I discern by the faint light”.
And he to me: “These things will be made known to you
when we bring our steps to a halt
on the sorrowful bank of Acheron”.
Then with my eyes ashamed and lowered,
fearing lest my words should trouble him,
until the river I refrained from speaking.
And behold, coming toward us in a boat,
an old man, white with ancient hair,
crying: “Woe to you, wicked souls!
Never hope to see heaven:
I come to lead you to the other bank
into eternal darkness, into heat and frost.
And you who are standing there, living soul,
depart from these who are dead”.
But when he saw that I did not depart,
he said: “By another way, by other ports
you will come to shore, not here, to cross:
a lighter vessel must carry you”.
And my guide to him: “Charon, do not torment yourself:
it is so willed there where there is power to do
whatever is willed, and ask no more”.
Then the shaggy cheeks fell silent
of the boatman of the livid marsh,
who had wheels of flame around his eyes.
But those souls, who were weary and naked,
changed color and chattered their teeth,
as soon as they heard the cruel words.
They cursed God and their parents,
the human race and the place and the time and the seed
of their begetting and of their births.
Then they all drew together,
weeping bitterly, to the evil bank
that awaits every man who does not fear God.
Charon the demon, with eyes of burning coals,
beckoning to them, gathers them all;
he strikes with his oar whoever lingers.
As in autumn the leaves fall
one after another, until the branch
sees upon the ground all its stripped foliage,
so too the evil seed of Adam
cast themselves from that shore one by one,
at signals, like a bird at its call.
Thus they go over the dark water,
and before they have disembarked on the other side,
a new company gathers again on this side.
“My son”, said the courteous master,
“those who die in the wrath of God
all gather here from every land;
and they are ready to cross the river,
because divine justice spurs them,
so that fear turns into desire.
No good soul ever crosses here;
and therefore, if Charon complains of you,
you can well know now what his words mean”.
When this was finished, the dark plain
trembled so violently, that from the terror
the memory still bathes me in sweat.
The tearful earth gave forth a wind,
which flashed a crimson light
which overwhelmed every one of my senses;
and I fell like a man whom sleep overtakes.
