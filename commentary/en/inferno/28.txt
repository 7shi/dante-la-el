Who could ever, even with unfettered words,
tell fully of the blood and of the wounds
that I now saw, though telling it many times?
Every tongue would certainly fall short
because of our speech and because of the mind
which have little capacity to encompass so much.
If all the people were gathered again
who once, upon the fortune-tossed land
of Apulia, grieved for their own blood
because of the Trojans and because of the long war
that made so high a heap of spoils of rings,
as Livy writes, who does not err,
together with those who felt the pain of blows
for opposing Robert Guiscard;
and the others whose bones are still gathered
at Ceprano, where false was
every Apulian, and there at Tagliacozzo,
where old Alardo won without arms;
and one his pierced limb and another his severed one
were to show, it would be nothing toward equalling
the foul condition of the ninth bolgia.
Indeed a barrel, through losing a middle or side stave,
was not so opened as one I saw,
split from the chin down to where one farts.
Between his legs his entrails hung;
his inner organs showed, and the wretched sack
that makes shit of what is swallowed.
While I fasten all my gaze upon him,
he looked at me and with his hands opened his breast,
saying: “Now see how I tear myself apart!
see how mutilated Muhammad is!
Before me Ali goes weeping,
split in the face from the chin to the forelock.
And all the others whom you see here,
sowers of discord and of schism
were while alive, and therefore are split like this.
A devil is here behind us who trims us
so cruelly, to the edge of his sword
putting each of this company again,
when we have gone around the sorrowful road;
because the wounds have closed again
before anyone goes before him again.
But who are you who gaze upon the crag,
perhaps to delay going to the punishment
that is adjudged upon your accusations?”
“Neither has death yet reached him, nor does guilt lead him”,
replied my master, “to torment him;
but to give him full experience,
I, who am dead, must lead him
through hell down here from circle to circle;
and this is as true as that I speak to you.”
There were more than a hundred who, when they heard it,
stopped in the ditch to look at me
in wonder, forgetting their torment.
“Now tell Fra Dolcino, then, to arm himself,
you who perhaps will see the sun before long,
if he does not wish to follow me here soon,
so well with provisions, that a blockade of snow
may not bring victory to the Novarese,
which otherwise would not be easy to gain.”
After lifting one foot to go away,
Muhammad spoke these words to me;
then he stretched it to the ground to depart.
Another, who had his throat pierced
and his nose cut off just below the eyebrows,
and had only a single ear,
having stopped to gaze in wonder
with the others, before the others opened his windpipe,
which was red on the outside all around,
and said: “O you whom guilt does not condemn
and whom I saw up in the Italian land,
unless too great a likeness deceives me,
remember Pier da Medicina,
if ever you return to see the sweet plain
that slopes down from Vercelli to Marcabò.
And let the two best men of Fano know,
Messer Guido and also Angiolello,
that, if foresight here is not in vain,
they will be thrown out of their vessel
and drowned with weights near Cattolica
through the treachery of a wicked tyrant.
Between the islands of Cyprus and Majorca
Neptune never saw so great a crime,
not by pirates, not by the people of Greece.
That traitor who sees with only one,
and holds the city that someone here with me
would wish never to have seen,
will have them come to a conference with him;
then he will act so that against the wind of Focara
they will have no need of vow or prayer.”
And I to him: “Show me and make clear,
if you wish me to carry news of you above,
who is the one for whom the sight was bitter.”
Then he placed his hand upon the jaw
of one of his companions and opened his mouth,
crying: “This is he, and he does not speak.
This man, banished, drowned the doubt
in Caesar, declaring that one prepared
always suffered harm through waiting.”
Oh how dismayed he seemed to me
with his tongue cut away in his throat,
Curio, who had been so bold in speaking!
And one who had both hands cut off,
raising his stumps through the murky air,
so that the blood made his face foul,
cried: “You will also remember Mosca,
who said, alas!, “A thing done has an end”,
which was an evil seed for the Tuscan people.”
And I added to him: “And death to your lineage”;
because of which he, piling grief upon grief,
went away like a sorrowful and maddened person.
But I remained to watch the throng,
and saw a thing such that I would be afraid,
without further proof, even to recount it;
except that conscience reassures me,
the good companion that gives a person courage
under the armor of feeling itself pure.
I certainly saw, and still seem to see it,
a trunk without a head walking just as
the others of the sorrowful flock were walking;
and it held the severed head by the hair,
dangling from its hand like a lantern:
and that head looked at us and said: “Oh me!”
Of himself he made a lamp for himself,
and they were two in one and one in two;
how this can be, he knows who governs thus.
When he was directly at the foot of the bridge,
he raised his arm high with the whole head
to bring his words closer to us,
which were: “Now see the grievous punishment,
you who, breathing, go looking at the dead:
see if any is as great as this.
And so that you may carry news of me,
know that I am Bertran de Born, the one
who gave the young king the evil counsels.
I made the father and the son rebels against each other;
Ahithophel did no more with Absalom
and David through his wicked promptings.
Because I divided persons so closely joined,
divided I carry my brain, alas!,
from its source, which is in this trunk.
Thus is retribution observed in me.”
