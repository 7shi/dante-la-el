O Simon Magus, O wretched followers
who the things of God, which to goodness
ought to be wedded, and you, rapacious ones,
for gold and for silver prostitute,
now must the trumpet sound for you,
because you are in the third bolgia.
Already, at the next tomb, we had
climbed to that part of the crag
that hangs directly over the middle of the ditch.
O supreme wisdom, how great is the art
that you display in heaven, on earth, and in the evil world,
and how justly your power distributes!
I saw along the sides and on the bottom
the livid stone full of holes,
all of one width, and each was round.
They seemed to me neither less spacious nor greater
than those that are in my beautiful San Giovanni,
made as places for those who baptize;
one of which, not many years ago,
I broke for someone who was drowning inside it:
and let this be a seal that undeceives everyone.
Out of the mouth of each there protruded
a sinner's feet and the legs
up to the thick part, and the rest remained inside.
Both soles were aflame on every one of them;
so that the joints writhed so violently,
that they would have snapped ropes and cords.
As the flaming of greasy things is wont
to move only over the outermost surface,
so it was there from the heels to the toes.
“Who is that one, master, who torments himself
writhing more than his other companions,”
I said, “and whom a redder flame sucks at?”
And he to me: “If you wish me to carry you
down there by that bank which slopes more gently,
from him you will learn of himself and of his wrongs.”
And I: “As much is pleasing to me as pleases you:
you are master, and you know that I do not depart
from your will, and you know what is left unspoken.”
Then we came onto the fourth embankment;
we turned and descended to the left
down there into the pierced and narrow bottom.
The good master still from his hip
did not set me down until he brought me to the hole
of the one who was lamenting with his shanks.
“O whoever you are who hold your upper parts below,
sorrowful soul planted like a stake,”
I began to say, “if you can, speak.”
I stood like the friar who shrives
the treacherous assassin, who, after being fixed in place,
calls him back so that death is delayed.
And he cried: “Are you already standing there,
are you already standing there, Boniface?
By several years the writing lied to me.
Are you so soon sated with that wealth
for which you did not fear to take by deceit
the beautiful lady, and then to ravage her?”
I became like those who stand,
through not understanding what is answered them,
as though abashed, and do not know how to reply.
Then Virgil said: “Tell him at once:
‘I am not he, I am not he whom you believe’”;
and I answered as I was instructed.
At this the spirit twisted both his feet;
then, sighing and with a weeping voice,
he said to me: “Then what do you ask of me?
If knowing who I am concerns you so much,
that you have therefore come down the bank,
know that I was clothed in the great mantle;
and truly I was a son of the she-bear,
so greedy to advance the bear cubs,
that above I put wealth, and here myself, into a purse.
Beneath my head the others are dragged
who preceded me in practicing simony,
flattened within the fissures of the stone.
Down there I too shall fall when
the one whom I believed you to be arrives,
when I made the sudden inquiry.
But longer already is the time that I have roasted my feet
and that I have been thus upside down,
than he will stay planted with his feet red:
for after him will come, of fouler deeds,
from the direction of the west, a lawless shepherd,
such that he must cover both him and me.
He will be a new Jason, of whom one reads
in Maccabees; and as yielding to that one was
his king, so to this one will be he who rules France.”
I do not know if I was too rash here,
for I answered him in this strain:
“Ah, now tell me: how much treasure was required
by Our Lord first from Saint Peter
before he placed the keys in his keeping?
Certainly he asked only “Follow me”.
Neither Peter nor the others took from Matthias
gold or silver, when he was chosen by lot
for the place that the wicked soul lost.
Therefore stay where you are, for you are well punished;
and guard well the ill-gotten money
that made you bold against Charles.
And were it not that I am still forbidden it
by reverence for the supreme keys
that you held in the joyful life,
I would use still harsher words;
for your avarice brings misery to the world,
trampling the good and raising up the wicked.
Of you shepherds the Evangelist took notice,
when she who sits upon the waters
was seen by him committing whoredom with kings;
she who was born with the seven heads,
and from the ten horns received support,
so long as virtue pleased her husband.
You have made yourselves a god of gold and silver;
and what other difference is there between you and the idolater,
except that he worships one, and you worship a hundred?
Ah, Constantine, of how much evil was mother,
not your conversion, but that dowry
which the first wealthy father received from you!”
And while I sang him such notes,
whether anger or conscience was biting him,
he kicked violently with both soles.
I firmly believe that it pleased my guide,
with so contented a face he listened throughout
to the sound of the true words spoken.
Therefore he took me with both arms;
and after he had drawn me wholly up to his breast,
he climbed back by the way he had descended.
Nor did he tire of holding me clasped to him,
until he carried me onto the crown of the arch
that forms a passage from the fourth to the fifth embankment.
There he gently set down his burden,
gently, on the rugged and steep crag
that would be a difficult passage for goats.
From there another valley was revealed to me.
