Upon the edge of a high bank
that great broken rocks formed in a circle,
we came above a more cruel throng;
and there, because of the horrible excess
of the stench that the deep abyss casts up,
we drew back close to a lid
of a great tomb, where I saw an inscription
that said: ‘Pope Anastasius I hold,
whom Photinus drew from the straight way’.
“Our descent must be slow,
so that first our sense may grow a little accustomed
to the foul breath; and then we shall not mind it”.
Thus the master; and I, “Some compensation”,
I said to him, “find, so that time may not pass
wasted”. And he: “You see that I am thinking of that”.
“My son, within those rocks”,
he then began to say, “are three small circles,
descending step by step, like those you leave behind.
All are full of accursed spirits;
but so that afterward sight alone may suffice you,
understand how and why they are confined.
Of every malice that earns hatred in heaven,
injury is the end, and every such end
distresses another either through force or through fraud.
But because fraud is an evil peculiar to humanity,
it displeases God more; and therefore below are
the fraudulent, and greater pain assails them.
The first circle belongs entirely to the violent;
but because violence is done to three kinds of person,
it is divided and constructed in three rings.
Against God, oneself, and one's neighbor one can
do violence, I mean against them and their possessions,
as you shall hear with clear explanation.
Death by violence and grievous wounds
are inflicted upon one's neighbor, and upon his possessions
destruction, fires, and harmful seizures;
hence murderers and all who wound maliciously,
destroyers and plunderers, all are tormented by
the first ring, in separate bands.
A person can lay a violent hand upon himself
and upon his goods; and therefore in the second
ring it is necessary that he repent without avail
whoever deprives himself of your world,
gambles away and dissipates his wealth,
and weeps where he ought to be joyful.
Violence can be done against the deity,
by denying and blaspheming it in the heart,
and by despising nature and its goodness;
and therefore the smallest ring stamps
with its mark both Sodom and Cahors
and whoever speaks while despising God in his heart.
Fraud, by which every conscience is bitten,
a person can practice against one who trusts him
and against one who does not hold such trust.
This latter kind appears to sever
only the bond of love that nature makes;
hence in the second circle nest
hypocrisy, flattery, and those who practice sorcery,
falsification, theft, and simony,
pimps, corrupt officials, and similar filth.
Through the other kind, that love is forgotten
which nature makes, and that which is afterward added,
from which special trust is created;
hence in the smallest circle, where lies the point
of the universe on which Dis sits,
whoever betrays is consumed eternally”.
And I: “Master, quite clearly proceeds
your reasoning, and distinguishes quite well
this abyss and the people it contains.
But tell me: those of the slimy marsh,
those whom the wind drives, and whom the rain beats,
and who clash with such harsh tongues,
why not within the red city
are they punished, if God holds them in wrath?
and if he does not, why are they in such a state?”.
And he to me, “Why does it stray so far”,
he said, “your intellect, from its usual course?
or where else is your mind looking?
Do you not remember those words
with which your Ethics discusses
the three dispositions that heaven will not tolerate,
incontinence, malice, and mad
bestiality? and how incontinence
offends God less and earns less blame?
If you consider this judgment well,
and call to mind who those are
who endure punishment outside above,
you will see clearly why from these wicked souls
they are separated, and why with less wrath
divine vengeance strikes them”.
“O sun that heals all troubled sight,
you satisfy me so when you resolve,
that, no less than knowing, doubting pleases me.
Turn back a little once again”,
I said, “to where you say that usury offends
divine goodness, and untie the knot”.
“Philosophy”, he told me, “to one who understands it,
points out, not merely in a single passage,
how nature takes its course
from the divine intellect and from its art;
and if you study your Physics well,
you will find, after not many pages,
that that, as far as it can, your art
follows, as the pupil follows the master;
so that your art is almost God's grandchild.
From these two, if you call to mind
Genesis from its beginning, it is necessary
for humankind to derive its livelihood and prosper;
and because the usurer takes another path,
nature in itself and in its follower
he despises, since he places his hope elsewhere.
But follow me now, for I wish to go;
for the Fishes are darting up over the horizon,
and the Wain lies wholly over the northwest wind,
and the bank is descended farther over there”.
