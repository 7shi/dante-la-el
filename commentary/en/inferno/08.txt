I say, continuing, that long before
we were at the foot of the tall tower,
our eyes went upward to its summit
because of two little flames that we saw placed there,
and another from afar return the signal,
so far that the eye could scarcely catch it.
And I turned to the sea of all wisdom;
I said: “What does this say? and what reply gives
that other fire? and who are those who made it?”.
And he to me: “Across the filthy waves
you can already discern what is awaited,
if the marsh's smoke does not hide it from you”.
Never did a bowstring shoot an arrow from itself
that sped so swiftly through the air,
as I saw a little boat
come toward us over the water at that moment,
under the command of a single boatman,
who shouted: “Now you have come, wicked soul!”.
“Phlegyas, Phlegyas, you shout in vain”,
said my lord, “this time:
you shall have us only while we cross the mire”.
Like one who hears of a great deception
practiced on him, and then resents it,
so Phlegyas became in his pent-up anger.
My guide descended into the boat,
and then made me enter after him;
and only when I was inside did it seem laden.
As soon as my guide and I were in the vessel,
the ancient prow went cutting through
more water than it usually does with others.
While we sped across the stagnant pool,
one covered in mud rose before me,
and said: “Who are you who come before your time?”.
And I to him: “Though I come, I do not stay;
but who are you, who have become so foul?”.
He answered: “You see that I am one who weeps”.
And I to him: “With weeping and with mourning,
accursed spirit, remain here;
for I recognize you, though you are wholly filthy”.
Then he stretched both hands toward the boat;
whereupon the watchful master thrust him back,
saying: “Away there with the other dogs!”.
Then he encircled my neck with his arms;
he kissed my face and said: “Indignant soul,
blessed is she who bore you in her womb!
That man was an arrogant person in the world;
no goodness adorns his memory:
thus his shade is furious here.
How many now regard themselves up there as great kings
who here will stand like pigs in mire,
leaving behind horrible contempt for themselves!”.
And I: “Master, I would be very eager
to see him plunged into this swill
before we leave the lake”.
And he to me: “Before the shore
is allowed to appear to you, you shall be satisfied:
you shall have the pleasure of such a desire”.
Shortly after this I saw such torment
inflicted on this man by the muddy people,
that I still praise and thank God for it.
They all shouted: “At Filippo Argenti!”;
and the hot-tempered Florentine spirit
turned his teeth upon himself.
There we left him, and I tell no more of him;
but a lamentation struck my ears,
so that I opened my eyes wide, intent ahead.
The good master said: “Now, my son,
the city named Dis draws near,
with its burdened citizens, with its great host”.
And I: “Master, already its mosques
I clearly discern there within the valley,
crimson as though out of fire they
had come”. And he said to me: “The eternal fire
that burns within makes them appear red,
as you see in this lower hell”.
At last we came within the deep moats
that encircle that desolate city:
the walls seemed to me to be iron.
Not without first making a wide circuit,
we came to a place where the boatman loudly
“Get out here”, shouted: “here is the entrance”.
I saw more than a thousand upon the gates
who had rained down from heaven, who angrily
said: “Who is this who without death
goes through the kingdom of the dead?”.
And my wise master made a sign
that he wished to speak to them in secret.
Then they somewhat restrained their great disdain
and said: “You come alone, and let that one go
who so boldly entered this realm.
Let him return alone along the foolish road:
let him try, if he knows how; for you shall remain here,
who have guided him through so dark a land”.
Think, reader, whether I lost heart
at the sound of those accursed words,
for I did not believe I would ever return here.
“O my dear guide, who more than seven
times have restored my confidence and drawn me
from grave danger that stood before me,
do not leave me”, I said, “so undone;
and if going farther is denied us,
let us quickly retrace our steps together”.
And that lord who had led me there
said to me: “Do not fear; for our passage
no one can take from us: by such a one is it granted.
But wait for me here, and your weary spirit
comfort and nourish with good hope,
for I shall not leave you in the lower world”.
So he goes away, and there leaves me,
my gentle father, and I remain in doubt,
for yes and no contend within my head.
I could not hear what he put before them;
but he did not stay there with them long,
before they all raced back inside.
Those adversaries of ours shut the gates
against my lord's breast, who remained outside
and turned back toward me with slow steps.
His eyes were on the ground, and his brows were shorn
of all confidence, and he said amid sighs:
“Who has denied me the houses of sorrow!”.
And to me he said: “Though I grow angry,
do not be dismayed, for I shall overcome this trial,
whoever moves about within to defend it.
This arrogance of theirs is not new;
for they once exercised it at a less hidden gate,
which still stands without a fastening.
Above it you saw the deadly inscription:
and already on this side of it descends the slope,
passing through the circles without escort,
one through whom the city shall be opened to us”.
