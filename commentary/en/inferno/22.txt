I have seen horsemen break camp,
and begin battle and hold their muster,
and sometimes retreat to save themselves;
I have seen scouts through your land,
O Aretines, and seen raiding bands go forth,
clash in tournaments and run the joust;
now with trumpets, and now with bells,
with drums and with signals from castles,
and with devices of our own and foreign ones;
Nor ever with so strange a shawm
did I see horsemen move, or foot soldiers,
or a ship by a signal from land or star.
We were going with the ten demons.
Ah, savage company! But in church
with saints, and in the tavern with gluttons.
Yet my attention was upon the pitch,
to see every feature of the ditch
and of the people who were scorched within it.
As dolphins, when they give a sign
to sailors with the arching of their backs
that they should take measures to save their ship,
so at times, to ease the pain,
one of the sinners would show his back
and hide it in less time than lightning flashes.
And as at the water's edge of a ditch
the frogs stay with only their snouts out,
so that they hide their feet and all their bulk,
so on every side stood the sinners;
but as Barbariccia drew near,
so they withdrew beneath the bubbling surface.
I saw, and still my heart shudders at it,
one linger so, as it happens
that one frog remains and another darts away;
and Graffiacan, who was most directly opposite him,
hooked him by his pitch-coated hair
and hauled him up, so that he seemed to me an otter.
I already knew the names of every one,
so well I noted them when they were chosen,
and afterward, as they called each other, listened how.
“O Rubicante, see that you set on him
your great claws upon the back, so that you flay him!”,
all the accursed cried together.
And I: “My master, see, if you can,
that you learn who is the wretch
fallen into the hands of his adversaries.”
My guide drew close beside him;
asked him where he was from, and he replied:
“I was born of the kingdom of Navarre.
My mother placed me as a servant to a lord,
who had borne me by a scoundrel,
a destroyer of himself and of his possessions.
Then I was a member of good King Theobald's household;
there I took to practising barratry,
for which I render account in this heat.”
And Ciriatto, from whose mouth protruded
on either side a tusk like a boar's,
made him feel how one of them could rip.
Among wicked cats the mouse had come;
but Barbariccia enclosed him with his arms
and said: “Stand back, while I hold him fast.”
And toward my master he turned his face;
“Ask,” he said, “again, if there is more you desire
to learn from him, before another tears him apart.”
My guide then: “Now tell me: among the other sinners
do you know anyone who is Italian
beneath the pitch?” And he: “I parted,
a little while ago, from one who was from near there.
Would that I were still covered with him,
for then I would fear neither claw nor hook!”
And Libicocco: “We have endured too much,”
he said; and caught his arm with the hook,
so that, tearing, he carried off a piece of flesh.
Draghignazzo too wished to seize him
down by the legs; at which their captain
turned all around with an angry look.
When they had calmed a little,
of him, who still gazed at his wound,
my guide asked without delay:
“Who was the one from whom an ill departure
you say you made to come ashore?”
And he replied: “It was Friar Gomita,
the one from Gallura, vessel of every fraud,
who held his lord's enemies in his hands,
and dealt with them so that each praises him for it.
He took money and let them go outright,
as he says; and in his other offices too
he was no petty barrator, but a sovereign one.
With him keeps company Don Michel Zanche
of Logudoro; and in speaking of Sardinia
their tongues do not feel weary.
Alas, look at the other one gnashing his teeth;
I would say more, but I fear that he
is preparing to scratch my scabby head.”
And the great commander, turning to Farfarello,
who was rolling his eyes to strike,
said: “Stand aside there, wicked bird!”
“If you wish to see or hear,”
the frightened man then began again,
“Tuscans or Lombards, I will make some come;
but let the Malebranche stand a little apart,
so that they may not fear their vengeance;
and I, sitting in this very place,
for the one that I am, I will make seven come
when I whistle, as is our custom
to do whenever one of us comes out.”
At these words Cagnazzo raised his snout,
shaking his head, and said: “Hear the trickery
he has devised to throw himself down!”
At which he, who had snares in great abundance,
replied: “Malicious indeed am I,
when I contrive greater misery for my own.”
Alichin could not restrain himself and, in opposition
to the others, said to him: “If you plunge down,
I will not come after you at a gallop,
but beat my wings above the pitch.
Let us leave the ridge, and let the bank be a screen,
to see whether you alone are worth more than we.”
O you who read, you shall hear of a strange sport:
each turned his eyes toward the other side,
he first who had been most opposed to doing so.
The Navarrese chose his moment well;
he planted his feet on the ground, and in an instant
jumped and freed himself from their commander.
At this each was stung with guilt,
but he most who had caused the mistake;
so he darted forward and cried: “You are caught!”
But little did it avail him: for fear was what his wings
could not outstrip; the one went under,
and the other, flying, turned his breast upward:
just so the duck suddenly,
when the falcon approaches, dives down,
and it returns upward angry and defeated.
Calcabrina, angered by the trick,
kept flying after him, eager
that the sinner should escape so he might have a fight;
and as soon as the barrator had disappeared,
he turned his claws upon his companion,
and grappled with him above the ditch.
But the other was indeed a fierce sparrowhawk
in clawing him soundly, and both
fell into the middle of the boiling pool.
The heat immediately made them release their grip;
but even so there was no getting up,
so thoroughly had their wings become mired.
Barbariccia, distressed with the others of his band,
made four of them fly to the other side
with all their grappling hooks, and very swiftly
on this side and that they descended to their posts;
they stretched their hooks toward the mired pair,
who were already cooked beneath the crust.
And we left them thus entangled.
