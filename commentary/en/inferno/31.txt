One and the same tongue first bit me,
so that it colored both my cheeks,
and then offered me the medicine;
so I hear that the spear was accustomed to act,
of Achilles and his father, being the cause
first of a grievous and then of a good gift.
We turned our backs upon the wretched valley,
up along the bank that encircles it,
crossing without a word.
There it was less than night and less than day,
so that my sight went little way ahead;
but I heard a loud horn sound,
so loudly that it would have made every thunderclap faint,
which, as I followed its course back toward it,
directed my eyes wholly to one place.
After the grievous rout, when
Charlemagne lost his holy company,
Roland did not sound so terribly.
Only a little while had I kept my head turned that way,
when I seemed to see many tall towers;
hence I: “Master, tell me, what town is this?”.
And he to me: “Because you range
through the darkness from too far away,
it follows that you then stray in your imagining.
You will see clearly, if you reach that place,
how much the senses are deceived by distance;
therefore spur yourself on a little more”.
Then affectionately he took me by the hand
and said: “Before we go farther,
so that the reality may seem less strange to you,
know that they are not towers, but giants,
and they are in the pit around its rim
from the navel downward, every one of them.”
As when the mist disperses,
the gaze little by little makes out
what the vapor that thickens the air conceals,
so, piercing the thick and dark air,
drawing nearer and nearer toward the bank,
my error fled and my fear grew;
for, just as upon its circular enclosure
Monteriggioni crowns itself with towers,
so the bank that surrounds the pit
they towered above with half their bodies,
the horrible giants, whom threatens
Jupiter from heaven still whenever he thunders.
And already I could discern the face of one,
his shoulders and chest and much of his belly,
and both his arms down along his sides.
Nature certainly, when she abandoned the art
of making such creatures, did very well
to deprive Mars of such agents.
And if of elephants and whales
she does not repent, whoever looks closely
holds her the more just and discerning for it;
for where the instrument of the mind
is joined to evil will and to power,
human beings can make no defense against it.
His face seemed to me as long and broad
as the pine cone of Saint Peter's in Rome,
and his other bones were in proportion to it;
so that the bank, which was a loincloth
from his middle downward, showed so very much of him
above it, that of reaching his hair
three Frisians would have boasted in vain;
for I could see thirty great spans of him
below the place where a man fastens his cloak.
“Raphèl maì amècche zabì almi”,
the savage mouth began to cry,
for which no sweeter psalms were fitting.
And my leader to him: “Foolish soul,
keep to your horn, and vent yourself with it
when anger or another passion touches you!
Search about your neck, and you will find the cord
that holds it tied, O bewildered soul,
and see it lying like a band across your great chest”.
Then he said to me: “He himself accuses himself;
this is Nimrod, through whose evil design
a single language is no longer used in the world.
Let us leave him alone and not speak in vain;
for every language is so to him
as his is to others, which is known to no one.”
We therefore journeyed farther,
turning to the left; and a crossbow shot away
we found the other, far fiercer and larger.
Whoever the master was that bound him,
I cannot say, but he held pinioned
the other arm in front and his right arm behind
by a chain that kept him bound
from the neck downward, so that over his exposed body
it wound itself as far as the fifth turn.
“This proud one wished to make trial
of his power against highest Jupiter”,
said my leader, “hence he has such a reward.
Ephialtes is his name, and he performed his great feats
when the giants frightened the gods;
the arms that he wielded, he never moves again.”
And I to him: “If it is possible, I would wish
that of the immeasurable Briareus
my eyes might have experience”.
Hence he replied: “You will see Antaeus
near here, who speaks and is unbound,
who will set us at the bottom of all evil.
The one you wish to see is much farther on
and is bound and shaped like this one,
except that he seems fiercer in the face”.
Never was an earthquake so violent
that it shook a tower so strongly
as Ephialtes was quick to shake himself.
Then I feared death more than ever,
and nothing more than the fright was needed,
had I not seen the bonds.
We then proceeded farther,
and came to Antaeus, who fully five ells,
not counting his head, rose out of the cavern.
“O you who in the fortunate valley
that made Scipio heir to glory,
when Hannibal with his men turned their backs,
once took a thousand lions as prey,
and who, had you been at the great war
of your brothers, it still seems to be believed
that the sons of earth would have won:
set us down below, and let no disdain for it come over you,
where the cold locks Cocytus.
Do not make us go to Tityus or Typhon:
this man can give some of what is desired here;
therefore bend down and do not turn aside your snout.
He can yet restore your fame in the world,
for he lives, and still expects a long life
if grace does not call him to itself before his time.”
Thus spoke the master; and the other quickly
stretched out his hands and took my leader,
with which Hercules once felt a mighty grip.
Virgil, when he felt himself being taken,
said to me: “Come here, so that I may take you”;
then made it so that he and I were one bundle.
As the Garisenda appears when one looks at it
from beneath its leaning side, when a cloud passes
above it so that it seems to lean against it:
so Antaeus appeared to me as I watched intently
to see him bend, and there was such a moment
that I would have wished to go by another road.
But gently, to the bottom that devours
Lucifer together with Judas, he set us down;
nor, thus bent, did he remain there,
and like a mast in a ship he rose.
