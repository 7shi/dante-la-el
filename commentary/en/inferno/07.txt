“Pape Satàn, pape Satàn aleppe!”,
Plutus began with his harsh, clucking voice;
and that gentle sage, who knew all,
said to comfort me: “Let not
your fear harm you; for, whatever power he may have,
he shall not prevent our descending this rock”.
Then he turned to that swollen face,
and said: “Be silent, accursed wolf!
consume yourself within with your own rage.
Not without cause is this journey into the depths:
it is willed on high, there where Michael
took vengeance for the proud rebellion”.
As the sails swollen by the wind
fall tangled when the mast breaks,
so fell to earth the cruel beast.
Thus we descended into the fourth hollow,
taking in more of the sorrowful slope
that holds all the evil of the universe.
Ah, justice of God! who heaps up so many
strange torments and pains as I saw?
and why does our guilt so waste us?
As does the wave there above Charybdis,
which breaks against the one it meets,
so must the people here dance their round.
Here I saw people far more numerous than elsewhere,
on one side and the other, with great howls,
rolling weights by force of the chest.
They struck against one another; and then, right there,
each turned around, rolling backward,
crying: “Why do you hoard?” and “Why do you squander?”.
Thus they returned along the gloomy circle
on either side to the opposite point,
shouting at one another again their insulting refrain;
then each turned, when he had arrived,
through his half-circle to the other joust.
And I, whose heart was almost pierced,
said: “My master, now explain to me
what people these are, and whether all were clerics,
these tonsured ones upon our left”.
And he to me: “All of them were squint-eyed
so greatly in their minds in the first life,
that none made any expenditure there with measure.
Their voices bark it out clearly enough,
when they come to the two points of the circle
where opposing guilt divides them.
These were clerics, who have no covering
of hair upon the head, and popes and cardinals,
in whom avarice practices its excess”.
And I: “Master, among such as these
I surely ought to recognize some
who were defiled by these evils”.
And he to me: “You gather a vain thought:
the undiscerning life that made them filthy,
now makes them dark to every recognition.
Eternally they will come to the two collisions:
these will rise again from the tomb
with the fist closed, and these with their hair shorn.
Wrong giving and wrong keeping the beautiful world
have taken from them, and set them to this brawl:
what it is, I do not embellish with words.
Now you can see, my son, the fleeting mockery
of the goods entrusted to fortune,
for which humankind fights;
for all the gold that is beneath the moon
and that ever existed, of these weary souls
could not give rest to a single one”.
“My master”, I said, “now tell me also:
this fortune of whom you speak to me,
what is she, who holds the world's goods so in her claws?”.
And he to me: “Oh foolish creatures,
how great is the ignorance that afflicts you!
Now I would have you take in my judgment of her.
He whose wisdom transcends all,
made the heavens and gave them guides
so that every part shines upon every part,
distributing the light equally.
Similarly, for worldly splendors
he appointed a general minister and guide
who would transfer, in due time, the empty goods
from people to people and from one lineage to another,
beyond the resistance of human understanding;
whereby one people rules and another languishes,
following the judgment of this one,
which is hidden like the snake in the grass.
Your knowledge cannot oppose her:
she provides, judges, and pursues
her rule as the other gods do theirs.
Her changes have no truce:
necessity makes her swift;
so often comes one who succeeds in turn.
This is she who is so much crucified
even by those who ought to give her praise,
giving her blame unjustly and an evil reputation;
but she is blessed and does not hear this:
joyful among the other first-created beings,
she turns her sphere and rejoices in her bliss.
Now let us descend to greater misery;
already every star is sinking that was rising
when I set out, and too long a stay is forbidden”.
We cut across the circle to the other bank
above a spring that bubbles and pours out
through a ditch that flows from it.
The water was far darker than deep purple;
and we, in company with the dusky waves,
entered downward along a strange path.
Into the marsh that is named Styx goes
this dismal stream, when it has descended
to the foot of the malignant gray slopes.
And I, who stood intent on looking,
saw muddy people in that swamp,
all naked, with angry faces.
These struck one another not only with the hand,
but with the head and with the chest and with the feet,
tearing one another with their teeth, piece by piece.
The good master said: “Son, now you see
the souls of those whom anger overcame;
and I also wish you to believe for certain
that beneath the water are people who sigh,
and make this water bubble at the surface,
as your eye tells you, wherever it turns.
Embedded in the mud, they say: “Sullen were we
in the sweet air that is gladdened by the sun,
carrying within a smoke of acedia:
now we grieve in the black mire”.
This hymn they gurgle in their throats,
for they cannot say it in whole words”.
Thus we went around the filthy pool
in a great arc between the dry bank and the mire,
with our eyes turned toward those who swallow mud.
We came to the foot of a tower at last.
