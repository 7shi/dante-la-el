Singing like a woman in love,
she continued upon the ending of her words:
‘Blessed are those whose sins are covered!’.
And like nymphs who wandered alone
through the woodland shadows, desiring,
some to see, some to flee the sun,
then she moved against the river's course, walking
up along the bank; and I abreast of her,
following small step with small.
Her steps and mine together were not a hundred,
when the banks alike made a turn,
so that I found myself facing east.
Nor had our way yet thus carried us much farther,
when the woman turned wholly toward me,
saying: “My brother, look and listen”.
And behold, a sudden radiance swept
from every side through the great forest,
so that it made me wonder if it was lightning.
But because lightning ceases as it comes,
and that, enduring, shone more and more,
in my thoughts I said: ‘What is this?’.
And a sweet melody ran
through the luminous air; so that righteous zeal
made me rebuke Eve's audacity,
who, where earth and heaven obeyed,
a woman, alone and only just formed,
could not endure remaining beneath any veil;
beneath which, had she remained devout,
I would have those ineffable delights
felt sooner and for a longer time.
While I walked among so many first fruits
of eternal pleasure, wholly rapt,
and desirous still of greater joys,
before us, just like a blazing fire,
the air became for us beneath the green branches;
and the sweet sound was now discerned as singing.
O most holy Virgins, if hunger,
cold or vigils I ever endured for you,
a cause spurs me to ask you for your favor.
Now Helicon must pour forth for me,
and Urania help me with her choir
to put into verse things difficult to conceive.
A little farther on, seven trees of gold
were falsely suggested to my sight by the long stretch
of space that still lay between us and them;
but when I had come so near to them,
that the common object, which deceives the senses,
lost none of its attributes through distance,
the faculty that prepares material for reason's judgment,
apprehended them as the candlesticks they were,
and in the voices of the singing, ‘Hosanna’.
Above, the beautiful array blazed
far brighter than the moon in a clear sky
at midnight in the middle of its month.
I turned, full of wonder,
toward good Virgil, and he answered me
with a look laden with no less amazement.
Then I turned my gaze back to the lofty things
that were moving toward us so slowly,
that newly wedded brides would have outpaced them.
The woman rebuked me: “Why do you burn only
so with longing for the living lights,
and not look at what comes behind them?”.
People I then saw, as behind their leaders,
coming after them, clothed in white;
and such whiteness never was here on earth.
The water caught the light upon my left flank,
and gave me back my left side,
if I looked into it, just like a mirror.
When I had reached such a place on my bank,
that only the river kept me apart,
to see better I brought my steps to a halt,
and saw the little flames move onward,
leaving behind them the air painted,
and they had the appearance of trailing pennants;
so that overhead there remained marked out
in seven bands, all in those colors
with which the Sun makes the rainbow and Delia her girdle.
These standards stretched backward farther
than my sight; and, as far as I could judge,
the outermost were ten paces apart.
Beneath so fair a sky as I describe,
twenty-four elders, two by two,
came crowned with lilies.
All were singing: “Blessed are you
among the daughters of Adam, and blessed
may your beauties be forever!”
After the flowers and the other fresh grasses
opposite me upon the other bank
were freed of those chosen people,
as light follows light in heaven,
there came after them four living creatures,
each crowned with green leaves.
Each was feathered with six wings;
the feathers full of eyes; and the eyes of Argus,
if they were alive, would be like these.
To describe their forms I spend no more
rhymes, reader; for another expense presses me,
so much that I cannot be lavish with this one;
but read Ezekiel, who depicts them
as he saw them from the cold region
come with wind and with cloud and with fire;
and as you will find them in his pages,
such were they here, except that as to their wings
John agrees with me and differs from him.
The space within those four contained
a triumphal chariot upon two wheels,
which came drawn by a griffin's neck.
It stretched both its wings upward
between the middle band and the three on either side,
so that, cleaving the air, it harmed none.
So high they rose that they could not be seen;
it had limbs of gold insofar as it was bird,
and the others white, mingled with crimson.
Not that Rome with so fine a chariot
gladdened Africanus, or indeed Augustus,
but even the Sun's would be poor beside it;
the Sun's, which, straying from its course, was burned
at the prayer of the devout Earth,
when Jove was inscrutably just.
Three women in a circle beside the right wheel
came dancing; one so red
that she would scarcely have been noticed within fire;
another was as though her flesh and bones
had been made of emerald;
the third seemed snow just fallen;
and now they seemed led by the white,
now by the red; and from the latter's song
the others took their pace, both slow and swift.
On the left four made festival,
clothed in purple, following the measure
of one of them who had three eyes in her head.
After all the company just described
I saw two old men in different dress,
but alike in bearing, dignified and grave.
One appeared to be among the followers
of that supreme Hippocrates whom nature
made for the creatures she holds dearest;
the other showed the opposite concern
with a sword shining and sharp,
such that it frightened me on this side of the stream.
Then I saw four of humble appearance;
and behind them all, a solitary old man
coming, asleep, with an alert face.
And these seven, like the first company,
were clothed, but with lilies
they made no garden about their heads,
rather with roses and other crimson flowers;
a gaze from a little distance would have sworn
that all were aflame above their brows.
And when the chariot was opposite me,
a thunderclap was heard, and those worthy people
seemed forbidden to go any farther,
stopping there with the foremost standards.
