While thus along the edge, one before the other,
we went our way, and often the good master
said to me: “Take care: let my warning profit you”;
the sun struck me upon my right shoulder,
which already, with its rays, all the west
was changing from a blue aspect to white;
and with my shadow I made more glowing
the flame appear; and to this sign alone
I saw many shades, as they walked, pay attention.
This was the cause that prompted
them to speak of me; and they began
to say: “That one's body does not seem a semblance”;
then toward me, as close as they could,
some came, always taking care
not to come out where they would not be burned.
“O you who go, not from being slower,
but perhaps from reverence, behind the others,
answer me, who burn in thirst and fire.
Nor is your answer needed by me alone;
for all these have greater thirst for it
than an Indian or Ethiopian has for cold water.
Tell us how it is that you make yourself a wall
against the sun, just as though you had not yet
entered within the net of death”.
So one of them spoke to me; and I would
already have revealed myself, had I not attended
to another novelty that then appeared;
for through the middle of the burning path
came people facing these,
who held me suspended in contemplation.
There I see on either side make itself ready
each shade, and kiss one another
without stopping, content with a brief celebration;
so within their dark company
one ant touches muzzles with another,
perhaps to learn their route and their fortunes.
As soon as they break off their friendly greeting,
before their first step carries them beyond it,
each strives to outshout the other:
the newly arrived people: “Sodom and Gomorrah”;
and the other: “Pasiphaë enters the cow,
so that the young bull may rush to her lust.”
Then, like cranes that to the Riphaean mountains
might fly in part, and in part toward the sands,
these shunning the frost, those the sun,
one company goes away, the other comes on;
and weeping, they return to their former songs
and to the cry that most befits them;
and they draw near me again, as before,
those same ones who had entreated me,
attentive to listen, as their faces showed.
I, who had twice seen their desire,
began: “O souls assured
of having, whenever it may be, a state of peace,
neither unripe nor ripe have remained
my limbs back there, but they are here with me
with their blood and with their joints.
From here I go upward to be blind no longer;
a lady is above who obtains grace for me,
through which I carry what is mortal through your world.
But so may your greatest desire be satisfied
soon, so that the heaven may shelter you
which is full of love and spreads most widely,
tell me, so that I may yet inscribe pages with it,
who you are, and who that throng is
which goes away behind your backs”.
No differently is the mountaineer bewildered
in amazement, and falls silent as he gazes,
when, rough and wild, he enters the city,
than each shade seemed in its appearance;
but after they were relieved of their amazement,
which in lofty hearts is quickly quelled,
“Blessed are you, who from our regions”,
began again the one who first questioned me,
“to die a better death, take experience aboard!
The people who do not come with us offended
in that for which Caesar once, in triumph,
heard ‘Queen’ called out against him:
therefore they depart crying ‘Sodom’,
reproaching themselves, as you have heard,
and aid the burning with their shame.
Our sin was hermaphroditic;
but because we did not observe human law,
following appetite like beasts,
in reproach of ourselves, we recite,
when we part, the name of her
who made herself a beast within the beast-shaped planks.
Now you know our deeds and what we were guilty of:
if perhaps you wish to know who we are by name,
there is no time to tell, nor would I know.
I shall indeed lessen your desire concerning me:
I am Guido Guinizzelli, and already I purify myself
because I repented well before the final extremity.”
As, in the sorrow of Lycurgus,
two sons became on seeing their mother again,
so I became, but do not rise to such lengths,
when I hear him name himself, the father
of me and of my other betters who ever
used sweet and graceful rhymes of love;
and without hearing or speaking I went thoughtfully
a long while gazing at him,
nor, because of the fire, did I draw any nearer.
When I had fed my fill of gazing,
I offered myself wholly ready for his service
with the affirmation that makes others believe.
And he to me: “You leave such a trace,
from what I hear, within me, and one so clear,
that Lethe cannot remove it or make it dim.
But if your words just now swore truly,
tell me what is the reason why you show
in speech and gaze that you hold me dear”.
And I to him: “Your sweet sayings,
which, as long as modern usage shall endure,
will continue to make their inks precious.”
“O brother,” he said, “this one whom I distinguish for you
with my finger,” and he pointed to a spirit ahead,
“was a better craftsman of the mother tongue.
Verses of love and prose of romances
he surpassed them all; and let the fools talk
who believe that the man of Limousin surpasses him.
Toward reputation more than truth they turn their faces,
and thus they fix their opinion
before art or reason is heard by them.
So did many of the older poets with Guittone,
from cry to cry giving praise to him alone,
until truth, with more than one poet, overcame him.
Now if you have so ample a privilege,
that you are permitted to go to the cloister
in which Christ is abbot of the community,
say to him for me a Paternoster,
as much as is needed for us in this world,
where the ability to sin is no longer ours.”
Then, perhaps to make room for another, since
he had him near, he vanished through the fire,
like a fish through water going to the bottom.
I moved a little forward toward the one pointed out,
and said that for his name my desire
was preparing a gracious place.
He freely began to say:
“So much does your courteous request please me,
that I neither can nor wish to hide myself from you.
I am Arnaut, who weep and go singing;
sorrowfully I see my past folly,
and joyfully I see ahead the joy I hope for.
Now I pray you, by that power
that guides you to the summit of the stairway,
remember in due time my pain!”
Then he hid himself in the fire that refines them.
