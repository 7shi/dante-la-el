Although the sudden flight
scattered them across the plain,
turned toward the mountain where justice goads us,
I drew close to my faithful companion:
and how would I have run without him?
who would have drawn me up the mountain?
He seemed to me reproached by his own self:
O dignified and spotless conscience,
how bitter a bite a small fault is to you!
When his feet left off their haste,
which diminishes the dignity of every act,
my mind, which before had been confined,
widened its attention, as though eager,
and I turned my face toward the slope
that rises highest toward the sky.
The sun, which flamed red behind me,
was broken before me in the shape
that its rays, meeting me, assumed.
I turned to my side in fear
of being abandoned, when I saw
the earth dark only in front of me;
and my comfort: “Why do you still distrust?”,
he began to say, turning fully toward me;
“do you not believe me with you and that I guide you?
It is already evening there where is buried
the body within which I used to cast a shadow;
Naples has it, and from Brindisi it was taken.
Now, if nothing is shaded before me,
marvel no more than at the heavens,
where one does not obstruct another's rays.
To suffer torments, heats and frosts
the Power disposes bodies such as these
which does not wish how it does so to be revealed to us.
Mad is whoever hopes that our reason
can traverse the infinite way
that one substance in three persons holds.
Be content, human kind, with the that;
for, if you had been able to see everything,
there would have been no need for Mary to give birth;
and you have seen desiring without fruit
such men as would have had their desire stilled,
which is given them eternally for grief:
I speak of Aristotle and of Plato
and of many others”; and here he lowered his brow,
and said no more, and remained troubled.
Meanwhile we came to the mountain's foot;
there we found the rock so steep,
that ready legs would have been useless there.
Between Lerici and La Turbie the most desolate,
the most broken rocky slope is a stairway,
compared with that one, easy and open.
“Now who knows on which side the slope descends”,
said my master, stopping his steps,
“so that one who goes without wings may climb?”.
And while he, keeping his face lowered,
examined his mind about the way,
and I looked up around the rock,
on the left there appeared to me a company
of souls, who moved their feet toward us,
yet did not seem to, so slowly did they come.
“Raise”, I said, “master, your eyes:
here on this side are those who will give us counsel,
if you cannot obtain it from yourself”.
He looked then, and with a confident air
replied: “Let us go there, for they come slowly;
and you, make firm your hope, sweet son”.
That company was still far away,
I mean after our thousand steps,
as far as a good thrower would cast with his hand,
when they all drew close to the hard rocks
of the high cliff, and stood still and huddled
as one who goes in doubt stands still to look.
“O you whose end was good, O spirits already chosen”,
Virgil began, “by that peace
which I believe awaits you all,
tell us where the mountain slopes,
so that going upward may be possible;
for losing time displeases more the one who knows more”.
As little sheep come out of the fold
by ones, by twos, by threes, and the others stand
timidly, lowering eye and muzzle to the ground;
and what the first does, the others also do,
crowding against her, if she stops,
simple and quiet, and they do not know why;
so I saw the foremost begin to approach
of that fortunate flock then,
modest in face and dignified in gait.
As those in front saw broken
the light upon the ground on my right side,
so that the shadow lay between me and the cliff,
they stopped, and drew back a little,
and all the others who were coming after,
not knowing why, did the same.
“Without your asking I confess to you
that this is a human body that you see;
therefore the sunlight on the ground is split.
Do not marvel, but believe
that not without power that comes from heaven
does he seek to surmount this wall”.
So spoke my master; and that worthy company
“Turn back”, said, “go ahead of us then”,
making a sign with the backs of their hands.
And one of them began: “Whoever
you are, as you go thus, turn your face:
consider whether you ever saw me over there”.
I turned toward him and looked at him intently:
he was blond and beautiful and noble in appearance,
but a blow had divided one of his brows.
When I had humbly denied
ever having seen him, he said: “Now look”;
and showed me a wound high on his breast.
Then, smiling, he said: “I am Manfred,
grandson of Empress Constance;
therefore I pray you that, when you return,
you go to my fair daughter, mother
of the honor of Sicily and of Aragon,
and tell her the truth, if something else is said.
After I had my body broken
by two mortal thrusts, I surrendered myself,
weeping, to him who willingly forgives.
Horrible were my sins;
but infinite goodness has such great arms,
that it takes whatever turns toward it.
If the shepherd of Cosenza, who to the hunt
for me was sent by Clement then,
had read this page in God aright,
the bones of my body would still be
at the bridge's head near Benevento,
under the guard of the heavy cairn.
Now the rain wets them and the wind moves them
outside the kingdom, close along the Verde,
where he transferred them with lights extinguished.
Through their curse is not so lost,
that it cannot return, eternal love,
as long as hope has any bloom of green.
It is true that whoever dies in defiance
of Holy Church, although he repents at the end,
must remain outside this cliff,
for every span of time he has remained, thirty,
in his presumption, if such a decree
is not shortened through good prayers.
See now if you can make me glad,
by revealing to my good Constance
how you have seen me, and also this prohibition;
for here, through those over there, much advancement is made”.
