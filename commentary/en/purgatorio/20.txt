Against a better will, a will struggles in vain;
therefore against my own pleasure, to please him,
I drew the sponge from the water unsatisfied.
I moved; and my leader moved through the
clear spaces close along the rock,
as one walks on a wall close to the battlements;
for the people who pour out drop by drop
through their eyes the evil that occupies the whole world,
crowd too close to the outer edge on the other side.
Accursed be you, ancient she-wolf,
who have more prey than all the other beasts
because of your endlessly deep hunger!
O heaven, in whose turning it seems people believe
the conditions down here to be transformed,
when will the one come through whom she departs?
We went with slow and short steps,
and I attentive to the shades, whom I heard
pitifully weeping and lamenting;
and by chance I heard “Sweet Mary!”
called out before us amid the weeping
as a woman does who is in childbirth;
and continue: “You were so poor,
as can be seen from that lodging
where you laid down your holy child”.
Next I heard: “O good Fabricius,
with poverty you preferred virtue
rather than to possess great wealth with vice”.
These words had pleased me so much,
that I drew farther on to learn about
that spirit from whom they seemed to have come.
He was still speaking of the generosity
that Nicholas showed to the maidens,
to bring their youth to an honorable life.
“O soul who speaks so well,
tell me who you were”, I said, “and why you alone
renew these worthy praises.
Your words shall not be without reward,
if I return to complete the short journey
of that life which flies toward its end”.
And he: “I will tell you, not for comfort
that I expect from there, but because so much
grace shines in you before you are dead.
I was the root of the evil plant
that overshadows all the Christian land,
so that good fruit is seldom gathered from it.
But if Douai, Lille, Ghent and Bruges
could, vengeance would soon be taken for it;
and I ask it of him who judges all.
I was called Hugh Capet over there;
from me were born the Philips and the Louises
by whom France has lately been ruled.
I was the son of a butcher of Paris:
when the ancient kings failed
all except one who had become a monk in gray garments,
I found held fast in my hands the reins
of the kingdom's government, and so much power
newly acquired, and myself so rich in friends,
that to the widowed crown was raised
the head of my son, from whom
the consecrated bones of these men began.
As long as the great Provençal dowry
had not taken shame away from my bloodline,
it was of little power, yet did no harm.
Then it began with force and falsehood
its plunder; and afterward, to make amends,
it seized Ponthieu and Normandy and Gascony.
Charles came into Italy and, to make amends,
made a victim of Conradin; and then
sent Thomas back to heaven, to make amends.
I see a time, not long after today,
that draws another Charles out of France,
to make himself and his kin better known.
He comes out without arms and only with the lance
with which Judas jousted, and he thrusts that
so that he makes Florence's belly burst.
From this not land, but sin and shame
he will gain, all the heavier for himself,
the more lightly he accounts such harm.
The other, who once came from a ship as a captive,
I see selling his daughter and bargaining over her
as corsairs do over other female slaves.
O avarice, what more can you do to us,
since you have drawn my bloodline so close to you,
that it cares nothing for its own flesh?
That the evil to come and that already done may seem less,
I see the fleur-de-lis enter Anagni,
and Christ be taken captive in his vicar.
I see him mocked once again;
I see the vinegar and gall renewed,
and him slain among living robbers.
I see the new Pilate so cruel,
that this does not satisfy him, but without decree
he carries his covetous sails into the Temple.
O my Lord, when shall I be glad
to see the vengeance which, concealed,
makes your anger sweet in your secrecy?
What I was saying of that only bride
of the Holy Spirit, and which made you
turn toward me for some explanation,
for so long it is a response to all our prayers
as the day lasts; but when night falls,
we take up a contrary sound in its place.
We repeat then the tale of Pygmalion,
whom a traitor and a thief and a murderer of kin
his gluttonous lust for gold made;
and the misery of avaricious Midas,
which followed his greedy request,
at which one must always laugh.
Then each recalls foolish Achan,
how he stole the spoils, so that the wrath
of Joshua still seems to bite him here.
Then we accuse Sapphira with her husband;
we praise the kicks that Heliodorus received;
and in infamy around the whole mountain goes
Polymestor, who slew Polydorus;
lastly the cry goes up here: ‘Crassus,
tell us, since you know: what does gold taste like?’.
Sometimes one speaks loudly and another softly,
according to the feeling that spurs us to go
now at a greater and now at a lesser pace:
therefore, in the good spoken of here by day,
just now I was not alone; but here nearby
no other person raised his voice”.
We had already departed from him,
and were striving to cover the road
as far as our strength permitted us,
when I felt, like something falling,
the mountain tremble; whereupon a chill seized me
such as commonly seizes one going to death.
Surely Delos did not shake so violently,
before Latona made her nest in it
to give birth to the two eyes of heaven.
Then from every side a cry began
such that the master turned toward me,
saying: “Do not fear, while I guide you”.
‘Glory in the highest’ all ‘to God’
were saying, from what I understood from those nearby,
so that the cry could be understood.
We stood motionless and in suspense
like the shepherds who first heard that song,
until the trembling ceased and it was ended.
Then we resumed our holy journey,
looking at the shades who lay upon the ground,
already returned to their accustomed weeping.
Never did any ignorance with so much conflict
make me desirous of knowing,
if my memory does not err in this,
as I then seemed, on reflection, to have;
nor, because of the haste, did I dare to ask,
nor could I discern anything there by myself:
thus I went on, timid and thoughtful.
