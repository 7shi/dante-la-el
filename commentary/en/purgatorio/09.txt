The concubine of ancient Tithonus
was already growing white at the balcony of the east,
outside the arms of her sweet beloved;
her brow was shining with gems,
set in the shape of the cold creature
that strikes people with its tail;
and night, of the steps by which it climbs,
had taken two in the place where we were,
and the third was already bending its wings downward;
when I, who had with me something of Adam,
overcome by sleep, sank down upon the grass
there where all five of us were already sitting.
At the hour when its mournful laments begin
the little swallow near the morning,
perhaps in memory of its earliest woes,
and when our mind, wandering
farther from the flesh and less held by thoughts,
is almost divine in its visions,
in a dream I seemed to see suspended
an eagle in the sky with golden feathers,
with its wings spread and intent on descending;
and I seemed to be there where were
his own abandoned by Ganymede,
when he was snatched up to the highest assembly.
Within myself I thought: ‘Perhaps this one strikes
only here by habit, and perhaps from any other place
disdains to carry anyone upward in its talons’.
Then it seemed to me that, after circling a little,
terrible as lightning, it descended,
and snatched me upward as far as the fire.
There it seemed that she and I were burning;
and so fiercely did the imagined blaze scorch,
that sleep had to be broken.
Not otherwise did Achilles start awake,
turning his awakened eyes around
and not knowing where he was,
when his mother from Chiron to Scyros
stole him away sleeping in her arms,
from where the Greeks afterward took him away;
than I started, as from my face
sleep fled, and I grew deathly pale,
as does a man who, frightened, turns to ice.
At my side was only my comfort,
and the sun was already more than two hours high,
and my face was turned toward the sea.
“Have no fear”, said my lord;
“be reassured, for we are at a good point;
do not hold back, but let all your strength expand.
You have now arrived at purgatory:
see there the cliff that encloses it all around;
see the entrance where it seems to be parted.
Just now, in the dawn that precedes the day,
when your soul was sleeping within you,
upon the flowers with which that place below is adorned
a woman came, and said: “I am Lucia;
let me take this man who sleeps;
thus I shall ease him on his way”.
Sordello remained, and the other noble figures;
she took you, and when the day grew bright,
came up here; and I followed in her footsteps.
Here she set you down, but first showed me
her beautiful eyes that open entrance;
then she and sleep departed together”.
Like a man who in doubt is reassured
and who changes his fear into comfort,
after the truth has been revealed to him,
I changed; and when free from care
my guide saw me, up along the cliff
he moved, and I behind him toward the height.
Reader, you see well how I raise
my subject matter, and therefore with greater art
do not marvel if I reinforce it.
We drew near, and were at a point
where, in the place that had first seemed broken to me,
just like a fissure that divides a wall,
I saw a gate, and three steps below it
to reach it, of different colors,
and a gatekeeper who as yet said nothing.
And as I opened my eyes wider and wider toward him,
I saw him sitting upon the highest step,
such in his face that I could not endure him;
and he had an unsheathed sword in his hand,
which reflected the rays toward us so strongly,
that I often directed my face toward it in vain.
“Speak from where you are: what do you want?”,
he began to say, “where is your escort?
Take care that coming up does not harm you”.
“A lady from heaven, versed in these things”,
my master answered him, “just now
told us: ‘Go there: there is the gate’”.
“And may she advance your steps toward good”,
the courteous gatekeeper began again:
“Come forward, then, to our steps”.
There we came; and the first step
was white marble so polished and clear,
that I saw myself reflected in it as I appear.
The second was coloured darker than dark purple,
made of a rough and scorched stone,
cracked lengthwise and crosswise.
The third, which masses above,
seemed to me porphyry, as flaming
as blood that spurts from a vein.
Upon this he rested both his feet,
the angel of God, sitting upon the threshold
that seemed to me a stone of diamond.
Up the three steps, with good will,
my guide drew me, saying: “Ask
humbly that he undo the lock”.
Devoutly I cast myself at the holy feet;
I asked for mercy and that he open for me,
but first I struck myself three times upon the breast.
Seven Ps he inscribed upon my forehead
with the point of the sword, and “See that you wash,
when you are inside, these wounds” he said.
Ash, or earth dug out dry,
would be of one color with his garment;
and from beneath it he drew two keys.
One was of gold and the other was of silver;
first with the white and then with the yellow
he worked upon the door so that I was satisfied.
“Whenever one of these keys fails,
so that it does not turn rightly in the lock”,
he said to us, “this passage does not open.
One is more precious; but the other calls for much
skill and ingenuity before it unlocks,
because it is the one that disentangles the knot.
From Peter I hold them; and he told me to err
rather in opening than in keeping it shut,
provided that people cast themselves at my feet”.
Then he pushed the door of the sacred gateway,
saying: “Enter; but I make you aware
that whoever looks behind returns outside”.
And when there were turned within their hinges
the pivots of that sacred portal,
which are of metal, resonant and strong,
it did not roar so loudly or prove so harsh,
Tarpeia, when the good man was taken from it,
Metellus, through which it afterward remained lean.
I turned attentively toward the first sound,
and ‘We praise you, God’ I seemed
to hear in a voice mingled with the sweet sound.
Precisely such an impression was given me
by what I heard, as one is wont to receive
when one is singing with organs;
so that now the words are understood, now not.
