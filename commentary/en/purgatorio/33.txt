‘God, the nations have come’, alternating
now three, now four, sweet psalmody,
the women began, and weeping;
and Beatrice, sighing and compassionate,
listened to them in such a state that little
more was Mary changed at the cross.
But after the other virgins gave way
for her to speak, standing upright on her feet,
she answered, flushed like fire:
‘A little while, and you will not see me;
and again, my beloved sisters,
a little while, and you will see me.’
Then she set all seven of them before her,
and behind her, merely beckoning, set in motion
me and the lady and the sage who had remained.
Thus she went on; and I do not believe that there had been
set upon the ground her tenth step,
when with her eyes she struck my eyes;
and with a tranquil look, “Come more quickly”,
she said to me, “so that, if I speak with you,
you may be well placed to listen to me”.
As soon as I was, as I ought to be, beside her,
she said to me: “Brother, why do you not venture
to question me now, as you walk with me?”.
As to those who, too reverent,
are speaking before their superior,
so that they cannot bring a clear voice to their lips,
so it happened to me, who with an incomplete sound
began: “My lady, my need
you know, and what is good for it”.
And she to me: “From fear and shame
I wish you now to disentangle yourself,
so that you no longer speak like someone dreaming.
Know that the vessel which the serpent broke,
was and is not; but let whoever bears the blame for it believe
that God's vengeance does not fear sops.
Not forever will it be without an heir,
the eagle that left its feathers on the chariot,
through which it became a monster and then prey;
for I see with certainty, and therefore tell it,
stars already near to bringing us a time,
safe from every hindrance and every barrier,
in which a five hundred, ten, and five,
a messenger of God, will slay the thieving woman
together with that giant who commits crimes with her.
And perhaps my dark narration,
like Themis and the Sphinx, persuades you less,
because, in their manner, it clouds the understanding;
but soon the events will be the Naiads,
who will solve this difficult enigma
without harm to sheep or crops.
Take note; and just as they are delivered by me,
so record these words for the living
of the life that is a running toward death.
And keep in mind, when you write them,
not to conceal how you have seen the tree
that has now been twice despoiled here.
Whoever robs that tree or breaks it,
with blasphemy in deed offends God,
who created it holy for his use alone.
For biting that tree, in punishment and longing,
five thousand years and more the first soul
yearned for him who punished the bite in himself.
Your understanding sleeps, if it does not judge
that for a special reason it is so lofty
and so inverted at its crown.
And if they had not been water of the Elsa,
the vain thoughts surrounding your mind,
and pleasure in them a Pyramus to the mulberry,
from these circumstances alone
the justice of God, in the prohibition,
you would recognize in the tree's moral meaning.
But because I see you in your understanding
turned to stone and, thus petrified, stained,
so that the light of my speech dazzles you,
I wish further, if not written, at least pictured,
that you carry it away within yourself for the reason
that one carries the pilgrim's staff encircled with palm.”
And I: “As sealing wax,
which does not change the figure imprinted,
my brain is now stamped by you.
But why so far above my sight
does your longed-for word fly,
that the more it strives, the more it loses it?”.
“So that you may recognize,” she said, “that school
which you have followed, and see its teaching
how it can follow my word;
and see your way from the divine
distant as far as is separated
from earth the heaven that hastens highest”.
Therefore I answered her: “I do not remember
ever having estranged myself from you,
nor have I any consciousness of it that reproaches me”.
“And if you cannot remember it”,
she answered smiling, “now recall
how you drank of Lethe today;
and if fire is inferred from smoke,
that forgetfulness clearly proves
fault in your will directed elsewhere.
Yet from now on will be bare
my words, as far as it is fitting
to uncover them to your unpracticed sight”.
And more brilliant and with slower steps
the sun held the meridian circle,
which shifts here and there according to who looks at it,
when there halted, just as there halts
one who goes before people as their guide
if he finds something strange or traces of it,
the seven women at the edge of a pale shadow,
such as beneath green leaves and dark branches
the mountain casts over its cold streams.
Before them Euphrates and Tigris
I seemed to see issuing from one spring,
and, like friends, parting slowly.
“O light, O glory of humankind,
what water is this that here unfolds
from one source and parts itself from itself?”.
At this request I was told: “Ask
Matelda to tell it to you”. And here answered,
as one does who clears herself of blame,
the beautiful lady: “This and other things
have been told him by me; and I am sure
that the water of Lethe did not hide it from him”.
And Beatrice: “Perhaps a greater care,
which often robs the memory,
has darkened the eyes of his mind.
But see Eunoë flowing away there:
lead him to it, and as you are accustomed,
revive his fainting power.”
Like a gentle soul, who makes no excuse,
but makes another's will her own
as soon as it is outwardly revealed by a sign;
so, after I had been taken by her,
the beautiful lady moved, and to Statius
said with gracious dignity: “Come with him”.
If I had, reader, more space
to write, I would yet sing in part
of the sweet draught that never would have sated me;
but because all the pages are full
planned for this second canticle,
the bridle of art does not let me go farther.
I returned from the most holy water
remade like new plants
renewed with new foliage,
pure and ready to ascend to the stars.
