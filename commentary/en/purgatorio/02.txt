Already the sun had reached the horizon
whose meridian circle covers
Jerusalem with its highest point;
and night, which circles opposite to him,
was coming forth from the Ganges with the Scales,
which fall from her hands when she grows longer;
so that the white and crimson cheeks,
there where I was, of beautiful Aurora
through advancing age were turning orange.
We were still alongside the sea,
like people who think about their journey,
who go in their hearts and remain with their bodies.
And behold, just as, overtaken by the morning,
through the thick vapors Mars glows red
down in the west above the surface of the sea,
so there appeared to me, may I see it again,
a light coming over the sea so swiftly,
that no flight can equal its motion.
From which, when I had drawn away a little
my eyes to question my guide,
I saw it again, grown brighter and larger.
Then on either side of it there appeared to me
something white I could not make out, and beneath
little by little another emerged from it.
My master still did not say a word,
until the first white shapes appeared as wings;
then, when he clearly recognized the boatman,
he cried: “Make haste, make haste to lower your knees.
Behold the angel of God: fold your hands;
from now on you will see ministers of this kind.
See how he disdains human instruments,
so that he wants no oar, nor any other sail
than his wings, between shores so far apart.
See how he holds them upright toward heaven,
working the air with his eternal feathers,
which do not change like mortal hair.”
Then, as closer and closer toward us came
the divine bird, brighter he appeared:
so that my eyes could not endure him near,
but I lowered them; and he came to shore
with a little vessel, swift and light,
so much so that the water swallowed none of it.
At the stern stood the heavenly helmsman,
such that merely describing him would bring blessedness;
and more than a hundred spirits sat within.
‘When Israel went out of Egypt’
they were all singing together with one voice
with all that is written afterward in that psalm.
Then he made over them the sign of the holy cross;
at which they all threw themselves upon the shore:
and he went away, as he had come, swiftly.
The crowd that remained there, unfamiliar
seemed with the place, looking all around
like someone who experiences new things.
On every side was shooting forth daylight
the sun, which with its unerring arrows
had driven Capricorn from the middle of the sky,
when the newly arrived people raised their faces
toward us, saying to us: “If you know,
show us the way to go to the mountain.”
And Virgil replied: “You believe
perhaps that we are familiar with this place;
but we are pilgrims just as you are.
Just now we arrived, a little before you,
by another way, which was so rough and hard,
that climbing now will seem to us like play.”
The souls, who had become aware of me,
through my breathing, that I was still alive,
grew pale with wonder.
And as to a messenger who carries an olive branch
people flock to hear news,
and no one shows reluctance to crowd the others,
so upon my face were fixed those
fortunate souls, every one,
almost forgetting to go and make themselves beautiful.
I saw one of them draw forward
to embrace me with such great affection,
that it moved me to do the same.
Oh shades, empty except in appearance!
three times I clasped my hands behind it,
and as many times brought them back to my breast.
With wonder, I believe, my face was painted;
at which the shade smiled and drew back,
and I, following it, pressed farther forward.
Gently he told me to stop;
then I knew who he was, and begged
that, to speak with me, he should pause a little.
He answered me: “Just as I loved you
in the mortal body, so I love you, now unbound:
therefore I stop; but why do you journey?”
“My Casella, to return once more
there where I am, I make this journey,”
I said; “but why has so much time been taken from you?”
And he to me: “No wrong has been done to me,
if he who takes passengers when and whom he pleases,
has several times denied me this passage;
for his will is formed from a just will:
nevertheless, for three months he has taken
whoever has wished to enter, in complete peace.
Thus I, who had now turned toward the shore
where the water of the Tiber turns salty,
was kindly taken aboard by him.
Toward that river mouth he has now directed his wing,
because there continually gathers
whoever does not descend toward Acheron.”
And I: “If no new law takes from you
memory or practice of the song of love
which used to quiet all my sorrows,
may it please you with that to console a little
my soul, which, with its body
coming here, is so greatly wearied!”
‘Love that speaks within my mind’
he then began so sweetly,
that the sweetness still sounds within me.
My master and I and those people
who were with him seemed so content,
as though nothing else touched anyone's mind.
We were all absorbed and attentive
to his notes; and behold the venerable old man
shouting: “What is this, sluggish spirits?
What negligence, what lingering is this?
Run to the mountain to strip off the crust
that does not allow God to be manifest to you.”
As when, picking up grain or darnel,
the doves gathered at their feeding,
quiet, without showing their usual pride,
if something appears of which they are afraid,
suddenly they leave the food alone,
because they are assailed by a greater concern;
so I saw that newly arrived company
leave the song and flee toward the slope,
like someone who goes without knowing where he will emerge;
nor was our departure any less swift.
