It was already the hour that turns longing
for sailors and softens the heart
on the day they have said farewell to dear friends;
and that the new pilgrim with love
pierces, if he hears a bell from afar
that seems to mourn the dying day;
when I began to make vain
my hearing and to gaze at one of the souls
risen, who asked with its hand for a hearing.
It joined and raised both palms,
fixing its eyes toward the east,
as if saying to God: ‘I care for nothing else’.
‘Before the ending of the light’ so devoutly
came from its mouth and with such sweet notes,
that it made me forget myself;
and the others then sweetly and devoutly
followed it through the whole hymn,
keeping their eyes upon the heavenly spheres.
Sharpen here, reader, your eyes well upon the truth,
for the veil is now indeed so thin,
that surely passing through it is easy.
I saw that noble host
then silently gaze upward,
as if awaiting, pale and humble;
and I saw come forth from on high and descend
two angels with two flaming swords,
truncated and deprived of their points.
Green as little leaves just newly born
were their garments, which by green wings
were struck and fanned as they trailed behind.
One came to rest a little above us,
and the other descended to the opposite bank,
so that the company was enclosed between them.
I could clearly discern their fair-haired heads;
but in their faces my sight lost itself,
like a faculty confounded by excess.
“Both come from the bosom of Mary”,
said Sordello, “to guard the valley,
because of the serpent that will come shortly”.
So I, not knowing by which path,
turned about, and drew very close,
all chilled, to the trusted shoulders.
And Sordello again: “Now let us descend
among the great shades, and we shall speak to them;
it will be most pleasing to them to see you”.
Only three steps, I think, had I descended,
and I was below, and saw one who gazed
only at me, as if he wished to know me.
It was already the time when the air grew dark,
but not so much that between his eyes and mine
it did not make clear what it had hidden before.
He came toward me, and I went toward him:
noble Judge Nino, how much it pleased me
when I saw you were not among the damned!
No kindly greeting was left unsaid between us;
then he asked: “How long is it since you came
to the mountain's foot across the distant waters?”.
“Oh!”, I said to him, “through the sorrowful places
I came this morning, and I am in my first life,
though the other, by journeying so, I gain”.
And when my answer was heard,
Sordello and he drew back
like people suddenly bewildered.
One turned to Virgil, and the other to one
who sat there, crying: “Up, Corrado!
come to see what God has willed through grace”.
Then, turned to me: “By that singular gratitude
that you owe to him who so conceals
his first cause that there is no crossing to it,
when you are beyond the broad waters,
tell my Giovanna to cry out for me
there where the innocent are answered.
I do not believe that her mother loves me any longer,
since she changed her white headbands,
which she must, poor woman, long for again.
Through her one may quite easily understand
how long the fire of love endures in woman,
if sight or touch does not often kindle it.
It will not make so fine a tomb for her,
the viper that leads the Milanese into the field,
as the cock of Gallura would have done”.
So he spoke, marked with the stamp,
upon his face, of that righteous zeal
which burns in measure in the heart.
My eager eyes kept moving toward the sky,
precisely where the stars are slowest,
like a wheel nearest its axle.
And my guide: “Son, what are you looking at up there?”.
And I to him: “At those three little flames
with which the pole on this side wholly burns”.
Then he to me: “The four bright stars
that you saw this morning are low on the other side,
and these have risen where those were”.
And as he spoke, Sordello drew him toward himself,
saying: “See there our adversary”;
and pointed his finger so that he would look that way.
On that side where there is no barrier to
the little valley, there was a snake,
perhaps like the one that gave Eve the bitter food.
Through the grass and flowers came the evil streak,
turning its head from time to time, and its back
licking like a beast that smooths itself.
I did not see, and therefore cannot tell,
how the heavenly hawks took flight;
but I clearly saw both of them in motion.
Hearing the air cleft by the green wings,
the serpent fled, and the angels turned,
flying back upward together to their stations.
The shade that had drawn close to the judge
when he called, throughout that entire assault
never ceased from gazing at me.
“May the lamp that leads you upward
find in your will as much wax
as is needed up to the highest enamel”,
it began, “if true news
of Val di Magra or some neighbouring part
you know, tell it to me, for I was once great there.
I was called Corrado Malaspina;
I am not the elder, but I descended from him;
toward my own I bore the love that is refined here”.
“Oh!”, I said to him, “through your lands
I have never been; but where does one dwell
throughout all Europe where they are not known?
The fame that honours your house
proclaims its lords and proclaims the region,
so that even one who has never been there knows of it;
and I swear to you, so may I go on high,
that your honoured family does not strip itself
of the distinction of purse and sword.
Custom and nature so favour it,
that, though the guilty head twists the world awry,
it alone goes straight and scorns the evil path”.
And he: “Now go; for the sun will not lie down again
seven times in the bed that the Ram
with all four feet covers and straddles,
before that courteous opinion
will be nailed into the middle of your head
with greater nails than another person's speech,
unless the course of judgment is halted”.
