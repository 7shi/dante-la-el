While I was anxious over my extinguished sight,
from the radiant flame that extinguished it
came a breath that made me attentive,
saying: “While you recover
the sight that you have exhausted upon me,
it is well that you compensate for it by reasoning.
Begin, then; and say where is directed
your soul, and reckon that
sight in you is lost and not dead:
because the lady who through this divine
region leads you has in her gaze
the power that the hand of Ananias had”.
I said: “At her pleasure, sooner or later,
let healing come to the eyes that were gates
when she entered with the fire with which I always burn.
The good that makes this court content,
is Alpha and O of all the writing
Love reads to me, whether softly or forcefully.”
That same voice which fear
had taken from me at the sudden dazzling,
made me eager to reason further;
and said: “Surely through a finer sieve
you must clarify yourself: you must say
who directed your bow towards such a target”.
And I: “Through philosophical arguments
and through authority that descends from here
such love must necessarily be impressed in me:
for the good, insofar as it is good, as it is understood,
so kindles love, and love so much greater
the more goodness it contains within itself.
Therefore towards the essence in which there is such superiority,
that every good found outside it
is nothing other than a light from its ray,
more than towards any other must move
the mind, in loving, of everyone who discerns
the truth on which this proof is founded.
Such truth is laid before my intellect
by him who demonstrates to me the first love
of all the everlasting substances.
The voice of the truthful author sets it forth,
who says to Moses, speaking of himself:
‘I shall make you see every excellence’.
You too set it forth for me, when beginning
the lofty proclamation that cries out the mystery
from here down there above every other proclamation”.
And I heard: “Through human intellect
and through authorities that accord with it,
the highest of your loves looks towards God.
But say further whether you feel other cords
drawing you towards him, so that you may declare
with how many teeth this love bites you”.
The holy intention was not hidden
of the eagle of Christ; rather, I perceived
where he wished to lead my profession.
Therefore I began again: “All those bites
that can make the heart turn towards God
have joined together in my charity:
for the being of the world and my own being,
the death that he endured so that I might live,
and what every faithful person hopes for, as I do,
together with the aforesaid living knowledge,
have drawn me from the sea of misdirected love,
and have set me on the shore of the right.
The leaves with which the whole garden is clothed,
of the eternal gardener, I love so much
as goodness is bestowed upon them by him.”
As soon as I fell silent, a most sweet song
resounded through heaven, and my lady
said with the others: “Holy, holy, holy!”.
And as one awakens at a piercing light
through the visual spirit that hastens back
to the brightness passing from layer to layer,
and the awakened person recoils from what he sees,
so unknowing is the sudden awakening
until the faculty of judgment comes to its aid;
so from my eyes every mote
Beatrice drove away with the ray of hers,
which shone from more than a thousand miles away:
so that afterwards I saw better than before;
and, almost stupefied, I asked
about a fourth light that I saw among us.
And my lady: “Within those rays
gazes lovingly upon its maker the first soul
that the primal power ever created”.
Like the bough that bends its top
as the wind passes, and then rises
through its own strength that lifts it,
so did I while she was speaking,
in amazement, and then I was reassured
by a desire to speak with which I burned.
And I began: “O fruit that ripe
alone were brought forth, O ancient father
to whom every bride is daughter and daughter-in-law,
as devoutly as I can, I implore you
to speak to me: you see my wish,
and, to hear you sooner, I do not express it”.
Sometimes a covered animal stirs,
so that its feeling must be apparent
through the covering's following its movements;
and similarly the first soul
made visible to me through its covering
how joyfully it came to please me.
Then it breathed: “Without its being expressed
by you, I discern your wish better
than you discern whatever is most certain to you;
because I see it in the truthful mirror
that makes of itself a likeness of other things,
and nothing makes of itself a likeness of him.
You wish to hear how long it is since God placed me
in the lofty garden, where this lady
prepared you for so long a stairway,
and how long it delighted my eyes,
and the true cause of the great displeasure,
and the language that I used and that I made.
Now, my son, not the tasting of the tree
was in itself the cause of so great an exile,
but only the passing beyond the boundary.
From the place whence your lady set Virgil in motion,
four thousand three hundred and two revolutions
of the sun I longed for this assembly;
and I saw it return to all the lights
along its path nine hundred and thirty
times, while I remained upon the earth.
The language that I spoke was wholly extinguished
before the work that could not be completed
occupied the people of Nimrod:
for no product of reason ever,
because of human pleasure, which renews itself
following the heavens, was lasting forever.
It is a natural operation that a human being speaks;
but in this way or that, nature leaves
you then to act according to what pleases you.
Before I descended to the anguish of hell,
I was the earthly name of the highest good
from whom comes the joy that clothes me;
and afterwards he was called El: and this is fitting,
because the usage of mortals is like a leaf
on a branch, which departs and another comes.
On the mountain that rises highest above the water,
I remained, with life pure and dishonoured,
from the first hour to the one that follows,
as the sun changes quadrant, the sixth hour.”
