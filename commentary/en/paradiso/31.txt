In the form, then, of a white rose
the holy host revealed itself to me
which in his blood Christ made his bride;
but the other, which in flight beholds and sings
the glory of him who fills it with love
and the goodness that made it so great,
just like a swarm of bees that enters flowers
at one time, and at another returns
to where its labour takes on sweetness,
descended into the great flower adorned
with so many petals, and from there rose again
to where its love forever dwells.
Their faces were all of living flame
and their wings of gold, and the rest so white,
that no snow attains that degree.
When they descended into the flower, from tier to tier
they imparted something of the peace and the ardour
that they acquired by fanning their sides.
Nor did the interposing between the height and the flower
of so great a flying multitude
impede the sight and the splendour:
for divine light is penetrating
throughout the universe according to its worthiness,
so that nothing can be an obstacle to it.
This secure and joyous kingdom,
thronged with people ancient and new,
had all its sight and love upon one mark.
O threefold light that in a single star
sparkling before their sight, so satisfies them!
look down here upon our storm!
If the barbarians, coming from a region
that each day is covered by Helice,
revolving with her son in whom she delights,
seeing Rome and its lofty works,
were amazed, when the Lateran
rose above all mortal things;
I, who to the divine from the human,
to the eternal from time had come,
and from Florence into a people just and sound,
with what astonishment must I have been filled!
certainly between that and joy it gave me
pleasure to hear nothing and remain silent.
And like a pilgrim who refreshes himself
by looking about the temple of his vow,
and already hopes to tell again how it stands,
passing through the living light,
I moved my eyes along the tiers,
now up, now down, and now circling round.
I saw faces disposed to charity,
adorned with another's light and their own smiles,
and gestures graced with every dignity.
The general form of paradise
my gaze had already wholly comprehended,
without yet remaining fixed on any part;
and I turned with rekindled desire
to ask my lady about things
concerning which my mind was in suspense.
One thing I intended, and another answered me:
I thought to see Beatrice, and I saw an elder
clothed like the people in glory.
He was suffused about the eyes and cheeks
with kindly joy, with a loving demeanour
such as befits a tender father.
And “Where is she?”, I immediately said.
At which he said: “To fulfil your desire
Beatrice moved me from my place;
and if you look up into the third circle
from the highest tier, you will see her again
upon the throne her merits allotted her.”
Without answering, I raised my eyes,
and saw her making herself a crown
by reflecting from herself the eternal rays.
From that region which thunders highest above
no mortal eye is so far distant,
however deep into the sea it sinks,
as my sight there was from Beatrice;
but it made no difference to me, for her image
did not descend to me mingled with a medium.
“O lady in whom my hope flourishes,
and who endured, for my salvation,
leaving your footprints in hell,
for all the many things that I have seen,
from your power and from your goodness
I acknowledge the grace and the strength.
You have drawn me from servitude into freedom
by all those paths, by all the means
through which you had the power to do this.
Preserve your munificence within me,
so that my soul, which you have made sound,
pleasing to you, may be released from the body.”
So I prayed; and she, as far away
as she appeared, smiled and looked at me;
then turned again to the eternal fountain.
And the holy elder: “So that you may complete
perfectly”, he said, “your journey,
for which prayer and holy love sent me,
fly with your eyes through this garden;
for seeing it will prepare your gaze
better for ascending along the divine ray.
And the queen of heaven, for whom I burn
wholly with love, will grant us every grace,
because I am her faithful Bernard.”
Like someone who perhaps from Croatia
comes to see our Veronica,
who, because of his long-standing hunger, cannot have enough of it,
but says within his thoughts, while it is displayed:
‘My Lord Jesus Christ, true God,
was your appearance really like this?’;
so was I as I gazed upon the living
charity of him who in this world,
through contemplation, tasted of that peace.
“Son of grace, this joyous state”,
he began, “will not be known to you,
keeping your eyes only down here at the bottom;
but look at the circles as far as the most remote,
until you see seated the queen
to whom this kingdom is subject and devoted.”
I raised my eyes; and as in the morning
the eastern part of the horizon
surpasses that where the sun declines,
so, as though going from valley to mountain
with my eyes, I saw a part at the highest edge
surpass in light all the rest of the front.
And as there where the chariot-pole is awaited
which Phaethon drove so badly, it grows more fiery,
and on either side the light grows dim,
so that peaceful oriflamme
grew more vivid at its centre, and on every side
equally diminished its flame;
and at that centre, with wings outspread,
I saw more than a thousand angels celebrating,
each distinct in brilliance and in art.
I saw there, at their play and at their songs,
a beauty smile, which was gladness
in the eyes of all the other saints;
and if I had such abundance in speech
as in imagination, I would not dare
attempt the least part of her delight.
Bernard, when he saw my eyes
fixed and intent upon the object of his burning love,
turned his own to her with such affection,
that he made mine more ardent to gaze again.
