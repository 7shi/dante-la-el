Overwhelmed with wonder, to my guide
I turned, like a little child who runs
always to where he trusts the most;
and she, like a mother who comes to the aid
at once of her pale and breathless son
with her voice, which is accustomed to soothe him,
said to me: “Do you not know that you are in heaven?
and do you not know that heaven is wholly holy,
and whatever is done here comes from righteous zeal?
How the singing would have transformed you,
and my smiling, you can now imagine,
since the cry has moved you so greatly;
in which, had you understood its prayers,
already you would know the vengeance
that you will see before you die.
The sword from up here cuts neither hastily
nor late, except in the judgment of the one
who awaits it with desire or with fear.
But turn now toward the others;
for you will see many illustrious spirits,
if, as I say, you turn your gaze.”
As she wished, I turned my eyes again,
and saw a hundred little spheres that together
grew more beautiful with their mutual rays.
I stood like one who represses within himself
the sting of desire, and does not venture
to ask, so much he fears asking too much;
and the largest and most luminous
of those pearls came forward,
to satisfy my desire concerning itself.
Then within it I heard: “If you could see
as I do the charity that burns among us,
your thoughts would be expressed.
But so that you, by waiting, may not delay
in reaching the lofty goal, I shall give you an answer
to your thought alone, since you are so restrained.
That mountain on whose slope Cassino stands
was once frequented at its summit
by people deceived and ill-disposed;
and I am the one who first brought up there
the name of him who brought to earth
the truth that raises us so high;
and so much grace shone upon me,
that I drew the surrounding villages away
from the impious worship that seduced the world.
These other flames, all contemplative
men, were kindled by that warmth
which brings forth holy flowers and fruits.
Here is Macarius, here is Romuald,
here are my brothers who within the cloisters
planted their feet and kept their hearts steadfast.”
And I to him: “The affection you show
in speaking with me, and the kindly aspect
that I see and note in all your flames,
have so expanded my confidence,
as the sun does the rose when, opened,
it becomes as wide as its power allows.
Therefore I pray you, and you, father, assure me
whether I may receive so much grace that I
may see you with your form unveiled.”
And he: “Brother, your lofty desire
will be fulfilled in the highest sphere,
where all the others are fulfilled, and mine.
There is perfect, ripe, and whole
every desire; in that alone
every part is where it always was,
because it is not in space and does not turn upon poles;
and our ladder reaches up to it,
which is why it vanishes thus from your sight.
As far as up there the patriarch saw it
Jacob extend its upper part,
when it appeared to him so laden with angels.
But now, to climb it, no one lifts
his feet from earth, and my rule
remains to waste the paper.
The walls that used to be an abbey
have become dens, and the cowls
are sacks full of bad flour.
But grievous usury does not rise so high
against God's pleasure as that revenue
which makes the hearts of monks so mad;
for whatever the Church holds, all of it
belongs to the people who ask for God's sake;
not to relatives, nor to anything more shameful.
The flesh of mortals is so yielding,
that down below a good beginning does not last
from the oak's first growth to its bearing acorns.
Peter began without gold and without silver,
and I with prayer and with fasting,
and Francis humbly his community;
and if you look at the beginning of each,
then look again at where it has strayed,
you will see white turned to black.
Yet Jordan turned backward
was more, and the sea's fleeing, when God willed,
marvelous to behold than the remedy here.”
Thus he spoke to me, and then withdrew
to his company, and the company drew close;
then, like a whirlwind, it all swept upward.
The gentle lady urged me after them
with a single sign, up that ladder,
so greatly did her power overcome my nature;
nor ever here below, where one rises and falls
naturally, was there motion so swift
that it could match my flight.
So may I ever return, reader, to that devout
triumph for which I often weep
for my sins and beat my breast,
you could not in as much time have withdrawn and put
your finger in the fire as that in which I saw the sign
that follows Taurus and was within it.
O glorious stars, O light pregnant
with great power, from which I acknowledge
all my intellect, whatever it may be,
With you was rising and with you setting
he who is the father of all mortal life,
when first I breathed the Tuscan air;
and then, when grace was granted me
to enter the lofty wheel that turns you,
your region was allotted to me.
To you devoutly now sighs
my soul, to acquire strength
for the difficult passage that draws it toward itself.
“You are so near the ultimate salvation,”
Beatrice began, “that you must
have your eyes clear and keen;
and therefore, before you enter further into it,
look down again, and see how much of the world
I have already set beneath your feet;
so that your heart, as joyful as it can be,
may present itself to the triumphant host
that comes rejoicing through this rounded ether.”
With my gaze I returned through all
the seven spheres, and saw this globe
such that I smiled at its paltry appearance;
and I approve as best that judgment
which holds it of least account; and whoever thinks of something else
can be called truly upright.
I saw Latona's daughter shining
without that shadow which had been for me the reason
why I once believed her rare and dense.
The sight of your son, Hyperion,
there I endured, and saw how there move
around and near him Maia and Dione.
From there appeared to me the tempering of Jupiter
between his father and his son; and from there became clear to me
the changes they make in their positions;
and all seven revealed to me
how great they are and how swift,
and how they stand in distant stations.
The little plot that makes us so fierce,
as I turned with the eternal Twins,
appeared to me entire, from the hills to the river mouths;
then I turned my eyes back to the beautiful eyes.
