When he who lights the whole world
descends so far from our hemisphere,
that daylight is consumed on every side,
the sky, which before is lit by him alone,
suddenly becomes visible again
through many lights, in which one shines;
and this act of heaven came to my mind,
when the emblem of the world and of its rulers
fell silent in its blessed beak;
because all those living lights,
shining far more brightly, began songs
that slip and fall away from my memory.
O sweet love that cloaks yourself in smiles,
how ardent you appeared in those flutes,
whose breath was only holy thoughts!
After the dear and shining gems
with which I saw the sixth light set
had silenced their angelic peals,
I seemed to hear the murmuring of a river
that descends clear down from stone to stone,
showing the abundance of its mountain source.
And as sound at the neck of the cithara
takes its form, and just as at the opening
of the pipe does the wind that enters,
so, with delay of waiting set aside,
that murmuring of the eagle rose
up through its neck, as though it were hollow.
It became a voice there, and from there issued
through its beak in the form of words,
such as the heart awaited where I wrote them.
“The part in me that sees and bears the sun
in mortal eagles,” it began to say to me,
“must now be gazed at steadily,
because of the fires from which I form my figure,
those through which the eye in my head sparkles,
they are the highest of all their ranks.
He who shines in the middle as the pupil,
was the singer of the Holy Spirit,
who transferred the ark from town to town:
now he knows the merit of his song,
insofar as it sprang from his own counsel,
through the reward that equals it.
Of the five who form a circle as my brow,
the one who comes closest to my beak,
comforted the poor widow for her son;
now he knows how dearly it costs
not to follow Christ, through the experience
of this sweet life and of its opposite.
And he who follows on the circumference
of which I speak, along the upper arc,
delayed death through true repentance:
now he knows that eternal judgment
does not change when worthy prayer
makes tomorrow there below of what was due today.
The next one, with the laws and with me,
under a good intention that bore bad fruit,
to yield to the shepherd, made himself Greek:
now he knows how the evil derived
from his good action does not harm him,
although the world is ruined by it.
And the one you see on the descending arc,
was William, whom that land mourns
which weeps over Charles and Frederick living:
now he knows how enamored becomes
heaven of the just king, and through the appearance
of his radiance he makes it visible still.
Who would believe down in the erring world
that Ripheus the Trojan in this circle
should be the fifth of the holy lights?
Now he knows much of what the world
cannot see of divine grace,
although his sight does not discern its depths.”
Like a lark that ranges through the air,
first singing, and then falls silent, content
with the last sweetness that satisfies it,
so seemed to me the image of the imprint
of eternal pleasure, at whose desire
each thing becomes what it is.
And although I was, to my doubt,
there like glass to the color that clothes it,
it could not bear to wait a while in silence,
but from my mouth, “What things are these?”
it drove by the force of its own weight:
at which I saw great celebrations of flashing light.
Then afterward, with its eye more brightly kindled,
the blessed emblem answered me,
so as not to keep me suspended in wonder:
“I see that you believe these things
because I say them, but you do not see how;
so that, though believed, they remain hidden.
You act like one who a thing by name
understands well, but its whatness
cannot see unless another reveals it.
The kingdom of heaven suffers violence
from ardent love and living hope,
which conquer the divine will:
not in the way that one person overpowers another,
but conquers it because it wishes to be conquered,
and, conquered, it conquers through its goodness.
The first soul of the brow and the fifth
make you marvel, because you see with them
the region of the angels adorned.
They did not leave their bodies, as you believe,
as pagans, but as Christians, in firm faith,
one in the feet that were to suffer, the other in those that had suffered.
For one from hell, whence there is no return
ever to a good will, returned to its bones;
and this was the reward of living hope:
of living hope, which put its power
into the prayers made to God to raise it,
so that its will might be moved.
The glorious soul of which we speak,
returned to the flesh, where it remained but briefly,
believed in him who could help it;
and in believing was kindled into such a fire
of true love, that at its second death
it was worthy to come to this joy.
The other, through grace that from so deep
a fountain drips, that never a creature
has thrust its eye as far as the first wave,
placed all his love down there in righteousness:
wherefore, from grace to grace, God opened for him
the eye to our future redemption;
thus he believed in it, and no longer endured
from that time on the stench of paganism;
and he rebuked the perverse peoples for it.
Those three women served him as baptism
whom you saw beside the right wheel,
more than a thousand years before baptizing.
O predestination, how remote
is your root from those sights
that do not see the first cause entire!
And you mortals, restrain yourselves
in judging: for we, who see God,
do not yet know all the elect;
and such a lack is sweet to us,
because our good is perfected in this good,
that what God wills, we too will.”
Thus from that divine image,
to make clear for me my short sight,
sweet medicine was given to me.
And as a good cithara-player makes a good singer
be accompanied by the quivering of the string,
through which the song gains greater pleasure,
so, while it spoke, I remember
that I saw the two blessed lights,
just as the blinking of eyes keeps time together,
move their little flames with its words.
