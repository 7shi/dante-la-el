‘To the Father, to the Son, to the Holy Spirit’,
began, ‘glory!’, all of paradise,
so that the sweet song intoxicated me.
What I saw seemed to me a smile
of the universe; so that my intoxication
entered through hearing and through sight.
Oh joy! oh ineffable gladness!
oh life complete in love and peace!
oh secure wealth without craving!
Before my eyes the four torches
stood burning, and the one that came first
began to grow more radiant,
and became in its appearance such
as Jupiter would become, if he and Mars
were birds and exchanged feathers.
Providence, which there assigns
turn and office, in the blessed choir
had imposed silence on every side,
when I heard: “If I change color,
do not marvel, for, as I speak,
you will see all these change color.
He who usurps on earth my place,
my place, my place, which stands vacant
in the presence of the Son of God,
has made of my burial ground a sewer
of blood and stench; at which the perverse one
who fell from up here, down there is appeased”.
With that color which, from the sun opposite,
paints a cloud at evening and at morning,
I then saw all heaven overspread.
And as an honest woman who remains
sure of herself, yet at another's failing,
merely hearing of it, grows bashful,
so Beatrice changed her countenance;
and such an eclipse I believe there was in heaven
when the supreme power suffered.
Then his words proceeded
in a voice so changed from itself,
that his appearance had changed no more:
“The bride of Christ was not nourished
with my blood, with Linus's, with that of Cletus,
to be used for the acquisition of gold;
but for the gaining of this joyful life
both Sixtus and Pius and Callistus and Urban
shed their blood after much weeping.
It was not our intention that on the right hand
of our successors one part should sit,
and another on the other side, of the Christian people;
nor that the keys that were granted to me,
should become an emblem on a banner
that fought against the baptized;
nor that I should be the image on a seal
for privileges sold and mendacious,
at which I often blush and flash with fire.
In a shepherd's garb, ravening wolves
are seen from up here throughout all pastures:
O defense of God, why do you still lie idle?
Of our blood, men of Cahors and Gascony
prepare themselves to drink: O good beginning,
to what vile end must you fall!
But high providence, which with Scipio
defended for Rome the glory of the world,
will soon bring aid, as I perceive;
and you, son, who because of your mortal weight
will yet return below, open your mouth,
and do not hide what I do not hide”.
Just as frozen vapors fall like snow
down through our air, when the horn
of the heavenly goat touches the sun,
upward I thus saw the ether adorned
become, and snow with triumphant vapors
that had made their stay there with us.
My sight followed their forms,
and followed until the intervening space, by its vastness,
denied it passage any farther.
Whereupon the lady, who saw me freed
from gazing upward, said to me: “Lower
your eyes and see how you have turned”.
From the time when I had first looked,
I saw that I had moved through all the arc
that the first clime makes from its middle to its end;
so that I saw beyond Cadiz the passage
mad, of Ulysses, and on this side near the shore
on which Europa became a sweet burden.
And more would have been revealed to me of the site
of this little plot; but the sun was moving
beneath my feet, a sign and more away.
My enamored mind, which communes in love
always with my lady, to turn back
my eyes to her burned more than ever;
and if nature or art made lures
to catch the eyes, so as to possess the mind,
in human flesh or in its images,
all gathered together would seem nothing
beside the divine beauty that shone upon me,
when I turned toward her smiling face.
And the power that her gaze bestowed on me,
tore me from Leda's lovely nest,
and thrust me into the swiftest heaven.
Its parts, most living and exalted,
are so uniform that I cannot say
which Beatrice chose for my place.
But she, who saw my desire,
began, smiling so joyfully,
that God seemed to rejoice in her face:
“The nature of the world, which keeps still
the center and moves all else around it,
begins here as from its boundary;
and this heaven has no other where
than the divine mind, in which is kindled
the love that turns it and the power that it rains down.
Light and love embrace it in a circle,
as this does the others; and that enclosure
only he who girds it understands.
Its motion is not measured by another,
but the others are measured by this,
as ten by its half and by its fifth;
and how time may hold in such a flowerpot
its roots and in the others its branches,
can now be manifest to you.
Oh greed, which submerges mortals
so far beneath you that no one has the power
to lift his eyes out of your waves!
The will indeed flowers in human beings;
but the continual rain turns
true plums into blighted fruit.
Faith and innocence are found
only in little children; then each
flees before their cheeks are covered.
One, while still stammering, fasts,
who later devours, with his tongue set free,
any food in any moon;
and one, while stammering, loves and listens to
his mother, who, with speech complete,
then longs to see her buried.
Thus the white skin turns black
at the first sight of the fair daughter
of him who brings morning and leaves evening.
You, so that you may not marvel,
consider that on earth there is no one who governs;
whence the human family goes so astray.
But before January passes wholly out of winter
because of the hundredth part neglected down there,
these supernal circles will send forth such rays,
that the storm that is so long awaited,
will turn the sterns to where the prows are,
so that the fleet will run straight;
and true fruit will come after the flower”.
