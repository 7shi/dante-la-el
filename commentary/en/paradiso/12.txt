As soon as the last word
was spoken by the blessed flame,
the holy mill began to turn;
and in its turning had not made a full round
before another ring enclosed it,
and matched motion with motion and song with song;
a song that so surpasses our muses,
our sirens, in those sweet trumpets,
as primal light its reflection.
As there curve through a delicate cloud
two arches, parallel and alike in color,
when Juno commands her handmaid,
the outer one arising from the inner one,
after the manner of the speech of that wandering nymph
whom love consumed as the sun consumes vapors,
and make people here foresee,
through the covenant that God made with Noah,
that the world will never again be flooded:
so of those everlasting roses
the two garlands turned about us,
and so the outer answered the inner.
After the dance and the other great rejoicing,
both in singing and in flashing
light with light, joyous and gentle,
had ceased together, at one instant and by one will,
just as the eyes, at the pleasure that moves them,
must close and lift together;
from the heart of one of the new lights
a voice arose, which to the needle pointing to the star
made me seem akin as I turned toward its source;
and began: “The love that makes me beautiful
draws me to speak of the other leader
on whose account such good is spoken here of mine.
It is fitting that, where one is, the other be brought in:
so that, as they campaigned together,
so their glory may shine together.
The army of Christ, which so dearly
cost to rearm, behind the banner
was moving slowly, doubtful and thin in its ranks,
when the emperor who reigns forever
made provision for the army, which was in peril,
through grace alone, not because it was worthy;
and, as has been said, came to his bride's aid
with two champions, at whose deeds, at whose words
the people who had strayed rallied.
In that region where rises to open
gentle Zephyr the new leaves
in which Europe is seen to clothe herself,
not very far from the beating of the waves
behind which, after its long course,
the sun at times hides itself from everyone,
stands fortunate Calaroga
under the protection of the great shield
in which the lion lies beneath and stands above:
within it was born the loving champion
of the Christian faith, the holy athlete
kind to his own and stern toward enemies;
and as soon as it was created, imbued was
his mind so deeply with living virtue
that, in his mother's womb, he made her a prophet.
After the marriage vows had been completed
at the sacred font between him and the Faith,
where they endowed each other with salvation,
the woman who gave assent for him
saw in sleep the wondrous fruit
that was to come from him and from his heirs;
and so that he might be what he was in meaning,
from here an inspiration arose to name him
with the possessive of the one to whom he wholly belonged.
Dominic he was called; and I speak of him
as of the husbandman whom Christ
chose for his garden to help him.
He truly seemed Christ's messenger and familiar:
for the first love that became manifest in him,
was for the first counsel that Christ gave.
Often he was silent and awake,
found upon the ground by his nurse,
as though he were saying: ‘I have come for this’.
Oh, his father truly Felice!
oh, his mother truly Giovanna,
if, interpreted, it means what is said!
Not for the world, for which people now toil
in pursuit of Ostiense and Taddeo,
but for love of the true manna
in a short time he became a great teacher;
so that he began to go about the vineyard
that soon turns white if the vine grower is bad.
And to the see that once was generous
more toward the righteous poor—not through its own fault,
but through him who occupies it, who goes astray,
not permission to pay two or three instead of six,
not the benefit of the first vacant position,
not tithes, which belong to God's poor,
did he ask, but against the erring world
permission to fight for the seed
of which twenty-four plants encircle you.
Then, with learning and determination together,
with the apostolic commission he set forth
like a torrent driven by a spring on high;
and against the heretical thickets struck
his force, most vigorously there
where the resistance was greatest.
From him then sprang various streams
by which the Catholic garden is watered,
so that its saplings stand more alive.
If such was one wheel of the two-wheeled chariot
in which Holy Church defended herself
and won in the field her internal conflict,
then surely quite plain to you should be
the excellence of the other, of whom Thomas
before my coming spoke so generously.
But the track that the highest part made
of its circumference is abandoned,
so that there is mold where there was wine-crust.
His family, which set out straight
with feet upon his tracks, has turned so far,
that it casts the foot in front toward the one behind;
and soon the harvest will be seen
of this bad cultivation, when the tares
will lament that the granary is denied them.
Yet I say, whoever searched leaf by leaf
our volume would still find a page
where he would read “I am what I have always been”;
but it will be neither from Casale nor from Acquasparta,
whence come such men to the written rule,
that one evades it and the other constricts it.
I am the soul of Bonaventure
of Bagnoregio, who in great offices
always put worldly concerns second.
Illuminato and Augustine are here,
who were among the first barefoot poor
who in the cord made themselves friends of God.
Hugh of Saint Victor is here with them,
and Peter the Eater and Peter of Spain,
who below shines in twelve little books;
Nathan the prophet and the metropolitan
Chrysostom and Anselm and that Donatus
who deigned to set his hand to the first art.
Rabanus is here, and beside me shines
the Calabrian abbot Joachim
endowed with the spirit of prophecy.
To praise so great a champion
I was moved by the ardent courtesy
of Brother Thomas and his discerning speech;
and moved this company along with me”.
