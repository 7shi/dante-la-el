After your Charles, fair Clemence,
had enlightened me, he told me of the deceits
that his offspring was to suffer;
but he said: “Be silent and let the years move on”;
so that I can say only that weeping
justly deserved will follow upon your wrongs.
And already the life within that holy light
had turned back to the Sun that fills it
as that good which suffices for all things.
Ah, deluded souls and impious creatures,
who turn your hearts away from such a good,
directing your brows toward vanity!
And behold, another of those splendors
came toward me, and its wish to please me
it signified by brightening outwardly.
The eyes of Beatrice, which were fixed
upon me, as before, of dear assent
to my desire made me certain.
“Ah, grant swift fulfillment to my wish,
blessed spirit”, I said, “and give me proof
that I can reflect in you what I am thinking!”.
Whereupon the light that was still new to me,
from its depths, whence it had sung before,
continued like one who delights in doing good:
“In that part of the wicked land
of Italy that lies between Rialto
and the springs of Brenta and Piave,
a hill rises, and does not rise very high,
from where once descended a little flame
that made a great assault upon the region.
From one root were born both I and it:
I was called Cunizza, and here I shine
because the light of this star overcame me;
but gladly I forgive myself
the cause of my allotted place, and it does not grieve me;
which might perhaps seem difficult to your common people.
Of this radiant and precious jewel
of our heaven that is nearest to me,
great fame remained; and before it dies,
this hundredth year will yet return five times:
see whether a person should make himself excellent,
so that the first life may leave another behind.
And this the present crowd does not consider,
whom Tagliamento and Adige enclose,
nor, though scourged, does it yet repent;
but soon it shall be that Padua at the marsh
will change the water that bathes Vicenza,
because its people are harsh toward their duty;
and where Sile and Cagnan join,
one lords it and goes with head held high,
for whom the net to catch him is already being made.
Feltre too will weep for the wrongdoing
of its impious shepherd, which will be shameful
so greatly that none entered Malta for its like.
Very large would be the vat
that could receive the blood of Ferrara,
and weary he who weighed it ounce by ounce,
which this courteous priest will give away
to show himself partisan; and such gifts
will conform to the way of life in that country.
Above are mirrors, which you call Thrones,
from which God in judgment shines upon us;
so that these words seem good to us”.
Here she fell silent; and gave me a sign
that she had turned to something else, by the wheeling
into which she entered as she had before.
The other joy, which was already known to me
as something precious, became to my sight
like a fine ruby struck by the sun.
Through rejoicing up there, radiance is gained,
as a smile is here; but below grows dark
the outward shade, as the mind is sorrowful.
“God sees all, and your sight enters into him”,
I said, “blessed spirit, so that no
desire can hide itself from you.
Then your voice, which delights heaven
always with the song of those devout fires
that make their cowls of six wings,
why does it not satisfy my desires?
Indeed I would not wait for your request,
if I could enter into you as you enter into me”.
“The greatest valley into which water spreads”,
his words then began,
“out of that sea which garlands the earth,
between discordant shores, against the sun,
extends so far that it makes a meridian
where it is wont first to make a horizon.
Upon the shore of that valley I dwelt
between Ebro and Magra, which in its short course
divides the Genoese from the Tuscan.
At almost the same sunset and sunrise
lie Bougie and the city from which I came,
which once made its harbor warm with its blood.
Folco was what those people called me to whom
my name was known; and this heaven
takes my imprint, as I took its;
for no more fiercely burned the daughter of Belus,
wronging both Sychaeus and Creusa,
than I, as long as it suited my hair;
nor that Rhodopean woman who was deceived
by Demophoon, nor Hercules
when he held Iole enclosed within his heart.
Yet here one does not repent, but rejoices,
not in the fault, which does not return to mind,
but in the power that ordered and provided.
Here one contemplates the art that adorns
so great an affection, and discerns the good
for which the world above turns the one below.
But so that all your wishes fulfilled
you may carry away, which were born in this sphere,
I must proceed still further.
You wish to know who is within this light
that here beside me sparkles so
like a ray of sunlight in pure water.
Now know that there within takes her rest
Rahab; and joined to our order,
our order is sealed with her in its highest rank.
By this heaven, in which comes to a point the shadow
that your world casts, before any other soul
of Christ's triumph she was taken up.
It was indeed fitting to leave her as a palm
in some heaven of the lofty victory
that was won with the one palm and the other,
because she furthered the first glory
of Joshua in the Holy Land,
which little touches the pope's memory.
Your city, which is a plant of him
who first turned his back upon his maker
and whose envy is so greatly wept for,
produces and spreads the accursed flower
that has led astray the sheep and the lambs,
because it has made a wolf of the shepherd.
For this the Gospel and the great doctors
are abandoned, and only to the Decretals
does study attend, as appears from their margins.
To this attend the pope and the cardinals;
their thoughts do not go to Nazareth,
there where Gabriel opened his wings.
But the Vatican and the other chosen parts
of Rome that have been a burial ground
for the soldiery that followed Peter,
will soon be free of the adultery”.
