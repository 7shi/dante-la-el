When both the children of Latona,
covered by the Ram and the Balance,
together make the horizon their girdle,
as long as from the moment when the zenith balances them
until the one and the other from that girdle,
changing hemispheres, slip out of balance,
so long, with her face painted with a smile,
Beatrice was silent, gazing
fixedly at the point that had overcome me.
Then she began: “I tell, and do not ask,
what you wish to hear, because I have seen it
where every where and every when converge.
Not to gain an increase of goodness for himself,
which cannot be, but so that his splendor
might, shining forth, say “I subsist”,
in his eternity outside time,
beyond every other comprehension, as pleased him,
eternal love opened itself into new loves.
Nor did he lie before as though in slumber;
for neither before nor after attended
the moving of God upon these waters.
Form and matter, joined together and unmixed,
came forth into existence that had no defect,
like three arrows from a three-stringed bow.
And as in glass, in amber or in crystal
a ray shines so that between its coming
and its being wholly there is no interval,
so the threefold effect of its lord
shone forth into its being all at once
without distinction in its beginning.
Order and structure were created together
with the substances; and those were highest
in the world in which pure actuality was produced;
pure potentiality occupied the lowest part;
in the middle, potentiality with actuality was bound
by such a bond that it is never unbound.
Jerome wrote for you of a long stretch
of centuries with the angels created
before the rest of the world was made;
but this truth is written in many places
by the writers of the Holy Spirit,
and you will perceive it if you look closely;
and reason too sees this to some extent,
for it would not allow that the movers
should remain so long without their perfection.
Now you know where and when these loves
were created, and how: so that quenched
in your desire already are three flames.
Nor could one reach twenty in counting
as quickly as a part of the angels
disturbed the substance underlying your elements.
The other remained, and began this art
that you discern, with such delight
that it never ceases from circling.
The beginning of the fall was the accursed
self-exaltation of him whom you saw
constrained by all the weights of the world.
Those whom you see here were humble
in acknowledging that they came from the goodness
that had made them ready for such great understanding:
therefore their vision was exalted
with illuminating grace and with their merit,
so that their will is firm and full;
and I do not wish you to doubt, but to be certain,
that receiving grace is meritorious
according to how open the affection is to it.
Now concerning this consistory
you can contemplate much, if my words
are gathered in, without further help.
But because on earth throughout your schools
it is taught that the angelic nature
is such that it understands and remembers and wills,
I shall speak further, so that you may see clearly
the truth that is confused down there,
through equivocation in such teaching.
These substances, once they rejoiced
in the face of God, did not turn their gaze
from it, from which nothing is hidden:
therefore they do not have their seeing interrupted
by a new object, and therefore there is no need
to recollect through a separate concept;
so that down there, without sleeping, people dream,
believing and not believing they speak truth;
but in the one there is more guilt and more shame.
You do not follow a single path down there
in philosophizing: so far are you carried
by the love of appearances and the thought of them!
And even this is endured here above
with less indignation than when set aside
is divine Scripture, or when it is distorted.
No thought is given there to how much blood it costs
to sow it in the world, and how pleasing is
whoever humbly draws close to it.
To make a show, each one strives and makes
his own inventions; and those are expounded
by the preachers, and the Gospel is left unspoken.
One says that the moon turned back
during Christ's passion and interposed itself,
so that the sun's light did not reach down below;
and he lies, for the light hid itself
of itself: therefore to the Spaniards and to the Indians
as to the Jews, such an eclipse was manifest.
Florence does not have as many Lapi and Bindi
as there are such fables each year
shouted from pulpits here and there:
so that the little sheep, who do not know,
return from pasture fed on wind,
and not seeing the harm does not excuse them.
Christ did not say to his first company:
“Go, and preach nonsense to the world”;
but gave them a foundation of truth;
and that alone resounded in their cheeks,
so that, in fighting to kindle the faith,
of the Gospel they made shield and lances.
Now men go with jokes and buffoonery
to preach, and provided there is a good laugh,
the hood swells, and nothing more is required.
But such a bird nests in the hood's tail,
that if the common people saw it, they would see
the pardon in which they place their trust:
through which such folly has grown on earth
that, without proof of any testimony,
people would rush to every promise.
On this Saint Anthony's pig grows fat,
and many others who are still more swinish,
paying with coin that bears no mint-mark.
But because we have strayed far, turn
your eyes now toward the straight road,
so that the way may shorten with the time.
This nature rises so far
in number that never was there mortal speech
or thought that could reach so far;
and if you look at what is revealed
through Daniel, you will see that in his thousands
a definite number is concealed.
The primal light, which irradiates it all,
is received within it in as many ways,
as there are splendors with which it joins.
Hence, because upon the act that comprehends
affection follows, the sweetness of loving
burns hot or warm within it in different degrees.
See now the height and the breadth
of the eternal power, since so many
mirrors it has made for itself in which it is refracted,
remaining one in itself as before.”
