Perhaps six thousand miles away
the sixth hour burns for us, and this world
already inclines its shadow almost to a level bed,
when the middle of the sky, deep above us,
begins to grow such that some star
loses its appearance even at this depth;
and as the most radiant handmaid comes
of the sun farther onward, so the sky closes
from sight to sight, up to the most beautiful.
Not otherwise the triumph that plays
forever around the point that overcame me,
seeming enclosed by that which it encloses,
little by little faded from my sight:
so that to turn my eyes back to Beatrice
seeing nothing and love compelled me.
If all that has been said of her thus far
were gathered wholly into a single praise,
it would be little to suffice for this occasion.
The beauty that I saw goes beyond measure
not merely beyond us, but certainly I believe
that only its maker enjoys it wholly.
At this passage I concede myself defeated
more than ever by a point in his theme
was comic or tragic poet overcome:
for, like sunlight in the sight that trembles most,
so the remembrance of her sweet smile
diminishes my mind's command of itself.
From the first day that I saw her face
in this life, up to this vision,
my song has not been barred from following;
but now my following must cease
any farther after her beauty, through poetry,
as every artist does at their utmost limit.
Such as I leave her to a greater proclamation
than that of my trumpet, which draws
its lofty matter toward its close,
with the bearing and voice of a ready leader
she began again: “We have come forth
from the greatest body into the heaven that is pure light:
intellectual light, full of love;
love of true good, full of joy;
joy that transcends every sweetness.
Here you will see both the one and the other host
of paradise, and one in those appearances
that you will see at the final judgment”.
Like a sudden flash that scatters
the visual spirits, so that it deprives
the eye of the power to see the strongest objects,
so living light shone all around me,
and left me wrapped in such a veil
of its radiance, that nothing appeared to me.
“Always the love that gives this heaven peace
welcomes into itself with such a greeting,
to make the candle ready for its flame”.
No sooner had there entered into me
these brief words than I understood
myself to rise above my own powers;
and I was rekindled with new sight
such that no light is so pure
that my eyes could not have withstood it;
and I saw light in the form of a river
tawny with radiance, between two banks
painted with wondrous springtime.
From that river living sparks came forth,
and on every side entered into the flowers,
like rubies that gold encircles;
then, as though intoxicated by the fragrances,
they plunged back into the wondrous flood,
and if one entered, another came out.
“The lofty desire that now inflames and urges you,
to gain knowledge of what you see,
pleases me the more, the more it swells;
but of this water you must drink
before so great a thirst in you is quenched”:
so said to me the sun of my eyes.
She also added: “The river and the topazes
that enter and emerge and the smiling of the plants
are shadow-bearing prefaces to their truth.
Not that these things are unripe in themselves;
but there is a deficiency on your side,
for you do not yet have sight so exalted”.
There is no infant that so suddenly rushes
with its face toward the milk, if it awakens
much later than its usual hour,
as I did, to make still better mirrors
of my eyes, bending toward the wave
that flows forth so that one may grow better in it;
and as soon as the rim drank of it
of my eyelids, so it appeared to me
to have become round from its length.
Then, like people who have been beneath masks,
who seem other than before, if they strip off
the likeness not their own in which they vanished,
so they changed for me into greater festivities
the flowers and the sparks, so that I saw
both courts of heaven made manifest.
O splendor of God, through which I saw
the lofty triumph of the true kingdom,
grant me power to tell how I saw it!
There is light up there that makes visible
the Creator to that creature
that has its peace only in seeing him.
It extends in a circular shape,
so far that its circumference
would be too broad a girdle for the sun.
Its whole appearance is formed of a ray
reflected from the summit of the first moving sphere,
which draws from it life and power.
And as a hillside in the water at its foot
mirrors itself, as though to see itself adorned,
when it is rich in greenery and little flowers,
so, standing above the light on every side,
I saw mirrored in more than a thousand tiers
all of us who have returned up there.
And if the lowest tier contains within itself
so great a light, how great is the breadth
of this rose in its outermost petals!
My sight in the breadth and in the height
did not lose itself, but took in all
the measure and the nature of that gladness.
Near and far, there, neither adds nor takes away:
for where God governs without intermediary,
natural law has no bearing.
Into the yellow of the everlasting rose,
which rises tier on tier and spreads and breathes
a fragrance of praise to the sun that makes eternal spring,
like one who is silent and wishes to speak,
Beatrice drew me, and said: “Behold
how great is the assembly of the white robes!
See how widely our city extends;
see our seats so full,
that few more people are awaited here.
And in that great seat on which you fix your eyes
because of the crown already placed upon it,
before you dine at this wedding feast,
will sit the soul that will be imperial below,
of lofty Henry, who to set Italy straight
will come before she is ready.
The blind greed that bewitches you
has made you like the little child
who dies of hunger and drives away the nurse.
And there will be a prefect in the divine court
then such that openly and secretly
he will not walk one path with him.
But only a little longer will he be tolerated by God
in the holy office; for he will be thrust down
where Simon Magus is according to his deserts,
and will make the one from Anagni go farther down”.
