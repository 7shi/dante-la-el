Already was rejoicing alone in his own thought
that blessed mirror, and I was tasting
my own, tempering the bitter with the sweet;
and that lady who was leading me to God
said: “Change your thought; consider that I am
near him who relieves every wrong”.
I turned toward the loving sound
of my comfort; and what love I then saw
in her holy eyes, here I leave untold:
not merely because I distrust my speech,
but because the mind cannot return
so far above itself, unless another guide it.
This much I can retell of that moment,
that, gazing at her, my affection
was freed from every other desire,
so long as the eternal delight, which directly
shone into Beatrice, from her beautiful face
was satisfying me with its reflected appearance.
Overcoming me with the light of a smile,
she said to me: “Turn and listen;
for paradise is not only in my eyes”.
As here at times one sees
affection in the face, if it is so great
that the whole soul is taken up by it,
so in the flaming of the holy radiance,
toward which I turned, I recognized the desire
in him to speak to me a little more.
He began: “On this fifth threshold
of the tree that lives from its summit
and always bears fruit and never loses a leaf,
are blessed spirits, who below, before
they came to heaven, were of great renown,
so that every muse would be enriched by them.
Therefore look at the arms of the cross:
the one whom I shall name will there perform the act
that its swift fire performs within a cloud”.
I saw a light drawn along the cross
by the naming of Joshua, as soon as it was done;
nor did I perceive the word before the deed.
And at the name of the exalted Maccabee
I saw another move, revolving,
and gladness was the whip that drove the top.
Thus for Charlemagne and for Roland
my attentive gaze followed two of them,
as an eye follows its falcon in flight.
Then William and Rainouart drew
and Duke Godfrey my sight
along that cross, and Robert Guiscard.
Then, moving and mingling among the other lights,
the soul that had spoken to me showed me
what an artist it was among the singers of heaven.
I turned toward my right side
to see in Beatrice my duty,
indicated either by speech or by gesture;
and I saw her eyes so pure,
so joyful, that her appearance
surpassed her other and her latest accustomed appearances.
And as, through feeling greater delight
in doing good, a man from day to day
perceives that his virtue is advancing,
so I perceived that my circling
together with the heaven had widened its arc,
seeing that miracle more richly adorned.
And as is the change in a brief span
of time in a fair-skinned woman, when her face
unburdens itself of the weight of shame,
such was the change before my eyes, when I turned,
because of the whiteness of the temperate star,
the sixth, which had received me within itself.
I saw within that Jovian torch
the sparkling of the love that was there
trace out our language before my eyes.
And as birds risen from a riverbank,
as though rejoicing together over their feeding grounds,
make of themselves now a round formation, now another,
so holy creatures within the lights
sang as they flew, and made themselves
now D, now I, now L in their formations.
First, singing, they moved to their own music;
then, becoming one of these signs,
they paused a little and fell silent.
O divine Pegasean muse, who make minds
glorious and grant them lasting life,
and they, with you, cities and kingdoms,
illuminate me with yourself, so that I may render
their figures as I have apprehended them:
let your power appear in these brief verses!
They showed themselves, then, in five times seven
vowels and consonants; and I noted
the parts just as they appeared to me to be spoken.
‘LOVE JUSTICE’, first
were the verb and noun of the whole inscription;
‘YOU WHO JUDGE THE EARTH’, were last.
Then in the M of the fifth word
they remained arranged; so that Jupiter
looked like silver marked there with gold.
And I saw other lights descend where
the top of the M was, and there come to rest,
singing, I believe, the good that draws them to itself.
Then, as when burning logs are struck,
innumerable sparks rise up,
from which fools are accustomed to draw omens,
more than a thousand seemed to rise from there,
lights ascending, some far and some a little,
just as the sun that kindles them allotted them;
and when each had settled in its place,
I saw the head and neck of an eagle
depicted by that patterned fire.
He who paints there has no one to guide him;
but he himself guides, and from him is recalled
that power which gives form within the nests.
The other blessed host, which seemed content
at first to form a lily upon the M,
with a little movement followed the outline.
O sweet star, what splendid and numerous gems
showed me that our justice
is an effect of the heaven that you adorn like a gem!
Therefore I pray to the mind in which originate
your motion and your power, that it look again
at the source of the smoke that corrupts your ray;
so that now once again he may be angry
at the buying and selling within the temple
which was built with miracles and martyrdoms.
O host of heaven whom I contemplate,
pray for those who are on earth,
all led astray by bad example!
Once it was customary to wage war with swords;
but now it is waged by taking away here or there
the bread that the loving Father withholds from no one.
But you who write only in order to erase,
consider that Peter and Paul, who died
for the vineyard you lay waste, are still alive.
You may indeed say: “I have fixed my desire
so firmly on him who chose to live alone
and who through dancing was brought to martyrdom,
that I know neither the fisherman nor Paul”.
