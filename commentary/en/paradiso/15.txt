A benevolent will, in which dissolves
always the love that breathes rightly,
as covetousness does in the wicked one,
imposed silence upon that sweet lyre,
and made the holy strings fall quiet
which heaven's right hand loosens and tightens.
How shall they be deaf to just prayers,
those substances which, to give me the desire
to pray to them, agreed to fall silent?
It is right that without end he should grieve
who, for love of a thing that does not last
eternally, strips himself of that love.
As through the tranquil and pure clear skies
a sudden fire darts from time to time,
stirring the eyes that were at rest,
and seems a star that changes place,
except that from the region where it flares
none is lost, and it endures but briefly:
so from the arm that stretches to the right
to the foot of that cross there sped a star
of the constellation that shines there;
nor did the gem leave its ribbon,
but along the radiant strip it sped,
so that it seemed fire behind alabaster.
So lovingly did the shade of Anchises present himself,
if our greatest Muse deserves belief,
when in Elysium he became aware of his son.
“O blood of mine, O poured abundantly
grace of God, for whom, as for you,
was heaven's gate ever opened twice?”
Thus spoke that light: whereupon I turned my attention to him;
then I turned my face toward my lady,
and on this side and that I was astonished;
for within her eyes there burned a smile
such that I thought with mine to touch the depth
of my glory and of my paradise.
Then, joyful to hear and to behold,
the spirit added to his opening words things
that I did not understand, so profoundly did he speak;
nor did he conceal himself from me by choice,
but of necessity, since his thought
rose beyond the range of mortals.
And when the bow of his burning affection
had so discharged itself that his speech descended
toward the mark of our understanding,
the first thing that I understood
was, “Blessed be you, three and one,
who are so gracious toward my seed!”
And he continued: “A welcome and long-standing hunger,
drawn from reading the mighty volume
where neither white nor dark is ever changed,
you have appeased, my son, within this light
in which I speak to you, thanks to her
who clothed you with feathers for the lofty flight.
You believe that your thought flows to me
from him who is first, just as there radiate
from one, if it is understood, five and six;
and therefore who I am and why I seem
more joyful to you, you do not ask me,
than any other in this happy throng.
You believe the truth; for the lesser and the great
of this life gaze into the mirror
in which, before you think, you disclose your thought;
but so that the sacred love in which I keep watch
with perpetual vision and which makes me thirst
with sweet desire may be more fully satisfied,
let your voice, confident, bold, and glad,
sound forth your will, sound forth your desire,
to which my answer is already decreed!”
I turned to Beatrice, and she heard
before I spoke, and smiled me a sign
that made the wings of my desire grow.
Then I began thus: “Affection and understanding,
when the primal Equality appeared to you,
became of equal weight for each of you,
because the sun that illumined and kindled you
is so equal in its heat and light
that all comparisons fall short.
But desire and capacity in mortals,
for the reason that is evident to you,
are differently feathered in their wings;
so I, who am mortal, feel myself in this
inequality, and therefore do not give thanks
except with my heart for the fatherly welcome.
I do beseech you, living topaz
who adorn this precious jewel,
that you satisfy me with your name.”
“O branch of mine in which I took delight
even in anticipation, I was your root”:
such a beginning, in reply, he made to me.
Then he said to me: “He from whom is taken
your family name, and who a hundred years and more
has circled the mountain on the first terrace,
was my son and was your great-grandfather:
it is indeed fitting that his long toil
you should shorten with your works.
Florence within the ancient circuit,
from which she still takes tierce and nones,
dwelt in peace, sober and chaste.
She had no necklace, no crown,
no embroidered gowns, no girdle
that was more to look at than the person.
Did not yet, at birth, cause fear
the daughter to her father, for the age and the dowry
did not, on either side, depart from due measure.
She had no houses empty of families;
Sardanapalus had not yet come there
to show what can be done in the bedchamber.
Montemalo had not yet been surpassed
by your Uccellatoio, which, as it is surpassed
in rising, so it will be in falling.
I saw Bellincion Berti go girded
with leather and bone, and come from the mirror
his wife without a painted face;
and I saw the men of the Nerli and the Vecchio
content with uncovered skins,
and their wives with spindle and distaff.
O fortunate women! Each was certain
of her burial place, and none as yet
was deserted in bed on account of France.
One kept watch, tending the cradle,
and, soothing, used the language
that first delights fathers and mothers;
another, drawing the tresses from the distaff,
would tell stories with her household
of the Trojans, of Fiesole, and of Rome.
There would then have been considered such a marvel
a Cianghella, a Lapo Salterello,
as Cincinnatus and Cornelia would be now.
To so peaceful, to so beautiful
a life of citizens, to so faithful
a citizenry, to so sweet a dwelling,
Mary gave me birth, invoked with loud cries;
and in your ancient Baptistery
I became at once a Christian and Cacciaguida.
Moronto was my brother, and Eliseo;
my wife came to me from the valley of the Po,
and from there your surname was derived.
Then I followed the emperor Conrad;
and he girded me with his knighthood,
so much through good deeds did I win his favor.
Behind him I went against the wickedness
of that law whose people usurp,
through the shepherds' fault, your rightful claim.
There I was by that foul people
released from the deceitful world,
whose love defiles many souls;
and came from martyrdom to this peace”.
