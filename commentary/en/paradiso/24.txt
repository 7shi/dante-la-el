“O fellowship chosen for the great supper
of the blessed Lamb, who feeds you
so that your desire is always full,
if by the grace of God this man foretastes
of what falls from your table,
before death prescribes his time,
give heed to his immense affection
and bedew him a little: you drink
always from the source whence comes what he thinks”.
Thus Beatrice; and those joyful souls
became spheres upon fixed poles,
flaming, as they turned, like comets.
And as wheels in the mechanism of clocks
turn so that the first, to one who attends,
seems motionless, and the last to fly;
so those circling dances, different-
ly dancing, of their richness
made me judge, swift and slow.
From that which I marked as most precious
I saw come forth a fire so happy,
that it left none there of greater brightness;
and three times around Beatrice
it turned with a song so divine,
that my imagination cannot retell it to me.
Therefore the pen skips, and I do not write it:
for our imagination, for such folds,
let alone speech, is too vivid a color.
“O holy sister mine, who so entreat us
devoutly, by your ardent affection
you loose me from that lovely sphere”.
Then, having stopped, the blessed fire
directed toward my lady the breath
that spoke thus as I have said.
And she: “O eternal light of the great man
to whom Our Lord left the keys,
which he brought down, of this wondrous joy,
test this man on points light and weighty,
as you please, concerning faith,
through which you walked upon the sea.
Whether he loves well and hopes and believes well,
is not hidden from you, because your sight is there
where everything is seen depicted;
but because this kingdom has made citizens
through the true faith, to glorify it,
it is well that speaking of it should fall to him”.
Just as the bachelor arms himself and does not speak
until the master proposes the question,
to support it, not to settle it,
so I armed myself with every argument
while she spoke, to be ready
for such a questioner and such a profession.
“Speak, good Christian, make yourself manifest:
what is faith?”. Whereupon I raised my brow
toward that light whence this was breathed;
then I turned to Beatrice, and she an encouraging
countenance showed me, that I should pour
the water forth from my inward spring.
“The Grace that grants me to confess myself”,
I began, “before the high captain,
make my thoughts well expressed”.
And I continued: “As the truthful pen
of your dear brother wrote for us, father,
who with you set Rome upon the good path,
faith is the substance of things hoped for
and evidence of things not appearing;
and this seems to me its essence”.
Then I heard: “You think rightly,
if you understand well why he placed it
among substances, and then among evidences”.
And I then: “The profound things
that here bestow their appearance upon me,
are so hidden from the eyes down there,
that their being there is in belief alone,
upon which high hope is founded;
and therefore it takes the meaning of substance.
And from this belief we must
reason syllogistically, without having other sight:
therefore it holds the meaning of evidence”.
Then I heard: “If whatever is acquired
below through teaching were thus understood,
there would be no place there for a sophist's wit”.
Thus came the breath from that burning love;
then it added: “Quite well have been examined
of this coin, already, the alloy and the weight;
but tell me whether you have it in your purse”.
Whereupon I: “Yes, I have it, so bright and so round,
that nothing in its stamp becomes doubtful to me”.
Next came forth from the profound light
that shone there: “This precious jewel
upon which every virtue is founded,
whence did it come to you?”. And I: “The abundant rain
of the Holy Spirit, which is poured
upon the old and upon the new parchments,
is a syllogism that has established it for me
so keenly that beside it
every demonstration seems dull to me”.
I then heard: “The ancient and the new
proposition that so convinces you,
why do you hold it to be divine speech?”.
And I: “The proof that discloses the truth to me
is the works that followed, for which nature
never heats iron nor strikes an anvil”.
I was answered: “Say, who assures you
that those works occurred? The very same thing
that seeks to be proved, nothing else, swears it to you”.
“If the world turned to Christianity”,
I said, “without miracles, this one
is such that the others are not a hundredth of it:
for you entered poor and fasting
into the field, to sow the good plant
that once was a vine and now is made a thornbush”.
This ended, the high holy court
resounded through the spheres with a ‘We praise God’
in the melody that is sung up there.
And that baron who thus from branch to branch,
examining me, had already drawn me,
so that we were nearing the highest leaves,
began again: “The Grace that holds loving converse
with your mind has opened your mouth
until now as it should be opened,
so that I approve what has come forth;
but now you must express what you believe,
and whence it presented itself to your belief”.
“O holy father, and spirit who see
what you so believed that you outstripped
younger feet toward the sepulcher”,
I began, “you wish me to make manifest
here the form of my ready belief,
and you also asked its cause.
And I answer: I believe in one God
alone and eternal, who moves all heaven,
unmoved, with love and with desire;
And for such belief I have not only proofs
physical and metaphysical, but it is given me
also by the truth that rains down from here
through Moses, through prophets and through psalms,
through the Gospel and through you who wrote
after the burning Spirit made you holy;
and I believe in three eternal persons, and these
I believe one essence so one and so threefold,
that it admits together ‘are’ and ‘is’.
Of the profound divine nature
which I now touch upon, my mind is sealed
many times by the teaching of the Gospel.
This is the beginning, this is the spark
that then spreads into a living flame,
and like a star in heaven sparkles in me”.
As the lord who hears what pleases him,
then embraces the servant, rejoicing
at the news, as soon as he falls silent;
so, blessing me in song,
three times encircled me, as soon as I fell silent,
the apostolic light at whose command
I had spoken: so greatly in speaking did I please him!
