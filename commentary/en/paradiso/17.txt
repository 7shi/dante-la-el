As came to Clymene, to make certain
of what he had heard against himself,
he who still makes fathers sparing toward their sons;
such was I, and such was I perceived
both by Beatrice and by the holy lamp
that earlier had changed its place for me.
Therefore my lady, “Send forth the flame
of your desire”, she said to me, “so that it may issue
clearly marked with the inward stamp:
not so that our knowledge may increase
through your speaking, but so that you may accustom yourself
to declare your thirst, so that someone may pour for you.”
“O dear root of mine, who rise so high
that, as earthly minds see
that a triangle cannot contain two obtuse angles,
so you see contingent things
before they exist in themselves, by gazing upon the point
to which all times are present;
while I was joined to Virgil
up on the mountain that heals souls
and descending into the world of the dead,
there were spoken to me about my future life
grave words, although I feel myself
truly foursquare against the blows of fortune;
therefore my desire would be satisfied
to understand what fortune draws near me:
for an arrow foreseen comes more slowly”.
Thus I spoke to that same light
that earlier had spoken to me; and as was wished
by Beatrice, my desire was confessed.
Not through riddles, in which foolish people
once were ensnared before he was slain,
the Lamb of God who takes away sins,
but in clear words and with precise
speech that paternal love replied,
concealed and revealed by its own smile:
“Contingency, which beyond the book
of your material world does not extend,
is wholly pictured in the eternal sight;
yet it does not thereby acquire necessity
any more than from the sight in which it is reflected
a ship that travels downstream.
From there, just as there comes to the ear
sweet harmony from an organ, there comes to me
into view the time being prepared for you.
As Hippolytus departed from Athens
because of his pitiless and treacherous stepmother,
so you must depart from Florence.
This is desired, and this is already being sought,
and soon it will be accomplished for the one plotting it
there where Christ is traded every day.
Blame will follow the injured party
in public outcry, as is usual; but vengeance
will bear witness to the truth that dispenses it.
You will leave every beloved thing
most dearly; and this is that arrow
which the bow of exile first shoots.
You will learn how salty tastes
another's bread, and how hard a path is
going down and climbing up another's stairs.
And what will weigh most heavily on your shoulders,
will be the wicked and foolish company
with which you will fall into this valley;
for, wholly ungrateful, wholly mad and impious,
it will turn against you; but, shortly afterward,
it, not you, will have its temples reddened as a result.
Of its brutishness its own course of action
will furnish proof; so that it will be honorable for you
to have made yourself a party unto yourself.
Your first refuge and your first lodging
will be the courtesy of the great Lombard
who bears the sacred bird upon the ladder;
who will hold you in such kindly regard,
that, between doing and asking, between you two,
the one that among others comes later will come first.
With him you will see the one who was imprinted,
at birth, so strongly by this star,
that his deeds will be remarkable.
People have not yet taken notice of him
because of his young age, for only nine years
have these spheres revolved around him;
but before the Gascon deceives the noble Henry,
sparks of his excellence will appear
in taking no account of money or hardships.
Known his magnificent deeds
will yet be, so that his enemies
will not be able to keep their tongues silent about them.
Look to him and to his benefactions;
through him many people will be transformed,
rich and poor changing their condition;
and from here you will carry written in your mind
things of him, and will not tell them”; and he said things
incredible to those who will be present.
Then he added: “Son, these are the explanations
of what was said to you; behold the snares
that lie hidden behind a few revolutions.
Yet I would not have you envy your neighbors,
since your life stretches into the future
far beyond the punishment of their treacheries”.
When, by falling silent, it showed itself finished,
the holy soul, with setting the weft
into that fabric I had offered it with the warp prepared,
I began, like one who longs,
in doubt, for counsel from a person
who sees and wills rightly and loves:
“I see clearly, my father, how
time spurs toward me, to deal me a blow
such as falls harder on him who gives way most;
therefore it is well that I arm myself with foresight,
so that, if the place dearest to me is taken away,
I should not lose the others through my verses.
Down through the world bitter without end,
and over the mountain from whose fair summit
my lady's eyes lifted me,
and then through heaven, from light to light,
I have learned what, if I repeat it,
will taste sharply sour to many;
and if I am a timid friend to truth,
I fear losing life among those
who will call this time ancient.”
The light in which my treasure smiled,
which I found there, first grew brilliant,
like a golden mirror in a ray of sunlight;
then it replied: “A conscience darkened
by either its own or another's shame
will indeed find your words harsh.
But nevertheless, with every falsehood removed,
make your entire vision manifest;
and just let them scratch where the mange is.
For if your voice will be troublesome
at the first taste, life-giving nourishment
it will leave afterward, when it has been digested.
This cry of yours will act like wind,
which strikes the highest treetops hardest;
and this provides no small ground for honor.
Therefore there have been shown to you in these spheres,
on the mountain and in the sorrowful valley,
only souls that are known to fame,
for the mind of the one who hears does not rest
nor establish firm belief through an example that has
its root unknown and hidden,
nor through another argument that is not evident.”
