O senseless care of mortals,
how defective are the syllogisms
those that make you beat your wings downward!
Some after law and some after aphorisms
went their way, and some pursuing priesthood,
and some to rule by force or by sophistries,
and some to rob and some to civic business,
some, in the delight of the flesh enwrapped,
labored, and some gave themselves to idleness,
when, released from all these things,
with Beatrice I was up in heaven
so gloriously received.
After each had returned to the
point of the circle where he had been before,
he stood still, like a candle in a candlestick.
And I heard within that light
which first had spoken to me, smiling,
a voice begin, growing purer:
“Just as I shine with its ray,
so, gazing into the eternal light,
I perceive whence you derive your thoughts.
You are in doubt, and wish that there be sifted
in language so clear and so expansive
my saying, so that it may be laid open to your understanding,
where before I said: ‘Where one grows fat’,
and where I said: ‘No second was born’;
and here there is need to distinguish well.
Providence, which governs the world
with that counsel in which every vision
of created beings is vanquished before it reaches the depths,
so that she might go toward her beloved,
the bride of him who, with loud cries,
wedded her with his blessed blood,
secure in herself and also more faithful to him,
she appointed two princes in her favor,
who on this side and on that would serve as her guides.
The one was wholly seraphic in ardor;
the other, through wisdom, on earth was
a radiance of cherubic light.
Of one I shall speak, for of both
one speaks in praising either, whichever one chooses,
because their works were directed to one end.
Between the Tupino and the water that descends
from the hill chosen by blessed Ubaldo,
a fertile slope hangs from a lofty mountain,
from which Perugia feels both cold and heat
through Porta Sole; and behind it lament
beneath a heavy yoke Nocera and Gualdo.
From this slope, where it breaks
most its steepness, a sun was born into the world,
as this one sometimes does from the Ganges.
Therefore whoever speaks of that place,
should not say Ascesi, for that would say too little,
but Orient, if he wishes to speak properly.
He was not yet very far from his rising,
when he began to make the earth feel
some comfort from his great virtue;
for, on behalf of such a lady, as a young man, into war
with his father he rushed, to whom, as to death,
no one unlocks the door of pleasure;
and before his spiritual court
and in his father's presence he united himself to her;
then day by day he loved her more deeply.
She, deprived of her first husband,
for eleven hundred years and more, despised and obscure,
until this man remained without invitation;
nor did it avail to hear that he found her secure
with Amyclas, at the sound of his voice,
he who made all the world afraid;
nor did it avail to be steadfast or fierce,
so that, where Mary remained below,
she wept with Christ upon the cross.
But so that I may not proceed too obscurely,
Francis and Poverty as these lovers
now understand in my extended discourse.
Their concord and their joyful countenances,
love and wonder and sweet regard
gave rise to holy thoughts;
so much so that the venerable Bernard
first took off his shoes, and after such great peace
ran, and, running, seemed to himself to be slow.
O unknown riches! O fruitful good!
Giles takes off his shoes, Sylvester takes off his shoes
behind the bridegroom, so pleasing is the bride.
Then that father and that master goes his way
with his lady and with that family
already bound by the humble cord.
Nor did faintheartedness weigh down his brows
because he was the son of Pietro Bernardone,
nor because he appeared wondrously despised;
but royally his austere intention
he disclosed to Innocent, and from him received
the first seal upon his religious order.
After the little band of poor folk grew
behind this man, whose wondrous life
would better be sung in the glory of heaven,
adorned with a second crown
was, through Honorius, by the Eternal Spirit
the holy purpose of this shepherd of the flock.
And after, through thirst for martyrdom,
in the proud presence of the Sultan
he preached Christ and the others who followed him,
and finding unripe for conversion
too much the people, and so as not to remain in vain,
he returned to the fruit of the Italian grass,
on the rugged rock between Tiber and Arno
from Christ he received the final seal,
which his limbs bore for two years.
When it pleased him who had allotted him to such great good
to draw him up to the reward
that he earned by making himself lowly,
to his brothers, as to rightful heirs,
he entrusted his dearest lady,
and commanded that they love her faithfully;
and from her lap his illustrious soul
wished to depart, returning to its kingdom,
and for his body he wished no other bier.
Consider now what manner of man he was who a worthy
colleague was in keeping the bark
of Peter on the high seas upon a straight course;
and this was our patriarch;
therefore whoever follows him, as he commands,
you can discern that he loads good merchandise.
But his flock for new food
has grown greedy, so that it cannot be
that it does not scatter over different pastures;
and the more his sheep, far removed
and wandering, go farther from him,
the emptier of milk they return to the fold.
There are indeed some who fear the harm
and draw close to the shepherd; but they are so few,
that little cloth supplies their cowls.
Now, if my words are not faint,
if your hearing has been attentive,
if you recall to mind what has been said,
your wish will in part be satisfied,
for you will see the tree from which the splinter is cut,
and you will see what the qualification implies:
‘Where one grows fat, if one does not stray.’”
