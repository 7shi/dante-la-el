“Virgin Mother, daughter of your son,
humble and exalted beyond any creature,
fixed goal of eternal counsel,
you are she who human nature
so ennobled, that its maker
did not disdain to become its creature.
In your womb love was rekindled,
through whose warmth in eternal peace
this flower has thus blossomed.
Here you are for us a noonday torch
of charity, and below, among mortals,
you are a living fountain of hope.
Lady, you are so great and have such worth,
that whoever desires grace and does not turn to you,
his longing seeks to fly without wings.
Your kindness not only comes to the aid
of whoever asks, but many times
freely runs ahead of the asking.
In you is mercy, in you compassion,
in you magnificence, in you is gathered
whatever goodness there is in any creature.
Now this man, who from the lowest pit
of the universe as far as here has seen
the spiritual lives one by one,
begs you, through grace, for strength
so great that he may rise with his eyes
higher toward the ultimate salvation.
And I, who never burned for my own seeing
more than I do for his, all my prayers
offer you, and pray that they may not fall short,
so that you may dispel for him every cloud
of his mortality with your prayers,
so that the highest delight may unfold before him.
Further I pray to you, queen, who can
whatever you will, that you preserve sound,
after so great a vision, his affections.
Let your protection overcome human impulses:
see Beatrice with so many blessed souls
clasping their hands to you in support of my prayers!”.
The eyes beloved and revered by God,
fixed on the speaker, showed us
how pleasing devout prayers are to her;
then they turned toward the eternal light,
into which one must not believe there is directed
by any creature an eye so clear.
And I, who to the end of all desires
was drawing near, as I ought,
brought the ardor of desire within me to its height.
Bernard was signaling to me, and smiling,
that I should look upward; but I was
already of myself such as he wished:
for my sight, becoming clear,
was entering more and more through the ray
of the lofty light that is true of itself.
From this point onward my seeing was greater
than speech reveals, which yields to such a vision,
and memory yields to such excess.
As is someone who sees while dreaming,
for whom after the dream the imprinted feeling
remains, and the rest does not return to the mind,
such am I, for almost wholly fades
my vision, and still there trickles for me
into the heart the sweetness born of it.
Thus snow loses its seal beneath the sun;
thus in the wind, on the light leaves,
the Sibyl's oracle was lost.
O highest light that rise so far
above mortal conceptions, to my mind
restore a little of what you appeared,
and make my tongue so powerful,
that a single spark of your glory
I may leave to future people;
for, by returning somewhat to my memory
and by sounding a little in these verses,
more will be understood of your triumph.
I believe, through the sharpness I endured
of the living ray, that I would have been lost,
if my eyes had turned away from it.
I remember that I was the bolder
for this in enduring, so that I joined
my gaze with the infinite power.
Oh abundant grace through which I dared
to fix my gaze through the eternal light,
so far that I spent all my sight upon it!
Within its depth I saw gathered inward,
bound with love into one volume,
what throughout the universe lies in scattered pages:
substances and accidents and their relations
as though fused together, in such a manner
that what I say is a mere glimmer.
The universal form of this knot
I believe I saw, because more abundantly,
in saying this, I feel myself rejoice.
One single instant is greater oblivion for me
than twenty-five centuries are for the enterprise
that made Neptune marvel at Argo's shadow.
Thus my mind, wholly suspended,
gazed fixedly, motionless and intent,
and ever grew more ardent in its gazing.
In that light one becomes such
that to turn from it toward any other sight
it is impossible ever to consent;
because the good, which is the object of the will,
is wholly gathered in it, and outside it
what is perfect there is deficient.
Now my speech will fall shorter,
even of what I remember, than that of an infant
who still bathes his tongue at the breast.
Not because more than one simple appearance
was in the living light that I beheld,
which is always such as it was before;
but through the sight that was gaining strength
within me as I gazed, one single appearance,
as I changed, was transformed for me.
Within the deep and clear subsistence
of the lofty light three circles appeared to me
of three colors and of one extent;
and one from the other, like rainbow from rainbow,
seemed reflected, and the third seemed fire
that is breathed equally from here and there.
Oh how short is speech and how faint
beside my conception! And this, beside what I saw,
is such that to call it ‘little’ is not enough.
O eternal light that dwell solely in yourself,
alone understand yourself, and understood by yourself
and understanding, love and smile upon yourself!
That circling which, thus conceived,
appeared in you as reflected light,
by my eyes for a while surveyed,
within itself, in its very own color,
seemed to me painted with our likeness:
therefore my gaze was wholly fixed upon it.
As is the geometer who wholly applies himself
to measuring the circle, and does not find,
through thought, that principle which he needs,
such was I at that new sight:
I wished to see how there fitted
the image to the circle, and how it finds its place there;
but my own wings were not equal to this:
except that my mind was struck
by a flash in which its desire was fulfilled.
Here the lofty imagination lacked power;
but already was turning my desire and will,
like a wheel that is moved evenly,
the love that moves the sun and the other stars.
