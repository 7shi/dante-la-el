O scant nobility of our blood,
if you make people glory in you
down here where our affections languish,
it will never be a wondrous thing to me:
for there where desire does not go astray,
I mean in heaven, I gloried in you.
Truly you are a mantle that quickly shortens:
so that, unless something is added day by day,
time goes around it with the shears.
With the ‘you’ that was first offered at Rome,
in which its people persevere the least,
my words began again;
whereupon Beatrice, who stood a little apart,
smiling, seemed like the woman who coughed
at Guinevere's first recorded transgression.
I began: “You are my father;
you give me all boldness to speak;
you raise me so that I am more than myself.
Through so many streams is filled with gladness
my mind, that it rejoices in itself
because it can bear so much without breaking.
Tell me then, my dear first forebear,
who were your ancestors and what were the years
that were numbered in your childhood;
tell me of the sheepfold of Saint John,
how large it was then, and who were the people
within it worthy of the highest seats.”
As quickens at the blowing of the winds
a coal into flame, so I saw that
light shine brighter at my tender words;
and as it grew more beautiful to my eyes,
so with a voice more sweet and gentle,
but not in this modern speech,
he said to me: “From the day when ‘Hail’ was spoken
to the birth in which my mother, who is now holy,
was delivered of me, with whom she was heavy,
to its Lion five hundred and fifty
and thirty times this fire came
to kindle itself again beneath its paw.
My ancestors and I were born in the place
where the last ward is first encountered
by him who runs in your annual race.
Let it suffice to hear this about my forebears:
who they were and whence they came there,
it is more fitting to keep silent than to speak.
All those who were there at that time
fit to bear arms between Mars and the Baptist,
were a fifth of those who are now alive.
But the citizenry, which is now mixed
with Campi, Certaldo, and Figline,
could be seen pure down to the humblest craftsman.
Oh, how much better it would be to have as neighbors
those people I speak of, and at Galluzzo
and at Trespiano to have your boundary,
than to have them within and endure the stench
of the peasant of Aguglione, of him of Signa,
who already has a sharp eye for corruption!
If the people who stray furthest in the world
had not been a stepmother to Caesar,
but kindly as a mother to her son,
One has become a Florentine, and changes money and trades,
who would have turned back to Simifonti,
there where his grandfather went begging;
Montemurlo would still belong to the Counts;
the Cerchi would be in the parish of Acone,
and perhaps in Valdigreve the Buondelmonti.
Always the mingling of people
was the beginning of the city's sickness,
as added food is of yours;
and a blind bull falls more swiftly
than a blind lamb; and often cuts
more and better one sword than five.
If you consider Luni and Orbisaglia,
how they have passed away, and how are passing
after them Chiusi and Sinigaglia,
to hear how families die out
will not seem to you a strange or difficult thing,
since cities themselves have an end.
All your things have their death,
just as you do; but it is hidden in some
that last a long time, and lives are short.
And as the turning of the heaven of the moon
covers and uncovers the shores without pause,
so Fortune does with Florence:
therefore it should not seem a wondrous thing,
what I shall say of the great Florentines
whose fame is hidden in time.
I saw the Ughi and saw the Catellini,
Filippi, Greci, Ormanni, and Alberichi,
already in decline, illustrious citizens;
and I saw, as great as they were ancient,
with those of la Sannella, those of l'Arca,
and Soldanieri and Ardinghi and Bostichi.
Above the gate that at present is laden
with new treachery of such weight
that soon it will be the ruin of the boat,
were the Ravignani, from whom descended
Count Guido and whoever the name
of the noble Bellincione has since taken.
He of la Pressa already knew how
one ought to govern, and Galigaio had
already in his house a gilded hilt and pommel.
Great already was the column of vair,
Sacchetti, Giuochi, Fifanti and Barucci
and Galli and those who blush for the bushel.
The stock from which the Calfucci sprang
was already great, and already had been raised
to the curule chairs the Sizii and Arrigucci.
Oh, how great I saw those who are undone
through their pride! And the balls of gold
adorned Florence in all its great deeds.
So did the fathers of those
who, whenever your bishop's seat is vacant,
grow fat by sitting in consistory.
The overweening stock that becomes a dragon
behind one who flees, and toward one who shows a tooth
or else a purse, grows gentle as a lamb,
was already rising, but from humble people;
so that it displeased Ubertin Donato
that his father-in-law later made him their kinsman.
Already Caponsacco had into the marketplace
come down from Fiesole, and already was
a good citizen Giuda, and Infangato.
I shall tell a thing incredible and true:
one entered the little circuit of walls through a gate
that was named after those of la Pera.
Each one who bears something of the beautiful emblem
of the great baron whose name and worth
the feast of Thomas renews,
received knighthood and privilege from him;
although he joins himself with the popular party
today, the one who borders it with a band.
Already there were Gualterotti and Importuni;
and Borgo would still be more peaceful,
if they had gone without new neighbors.
The house from which your weeping sprang,
through the just indignation that has slain you
and put an end to your happy life,
was honored, it and its allied families:
O Buondelmonte, how disastrously you fled
its marriage alliance at others' urging!
Many would be happy who are sad,
if God had yielded you to the Ema
the first time you came to the city.
But it was fitting for that mutilated stone
which guards the bridge, that Florence should make
a victim in its last days of peace.
With these people, and with others beside them,
I saw Florence in such repose
that it had no cause to weep.
With these people I saw glorious
and just its people, so that the lily
was never set reversed upon a spear,
nor made vermilion through division.”
