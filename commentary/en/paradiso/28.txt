After she who, against the present life
of wretched mortals, revealed the truth,
she who imparadises my mind,
as in a mirror the flame of a double candle
is seen by one who is lit by it from behind,
before he has it in sight or in thought,
and he turns to see whether the glass
tells him the truth, and sees that it agrees
with it as a note with its measure;
so my memory recalls
that I did, gazing into the beautiful eyes
from which Love made the cord to capture me.
And when I turned and my eyes were touched
by what appears in that sphere,
whenever one looks closely into its circling,
I saw a point that radiated light
so piercing that the sight it sets aflame
must close because of the intense sharpness;
and whatever star appears smallest from here
would seem a moon, if placed beside it
as star is placed beside star.
Perhaps as close as a halo seems
to encircle the light that paints it
when the vapor that bears it is thickest,
at such a distance around the point a circle of fire
revolved so swiftly that it would have surpassed
that motion which most swiftly girdles the world;
and this was encircled by another,
and that by the third, and the third then by the fourth,
the fourth by the fifth, and then the fifth by the sixth.
Above followed the seventh, already so spread
in breadth that Juno's messenger
in its entirety would be too narrow to contain it.
So too the eighth and the ninth; and each
moved more slowly, according as it was
farther in number from the first;
And that one had the purest flame
from which the pure spark was least distant,
I believe, because it partakes more of its truth.
My lady, who saw me in anxious thought
deeply perplexed, said: “From that point
depend heaven and all nature.
Look at that circle which is most closely joined to it;
and know that its movement is so swift
because of the burning love by which it is impelled”.
And I to her: “If the world were arranged
in the order that I see in those wheels,
what has been set before me would have satisfied me;
but in the sensible world one can
see the spheres as so much more divine,
the farther they are from the center.
Therefore, if my desire is to find its end
in this wondrous and angelic temple
that has only love and light for its bounds,
I must still hear how the copy
and the model do not follow the same course,
for on my own I contemplate this in vain”.
“If your fingers are not sufficient for such a knot,
it is no wonder:
so tight has it grown for lack of trying!”
Thus my lady; then she said: “Take in
what I shall tell you, if you wish to be satisfied;
and sharpen your understanding upon it.
The bodily circles are broad and narrow
according to the greater or lesser power
that spreads throughout all their parts.
Greater goodness wills to bring greater benefit;
a greater body holds greater benefit,
if its parts are equally perfect.
Therefore this one, which sweeps along the whole
remaining universe with itself, corresponds
to the circle that loves most and knows most:
therefore, if around the power you fit
your measure, not around the appearance
of the substances that appear round to you,
you will see a wondrous correspondence
of greater to more and of smaller to less,
in each heaven, to its intelligence”.
As splendid and serene remains
the hemisphere of air, when blows
Boreas from that cheek where he is gentler,
so that the murky haze is cleared and scattered
which troubled it before, and thus the sky smiles
with the beauties of every region of its realm;
so did I, after she had furnished me,
my lady, with her clear reply,
and like a star in heaven the truth was seen.
And after her words had ceased,
iron throws off sparks no differently
when seething, than the circles sparkled.
Each spark followed its own blaze;
and they were so many that their number
runs into thousands beyond the doubling of the chessboard.
I heard hosannas from choir to choir
to the fixed point that holds them in their places,
and will forever, where they have always been.
And she who saw the doubtful thoughts
within my mind said: “The first circles
have shown you Seraphim and Cherubim.
So swiftly they follow the bonds that bind them,
to resemble the point as much as they can;
and they can as far as they are exalted in vision.
Those other loves that go around them
are called Thrones of the divine countenance,
and thus they brought the first triad to its end;
and you must know that all have delight
according as their vision plunges deep
into the truth in which every intellect finds rest.
From this one can see how is founded
being blessed in the act that sees,
not in that which loves, which follows afterward;
and merit is the measure of seeing,
which grace brings forth, and good will:
thus one proceeds from degree to degree.
The other triad, which thus buds
in this everlasting spring
which nocturnal Aries does not strip bare,
perpetually warbles ‘Hosanna’
with three melodies, which sound in three
orders of joy into which it is divided.
In that hierarchy are the other divine beings:
first Dominions, and then Virtues;
the third order is that of Powers.
Then in the two next-to-last rejoicings
Principalities and Archangels circle;
the last is wholly composed of angelic festivities.
These orders all gaze upward in wonder,
and so prevail over those below that toward God
all are drawn and all draw.
And Dionysius with such great desire
set himself to contemplate these orders,
that he named and distinguished them as I do.
But Gregory later differed from him;
therefore, as soon as he opened his eyes
in this heaven, he laughed at himself.
And if such secret truth was uttered
by a mortal on earth, I would not have you marvel:
for he who saw it up here disclosed it to him
with much else of the truth about these circles”.
