Absorbed in his delight, that contemplative
freely assumed the office of teacher,
and he began these holy words:
“The wound that Mary closed and anointed,
she who is so beautiful at her feet
is she who opened it and pierced it.
In the rank formed by the third seats,
Rachel sits below this woman
with Beatrice, as you see.
Sarah and Rebecca, Judith and she
who was great-grandmother to the singer who, in sorrow
for his sin, said ‘Have mercy on me’,
you can see thus, from tier to tier,
descending, as I, naming each by her own name,
go down through the rose from petal to petal.
And from the seventh step downward, just as
down to it, Hebrew women follow one another,
dividing all the flower's foliage;
because, according to the gaze that
faith in Christ directed, these women are the wall
at which the sacred tiers divide.
On this side where the flower is mature
in all its petals, are seated
those who believed in Christ yet to come;
on the other side, where broken
by empty places are the semicircles, sit
those who turned their eyes to Christ already come.
And as on this side the glorious seat
of the lady of heaven and the other seats
beneath it make so great a division,
so, opposite, that of the great John,
who, holy throughout, the wilderness and martyrdom
endured, and then hell for two years;
and beneath him were likewise assigned to divide
Francis, Benedict, and Augustine
and others down to here, from circle to circle.
Now behold the lofty providence of God:
for both aspects of the faith
will fill this garden equally.
And know that below the tier that cuts
the two dividing lines halfway along their course,
one sits through no merit of one's own,
but through another's, under certain conditions:
for all these are spirits released
before they had true powers of choice.
You can clearly discern it from their faces
and also from their childish voices,
if you look at them well and listen to them.
Now you doubt, and doubting keep silent;
but I shall loosen the strong bond
in which your subtle thoughts constrain you.
Within the vastness of this realm
no chance event can have a place,
any more than sorrow or thirst or hunger:
for by eternal law is established
whatever you see, so that fittingly
the ring here corresponds to the finger;
and therefore these people, hastened
to true life, are not without cause
more and less exalted here among themselves.
The King through whom this kingdom rests
in so much love and so much delight
that no will dares aspire to more,
all minds in his joyous presence,
creating, he endows with grace at his pleasure
differently; and here let the effect suffice.
And this is expressly and clearly shown to you
in Holy Scripture in those twins
who had their anger stirred within their mother.
Therefore, according to the color of the hair,
of such grace, the highest light
must fittingly be worn as a crown.
Thus, without merit from their own conduct,
they are placed in different ranks,
differing only in their initial keenness.
In the earliest ages there sufficed,
together with innocence, for obtaining salvation,
only the faith of the parents;
after the earliest ages were completed,
males had to, for their innocent wings,
acquire strength through circumcision;
but after the time of grace came,
without the perfect baptism of Christ,
such innocence was held down below.
Look now upon the face that to Christ
is most like, for its brightness
alone can prepare you to see Christ”.
I saw upon her so much gladness
rain down, borne in the holy minds
created to fly across those heights,
that all I had seen before
had not held me in such wonder,
nor shown me so much likeness to God;
and that love which first descended there,
singing ‘Hail, Mary, full of grace’,
spread its wings before her.
The divine song was answered
from every side by the blessed court,
so that every face thereby grew more serene.
“O holy father, who for my sake endure
being down here, leaving the sweet place
in which you sit by eternal allotment,
who is that angel who with so much joy
looks into the eyes of our queen,
so filled with love that he seems made of fire?”
Thus I turned again to the teaching
of him who drew beauty from Mary,
as the morning star does from the sun.
And he to me: “Boldness and grace,
as much as can be in angel or in soul,
are all in him; and so we wish it to be,
for he is the one who bore the palm
down to Mary, when the Son of God
willed to take on the burden of our flesh.
But now follow with your eyes as I
go on speaking, and note the great patricians
of this most just and holy empire.
Those two who sit up there more blessed
through being nearest to the Empress,
are, as it were, two roots of this rose:
He who sits beside her on the left
is the father through whose daring taste
the human race tastes so much bitterness;
on the right you see that ancient father
of Holy Church to whom Christ the keys
entrusted of this lovely flower.
And he who saw all the grievous times,
before he died, of the lovely bride
who was won with the lance and with the nails,
sits beside him, and beside the other rests
that leader under whom lived on manna
the ungrateful, fickle, and rebellious people.
Opposite Peter you see Anne seated,
so happy to gaze upon her daughter,
that she does not move her eyes while singing hosanna;
and opposite the greatest father of a family
sits Lucy, who moved your lady
when you lowered your eyes toward ruin.
But because the time that holds you in sleep is fleeing,
here we shall stop, like a good tailor
who makes the gown according to the cloth he has;
and we shall direct our eyes to the primal love,
so that, looking toward him, you may penetrate
as far as possible through his radiance.
Yet, lest perhaps you fall backward,
moving your wings, believing you advance,
through prayer, grace must be obtained,
grace from her who can help you;
and you will follow me with your affection,
so that you do not part your heart from my words”.
